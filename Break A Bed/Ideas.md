# Break A Bed: Ideas

#break-a-bed #ideas

Back to [[Break A Bed/Project Overview|Project Overview]].

- **Upgrade shop:** spend coins on more damage per click (the Chop A Brick-style coin sink).
- **Tools / weapons:** pillows, hammers, chainsaws, each with its own damage and hit effect.
- **Pets** that hit beds for you.
- **Better sounds:** real hit, break and coin sounds from the Creator Store.
- **Boss bed:** a giant shared bed that the whole server breaks together for a big payout.
- **Gamepasses:** 2× coins, auto-clicker, faster respawn.
