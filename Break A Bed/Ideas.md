# Break A Bed: Ideas

#break-a-bed #ideas

- **Rebirths:** reset Cash and Strength for a permanent Strength multiplier (was in the first version, dropped in the rebuild).
- **Better sounds:** real chop, splinter and cash sounds from the Creator Store.
- **Karate animation:** a real uploaded chop animation (and a stance) instead of the code-made one.
- **More training items** past the Diamond Block, and more beds past the Cosmic Bed.
- **Pets** that chop beds for you.
- **Boss bed:** a giant shared bed that the whole server breaks together for a big payout.
- **Gamepasses:** 2× Strength, 2× Cash, auto-chop.
- **More leaderboards** (highest Level, most beds completed) next to the Total Damage one (done 2026-10-10).
