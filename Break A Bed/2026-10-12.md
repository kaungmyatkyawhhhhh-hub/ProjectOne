## Free Rewards #done
Built all 3 parts of [[Break A Bed/Free Rewards/Free Rewards|Free Rewards]], matching `preview-free-chest.png` and `preview-free-gift.png`.
- **FREE chest:** a real model `Workspace.FreeChest` (red chest with gold bands, a lock and a green gem) on a glowing green pad, on the paving left of the VIP lounge. Big bobbing green "FREE!" above it. Walk onto the pad: the lid swings open, Cash flies to your counter (20 breaks of your best bed, at least $500), then "Next chest in 14:59". The timer is saved (`ChestAt`), so rejoining doesn't skip it. The server checks you're really on the pad.
- **"Watch Ad To Collect" board** above the chest: green studded frame, tilted "CLICK TO CLAIM", "Watch Ad To Collect:", a big italic green amount (100 breaks, at least $2.5K, live), `money-medium` art and a play arrow. Uses Roblox rewarded video ads (`AdService`): it only shows when Roblox says an ad is available, and the reward comes through ProcessReceipt as a developer product. One every 10 minutes (`AdAt`, saved). **Hidden until the owner makes the ad's developer product** (see below).
- **Free Gift popup:** cyan "Free Gift!" title, `money-big` pile with a glow, big red "~ LIMITED TIME! ~", huge green "+$50K CASH!", the 3 step cards with red arrows (drawn thumbs-up, a yellow **Favorite** button that opens Roblox's favorite prompt, a blue **Join** button that opens the join-group prompt), and a green **Claim!**. Only the group join is checked (Myanmar Builders Club, on the server); otherwise "Join the group first!". Gives Cash (50 breaks, at least $50K) + x2 Strength 15 min + the Group Reward, once per account. It pops up once after the guide (after the daily reward closes) and can be reopened from Free Gifts ("Free Gift!" button) or the "Join group" line until claimed.
- New: client module `FreeRewards`; `GameConfig.FreeChest`, `AdReward`, `WelcomeGift`; saved `ChestAt`, `AdAt`, `WelcomeGiftClaimed`, `WelcomeGiftShown`.
- Tested with a fresh save: the popup waits for the guide and the daily reward, Claim gave $50K + x2 Strength 15:00 + Group Bonus; the chest opened and gave $500 with "Next chest in 14:38". No errors. Studio says a test ad is available. The ad itself can't be tested until the product exists.
