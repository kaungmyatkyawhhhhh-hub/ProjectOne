## First campaign (2026-10-11)
Roblox Ads Manager, experience "[🎉 RELEASE] Break a Bed".
- **Goal:** Plays. **Audience:** All players (370M–450M).
- **Budget:** 16 Ad Credit a day, 3 days. Ad Credit balance was 0, so credit has to be bought before it runs.
- Claude's advice: buy the credit, run 7 days instead of 3 so Roblox has time to learn, leave Auto-Reload off (the 9:00 AM Chicago start = 8:30 PM the owner's time, which is fine; ads spread over the whole day anyway).

## Results (2026-10-12)
- The owner spent **12K Robux** on ads.
- The game earns about **500 Robux a day** so far.
- Robux spent in the game so far (2026-10-12): **R$537** — R$501 from passes, R$36 from developer products. Two big spikes (~R$200 and ~R$250 in an hour) on 10/11 night and 10/12 morning.
- Day 1 retention showed **0%** on 2026-10-12. The game had only been out about 2 days, so the number may not be ready yet.
