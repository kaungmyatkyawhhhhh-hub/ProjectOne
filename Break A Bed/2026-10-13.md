# 2026-10-13

#break-a-bed

Two changes from the notes: the lower Sound Effects default ([[Break A Bed/Settings|Settings]]) and the left side of the screen staying together, with a phone layout ([[Break A Bed/UI Kit/UI Kit|UI Kit]]).

## Sound Effects default 40% #done
- `GameConfig.DefaultSfxVolume = 40` (was 85) and `DefaultMusicVolume = 60`. The Settings menu reads them from there.
- **Old saves:** a save whose Sound Effects is still exactly 85 (the old default, never changed) is moved to 40 once. Every save now stores `SfxDefaultV2 = true`, so it never happens again. Players who picked their own value keep it.

## Left side stays together + phone layout #done
- **One `LeftColumn`:** Shop, Boosts, the Auto Punch switch and the stats (Strength, Cash, Level, Group Bonus) are in one column with an 18 px gap between the buttons and the stats. It has its own ScreenGui with `CoreUISafeInsets`, clear of Roblox's top bar.
- **PC:** the column is centred on the left and scales with the screen (`screen height / 900`, between 0.6 and 1.2). At 1080p the buttons sit right above the stats; there's no more big gap.
- **Phones:**
  - The column is at the top-left under Roblox's top bar, and always stays above the thumbstick zone (the bottom 45%, or higher if Roblox's thumbstick is higher). It shrinks down to 0.6. On short phones, Shop and Boosts go side by side so the column fits.
  - Stat icons are about 60% size, and Group Bonus is hidden.
  - The stats aren't clickable, so a touch there still moves the character.
- **Right squares on phones:** about 70 px at the top-right, never down by the jump button. If one column doesn't fit above it, they use 2 columns, shrinking a little if needed.
- **Phone break / training screen:** the bed or post panel and the hint are at the top centre, EXIT (and AUTO) at the top right, and the right squares hide while you're breaking.
- **Toasts and the pad hint on phones:** moved up to the middle of the screen, away from the bottom corners.
- **Tested in the Device Emulator, landscape, every menu closed:**

  | Device | Left column / zone top | Squares / jump top | Walk | Jump |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | iPhone SE (iPhone 7 size) | ends y 186 / 206 | 2 columns, end 260 / 284 | 32 studs | yes |
  | iPhone 16 Pro Max | ends y 200 / 241 | 2 columns, end 328 / 349 | 33 studs | yes |
  | Samsung Galaxy A06 | ends y 178 / 185 | 2 columns, end 249 / 306 | 33 studs | yes |
  | iPad 10th gen | ends y 335 / 450 | 1 column, full size | 25 studs | yes |
  | PC 1080p | together on the middle-left | 1 column | – | – |

  The phone break screen was checked on the iPhone SE size. No errors.
- **Not tested:** a tall portrait window (the game is landscape on phones), and a real phone after publishing.

## Left side v2: Shop/Boosts in the middle, stats back, phone sizes #done
Replaces the "LeftColumn" version above (owner: the stats shouldn't move). Notes: [[Break A Bed/UI Kit/UI Kit|UI Kit]], "Left side: Shop and Boosts in the middle of the left edge" and "Phone sizes".
- **Stats back** where they were before LeftColumn: PC bottom-left, same size and anchoring. Phones: see below.
- **LEVEL** now sits on top of the bar's left end (PC and phone); the bar moved down a few pixels so it fits.
- **Shop / Boosts (+ Auto Punch switch)** are in a `LeftButtons` frame at the vertical middle of the left edge, on every screen. It's scaled by screen height / 900 (0.6–1.2), and on phones the buttons are 10% of the screen height. It only moves up when it would touch something: the thumbstick (20 px gap) on touch screens, or the stats on a very short PC window.
- **Phones (under 500 px tall), PC unchanged:**
  - Squares are 11–12% of the screen height (at most 48 px), badges and labels scale with them. They sit in a column on the right edge, vertically centred, and use 2 columns when 5 don't fit above the jump button. Never a row across the top.
  - Stat icons are 7% of the screen height.
  - **What I changed on the stats (phones only):** if they went back to their old phone spot (top-left, about 150 px down) they would sit on the buttons, which are now in the middle. So on touch screens they sit at the top-left right under Roblox's top bar, above the buttons and far from the thumbstick. Group Bonus is hidden there, so the stats stay small and nothing in them takes touches (the rest are plain labels).
- **Tested** (Device Emulator, landscape, every menu closed):

  | Screen | Buttons | Squares | Stats icons | Walk | Jump |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | iPhone SE size (iPhone 7, 667×375) | 10%, 25 px above the stick | 12%, 2 columns, 20 px above jump | 7% | 43 studs (thumbstick) | yes |
  | iPhone 14 Pro Max size (13 Pro Max, 926×428) | 10% | 11%, 2 columns | 7% | 42 studs | yes |
  | Samsung Galaxy A06 | 10%, moved up 9 px for the stick | 11%, 2 columns | 7% | 42 studs | yes |
  | iPad 10th gen (touch, PC-like sizes) | middle-left | 1 column | – | 40 studs | yes |
  | PC 1080p | middle-left | 1 column | bottom-left | 23 studs (W) | yes (Space) |
  | Tall window 1080×1920 | middle-left | 1 column | bottom-left | 23 studs (W) | yes (Space) |

  No errors.
- **Top middle:** no buttons there now. The small "Next bed" bar (and boost timers / guide card) still sits in the top middle; it wasn't moved.
