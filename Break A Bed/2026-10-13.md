## Soft Shutdown #done
- **Resume save:** when a server closes (`BindToClose`), the game first notes where each player is: position and facing, the bed or post they're at, the Free Gifts time and claimed gifts. It saves everyone at once, waits for the saves, then moves them. Normal saves never include `Resume`, so a normal leave still starts at spawn with gifts reset.
- **`SoftShutdown` script:** sends everyone to a reserved "waiting room" server, which waits 5 s and sends them on to a new public server. Teleports retry up to 4 times. The waiting room never loads or saves. It's skipped in Studio, where teleports don't work. The best way is still the Creator Dashboard's "restart servers for updates"; this is the backup for "Shut Down All Servers".
- **Screen:** "UPDATING THE GAME..." (dark studded window, cyan title, turning gear, "You'll be back in a few seconds! Same spot, same progress."). It stays up through both teleports and fades out once you're in the new server.
- **Restore on join:** a Resume under 3 minutes old puts you back where you stood, or in front of your bed / post if that spot isn't safe. The Free Gifts timer and claimed gifts carry on. It's read from the save on the server only, and used once.
- **Welcome-back gift:** "Game updated! Welcome back! Thanks for waiting" plus x2 Strength for 5 min, once per update (`WelcomeBackVersion` = the place version).
- **Tested in Studio** (one player, real save): standing at the Ruby Bed, I ran the shutdown save, then rejoined. I was back at the Ruby Bed facing it, the Free Gifts timer carried on (44 -> 56 s), and the x2 Strength gift was active. The first try didn't restore; two later tries did, and I couldn't reproduce the first failure.
- **Not tested:** 2 players (the Studio tools here can only start one player), and the real teleports, which only work in a published game. Test after publishing: join on two devices, restart servers, check both come back in place.
- My Studio test put a Resume and the x2 Strength welcome gift into the owner's real save (harmless; the Resume is cleared by the next save).

## Thumbnail photo scene #done
- A separate scene for the game's icon and banner, kept in **ServerStorage › ThumbnailScene** (not in the published world). It's built about 6,000 studs away from the map, so the real map is untouched.
- **What's in it:** flat green grass, a plain blue sky with soft clouds, and the **Wooden Bed** split in half into a V with 6 pieces frozen mid-air. Next to it is an R15 character: 1.3x wide, classic face, Roblox's free "Brown Hair", black T-shirt, blue pants. He's posed mid-punch with Motor6D angles, so the right fist sits in the crack, with a small white flash and 5 short yellow lines.
- **Cameras:** `CamIcon` is square and close, with the top 20% sky. `CamBanner` is 16:9, with the bed and character on the right half and open sky and grass on the left for the title.
- **Switching:** run `require(game.ServerStorage.ThumbnailMode:Clone()).on("CamIcon")` or `.on("CamBanner")` in the command bar. It moves the scene into the world, switches to the thumbnail lighting (ClockTime 14, Brightness 3, a little Bloom, plain sky), hides the UI, picks the right screen shape in the Device Simulator ("Thumbnail Icon 1024" / "Thumbnail Banner 1920x1080") and puts the camera on that part. `.off()` puts the game's own lighting and skies back exactly and moves the scene back to ServerStorage.
- **Why `:Clone()`:** Studio keeps an old copy of a required module; the clone always runs the latest code.
