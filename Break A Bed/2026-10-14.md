## Rebirth makes discovered beds and posts black again #done
- Owner: "when you rebirth the things you have discovered become undiscovered again". Before, Rebirth kept the discovered lists on purpose.
- **Server** (`Rebirths.rebirth`): empties `DiscoveredBeds` and `DiscoveredPosts` (before the beds and posts are cleared, so the screens update together), except Bed 1 and Post 1, which always stay discovered. The saved lists follow, so it also holds after rejoining.
- **Client** (`Silhouettes`): when a discovered flag disappears, that bed or post turns into its black "???" silhouette again, one model per frame (no hitch on phones). Discovering them again plays the same reveal as the first time.
- **Tested** with a throwaway fresh save (StudioFreshSave on, then back off): 6 beds and 4 posts discovered, then a real REBIRTH press -> discovered beds {1}, posts {1}; black beds went from 19 to 24 and black posts from 13 to 16; the posts show "???" and the next-bed pill says "Buy the ???". Your real save was not used.

## Head Start pays out right away #done
- Owner: "when you buy start each run with 1K or 10K or 100K, if you have under that amount, you get it, not just at the next rebirth".
- **Server** (`Rebirths.buyUpgrade`): after buying a Head Start level, if your Cash is **below** the new amount it's topped up to that amount at once (never more than that, and Cash above it is left alone). A toast says "Head Start! Your Cash is now $1K". It still applies at every rebirth too. The top-up isn't counted in the money leaderboard's "total earned" (it isn't earned Cash).
- **Tested** on a throwaway fresh save (flag set back to false afterwards): level 1 with $300 -> $1,000; level 2 with $50,000 -> stays $50,000; level 3 with $5,000 -> $100,000. Tokens were taken correctly (6 -> 0).

## Free vs paid boosts #done
From [[Break A Bed/Charts Plan|Charts Plan]] › "Free vs paid boosts plan (2026-10-14)".
- **Free boosts are only x2, 15 min max.** They have their own timer (`Boost<Kind>FreeEnds`), and the server forces every free reward boost to x2 and at most 15 min (a free timer never holds more than 15 min). Free = gifts, chests, spins, codes, daily, quests, starter quests, the Boost Inventory, the update thank-you gift. Bought = anything through a purchase receipt.
- **Swapped in the config:** Day 7 x3 Strength 30 min + x2 Cash 30 min -> **10 min of Strength** (StrengthSeconds 600) + x2 Cash 15 min. Quests: "Punch 2,000" and "Break a new bed" x3 -> 2 min of Strength; "Train 15 min" x3 -> x2 15 min; "Claim 6 gifts" x2 Cash 20 -> 15 min. MEGA Gift x3 -> x2 15 min + 2 min of Strength. Boosts already saved in someone's Boost Inventory are used as x2 (15 min max).
- **Bought boosts stack on a free x2:** `GameConfig.ActiveBoost` = the best bought level x2 if a free x2 (or the SERVER x2) is running. Tested: free x2 + bought x3 = **x6**; when the free one ended, **x3** stayed.
- **"Boost ended!" toast** (client `BoostOffer`): when a free x2 runs out, "Boost ended! Get x3 for 10 min" with a BUY button (the x3 boost of the same kind), at most once every 20 min, gone after 8 s. Tested on PC and the phone emulator.
  - The plan said "x3 for 15 min", but the x3 products are 10 min and their shop pictures have "10 MIN" printed in the image, so I kept the products as they are and the toast shows the real minutes. Changing them to 15 min needs new product pictures (and is a price change), so it's the owner's call.
- **Shop BOOSTS:** a gold-edged strip at the top of the Strength boosts: "FREE x2 ▶ BUY x5" and "Bought boosts stack on your free x2!".
- Tested on a throwaway fresh save (StudioFreshSave back to false after; checked).
