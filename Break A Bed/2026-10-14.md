## Break and training camera: 3/4 side view #done
From [[Break A Bed/How It Works|How It Works]] › "Camera fix for breaking and training".
- **New client module `SessionCamera`** (GameClient calls it on Break/Train and EXIT). A fixed 3/4 view: behind the player (away from the bed / post), turned **45 degrees** to the side, a little above (looks down 17 degrees; 32 at the training posts, which stand close together). The distance is worked out from the bed's / post's size and the screen, so big beds pull the camera further back (Cardboard Bed ~12 studs, Diamond Bed ~20).
- **Never inside or behind things:** it checks the lines from the bed, the character and the middle to the camera spot. If something solid is in the way (a wall, the Diamond Bed's glass zone wall, a lamp, a fence) it tries the other side, then moves in front of it. At a bed it also stays over the bed path, never out over the fence and grass.
- **Clear of the panels:** on PC the health panel and EXIT are at the bottom, so the character and bed sit in the upper part. On phones they're at the top, so the character and bed sit in the lower part. It reads where the panels really are on screen. The JOIN GROUP button hides while breaking / training.
- **Glides** in and out in 0.4 s (Quad Out); the small hit shake and zoom kick still play.
- **Not in the game:** there's no "Pillow Bed". I used the Cardboard Bed (bed 1, the smallest) and the Diamond Bed (bed 10, the big one).
- Tested on a throwaway fresh save, PC and the phone emulator (StudioFreshSave back to false, checked).
