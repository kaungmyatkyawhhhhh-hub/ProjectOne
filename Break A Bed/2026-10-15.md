## Hidden zones behind a cloud wall #done
From [[Break A Bed/How It Works|How It Works]] "Hidden zones in the clouds" (with all the owner's corrections) and Content Plan 11b. Client module `CloudZones`; server keeps `FurthestZone`.
- **Rule:** you see every zone you meet the Strength for, plus the next 2 (owner: at Strength 0 the wall is at **bed 4**). From there a solid cloud wall blocks the path.
- **Rebirth:** the wall does NOT come back. `FurthestZone` (saved with the Rebirth data, never reset) keeps it at the furthest spot ever reached; it only moves when Strength passes the old best.
- **Behind the wall is removed on your screen:** beds, tiles, props, signs, lamps and fences there are moved into a folder outside Workspace and come back on reveal; long pieces that run past the wall (path base, fence rails, grass) are cut off at the wall locally. So nothing shows from any angle.
- **Clouds:** a lumpy 3D blanket (our cloud / smoke sprites, no glow: LightEmission 0, LightInfluence 1) over the hidden part of the island only (island width + 6 studs each side), 25-40 studs tall, thickest right behind the wall, covering the top too.
- **Solid wall:** island width + 10 studs each side (94 studs), from below the floor to 60 up. Walking in = a soft cloud poof. Anyone already behind it goes back to spawn. Tested on the path and on the grass on both sides with jumping: blocked every time.
  - (An earlier test seemed to get past on the grass: the player had actually walked off the island edge and fallen, since the island floats. The wall now matches the island.)
- **Sign written on the cloud** (owner): a SurfaceGui on a flat invisible part against the front of the wall, not a billboard, not AlwaysOnTop: "🔒 Reach 💪 7" in dark blue-grey on a soft white cloud backing, gently bobbing, fading in only between 45 and 30 studs. The last visible bed's name tag hides while it shows. From spawn you see only clouds.
- **Camera:** zoom-out capped at 45 studs; the camera can't pass through the wall.
- **Revealing:** reaching the next zone's need moves the wall one zone: the clouds there blow apart (1.5 s; 0.6 s for beds broken before), a whoosh + bright chime, a gentle camera push (FOV -6, not while breaking / training), "NEW ZONE REVEALED!" with the bed name ("???" until discovered).
- **Belt light column:** if your next belt test is behind the wall, its column rises above the clouds (12 to 60 studs).
- **Intro (~7 s, Tap to skip):** glides low over the open zones up to the wall, rises over the sea of clouds (every hidden zone covered, the black-belt light far away), swoops to Sensei (eyes glow, gong, "Become a BLACK BELT!"), glides behind you. The coloured glows inside the clouds are mostly hidden by the cloud shapes; they could be brighter or sit higher.
- Built step by step as the owner sent corrections: first a cloud bank that faded in per zone, then the solid wall at 3 ahead (moved to bed 4), the sign as a cloud SurfaceGui, full map width (then cut back to the island), and the final fix (clouds only on the island, no glow, hidden things removed, camera limits).
