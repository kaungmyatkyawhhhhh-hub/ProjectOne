## Training #done
- **Auto train = the fastest punch rate** (the same as the fastest clicking), so clicking is never faster. The "Click to punch 2x faster!" hint is gone.
- **Bicep popups:** every punch pops a yellow bicep with the real gain ("+4", "+1.2K") that floats up to the side and fades (~0.8 s). It looked squished at first (owner); now it keeps its own shape.
- **Multiplier cubes** on your pad every 4-6 s, staying 6 s, bobbing and turning: **X2** green 70%, **X3** blue 25%, **X5** purple 5% (sparkles, "RARE!"). Click / tap one = 10 s of that multiplier on training Strength; only the highest counts. A "X2 10s" chip shows the timer. The server picks the cubes and checks each collect, and only you see yours.
- **Free camera** while training (rotate and zoom while locked in place).

## Breaking beds #done
- **Camera from the front:** on the far side of the bed, a little above, looking at you with the bed in between.
- **New prompt look everywhere** (client `PromptStyle`): a white square key with a thick black border, then a big white outlined word ("Break", "Train", "Buy"). No panel. Tapping it works on phones.
- **Every hit:** chips in the bed's own colours fly off and bounce, a white hit-marker ring flashes, a tilted outlined damage number pops out, the bed jolts.
- **Health bar:** pink fill on a dark red track, "418K / 612K" in white, a red plus icon on the left, a white damage chunk that shrinks.
- **CRITICAL!** in an orange-to-red gradient with a thick outline and a big tilted number.
- **Breaks:** the bed shatters into many pieces cut from its parts (fall, bounce, fade after ~3 s); cash bills burst across the whole screen; 3-4 "+cash" hexagons pop and fly to the Cash counter.
- **No Strength lock:** every bed says "Break". A live estimate sits under the health bar ("~1h 26m": green under 1 min, yellow under 10, red above). Health refills when you leave. Over 60 s: **BREAK IT** with the live price and "Too slow? Break it now!" shows above the panel and stays (beside the panel it overlapped the right-hand buttons).

## Bed zones (25) #done
- `ServerStorage.BedZonesSetup.run()` built a `Zone` on every bed tile: a themed floor, 2-4 props in the tile corners (off the walkway, lower than the sign) and one ambient effect from our own sprites (boxes, pool + beach ball, campfire + tent, log pile + axe, toy blocks + teddy, water + duck, IV + monitor, red carpet + gold pillars, gold bars + coins, diamonds, emeralds, rubies, snowman + ice spikes, lava lines, floating crystals, neon grid, gears + robot, UFO, moon rocks + flag, small sun, starry floor + planets, nebula clouds, black hole, comets, rainbow floor with infinity rings).
- Client `BedZones`: an undiscovered bed's zone is dark and still; when the bed is first broken the colours fade in and the effect starts. Low Graphics turns the effects off.

## Sounds #done
- Each bed plays **its own break sound** + `pieces_break` when it breaks (punches keep the normal hit sound).
- **Combo notes:** one heavenly piano note per bed break (do re mi fa so la ti, high do, then do again), back to do after 15 s without a break. On the 8th: a star sparkle and "COMBO x8!".
