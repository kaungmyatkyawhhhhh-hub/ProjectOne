# GYM ARC - Mutations brainstorm (for the update after next)

Owner (5 Nov): "remove tints permanently cuz there's skins now", "wanna add mutations on the next next update and we need to brainstorm about mutations".

## Research: how the hits do it
Plants vs Brainrots (Sportskeeda, Nov 2025), which copies Grow a Garden / Steal a Brainrot:
- A mutation = a visual change + a multiplier. Mostly luck on creation; some only during a weather/event.
- Ladder (Brainrots, income): Gold 2x (4.45%), Diamond 3x (0.45%), Frozen 3x (7.5%), Ruby 4x, Neon 5x (0.1%), Rainbow 6x (0.8%), event ones 6-8.5x (Magma, Underworld, Upside Down, Pumpkin), Galactic 8x (0.005%, Galactic weather only).
- Why it works: you SEE it on the thing (show-off), rare = chase, events bring players back for event-only mutations.

## GYM ARC options
- **A. Muscle mutations (recommended core):** on a Growth Spurt, one muscle group can mutate. The muscle visibly changes (gold veins, diamond skin, magma cracks, neon glow) and that group gets an EXP multiplier. Shows on your body to everyone; fits lopsided builds; ties to skins (mutations show over any alter skin).
- **B. Trainer mutations:** personal trainers mutate (Gold/Rainbow trainer + aura + bonus). Proven pet-sim pattern; good second layer.
- **C. Event mutations:** some only during live events / gym "weather" (Heatwave -> Scorched, Blackout -> Neon, Gem event -> Ruby/Diamond). Brings players back.
- Rules: earnable only, not sold for Robux (paid random-item rules); odds shown; never removed on Hall of Fame? (decide); cards/genetics still stack.

## Owner decision (5 Nov)
- No trainer mutations: "trainers dont need mutations since they have evolutions". Option B dropped.
- Direction: A (muscle mutations) + C (event-only mutations). Open: Hall of Fame keep/lose, how they roll, one per muscle or several.
