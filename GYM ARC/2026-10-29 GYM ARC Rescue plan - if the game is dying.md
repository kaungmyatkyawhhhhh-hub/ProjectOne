# Rescue plan: what if GYM ARC dies by January?

Back to [[00 GYM ARC Home|GYM ARC Home]] · Full plan: [[2026-10-29 GYM ARC Master plan - one ladder]] · Decisions: [[2026-10-29 GYM ARC Every decision - the whole game]]

Owner, 29 Oct: "I think by January the game might die (there's a possibility)."

## Honest view
- Yes, it's possible. ~10-20 players online, D1 ~4%, a third gone by 5 minutes. Many gym games sit at 0-50 players (genre research).
- But "dead" isn't decided by January; it's decided by **whether the first 5 minutes and day 2 get better**. Roblox Home judges a game on the last 28 days, so a fixed game can be picked up again (Gym League fell ~99% and is rebuilding with weekly updates).
- **Big problem for measuring:** with so few new players a day, retention numbers jump around and we can't tell if a fix worked. We need **enough new players to measure** (roughly 100+ new players a day).

## So: a 4-week rescue sprint first, big builds only if it works
Order changed to put the things that move retention numbers first and push big builds behind a decision gate.

| Week (Sat) | Do | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 31 Oct | Hall of Fame launch + prompt 95 | Already built; gives current players something |
| 7 Nov | **91** (Future You, Road, short tutorial, GAINS number, tank top) + new thumbnail/icon in Thumbnail Personalization | The biggest leak (minute 0-5) + more clicks |
| 8-14 Nov | **Small ad test ($20-50, brother)** pointed at the new first 5 minutes | Buys enough new players to MEASURE (not to grow yet) |
| 14 Nov | **96** quick parts first: smooth curve, never shrink, mass monster goal, height every alter, "next big thing" label | Mid-game leak; mostly settings |
| 21 Nov | **GATE 1** (read the numbers, see below) + **101** star track if anyone reached star 2 | Decide before spending weeks on big builds |

## Gate 1 (21-22 Nov): read 4 numbers from the ad-test players
| Number | Before | "Working" |
|---|---|---|
| Reach the first Growth Spurt | ~50% | 60%+ |
| Still playing at 5 min | 33% | 40%+ |
| Come back day 2 (D1) | ~4% | 6%+ and rising |
| Session length | ? | rising |
- **Working →** continue the plan (the schedule in [[2026-10-29 GYM ARC The whole game plan (research loop)]]: 96+105, 107, 98, 92, 93...), bigger ad budget from the brother (deal option A), Winter Lifts in December.
- **Not working →** don't build big things. Look at the step funnel, fix the ONE worst step, change the thumbnail/icon, run one more small test (Gate 2, ~5 Dec).

## Gate 2 (~5-6 Dec)
- **Working now →** back to the plan.
- **Still not working →** **maintenance mode**: GYM ARC stays up with small weekly things (codes, Winter Lifts event, a cosmetic), no big builds. Time goes to the next game, built from day one with everything we learned (UI kit, ProfileStore saving, analytics funnels, trainers/rolls system, thumbnail template, the Playbook rules). A game in maintenance still earns and can be revived later.

## What we never do in a panic
- No wipes or resets of player data, no taking away owned things (Fisch, PS99).
- No pay-to-win to squeeze money out of the last players.
- No copying the newest trend clone (late clones rarely win; genre research).
- No long silence: even in maintenance, something small every week.

## Why this order
Every big feature (Golden Era Gym, Ladder, Feats, Run Cards) only helps players who already stay past minute 5 and come back. If the top of the funnel doesn't improve, those builds are wasted weeks. If it does, they're what keeps players for months.
