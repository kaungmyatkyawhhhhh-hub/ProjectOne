# Why players stop grinding (and what to add)

Back to [[00 GYM ARC Home|GYM ARC Home]] · Playbook: [[Roblox Playbook - Rules for every game]], [[Playbook notes - Top games art study (Oct 2026)]]

Owner, 29 Oct: "most of the players leave and only 4% stay because there's no point to keep grinding."
Checked against what GYM ARC already has (Features index, Growth Spurts, Unlock Guides, Rivals, Legends, Arm Wrestling, Strongman Yard, Analytics) so nothing here repeats finished work.

## What GYM ARC already has (don't rebuild)
- Locked menu tiles with "LOCKED · Unlocks at Growth Spurt 2" + teaser + NEW tag ([[GYM ARC - Unlock Guides]], 22 Oct). The owner's "locked buttons say when they unlock" idea is mostly DONE; only check every locked thing uses it.
- Growth Spurt card with "Next: Pro Gym", READY popup, 25/50/75% milestone coins, transformation scene.
- 3 rivals (Benny, Coral, Dante), 8 visiting legends, arm wrestling vs NPCs, posing shows, Strongman Showdown, leaderboards, statue, Hall of Fame, trainers, genetics, titles, daily rewards, quests, streaks, invites, friend boost, basketball court, skate park.

**So GYM ARC does not lack features. It has too many side things and too few reasons that FEEL worth it.**

## Diagnosis (why "there's no point")
1. **The biggest leak is in the first minutes, not the endgame.** The funnel showed **54% of new players leave before the first Growth Spurt** (26 Oct). They quit before they ever want anything. The "point" has to be visible in the first 30 seconds.
2. **Strength is never USED.** In top power sims your power opens things: +1 Muscle to Break Walls (stronger = break the next wall), Muscle Legends (punch bigger rocks, beat players), Steal An Egg / Grow a Garden (a giant prize you can't get yet). In GYM ARC getting stronger mostly changes a hidden number (Gains x1.25) and the muscles reset after each spurt.
3. **Rewards are mostly coins and titles.** Rivals, legends, arm wrestling, showdowns all pay coins + a title. Nobody looks at you differently. Kids grind for things others can SEE: a bigger body, an aura, gear, a crown, a statue.
4. **No single ladder.** 15+ side activities (basketball, skate, posing, arm wrestling, strongman, crews, genetics, trainers...) compete for attention. A new kid can't tell what the main goal is or who he's trying to become.
5. **Strength never meets other players.** Arm wrestling, the showdown and the rivals were only tested against NPCs. "I'm the strongest in this server" is the strongest feeling in gym games and it's missing.

## What to add (ranked by impact, simplest first)

### 1. "Future You" from second one (fixes the 54% leak)
- In the first minute, show the player who they'll become: a glowing silhouette/hologram of their own avatar at the next height next to the Growth Spurt card ("YOU at Growth Spurt 1: 4'4""), and at the tutorial's goal step show the 6'0" version.
- Dante (8'0") and the Hall of Fame statues are already in town: the tutorial should walk past one and say "This could be you."
- Progression Road (from the 29 Oct skill-tree talk): one screen, Growth Spurt 1 → 6 → Alters, each stop shows the body and what unlocks.

### 2. The Champion Ladder ("become THAT person")
- Turn the rivals + legends into ONE visible ladder of ~12 champions, each bigger and stronger than the last (reuse Benny, Coral, Dante and the 8 legends, add a few). Show them on a board in the plaza: portraits, their height and strength, locked ones as silhouettes.
- Each shows a clear requirement: "Beat Dante: needs 50,000 STRENGTH · you: 31,400 (63%)".
- Beating one gives something VISIBLE and useful, not just coins: his signature aura or gear (with a small stat boost), his pose, and his spot on the ladder ("#7 Dante → YOU"). The last champion = the Hall of Fame.
- This is exactly the owner's idea: after every level there's a new, bigger person to become.

### 3. Things in the world you're not strong enough for yet
- Big objects that need a strength number, visible from the start: a giant boulder blocking a secret gym door, a truck you can pull, a 1-ton atlas stone in the plaza, the Hall of Fame gate. Each has a sign: "NEEDS 25,000 STRENGTH" with your % under it.
- Lifting one = a big moment (camera, crowd cheer, server message) + something permanent (a door opens, your name on the stone).
- Cheap: mostly signs + one lift animation using the Strongman stations' code.

### 4. Rewards people can SEE
- Rule for every new reward: if others can't see it on you or in the world, it's not the main reward. Coins and titles become the bonus, not the prize.
- Visible rewards: auras and gear with small stat boosts (owner: auras + gear have stats, looks don't), height, champion crowns, your name on boards/stones, the statue.

### 5. Server strength crown + real arm wrestling
- The strongest player in the server wears a crown + aura and gets a server message when they take it ("Kai is now the STRONGEST in this server!"). Instant reason to keep training while others are around.
- Arm wrestling vs real players with a "challenge" button on any player (both must accept), wins count on a weekly board.

### 6. Collection book
- One "Collection" screen: trainers, auras, gear, titles, alter skins, champions beaten, objects lifted, with % complete and a reward per finished row. Top sims (Pet Sim 99, Fish It!, Grow a Garden) all have a collect-them-all long tail.

### 7. A reason for tomorrow and Saturday
- Daily: keep login + streak + quests (done). Add one daily "Champion of the day" rematch with a bonus.
- Weekly: the Saturday live event (prompt 90 system) with a limited aura/gear only available that week.

### 8. Make the main goal obvious (owner's call)
- Until Growth Spurt 2, keep side activities (basketball, skate park, crews) quiet: no guides, no popups, no HUD space. They stay in the world, they just don't compete with the ladder. Then introduce one per spurt as an "unlock" (this also gives each spurt a new desire).

## Build order (one update each, check D1 and the first-spurt funnel after each)
1. Future You preview + tutorial walk-past + Progression Road. (Fixes the early leak.)
2. Champion Ladder board + visible champion rewards (reuse rivals/legends).
3. Strength-gated objects (3-4 in town) + server crown.
4. Skill tree (stat nodes, aura + gear unlocks) + equip stat preview "Current → New".
5. Collection book, then real-player arm wrestling + weekly board.

## Targets to judge it
- First-spurt funnel: clearly more than half of new players reach the first Growth Spurt (26 Oct: about half left before it).
- D1 from ~4% toward 8-10% (Roblox median is ~10%), 5-minute retention ~45%+ before running ads (Playbook rule).
