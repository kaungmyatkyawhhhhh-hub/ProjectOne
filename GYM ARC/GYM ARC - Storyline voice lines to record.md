# GYM ARC - Storyline voice lines to record

Owner (1 Nov): "the voice overs sound so ai ... make them sound like voice actors". Roblox's text to speech can't do that, so record the lines with real voices (you, friends, or hired actors) and the game plays them instead. Any line without a recording keeps the TTS voice. See [[2026-11-01 GYM ARC Storyline built - bullies, arm wrestling, voices]].

## How
1. Record each line below as its own file (.ogg or .mp3, under 7 s, a quiet room, the phone close to the mouth). Name it by its number, e.g. `05 Benny.ogg`.
2. Kid-safe and in character: Benny cocky teen, Coral cool beach girl, Dante calm and big, Hank gruff, Magnus slow and deep, Vince a showman, Coach Dex warm but firm.
3. Upload them in Creator Hub > Development Items > Audio (in the group that owns GYM ARC). Copy each audio ID.
4. Send me the IDs ("05 = 1234567890"). I put them in `Config/Story` > `Story.Recorded`.
5. Say every line **exactly** as written (the captions show this text).
