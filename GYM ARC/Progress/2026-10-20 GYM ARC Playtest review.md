# 2026-10-20 GYM ARC Playtest review

Prompt: [[2026-10-20 GYM ARC Prompt - 17 Playtest like a real player]].

**How it was played:** Studio Play mode with a phone-size layout (844x390), as a new kid who reads little and follows only what the game shows. Nothing was fixed during the test, except one change the owner asked for halfway: the Shop now shows from the start (it waited for tutorial + 5 minutes).

**Coverage:** Run 1 (brand-new save, about 20 min) was played in full. Run 2 (Growth Spurt 2 save) was played only for its first minutes. Run 3 (7'0" max save) was not played: the owner ended the test. Skateboard and dumbbells were not reached in any run, so they are not reviewed.

**What the shop and menus felt like (Run 1):**
- Store opens on Robux passes (best seller: Auto Rep, R$249). Boosts are all Robux. Auras are mostly level, pack or VIP; the coin auras cost 50k / 150k (about 15+ hours at my earning rate).
- Trainers 300-800 coins. One tap hired one (+5% EXP) with no "are you sure".
- Inventory titles with rarities: clear and good.
- Poses work ("Move or jump to stop").
- Crew: creating one needs typing a name and tag; nothing to do in a crew alone.

## Run 2 (Growth Spurt 2 save, 4,000 coins; first minutes only)
- Skipping the tutorial opened the daily streak calendar by itself. No "what's new" message at spawn: a returning player isn't told what changed since the last spurt.
- Walking to the Pro gym, the path goes over the arm-wrestle stools and auto-seats you (touching a seat sits you down).
- At 1:41: "Welcome to the pro gym. 11 new machines, including Calves and Obliques." That's a good unlock moment, but there was no goal after it other than "fill the bar again".
- With 4,000 coins the only coin things to buy were trainers. Nothing else in the game takes coins until the 50k aura.

## 2. Top 10 BORING moments (worst first)
1. **Stuck at 0 stamina on the tutorial's shake step (about 2 min).** The instruction points at a button that is off-screen on a machine, and auto-reps keep stamina at 0. Many kids quit here.
2. **Stamina breaks every 30 s.** 16 reps, then wait. In Run 1, about 4 of the first 20 minutes were spent waiting for stamina. Popular simulators let you keep clicking for minutes before any limit.
3. **Bar stuck at 78% / 96% (about 75 s each).** The Growth Spurt needs muscles you aren't training, and nothing says which. It feels like the game froze.
4. **Walking (75 studs to the first machine, long walks between machines).** About 1 min of the first 10 min was walking, with nothing to collect on the way.
5. **8:10-11:55 with nothing new.** After the tutorial, almost 4 minutes of the same reps: no unlock, popup or body change.
6. **Weight tiers unlocked silently.** Five tiers unlocked with no message. The best reward moment of a lifting game was invisible.
7. **Arm wrestling against NPCs.** A short tap race with no clear win/lose moment and no reason to come back.
8. **Coins have nothing to buy.** 1,000 coins at 17 min, 4,000 in Run 2, and the next coin item is a 50k aura. Earning coins feels pointless.
9. **The first spurt READY moment is missed.** The card is off-screen while you're on a machine, so the best moment of the first 20 min goes unnoticed until you stand up.
10. **Genetics screen at 0:23.** Numbers and grades before the kid has lifted once. It's a wall of text where a big "you rolled RARE!" moment should be.

## 3. Top 10 things to ADD
1. **A "next goal" arrow / tracker always on screen, even on machines** (e.g. "Train LEGS: Squat Rack" with a glowing path). Fixes the stuck-bar problem; every big simulator always tells you the next thing. *Small-medium.*
2. **Weight tier unlock popup** ("NEW WEIGHT: 95 lb!" with a sound, plate art and a body pump). Cheap, and it gives a reward every 1-2 minutes. *Small.*
3. **Coin sinks that matter early:** cheap cosmetics (gym outfits, shaker colors, gloves, belt colors), machine skins, 1k-10k auras, gym decor for a crew area. Coins need a use in the first 20 minutes. *Medium.*
4. **Collectibles on the walk** (protein scoops / coins / gym tokens on the floor, respawning) so walking is not dead time. *Small.*
5. **A visible body change at every spurt milestone** with a before/after mirror moment ("Look at you now!"). The body is the game's best feature but is easy to miss. *Medium.*
6. **Rebirth-style goal after 7'0"** (e.g. "Legend" ranks that keep your height but add a star, a title and a permanent bonus), so a max player has something to chase. Needs owner design. *Big.*
7. **More rival challenges like Benny,** one per machine group, shown on the next-goal tracker. The best goal in the run. *Medium.*
8. **A "what's new" card after each spurt** (new machines, new gym, new weight, new title) instead of only a toast. *Small.*
9. **Arm wrestling ladder:** NPC opponents in a ladder with rewards and a clear win screen, then players. *Medium.*
10. **Friend/crew boost that works alone at first** (e.g. "Train next to any player: +10%") and a crew goal bar, so crews mean something without a full crew. *Medium.*

## 4. Top 10 things to CHANGE
1. **Stamina:** now about 30 s of lifting then a wait. Should be: about 2-3 minutes of lifting for a new player, and REP with no stamina still gives some progress (slower), never a dead stop.
2. **Tutorial shake step:** now points at an off-screen button. Should be: the shake button appears next to REP (or the step waits until the player stands up), with an arrow.
3. **Machine screen hides everything:** now the left menu and right cards are gone on machines. Should be: a small Growth Spurt bar and the next goal stay visible while lifting.
4. **Two progress meters:** now the physique panel % and the Growth Spurt card % differ (27% vs 61%). Should be: one number everywhere.
5. **Spurt READY:** now no popup on a machine. Should be: a big full-screen "READY TO GROW!" with sound, wherever you are.
6. **Store default tab:** now opens on Robux passes, and boosts are Robux only. Should be: open on things coins can buy; keep passes on their own tab. Fair for kids and better for trust.
7. **Coach vs notification:** now they give different next steps at the same time. Should be: one voice, one instruction.
8. **Start-screen clutter:** now 7 buttons + 3 cards + 2 event messages + tutorial at once. Should be: during the tutorial, show only the buttons it has explained; event messages wait until after.
9. **Quests:** names are cut off and a spotting quest can't be done alone. Should be: full names (two lines), and solo-possible quests for the first day.
10. **Trainer hire:** one tap spends coins with no confirm. Should be: a confirm with the price and what it does.

## 6. Bugs and phone UI problems
**Bugs**
1. **Shake step soft-lock.** New save > first machine > keep lifting until stamina is 0 > the tutorial asks for the shake. The right column is off-screen on machines and the coach line is hidden (`CoachLine` Visible = false, placed off-screen). Only STOP gets you out.
2. **Stamina never refills on a machine with auto-reps on.** Auto-reps keep using it, so it stays at 0; the "Out of stamina" message stays on screen.
3. **STATS floating numbers stay on.** Tap STATS: the numbers float over the UI and don't go away.
4. **Overlapping E prompts.** Walk from the arm-wrestle table toward Coach Dex and press E: you sit at the table.
5. **Seats grab you when walking over them.** Path to the Pro gym over the arm-wrestle stools: you are auto-seated.
6. **Jump doesn't stand you up** from the arm-wrestle seat (only STAND UP).
7. **Two meters disagree** (physique 27% vs Growth Spurt card 61% at the same moment).
8. **Silent weight unlocks** (45 lb to 155 lb with no message).
9. **Output:** `ContentId: rbxassetid://203785492 failed due to: Asset type does not match requested type` (8 times). Some image or sound uses the wrong asset type.
10. **Two BillboardGuis are added to PlayerGui on every rep** (check they get cleaned up; could cost memory in long sessions).
11. UIBlox `FoundationProvider` warnings: Roblox's own core UI, not the game.

**Phone UI (844x390)**
- Weight -/+ buttons 23 px, AUTO TRAIN 21 px tall, BEST WEIGHT 30 px: all under the 44 px rule.
- The tutorial "all set" toast covers the panel tabs.
- Quest names cut off.
- The right column slides off-screen on machines (the shake and spurt cards can't be reached).
- Start screen too busy for a phone: event messages and the tutorial line stack in the middle over the gym.
- Fixed during the test (owner request): the Shop tile and the menu SHOP button now show from the start.

## 7. Top 5 priorities for the next update
1. **Fix the first 5 minutes:** the shake soft-lock, auto-rep stamina at 0, the hidden coach line, and the coach/notification conflict. This is where new players quit.
2. **Make stamina much longer for new players** and never a full stop.
3. **Always show "next goal" + the Growth Spurt bar on machines,** with the muscle that's missing ("Train LEGS"). One progress number everywhere.
4. **Reward moments:** weight tier popups, a big READY TO GROW screen, and a "what's new" card after each spurt. Aim for something new every 1-2 minutes.
5. **Give coins a use early** (cheap cosmetics / auras / skins), and open the Store on coin items, not Robux.

## Compared with popular Roblox simulators
The good ones give a reward every few seconds (numbers popping), an unlock every 1-2 minutes, always show the next goal and its price, and let you keep clicking with no hard stop. GYM ARC has a better core (real bodies that change, rivals, titles with jokes), but it hides its rewards (silent unlocks, off-screen READY) and stops the player too often (stamina, stuck bars, walking). Fix the pacing and show the rewards, and the body system becomes the hook.
