# 2026-10-21 GYM ARC Pump Shop building rebuilt

Prompt: [[2026-10-21 GYM ARC Prompt - 23 Rebuild PUMP SHOP building]]. Feature note: [[GYM ARC - Pump Shop]].

## Done
- The giant black box (64 x 68) is now a 2-storey corner store of 38 x 44 studs (about the neighbours' size, 31 studs tall) on the north-east corner of the block. Built by `tools/builders/PumpShop.luau` (v3).
- **Facade:** deep red brick with cream trim like the town (corner pilasters, floor band, cornice, parapet, sills and lintels, drainpipes, wall lamps).
- **Storefront:** black frames, two big display windows, an open glass double door, a black and gold striped awning. Behind the windows: 2 mannequins in tank tops and lifting belts, a stack of tubs with a NEW PRE-WORKOUT poster, warm spotlights.
- **Sign:** on the facade above the awning (black board, gold letters, gold frame, 3 gooseneck lamps), plus a hanging blade sign with a dumbbell and PUMP at the north corner.
- **No blank walls:** windows with curtains on every upper side, warm lit windows on the ground floor sides, a back door. The north wall (facing the spawn side) has a big original mural: a gold flexing arm in a red circle with a dumbbell, "GET PUMPED" (drawn with a SurfaceGui, no image upload).
- **Roof:** parapet, 2 AC units, a water tank, a hatch, a vent.
- **Inside:** smaller, brighter (wood floor, cream walls, wood wainscot, 6 warm lights). The 4 counters, signs and clerks as before (Supplements and Gear on the back wall, DNA Lab and Apparel on the sides), a shake fridge, a podium with a giant tub. A 7'0" player walks in through the door fine.
- **Street:** paving around it, 2 planters, a bench, a chalkboard A-frame "NEW: PRE-WORKOUT", a bike rack, 2 trees, a bin, a WELCOME mat. The 5 street lamps and the bus stop the first shop had moved away are back on the sidewalks, and the old driveway gap in the sidewalk is filled.
- **Freed space:** the west half of the old lot is a small park (lawn, a path, a round plaza with an oak and 3 benches, a lamp, trees, bushes, flowers; town models copied from the Street / Greenery folders).
- The teleport spot (PUMP SHOP button) is in front of the new doors; tested: it lands there and the 4 counter prompts and clerks work.
- The old building is kept in `ServerStorage.PumpShopBackup.PumpShop_old` (its counter tags removed so it doesn't count as a counter).

## Part count
Building outside ~260 (mostly small trim, no collision, no shadows), inside ~290, paving and props ~80, park ~180 (mostly the copied trees). Total 809.
