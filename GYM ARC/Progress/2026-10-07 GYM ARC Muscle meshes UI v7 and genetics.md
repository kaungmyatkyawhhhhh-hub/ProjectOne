# 2026-10-07 GYM ARC Muscle meshes UI v7 and genetics

Back to [[00 GYM ARC Home|GYM ARC Home]]

## What got done
- **UI v7**
  - Colored 3D side menu tiles, top-bar pills (coins, quests, daily reward, settings), frosted Muscles panel, tiles 0.75x with a drawer arrow.
  - Stat labels: click to expand, hover highlight, spring drift, push-apart, subtle tilt with the camera.
  - UI fonts: Oswald + Nunito everywhere (never Montserrat/Gotham).
- **Muscles**
  - Muscle v7: real EditableMesh muscles replace the blobs; v8: muscles wear your own Shirt/Pants, snatched waist, new calves, v8-1 smooth data.
  - Muscles load faster; red hover highlight and a rep flash with an On/Off setting; Crunch Bench trains Obliques.
  - Stats show EXP (50,000 for the first Growth Spurt), no "Max" text, no cap on stats, group EXP is the sum of its muscles, full look at 2x the goal and mass monster at 5x.
  - See [[GYM ARC - Muscles and bodies]].
- **Genetics**
  - Reveal redesigned over many rounds: DNA bar, truly 3D plates, real shadows, competition-plate printing with M WEIGHTS, a real gym scene with depth of field, tunable camera, one shared label panel. See [[GYM ARC - Genetics]].
- **World, poses, skating**
  - Poses package (held poses, judges, statue/mirror/NPC hookups), skate rework (momentum, carving, ollies, stance, new board).
  - Statue real size and marble, sliding door frames, pro gym floor and lighting polish, beach gym NPCs.
  - Equipment: M WEIGHTS on every plate, thinner plates with collars, Leg Press + Hack Squat.
  - Loading screen from the first frame, fixed entrance menu shot, glide in through the door.
- **Checks**
  - Full test pass and a bug review (saves, crews, arm wrestling, machines, muscles on death, panels, numbers).
