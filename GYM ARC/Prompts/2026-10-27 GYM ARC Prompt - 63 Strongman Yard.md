# 63 Strongman Yard

Back to [[00 GYM ARC Home|GYM ARC Home]]

Done 27 Oct: [[GYM ARC - Strongman Yard]].

```text
Build a STRONGMAN YARD: an outdoor training yard next to the Powerlifting Gym, part of it (same unlock: GROWTH SPURT 5, no new gym in the ladder). Strongman = lifting big, awkward real-world objects instead of machines. Follow CLAUDE.md Speed rules. Mobile first. Never undo the owner's manual Studio changes. No real brands or people.

1) PLACE + LOOK
- Behind or beside the Powerlifting Gym, reached through a big back door of the warehouse (and a gate from the street). Fenced with chain-link and old shipping containers as walls.
- Dusty dirt/gravel ground with rubber mats under the stations, chains, stacks of old tractor tires, wooden pallets, oil drums, a few floodlights on poles (white light), chalk buckets, a hand-painted sign "STRONGMAN YARD", a big scoreboard. Warm afternoon light; floodlights on at night.
- Room for a full server; wide lanes for 7'0" players.

2) STATIONS (each trains specific muscles, heaviest tiers so far, same EXP/tier rules and the Powerlifting gym bonus)
- ATLAS STONES: lift a huge round stone onto a platform (back, legs, core). 3 platforms at increasing heights.
- LOG PRESS: press a thick wooden log overhead (shoulders, arms, chest).
- YOKE CARRY: a giant steel frame on the shoulders, walk it down a 20-stud lane (legs, core, back).
- FARMER'S WALK: two heavy handles, walk them down a lane (arms/forearms, back, core).
- TRUCK PULL: pull an old pickup truck (original model, no brand) along a lane with a rope, hand over hand (back, arms, legs).
- TIRE FLIP: flip a giant tractor tire end over end along the yard (legs, back, chest).
- KEG TOSS: throw kegs over a high bar (shoulders, legs, core).
How they play: same tap/REP system as machines, but each rep has its own short animation (the stone rises, the log goes up, the tire flips over, the truck moves a bit). CARRY and PULL stations move the player down the lane while they tap; at the end of the lane they turn and go back.

3) STRONGMAN EVENT (the yard's unique feature)
- Every ~15 minutes: "STRONGMAN SHOWDOWN" for 2 minutes. Everyone in the yard can join one event (truck pull or atlas stones, it rotates).
- The scoreboard shows live results: the farthest truck pull / most stones lifted in the time.
- Top 3 get coins (prompt 61 minutes-of-coins rules) and a title the first time ("YARD KING"). Everyone who joins gets a small reward.
- Announce it in the yard only (not server-wide, prompt 48): a toast + an air-horn sound + the floodlights flash.

4) HOOKUPS
- Locked until Growth Spurt 5 together with the Powerlifting Gym (the gate shows the same locked sign).
- GO TO / map / GuidePath target "STRONGMAN YARD", unlock guide line from Coach Dex, 2 NPC strongmen training there, sounds (stone thud, chain rattle, truck creak, crowd cheer during the showdown).
- Speed: simple parts, reused models where possible, props no collisions/no shadows, streams only when near. Check a phone doesn't lag in the yard with 8 players.

TEST: unlock at Spurt 5, train on every station, a showdown with 2 players (scoreboard + rewards). Screenshots of the yard, each station mid-rep, and the scoreboard. Short progress note + feature note "GYM ARC - Strongman Yard" in the vault.
```
