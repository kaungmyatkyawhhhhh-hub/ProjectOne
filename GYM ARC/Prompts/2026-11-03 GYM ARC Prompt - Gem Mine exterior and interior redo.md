# GYM ARC Prompt - Gem Mine exterior pass + interior redo (3 Nov)
After [[2026-11-03 GYM ARC Gem Mine Gym built]]. Owner's two prompts (verbatim):

```text
GEM MINE GYM exterior pass (east block, keep the spot and the inside):
1) SHAPE: no box. Rebuild the rock as an irregular low-poly mountain: uneven peaks, the top sloping down toward the street, wider at the bottom, rounded corners, broken outline. Lower the back so the green hill still shows behind it.
2) BIG ENTRANCE facing the street: a wide cave mouth (about 2x a player's height+) framed with wooden mine timbers (two posts + a beam), hanging lanterns, mine cart rails running out onto the sidewalk with one cart full of gems, a "GEM MINE GYM" sign on the beam + "ALTER 15+".
3) GEMS EVERYWHERE, BIG: 6-10 large crystal clusters (purple, blue, pink, some 8-15 studs tall) bursting out of the peaks, corners and around the entrance, Neon/glass-free bright material with a soft glow; a purple light spilling out of the cave mouth onto the street.
4) GROUND IT: rubble, boulders and small crystals around the base and spilling onto the sidewalk edge (keep the walkway clear), a few dark cracks with glow.
5) Keep FPS: few real lights (entrance glow + 2-3), crystals as Neon parts not lights, reuse meshes. No Glass. Before/after screenshots from this same camera and from the street.
```

```text
GEM MINE GYM interior redo (keep the street entrance + exterior pass):
1) SIZE: dig DOWN. From the cave mouth a wide ramp/stairs (with timber supports + lanterns) leads ~25 studs below street level into a big cavern ~100 x 75 studs, ceiling 35-45 studs high with crystal stalactites. Nothing sticks out above ground except the mountain shell. Check the cavern doesn't collide with roads/other buildings' foundations and that streaming still loads it.
2) LAYOUT: a central open area with Flint's arm-wrestling table on a raised rock platform under a giant glowing crystal (chapter 7 spot); machines in clear rows along the walls with 8+ stud walkways; the mine cart track loops around the edge (carts with gems) instead of ending nowhere.
3) GEM EQUIPMENT: racks and benches in dark metal + gold trim; plates made of gems (purple / blue / pink translucent-look Neon or colored SmoothPlastic, NO Glass); a few special "crystal" machines as the gym's signature.
4) LIGHT: remove the office lamps. Light comes from the crystals (Neon) + a few PointLights inside big clusters (max ~6), lanterns on the timbers; purple/blue mood with warm lantern accents. Keep it bright enough to see the player.
5) Keep FPS: reuse meshes, few lights, no particles everywhere (a few sparkles near the big crystal only). Before/after screenshots from the entrance, from above and at player height.
```
