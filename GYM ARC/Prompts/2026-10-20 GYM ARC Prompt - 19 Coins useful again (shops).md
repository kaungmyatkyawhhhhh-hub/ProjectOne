# 19 Coins useful again (PUMP SHOP store)

Back to [[00 GYM ARC Home|GYM ARC Home]]

Owner, 20 Oct: at max Growth Spurt, coins feel useless. Build one big store in town (like the Gym League store) that sells everything for coins.

```text
Coins feel useless once players hit max Growth Spurt. Build ONE big store building in the town hub called "PUMP SHOP" that sells everything worth buying with coins. Read "GYM ARC\2026-10-20 GYM ARC Gameplay plan.md" first. Needs the boosts inventory from prompt 14. Follow CLAUDE.md Speed rules. UI is Black + Gold, mobile first. Coins only (no Robux in this store). Kids' game: no loot boxes or random paid rolls. No real brands.

FIRST: measure coins earned per minute at Growth Spurt 0, 2, 4 and max (active play). Report it. Set ALL prices from that: put them in one config module with a note on how each price was chosen.

1) THE BUILDING
- A big, clearly visible store in the town hub, between the gyms, close to spawn and on the way to the Beach Gym. Big lit "PUMP SHOP" sign on the roof (Black + Gold), glass front, open doors.
- Inside: 4 sections, each with its own counter/display and an NPC clerk: SUPPLEMENTS, GEAR, APPAREL, DNA LAB. Shelves of tubs and shakers, gear on wall hooks, mannequins wearing apparel. Realistic and premium (like the pro gym), not cluttered.
- Walking up to a counter (ProximityPrompt "Shop") opens that section's panel. The panel also has tabs, so you can browse every section from any counter.
- A "PUMP SHOP" button appears in the HUD after unlock that teleports you to the store entrance (cooldown 10 s).
- Leave room for a 5th section, ALTERS, which comes later.
- The old smoothie bar stays as decoration (or a quick "buy 1 Protein Shake" spot). Its shop moves to PUMP SHOP.

2) SUPPLEMENTS
- Protein Shake, Coconut Water, Creatine, Protein Powder, Supplements, and a new Pre-Workout (+25% rep speed for 5 min).
- Buying adds to the inventory (prompt 14). A boost costs about 3-5 minutes of coins at the player's current stage, so prices scale with Growth Spurt. Buy x1 / x5 / x10.

3) GEAR
- Lifting belt, wrist wraps, lifting straps, knee sleeves. Each shows on the character and gives a small permanent perk in its area (belt +5% squats/deadlifts, wraps +5% pressing, straps +5% pulling, sleeves +5% legs).
- 3 levels each: Basic, Pro, Elite (+5% / +10% / +15%). Elite costs a lot (an hour or more of max-level coins), so max players have long-term coin goals.
- Equip/unequip in a new Gear tab in the inventory. Show the gear on the mannequins.

4) APPAREL
- A starter list for coins: shorts colors, tank top colors, headbands, shaker bottle colors, gym bags. Try on before buying (preview on your character). Show owned items in the inventory.

5) DNA LAB
- DNA tokens for coins: keep the 25k base price, but the price goes up 10% each time you buy one in the same week (resets weekly), so rich players can't buy unlimited rerolls.
- A "Reroll genetics" shortcut here that opens the genetics screen.

6) KEEP PLAYERS SPENDING
- When coins pass the price of something the player doesn't own, show a small gold toast once: "You can afford: Pro Lifting Belt!" (max once per 5 min). Tapping it teleports to PUMP SHOP.
- Feature unlock (gameplay plan): PUMP SHOP opens at ~8 min with the inventory (Supplements only at first). Gear and Apparel open at Growth Spurt 2. A one-line Coach Dex intro each time.

Test: buy one of each with test coins, check perks apply and save. Screenshots: the building outside and inside, and one phone screenshot of the shop panel. Short progress note in the vault with the coins-per-minute numbers and the chosen prices.
```
