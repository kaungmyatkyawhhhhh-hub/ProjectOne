```text
The owner says the Starter Gym looks too packed since every machine was added (prompt 16), plus the dumbbells, the Trainer Agency and the boards. Re-layout it so it feels open, clean and easy to walk through, like a real well-organized gym. Keep the warm brick look. Never undo the owner's manual Studio changes. Follow CLAUDE.md Speed rules.
Owner screenshots: C:\Users\kaung\Downloads\gym_arc_starter_refs\ (starter_packed_1..4).

WHAT'S WRONG
- Machines are scattered with no clear rows or zones, so the room looks like a pile of equipment.
- Walkways are narrow and blocked; big 7'0" players can't move through easily.
- Too much in the middle (table + stools on a mat, medicine balls, random benches).
- A huge dark board sits in the front-left corner and blocks the view.
- Lots of pendant lamps at different heights add clutter.

HOW TO FIX
1) Make the room bigger: grow the floor about 30-40% (keep the walls, door and the glass wall to the pro gym lined up), and raise the ceiling a little. Move the walls out; don't squeeze.
2) Fewer duplicates: keep 2 of the most used machines only (Flat Bench, Squat Rack, and the tutorial machines). Everything else 1 copy. Remove the extra loose benches and props that don't do anything.
3) Clear zones, each in its own area with a hanging zone sign (like the pro gym):
   - FREE WEIGHTS: squat racks, benches, deadlift platforms in straight rows along the left wall, facing into the room.
   - MACHINES: all the selectorized/plate machines in neat rows on the right side, same spacing, all facing the same way.
   - CABLES + ARMS: pushdown, woodchop, curl machines grouped together.
   - DUMBBELLS: the dumbbell rack + its train spots along the mirror wall.
   - CARDIO: treadmills and bikes in one straight row by the windows.
   - STRETCH / RECOVERY: mats, foam rollers, medicine balls in one corner.
   - FRONT: the desk, Coach Dex and the Trainer Agency near the entrance, out of the training areas.
4) Wide aisles: a main center aisle from the door (at least 12 studs wide) and 8+ studs between rows, so 7'0" players and trainers following them never get stuck.
5) Remove the big dark board in the front-left (move its info to a wall-mounted board or the Agency board). Wall boards flat on the walls, not standing in the room.
6) Lighting: one neat grid of pendant lamps at the same height over the training rows, fewer lamps overall, keep the warm pools of light.
7) Keep the tutorial path short and clear (spawn -> Flat Bench -> Squat Rack), and move the tutorial arrows/beams to the new spots.
8) Keep every machine's MachineId, prompts, quests and unlocks working. Check the camera doesn't clip into walls on phones.

Screenshots from the same 4 angles as the owner's, plus one top-down view. Short progress note in the vault.
```
