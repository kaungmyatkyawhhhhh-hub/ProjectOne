# Chrome reset (title menu, left nav, main HUD)

Back to [[00 GYM ARC Home|GYM ARC Home]]

```text
GYM ARC UI — chrome reset. Apply to the title menu, left nav and main HUD.

Global rule. Two visual languages, strictly separated. Chrome (anything you read, navigate or buy) is quiet: dark graphite, thin dark outline, clean heavy italic white labels, 10px corner radius, no hex mesh, no diagonal shine, no stroked chunky font, no gold borders. Loud treatment (rarity colours, stroked chunky text, hex mesh, glow) is reserved for collectible tiles, roll reveals, evolutions and claim/equip/wear buttons only. Gold is used for numbers, progress and one highlighted element per screen — never as a default border.

Title menu. Keep logo, tagline, scene and player card. Buttons: remove the diagonal shine; 1px top highlight only; corner radius 10px. PLAY keeps the chunky stroked font on a deep red vertical gradient with dark outline and 5px darker bottom edge. SHOP in clean heavy italic dark text on a metallic gold gradient. SETTINGS and NEWS in clean heavy italic white on dark graphite with thin dark outline. Same sizes and spacing as now.

Left nav. Revert to the earlier version: larger rounded-square tiles with more spacing, white line icons, clean italic labels, no hex, no gold on every tile. Gold outline only on the active or notified tile. Locked tiles: dimmed, grey lock replacing the icon, label dimmed but readable. Badges: small red “!” chip in the top-right corner for notifications, small gold NEW tag inside the corner for new content; one badge per tile.

Main HUD. Coins stays in the Roblox topbar as a topbar icon. All other pills and cards (level, DAILY, settings, Today’s Quest, Next alter): dark graphite, thin dark outline, no gold border, clean italic labels. Gold appears only on the coin count, LVL number, percentages and progress bar fills. NEXT: SHREDDED gets the single gold outline on screen; remove its blue outline. “Finish faster: Shake Bundle” as small gold italic text. Hotbar items unchanged.

Hex mesh. Cell size ~18px. Panel backgrounds 5%, collectible tiles 15–20%, everything else 0%. Never on a child when the parent has it.
```
