# 17 Playtest like a real player

Back to [[00 GYM ARC Home|GYM ARC Home]]

```text
Play GYM ARC like a real kid on a phone and write an honest review. DON'T fix or change anything in this prompt. Only play, measure and report.
For this prompt only, you may take up to 15 screenshots and do several playtests (override the Speed rule).

HOW TO PLAY
- Use Play mode with the Studio MCP tools (character_navigation, keyboard/mouse input, screen_capture). Use a phone-size viewport (844x390) where you can.
- Play as a kid who has never seen the game, reads almost nothing, and gets bored fast. Only do what the game shows or tells you. Don't use admin commands or your knowledge of the code, except to set up the saves below.
- Run 1: brand-new save. Play the first 20 minutes: tutorial, first lifts, try to reach the first Growth Spurt. Write down the time of every milestone.
- Run 2: a save at Growth Spurt 2 (set it up with test data). Play 10 minutes: what's there to do, what's the next goal?
- Run 3: a 7'0" max save. Play 10 minutes: is there anything left to chase?
- Each run: try the shop, inventory, quests, crews, trainers, skateboard, arm wrestling, the posing poses, daily rewards, dumbbells and machines.

WHAT TO MEASURE
- Time to: first rep, first weight tier unlock, first quest done, first Growth Spurt.
- Every time you waited 10+ seconds with nothing fun to do (stamina, walking, loading, menus). Count them and add up the time.
- Every time it wasn't clear what to do next.
- How often something new or rewarding happened (popup, unlock, sound, visible body change). Note any gap longer than 2 minutes with nothing new.
- Phone UI: anything overlapping, too small, cut off or hard to tap.
- Bugs and errors (check the Output window).

THE REPORT
Save it as a vault note: GYM ARC\Progress\2026-10-20 GYM ARC Playtest review.md, with these sections:
1. Timeline of Run 1 (minute by minute, short).
2. Top 10 BORING moments, worst first (what happened, how long, why it's boring).
3. Top 10 things to ADD (what, why players would care, rough size: small / medium / big).
4. Top 10 things to CHANGE (what's there now, what it should be).
5. Confusing moments (where a new kid would get lost or quit).
6. Bugs (with steps to reproduce) and phone UI problems.
7. Your top 5 priorities for the next update, if you had to choose.
Be blunt. "It's fine" is not useful. Compare with what makes popular Roblox simulators fun to keep playing. Remember: no real brands, kids' game, monetization must stay fair.
At the end, tell me the note's name so I can share it with the planner.
```
