# 72 Feedback on every action

Back to [[00 GYM ARC Home|GYM ARC Home]]

```text
Make every action give feedback:

Reps: a small punch on the REP button, a bar shake on heavy reps, plate clanks.
Rewards: coins fly to the counter and count up, never just a number change.
Level ups and PRs: a short sound and flash, not spammed.
Buttons: every one has a press animation and a click sound.
```
