# GYM ARC ads - how to run the best ads + ChatGPT prompts (2 Nov)

Owner: "for ads what to do for the best ads. research and then tell me the prompt for it". Builds on [[Playbook notes - Discovery and marketing]] (section 4) and [[2026-11-02 GYM ARC Thumbnails - ChatGPT prompt]].

## Research (2 Nov 2026)
- Sponsored ads = your 16:9 thumbnails on Home + search. Up to **10 creatives per campaign, shown evenly**; the campaign page shows results **per creative** -> turn off losers, keep winners. Moderation up to 48 h for assets, ~24 h for campaigns. First **24 h = Learning**; plays/earnings report up to 48 h late. (Creator Hub Ads Manager docs)
- Objectives: Plays (broad, cheapest), Earnings (limited), Engagement (higher CPP, judge after 3-5 days). Audience: New Players; advanced targeting by location, age, gender, genre, device.
- "Games that are engaging may get better performance through ads system discounts" -> a game that holds players gets cheaper plays. Non-unique games can get no spend at all.
- Use **launch data / advanced join options** to give ad players an ad-only reward or tailored onboarding: Roblox says it increases conversions.
- Staff tips for thumbnails (also good for ad creatives): make them **visibly different** (different gameplay, characters, place, player motivation: competition / creativity / social), show new content, no clickbait, don't restart tests too often.
- No official CPP prices; cost only shows after spending.
- Ad players don't count for RFY ranking but help the game get retrieved; if ad players bounce, more spend doesn't help -> fix the freezing (2 Nov bug) first.

## Plan
1. Fix the freezing / map holes first (ads bring new players into it).
2. One campaign, objective PLAYS, audience NEW PLAYERS, phones + PC, ages that fit (9-17), daily budget, run 3+ days before judging.
3. 6 creatives, each a different motivation (below). After 3 days: turn off the 2-3 worst by CPP / click rate, add 2 new ones. Repeat weekly; new creatives with each update (GEM 7 Nov).
4. Ad-only welcome: ad players (launch data "ad") get the 2x new-player boost for 30 min instead of 15 (optional, Claude Code).
5. The winning ad creative also goes into Home thumbnail personalization.
