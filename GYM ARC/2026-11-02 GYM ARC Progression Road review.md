# GYM ARC - Progression Road v1 review (2 Nov)

Owner: "this is the your road thing. is it good" (4 screenshots, saved in GYM ARC\Screenshots\road\).
Related: [[2026-11-01 GYM ARC Roadmap after the Gem update]] (MY ROAD moved up), prompt 91, [[2026-10-29 GYM ARC Why players stop grinding - research]].

## Verdict: good base (~70%), 6 fixes before publishing
Works: YOU ARE HERE + NEXT with a % bar (goal gradient), height + EXP bonus on every stop, scrolls the whole road to Alter 15 + Hall of Fame, future tiers locked, alter cards in their colours.

1. **The bodies don't grow.** Every card has the same small default avatar, so the "Future You" fantasy (get huge and tall) isn't shown. Each stop must show the body at THAT stage: taller (scaled by height) and bigger muscles (the existing BodyStages), alter stops wearing their skin. The silhouette for locked stops is fine, but its SIZE must grow.
2. **Unlocks are unreadable.** Tiny grey icons; a kid can't tell what opens. Each card: one big icon + one word for the main unlock ("BEACH GYM", "ARM WRESTLING", "GOLDEN ERA GYM"); the rest in the details.
3. **Bottom banner** (corrected, owner: "the bottom banner is stuck on spurt 6 because i selected spurt 6"): it already shows the SELECTED card; my mistake. Real issue: the selected card has no visible highlight (Spurt 6 looks like every other card), so you can't see what the banner belongs to. Fix: the selected card gets a bright outline + slight lift. Also the "Opens:" text is grey on gold: low contrast.
4. **Too dark.** Locked cards are near-black; the future should look exciting. Locked = dimmed (60%), not black; alter cards show their skin texture as the card background (the AlterThemes art already exists).
5. **Wording.** Hall of Fame "TRAIN TO ITS GOAL" -> "REACH ALTER 15"; start card "YOU" button repeats YOU ARE HERE (remove). Gem card: "OPENS SAT" with the real countdown until the GEM event, then normal stops (16-20). Elements / Legend keep COMING SOON (dates can slip).
6. **Open on the player.** The road should open scrolled to YOU ARE HERE (check on an Alter 8 save), with the NEXT card fully visible.
