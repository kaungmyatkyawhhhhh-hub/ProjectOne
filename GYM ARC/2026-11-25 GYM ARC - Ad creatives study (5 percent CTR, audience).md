# GYM ARC: how to get 5%+ CTR ad creatives - study (25 Nov)

Owner: "we need like 10 creatives for testing and then try to get 5%+ [CTR] on all of them. study on how to get ultra high [CTR] creatives and our target audience".
Replaces the 8-creative idea in [[2026-11-25 GYM ARC - Ad creatives plan (8 creatives, 5 percent CTR)]] -> now **10**.

## 1. Our own ad data (Ads Manager, read 25 Nov) - the strongest evidence
| Campaign | Spent | Impr. | CTR | Plays | Creatives |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [RELEASE] 23 Oct | $192.80 | 1.77M | 3.3% | 35,173 | 8 dark "gym poster" creatives with slogans (LIFT YOUR WEIGHT, DISCIPLINE BUILDS FREEDOM...) |
| [HALL OF FAME] 31 Oct | $19.98 | 132k | 3.9% | 2,781 | - |
| [🎃] 5 Nov, run 1 | $15.34 | 73k | **5.0%** | 2,040 | 8 neon creatives (below) |
| [🎃] 5 Nov, run 2 (to 8 Nov) | $47.64 | 418k | 4.0% | 8,848 | the same 8 neon creatives |
Images: `Assets\ads_research\past_ads_*.jpg`.

**Per creative:**
- RELEASE poster set: all 3.2-3.4%, best 3.9% (the busy "DISCIPLINE BUILDS FREEDOM" poster).
- Neon set, run 1: 4.8 / 5.0 / 5.3 / 5.3 / 5.4 / 5.9%. Run 2: 3.5-4.8%.

**What this says:**
1. **Style beats slogans.** The dark, realistic gym posters with motivational text never passed 3.9%. The neon set reached 5-5.9%:
   - a bright white-faced Bacon Hair, close and big;
   - saturated pink / red / blue glow;
   - "+1" numbers, or a huge "1,735 LB";
   - glowing crystal plates.
2. **The same creatives drop as a campaign scales** (5.0% on 73k impressions -> 4.0% on 418k). Ad fatigue + broader audience. So "5%+ on every creative" holds in the first days; plan fresh variants every week.
3. The spread inside one campaign is small (about +-1%). The STYLE of the whole set and the TIMING (5 Nov = Halloween + fresh) moved CTR more than any single creative.
4. CTR is not plays: in RELEASE every creative had the same cost per play (~$0.005). Keep an eye on plays and playtime, not only CTR.

## 2. Our audience (Creator Hub Demographics, MAU 98,914 on 23 Nov)
- Country: US 24.7%, Brazil 7.0%, Indonesia 6.4%, Russia 5.2%, Philippines 4.0%, UK 3.6%, Mexico 3.4%.
- Gender: male 75.9%, female 13.9%, unknown 10.2%.
- Age: not age-checked 45.4% (mostly kids), 9-12 16.5%, 13-15 10.7%, 21+ 9.6%, 18-20 6.9%, 16-17 6.6%, under 9 5.0%.
- Language: English 52%, Spanish 11.4%, Russian 8.1%, Portuguese 7.2%, Indonesian 6.1%.

## 3. Outside research (web, 25 Nov)
- Roblox: a campaign holds up to 10 thumbnails, shown evenly at first; later the system favours the best one per user group. 16:9, 1920x1080.
- Roblox staff: testing more thumbnails gives bigger gains; don't restart tests within 7 days.
- Developer reports: CTR of 1-2% is normal; 7-9% is extremely rare.
- Conversion-ad rules (developer A/B write-ups):
  - one subject with a face, cropped tight (background about 20% of the frame);
  - a single high-contrast subject on a simple background (+20-40% CTR, informal reports);
  - warm bright colours stand out on Roblox's dark home page;
  - one clear emotion (power / surprise);
  - at most 5 visual elements;
  - text only if it names a reward or mechanic, 3 words max, readable at 150 px.
- Clicks that don't match the game hurt: ad players who bounce lower our ranking signals.

## 4. Rules for our 10 creatives
1. The approved character from icon G: Bacon Hair, Man face, glossy golden-orange skin. The face is big, bright and in the top half.
2. Carry over what made our 5%+ creatives work:
   - a saturated neon glow colour per creative (pink, red, electric blue, gold, purple);
   - one huge number ("+1", "1000 LB", "x2");
   - glowing crystal or neon plates (our Gem / Plate Forge content);
   - power and aggression in the face and pose.
3. Power Lifters contrast: dark background, the character the brightest thing; warm skin vs one cool glow.
4. No slogans, no "PLAY NOW" buttons, no long text. Logo small in a corner, or none (the neon no-logo ones did as well).
5. Real game content only (alters, plates, Gem Mine, Growth Spurt, arm wrestling).
6. Check every one at 300x169 and at 150 px before upload.

## 6. Test plan
- One campaign, Plays objective, new players, all 10, phone + PC, $5-10/day, 3-5 days (judge after the learning day).
- Turn off anything under 4% after day 3; replace it with a variant (new colour / pose) of the top creative. Expect CTR to drop as spend grows: refresh 2-3 creatives each week.
- Track CTR AND plays per click AND playtime per creative. Log them here.
