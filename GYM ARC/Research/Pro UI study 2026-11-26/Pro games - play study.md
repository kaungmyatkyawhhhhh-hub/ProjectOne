# Pro games - play study (2026-11-26)

Played live in the Roblox player on PC (1920x1080), about 6-8 min each, fresh account state in every game.
Goal: copy **principles**, never art, names or look.
Sound could not be recorded, so "sound" notes are missing everywhere.
Screenshots sit in one folder per game next to this file.

| # | Game | Players at the time | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Grow a Garden | 13.9K | full run, bought + planted + sold |
| 2 | Pet Simulator 99 | 86K | full run, hatched, claimed a free shop item |
| 3 | Gym League | ~510 | menus all opened; server kicked me ("please rejoin") before I trained |
| 4 | Arm Wrestle Simulator | 1.4K | onboarding to first win; menus never unlocked in my 8 min |
| 5 | Steal a Brainrot | 39K | all 4 menus opened; failed to catch the moving target to buy |

## Top 10 single details worth stealing (as principles)

| # | Detail | Seen in | Why it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bottom "menu" button that **becomes a 5-icon dock only while a window is open** | PS99 | Full menu access with almost zero HUD |
| 2 | Shop row that **expands into 3 pay options** (in-game / Robux / gift) | GAG | Robux is offered without its own screen, right where the kid already wants the item |
| 3 | **Goal card + 3D arrow above target + big ground arrow** at your feet | PS99 | Kid never has to read; just follow the arrow |
| 4 | **Surprise "2x TRAINING BOOST" title + timer chip** in the first 30 s | AWS | Early dopamine and shows boosts exist (later sold) |
| 5 | **Auto-train near the equipment, numbers fly into the stat pill**, goal bar fills in 2 s | AWS | Instant visible progress in a gym loop |
| 6 | Claim button that **morphs into the next countdown** | GL | Clear "done, come back in 23:59" without a popup |
| 7 | **Free -> paid -> free chain** with confetti on each claim | PS99 | Free claims train the click; the paid link sits in the middle |
| 8 | **Locked equipment shown with "3k Required"** billboards next to unlocked ones | AWS | Next goal is visible in the world, not in a menu |
| 9 | **Hold-E fight with tug bar, both avatars, taps/s and AUTO CLICK upsell** beside it | AWS | Upsell shown at the exact moment of pain |
| 10 | **Window icon breaking out of the frame** + title ending in "!" | PS99 | Makes plain white panels feel premium and playful |

## For GYM ARC (short)

1. Start like AWS: pick/meet moment -> only stat pills + one task + one goal bar.
2. Unlock menu buttons one by one (GAG/AWS), add a red badge when one appears.
3. Bottom dock idea from PS99 if we need more than 4 buttons.
4. Auto-train with flying numbers into the stat pill; one free timed boost in minute 1.
5. Billboards on locked machines ("Requires X") so the next goal lives in the world.
6. Shop rows that expand into in-game / Robux / gift; bundle ladder with one "best deal".
7. Every random thing gets an odds button. Every reward gets a 2-4 s celebration.
