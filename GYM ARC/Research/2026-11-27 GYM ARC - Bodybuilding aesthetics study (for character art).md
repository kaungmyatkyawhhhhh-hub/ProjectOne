# GYM ARC: bodybuilding aesthetics (for character art)

**Why:** the owner rejected the v4 icons: "our character's build look ugly because its not aesthetic", then "it needs the aesthetic proportions too, study about bodybuilding aesthetics first". The v4 giant was puffy: a balloon shirt, lumpy gold blob arms, no waist.

## Ratios (sources at the bottom)
- **Shoulders : waist = 1.618 : 1**, the Adonis index or golden ratio (measured as circumference). For drawn widths in heroic art, use shoulders about 3 head-widths and the **waist about half the shoulder width** (about 2 : 1), so the V still reads when the icon is small and blurred.
- **Chest : waist about 1.7–1.8**: Steve Reeves measured 54 in chest and 30 in waist.
- **Arm = neck = calf** (Reeves). The upper arm is never bigger than the head.
- **Upper arm : forearm about 1.24 : 1** (McCallum). **Thigh about 0.76 × waist** (McCallum); Reeves: thigh = half the chest.
- **Height 8.5–9 heads** for a heroic figure. Landmarks are one head apart: nipples, navel, crotch (Loomis via Proko). The waist is well under half the height.

## What reads as aesthetic vs ugly
- **Aesthetic:**
  - a V-taper and X-frame: wide collarbones, round capped delts, lats flaring from the armpit;
  - a tight, drawn-in waist (vacuum) and sweeping thighs;
  - symmetry, and flowing lines from one muscle to the next;
  - crisp separation lines and a fitted shirt.
- **Ugly or bloated:**
  - a thick waist, a gut, or a blocky mass with no taper;
  - arms bigger than the head;
  - no lower-body sweep;
  - swollen "balloon" shapes with no separations (our v4).
- **Pose:** a front double biceps shows everything. Chest high, ribs up, waist sucked in, abs flexed, knees soft.
- **Stylised game art:** keep correct anatomy underneath. Exaggerate the shoulder-to-waist ratio more than the muscle detail, because the silhouette has to read at 60 px.

## Prompt line
"8.5-heads heroic male, shoulders about 3 head-widths wide, waist half the shoulder width, V-taper / X-frame, round capped delts, wide collarbones, lats flaring from the armpits, tight vacuumed waist, upper arms no bigger than the head, arm = neck = calf, clean crisp muscle separations, symmetrical, flowing lines, no gut, no puffy or swollen shapes."
