# GYM ARC: Advent calendar, 3 decisions (owner OK, 12 Nov)

Adds to [[2026-11-12 GYM ARC - Christmas update decision (Advent calendar, 5 Dec)]]. Research:
- [[2026-11-12 Research - Modern Roblox advent calendar and pass UI (videos watched)]]
- [[2026-11-12 Research - Tap Simulator REBORN secret reveal sound breakdown (measured)]]

Owner (12 Nov): "yes i agree with ur decisions".

1. **Style: the GYM ARC kit, with Tap REBORN's energy.**
   - **Keep** the black + gold kit colours and the kit fonts. It's one game, and the root CLAUDE.md rule says new UI uses the kit.
   - **Borrow from Tap Simulator REBORN:** chunky thick outlines, 3D-rendered presents that overflow their tiles, holographic big-day tiles (7, 14, 21, 24), the tilted hero card, and the reveal motion with something new every ~0.3 s.
   - **Not borrowed:** the bright pink/purple/cyan gradients and the rounded cartoon font.
   - No sunburst ring anywhere.
   - The present tile and the reveal are built as reusable kit components.
2. **Opened from the world, reveal off the machine.**
   - The calendar opens from a Christmas tree or NPC in the gym, like Fisch, Steal a Brainrot and Grow a Garden. There's no window popping up by itself.
   - This fits the 12 Nov rule: Kit windows don't open by themselves while training; they wait until the player is off the machine ([[2026-11-12 GYM ARC - Shorter tutorial Chapter 1]]).
   - The claim reveal (short 2.5 s, or the full cutscene on big days) always plays off a machine.
3. **Dates: the update goes live Sat 5 Dec with days 1–5 open at once.**
   - Days 1–5 can all be claimed on 5 Dec, a catch-up burst on launch day.
   - Then one day per day, so **day 24 lands on 24 Dec** (Christmas Eve, the full cutscene).
   - Missed days later: frozen in ice, with a Robux Restore.
