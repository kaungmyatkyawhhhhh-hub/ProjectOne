## The math
- 50 CCU = 72,000 play-minutes a day.
  - At ~12 min a player that's ~6,000 players a day.
  - At ~20 min it's ~3,600 a day.
- Now ~15 CCU ≈ ~1,800 players a day. So we need ~3x: more players from Home AND longer sessions.
- The real problem: **Home recommendation fell from 2,406 players/day (14 Nov) to 189 (22 Nov)**, and thumbnail impressions are down 81%.
  - Since June 2026 Roblox ranks only on organic players: play-through rate (PTR), first-play bounce, play days D1 / D2-7 / D8-28, co-play and playtime, over 28 days.
  - Ads don't count for ranking, but they help Roblox *pick the game up to test* ("retrieval").
- An update triggers a test wave ("explore"). If that cohort plays well, Roblox keeps showing the game ("expand").
