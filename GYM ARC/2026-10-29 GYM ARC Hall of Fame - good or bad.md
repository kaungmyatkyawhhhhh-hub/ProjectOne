# Hall of Fame: good or bad for players? Will we lose players?

Back to [[00 GYM ARC Home|GYM ARC Home]] · Feature: [[GYM ARC - Hall of Fame]] · Related: [[2026-10-29 GYM ARC Why players stop grinding - research]], [[2026-10-29 GYM ARC Gym genre research]]

Owner, 29 Oct: "research if hall of fame is a good thing or bad thing for players and we will lose players or not. if we lose then another idea."

## What our Hall of Fame is
A second rebirth after **Alter 15** (≈ 1 h of Growth Spurts + 15 alters × ~27 min ≈ **8 hours of play**). Opt-in, with ARE YOU SURE.
- **Starts over:** height (back to small), Growth Spurts, muscles, alters and their gains.
- **Keeps:** trainers, coins, alter skins (as looks), titles, auras, gear, passes, LVL, gyms and features unlocked.
- **Gives:** a star (★1 = +25% EXP and start at Spurt 2, more per star), a statue of your peak body, ★ over your head, top-10 board, a ceremony.

## Will it lose players?
**New players: no.** Almost nobody reaches it. With ~4% coming back on day 2 and Alter 15 needing ~8 hours, maybe a handful of players will ever see the ENTER button. The Hall of Fame is not why 96% leave; the first 5 minutes are (about half leave before the first Growth Spurt).

**Your best players: yes, there's a real risk.** Research on prestige/rebirth:
- Players feel a loss much more strongly than an equal gain (loss aversion). If a design asks players to give something up, many avoid it or feel angry after (Bottom Feeder).
- A reset only feels good when the next run is **clearly faster right away** ("acceleration, not punishment": noticeably faster within ~30 seconds) and the old run doesn't feel wasted (idlegames.win, dev.to).
- Bad prestige = unclear reward, punishing reset, redoing the same chores.

Our weak spots:
1. **It takes away the HEIGHT, the main fantasy.** After 8 hours of growing to 7'0" (Alter 15), you're small again. Everything else is kept, but the thing they worked for is gone.
2. **Run 2 isn't fast enough.** +25% EXP and starting at Spurt 2 is a small speed-up; run 2 will feel like the same ~8 hours.
3. **The update is aimed at the few.** A Saturday launch + thumbnails about the Hall of Fame promise something new players can't reach for ~8 hours (icon/thumbnail "overpromise" risk).

**Good parts (keep):** opt-in with ARE YOU SURE; cosmetics/trainers/coins kept; status that people SEE (stars, statue, top-10 board); a real endless goal for top players.

## Verdict
Keep the Hall of Fame for Saturday (it's built, opt-in, and it won't hurt new players), but **make it a reward, not a punishment**, and watch the numbers. If top players stop playing after entering, switch to Plan B.

## Fixes before / right after Saturday (small, Config only)
1. **Run 2 must FEEL fast:** ★1 = **x2 EXP** and start at **Spurt 3**, so the 6 spurts take ~15-20 min instead of ~1 h. Each star a bit faster (keep the gains cap rules).
2. **Keep the height as status:** show "PEAK 7'0" ★1" (their real peak) on the overhead tag and profile; the statue shows the peak body (already). Optional: a "Legend Form" look that lets ★ players wear their peak height as a cosmetic in the Hall and posing stage.
3. **Say it clearly on the ENTER screen:** "Run 2 is 3x faster. You keep everything except your height and muscles."
4. **Thumbnails/announcement:** lead with GROW (the first-minute promise) and use the Hall of Fame as the "goal" ("Become a LEGEND"), not as the whole update.
5. **Measure it:** HallOfFame_Entered, sessions and days played in the 7 days after entering vs. players at Alter 15 who didn't enter, SecondRun_Done (already planned). Keep the star bonuses in Configs so they can be changed live.

## Plan B (if players quit after entering): "Fame without reset"
- After Alter 15 **nothing resets**. Players earn **Fame Stars** by doing legend feats instead:
  - beating the top of the Champion Ladder, Strongman records, winning posing shows / showdowns, weekly Hall of Fame challenges ("lift the most this week").
- Stars give the same visible status: ★ tag, statue, top-10 board, a small boost, titles.
- An **optional** "Rebirth for x2" button stays for players who like resets (their choice, never needed for stars).
- This is "status without loss": the best players keep their tall body AND keep climbing. Two paths serve two kinds of players: a reset layer for number-chasers, records/boards for show-offs.

## Sources
- [Prestige and Ascension in Idle Games Explained (idlegames.win)](https://idlegames.win/article/prestige-and-ascension-idle-games-explained/): clear rewards, fast reruns, preserved effort; punishing resets drive players away.
- [I Built 7 Idle Games in 30 Days (dev.to)](https://dev.to/aguier/i-built-7-idle-games-in-30-days-what-i-learned-about-incremental-design-5d3f): "prestige must feel like acceleration, not punishment"; next run noticeably faster within ~30 s.
- [Understanding the Player Brain: Loss Avoidance (Bottom Feeder)](https://bottomfeeder.substack.com/p/understanding-the-player-brain-pt): players hate losing things; framing changes how a mechanic feels (WoW "rested" vs fatigue).
- GYM ARC vault: [[GYM ARC - Hall of Fame]], [[GYM ARC - Body Alters]] (alters ~27 min each, 14-15 longer), analytics (about half leave before the first spurt).


## The 2nd, 3rd... Hall of Fame (owner, 29 Oct: "think about their 2nd hall of fame too")
The risk after star 1: every new run is the SAME content again. Prestige research: later resets must add **new choices and new things**, not just a bigger %, and each run must be **faster** (idlegames.win; "acceleration, not punishment").

**Rules for every star:**
1. **Each run is faster** (target, tune with tests/pacing): run 1 ~8 h → run 2 ~3 h → run 3 ~2.5 h → run 4 ~2 h → ~1.5 h by star 6 → ~1 h floor by star 10. Never slower than the run before.
2. **Each star gives something you can SEE**, not only %: a star tag colour, pedestal, aura, Legend Form colour, title.
3. **Each star (from 2) lets you pick 1 of 3 STAR PERKS** (permanent, mostly quality-of-life, never competition power): auto-claim Growth Spurts, start with Power Jump/feats unlocked, start at a higher spurt, quick transform (skip alter popups), +trainer EXP, keep 1 skill-tree branch. Choices = depth, not a repeat.
4. **Nothing is re-taught:** no tutorial, no guides, every gym/feature stays open (already true), Road shows only what's new.
5. **The next star's reward is always shown** (star track on the Hall of Fame screen).
6. **Alters 16+ (Gem, January) stay open after Alter 15**: entering the Hall of Fame is a choice you can make at Alter 15 or later; entering from a higher alter (e.g. 20) gives a bonus (an extra star perk pick), so nobody has to pick blindly.

| Star | Run time (target) | You get (visible) | Perk / boost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ~3 h for run 2 | Bronze ★ tag, HALL OF FAMER title, statue on a bronze pedestal, free trainer roll | x2 EXP, start at Spurt 3 |
| 2 | ~2.5 h | Silver ★, bronze star aura, Legend Form colour 1 | Pick 1 of 3 star perks |
| 3 | ~2 h | Hall of Fame trainer agency (star-only trainer pool), silver pedestal | +coins, perk pick |
| 4 | ~2 h | Silver star aura, a star-only gear piece | Start at Spurt 4, perk pick |
| 5 | ~1.5 h | Gold ★, gold pedestal, Legend Form colour 2, title | +coins, perk pick |
| 6-9 | ~1.5 h | One cosmetic each (pose, statue frame, tag effect) + Hall of Fame champions (star-only ladder) | Perk pick each |
| 10 | ~1 h | LEGEND title, gold star aura, diamond pedestal, name on the permanent LEGENDS wall | Big bonus (as now) |
| 11+ | ~1 h | A cosmetic every 5 stars | Small % per star (as now) |

**Status for repeat players:** the stars board (exists), a weekly **fastest run** board (speed-run race for star players), and the LEGENDS wall.
**Measure:** run time per star, how many start run 3 after run 2, days played per star. If players stop after star 2-3, add more perk choices before adding more %.
