# Hall of Fame: good or bad for players? Will we lose players?

Back to [[00 GYM ARC Home|GYM ARC Home]] · Feature: [[GYM ARC - Hall of Fame]] · Related: [[2026-10-29 GYM ARC Why players stop grinding - research]], [[2026-10-29 GYM ARC Gym genre research]]

Owner, 29 Oct: "research if hall of fame is a good thing or bad thing for players and we will lose players or not. if we lose then another idea."

## What our Hall of Fame is
A second rebirth after **Alter 15** (≈ 1 h of Growth Spurts + 15 alters × ~27 min ≈ **8 hours of play**). Opt-in, with ARE YOU SURE.
- **Starts over:** height (back to small), Growth Spurts, muscles, alters and their gains.
- **Keeps:** trainers, coins, alter skins (as looks), titles, auras, gear, passes, LVL, gyms and features unlocked.
- **Gives:** a star (★1 = +25% EXP and start at Spurt 2, more per star), a statue of your peak body, ★ over your head, top-10 board, a ceremony.

## Will it lose players?
**New players: no.** Almost nobody reaches it. With ~4% coming back on day 2 and Alter 15 needing ~8 hours, maybe a handful of players will ever see the ENTER button. The Hall of Fame is not why 96% leave; the first 5 minutes are (about half leave before the first Growth Spurt).

**Your best players: yes, there's a real risk.** Research on prestige/rebirth:
- Players feel a loss much more strongly than an equal gain (loss aversion). If a design asks players to give something up, many avoid it or feel angry after (Bottom Feeder).
- A reset only feels good when the next run is **clearly faster right away** ("acceleration, not punishment": noticeably faster within ~30 seconds) and the old run doesn't feel wasted (idlegames.win, dev.to).
- Bad prestige = unclear reward, punishing reset, redoing the same chores.

Our weak spots:
1. **It takes away the HEIGHT, the main fantasy.** After 8 hours of growing to 8'0", you're small again. Everything else is kept, but the thing they worked for is gone.
2. **Run 2 isn't fast enough.** +25% EXP and starting at Spurt 2 is a small speed-up; run 2 will feel like the same ~8 hours.
3. **The update is aimed at the few.** A Saturday launch + thumbnails about the Hall of Fame promise something new players can't reach for ~8 hours (icon/thumbnail "overpromise" risk).

**Good parts (keep):** opt-in with ARE YOU SURE; cosmetics/trainers/coins kept; status that people SEE (stars, statue, top-10 board); a real endless goal for top players.

## Verdict
Keep the Hall of Fame for Saturday (it's built, opt-in, and it won't hurt new players), but **make it a reward, not a punishment**, and watch the numbers. If top players stop playing after entering, switch to Plan B.

## Fixes before / right after Saturday (small, Config only)
1. **Run 2 must FEEL fast:** ★1 = **x2 EXP** and start at **Spurt 3**, so the 6 spurts take ~15-20 min instead of ~1 h. Each star a bit faster (keep the gains cap rules).
2. **Keep the height as status:** show "PEAK 8'0" ★1" on the overhead tag and profile; the statue shows the peak body (already). Optional: a "Legend Form" look that lets ★ players wear their peak height as a cosmetic in the Hall and posing stage.
3. **Say it clearly on the ENTER screen:** "Run 2 is 3x faster. You keep everything except your height and muscles."
4. **Thumbnails/announcement:** lead with GROW (the first-minute promise) and use the Hall of Fame as the "goal" ("Become a LEGEND"), not as the whole update.
5. **Measure it:** HallOfFame_Entered, sessions and days played in the 7 days after entering vs. players at Alter 15 who didn't enter, SecondRun_Done (already planned). Keep the star bonuses in Configs so they can be changed live.

## Plan B (if players quit after entering): "Fame without reset"
- After Alter 15 **nothing resets**. Players earn **Fame Stars** by doing legend feats instead:
  - beating the top of the Champion Ladder, Strongman records, winning posing shows / showdowns, weekly Hall of Fame challenges ("lift the most this week").
- Stars give the same visible status: ★ tag, statue, top-10 board, a small boost, titles.
- An **optional** "Rebirth for x2" button stays for players who like resets (their choice, never needed for stars).
- This is "status without loss": the best players keep their 8'0" body AND keep climbing. Two paths serve two kinds of players: a reset layer for number-chasers, records/boards for show-offs.
