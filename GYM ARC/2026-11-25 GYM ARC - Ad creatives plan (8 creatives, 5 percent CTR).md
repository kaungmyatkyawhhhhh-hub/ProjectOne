# GYM ARC ad creatives plan: 8 creatives, aim 5%+ CTR each (25 Nov)

Owner: "and for ads too, how many creatives? lets try to get 5%+ ctr in every creative ads".
Builds on [[2026-11-02 GYM ARC Ads - best practice and ChatGPT prompts]], [[2026-11-24 GYM ARC - Slow revive plan to 50 CCU before Christmas]], the approved icon look [[2026-11-25 GYM ARC - Icon rendered in Blender from the real avatar]].

## Facts (Roblox docs, checked 25 Nov)
- An ad creative = the 16:9 thumbnail shown on Home when the game is sponsored. Up to **10 per campaign**, shown evenly at first; Ads Manager shows results per creative, and the system then favours the best one per user group.
- Assets are moderated (up to ~48 h), campaigns ~24 h. Judge after 3-5 days, not the first day (learning).
- No official CTR benchmark is published. Our own Home thumbnail play-through rate was 3.23% (16 Nov). 5% is a stretch target, not a given.

## Decision: 8 creatives at launch (2 of the 10 slots kept for replacements)
Same finish as the approved icon G (our Blender render for layout -> ChatGPT finish -> Man face -> dark gym with warm lamps -> grade_guy.py + grade_color.py -> logo in code). Each one a DIFFERENT motivation, so the test actually learns something:
1. **Power / number:** 1000 LB dumbbell flex (T1, done).
2. **Transformation:** skinny noob -> giant (the Growth Spurt, the game's core hook).
3. **Competition:** arm wrestling a bully / boss across a table.
4. **Status:** #1 on the Hall of Fame board, crowd looking up at him.
5. **Collect / cosmetic:** ELEMENTS alters (Frost / Lava / Storm bodies) side by side.
6. **Social:** two friends spotting each other / flexing together (also the co-play signal).
7. **Overhead press:** giant barbell over his head, plates cut by the frame.
8. **Christmas:** Santa hat, lifting a giant present, snowy gym (for the 5 Dec update and the $200 burst).

## Rules for a high CTR (from the vault recipe + owner likes)
One big hero, a bright clean face (the Man face), one focal point, one huge number or nothing, strong Power-Lifters contrast, real game content (no clickbait: ad players who bounce hurt), readable at row size (test every one at ~300x169 and in a fake Home row before uploading).

## Run + cut loop
- Upload all 8. Plays objective, new players, phone + PC.
- After 3-5 days: any creative under ~3% CTR -> off; replace with a variant of the best one (same idea, new pose/colour).
- Keep the winner as the Home thumbnail too (Thumbnail Personalization).
- Log each creative's CTR here after each run.
