## Rules kept
- The platform's menu button (top-left of the game view) is never on screen: clips are cropped below the top 76 px and away from that corner. No platform logo or currency icon in our art.
- No invented numbers: every stat is read off the recording.
- Gameplay is shown inside cards and posters, never stretched to fill the vertical frame.

## Learned (for the next game video)
- A game recording in the collage style = gameplay as 1 s clips (30 jpg frames, speed-ramped 1.3-3.2x) inside prints, posters and stamp cards. 9 clips were enough for 12.6 s.
- In-game characters are too small to cut out sharply. Use: the game's own key art for the hero, its real UI panels as stickers (REP button, 455 LB panel, VOLTA card, TAP TO GROW), and one colour-keyed cut-out (the gold growth flash, masked by its flat colour).
- rembg on the thumbnail art returned only the head for one and the upper body for the other: look at every cut-out.
- Display fonts have no arrow glyph: draw the arrow as a shape.
- The Studio recording has the window bars in it: next time record the live game full-screen (pipeline skill section 1) so the whole 1080p is game.
