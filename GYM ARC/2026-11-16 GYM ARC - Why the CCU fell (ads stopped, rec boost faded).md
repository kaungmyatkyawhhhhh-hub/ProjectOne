# GYM ARC: why the CCU fell (50-100 → 10-50), 16 Nov

Owner: "why is my gym arc game starting to fall off? used to get 50-100 ccu average now its 10-50". Method: [[Brain/Methods/Data first]]. Source: Creator Hub analytics, read 16 Nov 22:55 MMT. Realtime CCU 20.

## 1. The paid ads stopped (the main cause)
New players with plays by source, last 56 days: **Sponsored Ads 41,351 (43%)**, Home Recommendation 32,826, Other 13,282, Search 5,203, Friends 4,619 (total 96,400).

| Day (UTC) | Total new | Sponsored Ads | Home Rec |
|---|---|---|---|
| ~31 Oct (big ad burst) | ~15,000 | ~13,000 | small |
| 7 Nov | 10,033 | 5,578 | 2,595 |
| 9 Nov | 2,666 | 0 | 1,864 |
| 11 Nov | 5,900 | 0 | 4,479 |
| 14 Nov | 3,379 | 0 | 2,406 |
- Every 50-100 CCU stretch was an ad burst. Since 8 Nov there have been 0 ad players.

## 2. Roblox's recommendation boost faded
- Home rec impressions jumped to ~60k users/day around 11 Nov (after the GEM update), then fell back to ~35k. Home rec plays: 4,479 → 2,406.
- **The signals Roblox ranks on (28 days):**
  - play through rate 3.23%;
  - first-play bounce: **14.9% leave in under 60 s, 22.3% in 61-180 s** (about 37% gone within 3 minutes; the dashboard warns high rates lower exposure);
  - play days D2-7: 0.28;
  - **co-play almost zero** (0.016 days);
  - playtime D1 12.1 min.
- The 7-day overview, vs the week before:
  - D1 retention 7.19% (-27%; it was 10-11% around 3-6 Nov, normally 5-9%, below the genre benchmark band);
  - playtime 14.2 min (-26%);
  - new users 4,450/day (-20%);
  - DAU 5,529 (-11%).

## 3. Ad players don't stay
| Source | D7 retention | 7-day playtime |
|---|---|---|
| Sponsored Ads | 0.63% | 7.4 min |
| Home Rec | 2.63% | 22.5 min |
| Friends | 0.45% | 10.2 min (34% conversion) |
- Ads bought CCU while they ran, but few of those players came back. That's why CCU drops as soon as a burst ends.

## Conclusion
- 50-100 CCU was ad-supported. ~20-40 is GYM ARC's organic level with today's recommendation signals.
- Organic growth needs better signals: the 3-minute bounce, co-play, playtime and the lag (same list as [[2026-11-13 GYM ARC - Debate kids simulation]]).
- Then run ads in bursts, so recommendations get a bigger push afterwards.
