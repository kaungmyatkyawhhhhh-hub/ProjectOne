# Auras rework: stronger, part of the game

Back to [[00 GYM ARC Home|GYM ARC Home]] · Feature: [[GYM ARC - Aura]] · Decisions: [[2026-10-29 GYM ARC Every decision - the whole game]]

Owner, 29 Oct: "auras look too soft and feel very weak; they should be more a part of the game, like Gym League auras."

## How Gym League does it (researched)
- **Spin for auras** in the Aura menu: 1,500 in-game cash a spin (or 19 Robux); a 149 R$ "Premium Reroll" with 10x Legendary/Mythical chance.
- **~20 auras by rarity:** Common 63.9% (x1.05), Rare 25% (x1.15-1.25), Epic 10% (x1.4-1.5), Legendary 1% (x1.7-1.8), Mythical 0.1% (x2); Robux-only Galaxy x2.5 (1,999 R$) and VIP Aura x1.5 (349 R$).
- **Every aura multiplies money**, and rarer ones "look significantly better" (big, loud effects). Auras stay in your inventory; swap any time.
- Their names copy anime IP (Super Saiyan, Ultra Instinct): we don't.

## GYM ARC today
- 13 auras (Ember, Frost, Storm, Golden, Titan, Inferno, Blood Moon, Void, Neon, Galaxy, Royal, Toxic, Shadow): earned by LVL / 7'0" / quests, coins (Toxic 50k, Shadow 150k), Aura Pack pass (5), VIP (Royal).
- Look: body-hugging rim glow + flames/lightning/smoke; walking = power 0.15 (very soft), full only while PUMPED; others full within 40 studs.
- Small perks since 22 Oct, not clearly shown. Planned: auras through the skill tree.

## Decisions
1. **Auras become a main collection + show-off system** (like trainers, but for looks + one stat):
   - **AURA SPIN** at a new "Aura Altar" in the town plaza: **coins only** (about 2 min of coins a spin at your stage), odds always shown, **pity** (Epic in 30, Legendary in 100, Mythic in 300). No Robux spins, no Robux luck (same rule as trainers; PolicyService rules don't bite).
   - **~24 auras in 5 rarities**, original names (no anime/brand names): Common 60% (x1.05 coins), Rare 27% (x1.15), Epic 10% (x1.35), Legendary 2.7% (x1.6), Mythic 0.3% (x2). The stat is **coins** (like Gym League) so auras don't fight the gains cap and never decide competitions.
   - **Signature auras you EARN, never spin** (the status ones): Golden (6'0"), Stone Titan (Alter 9), alter tier auras, champion auras (Champion Ladder), Hall of Fame star auras. These are the best-looking, and they show you did something.
   - Existing auras: everyone keeps theirs; each gets a rarity + coin stat. Aura Pack (199 R$) and VIP Royal stay, with their exact stat shown on the card.
2. **They look STRONG** (rarity = size and loudness):
   - Common: rim glow + light particles. Rare: + rising wisps. Epic: + ground ring + colour light. Legendary: + body-hugging flames/lightning + small light pillar. Mythic / Signature: + big ground ring, pillar of light, orbiting sparks, a sound loop.
   - **Walking = at least 50% power** (today 15%): people must see your aura across the gym. Lifting = 100%. PUMPED / PR = burst.
   - Equip moment: a 1-second "power up" burst + sound. A new Legendary+ aura from a spin: the rarity reveal ladder (like trainer rolls), a beam others can see (no server spam).
   - Your aura shows in the Growth Spurt / alter transformation scenes and on your Hall of Fame statue.
3. **Performance (phones):** your own aura full; others full within 40 studs for the nearest 6, glow-only further, off past 90 studs; Mythic/Signature capped at ~60 particles on phones; "Others' Auras" setting stays; everything calm with Reduced Motion.
4. **Skill tree change:** the skill tree no longer has an AURA branch (auras have their own system); it keeps UPGRADES, GEAR and FEATS. Update prompt 94.
5. **GAINS display:** coins boost from the aura shows as "AURA x1.35 coins" in the Stats breakdown.

## Where it sits on the ladder
- Aura Altar opens at **Growth Spurt 3** (instead of the skill tree's aura branch).
- Signature auras: Spurt 6 (Golden), Alter 5/10/15 tier auras, Alter 9 Stone Titan, champions, Hall of Fame stars.
