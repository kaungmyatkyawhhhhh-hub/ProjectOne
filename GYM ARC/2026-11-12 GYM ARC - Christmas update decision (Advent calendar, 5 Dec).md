# GYM ARC: Christmas update instead of a new Christmas game (12 Nov)

Back to [[00 GYM ARC Home|GYM ARC Home]].

**How it was decided (owner, 12 Nov):**
- The owner asked for a Christmas game, and Claude proposed a new game, "DON'T WAKE SANTA" (sneak/steal + rarity reveal, launch 28 Nov).
- The owner asked "is it good or too risky". The risks: a short season, a crowded December, the last Santa steal game is dead, steal games need full servers, and the deadline is tight.
- Owner: **"how about we just update our game" → yes, a Christmas update for GYM ARC.**

**Why GYM ARC:**
- It's live, with players and money: about 50K Robux every 2 weeks.
- The shareholder deal is now 20% for him.
- A seasonal event lifts a live game: the Rivals advent calendar took the game from 50K to 100K+ CCU in 2024.

**Plan (Claude's proposal, not yet built):**
- Launch **Sat 5 Dec**, before the big games' mid-December events and the school breaks. It ends early January.
- Parts:
  - snowy winter gym (keep the premium brick-and-steel look);
  - 24-day **Advent calendar**;
  - limited **Christmas Alters** paid for with an event currency (candy canes from training);
  - free gift drops in the server;
  - one limited pass/boost at 449 R$ or less.
- **Advent calendar style:**
  - the Tap Simulator REBORN bar, not the old flat grid;
  - chunky outlined gradient panels, 3D presents that pop out of their tiles, holographic big-day tiles;
  - the claim reveal plus the layered sound from the research notes;
  - normal days use a 2.5 s reveal; days 7, 14, 21 and 24 use the full cutscene formula;
  - missed days are frozen in ice with a Robux Restore button.
- Research:
  - [[2026-11-12 Research - Modern Roblox advent calendar and pass UI (videos watched)]]
  - [[2026-11-12 Research - Tap Simulator REBORN secret reveal sound breakdown (measured)]]

**Fits the roadmap ([[2026-11-11 GYM ARC - Next update decision (fix hotfix, then ELEMENTS 14 Nov)]]):**
- The Christmas update takes the 5 Dec slot (after LEGENDS on 28 Nov); POWER HOUR moves after it.
- If GATE 1 (21 Nov) says the funnel is still broken, the funnel fix comes first and the Christmas scope shrinks to calendar + winter map.
- UI must use the GYM ARC UI kit (root CLAUDE.md rule). Any new piece (the present tile, the reveal) is built as a reusable kit component in the same style.
