# GYM ARC - Legend NPCs

Back to [[00 GYM ARC Home|GYM ARC Home]].

Big, original bodybuilder characters who visit the starter gym now and then with a one-time challenge.

## How it works
- **Visits:** one legend shows up every 20-30 minutes and stays 3 minutes (first visit 2 minutes after a server starts). He stands left of the entrance aisle.
- **Challenge:** talk to him to take his one-time quest (for example "Do 15 reps on the Pull-Up Bar"). You can finish it any time after, even once he has left.
- **Reward:** 60 coins + 1 DNA Token and his matching Epic title.
- **The legends:** Lance Lats (Wingspan), Brody Pumpkins (Pumpkin Delts), Tank McFlex (Tank Mode), Kyle Swole (Certified Swole), Ricky Reps (Rep Machine), Big Beefington (Beefcake), Tiny Tim Gains (Small but Mighty), Chad Gainsworth (Gainsworth Approved). Each has a funny one-liner.
- **Legends Met:** a list of the legends you have met.
- All original characters (no real people).

## Where it lives
- `Config/Legends`, titles in `Config/Titles`
- `Server/LegendService`, `LegendClient`
