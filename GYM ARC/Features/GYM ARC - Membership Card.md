# GYM ARC - Membership Card

Back to [[00 GYM ARC Home|GYM ARC Home]].

Your gym membership tier, earned by lifetime reps.

## How it works
- **Tiers by lifetime reps:**
  - Bronze: 0
  - Silver: 500
  - Gold: 2,500
  - Platinum: 10,000
  - Diamond: 30,000
- **Where it shows:** on the player card / the returning player's profile on the menu.

## Where it lives
- `Config/ShowOff` (Membership), `Server/ShowOffService`, `MenuClient` (profile)
