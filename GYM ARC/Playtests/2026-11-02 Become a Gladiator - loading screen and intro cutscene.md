## Why it works
1. **It's the game in one story:** you are weak at the exact thing you'll do (lifting), the rival mocks it, you see the reward (your future body), you see the path (5 zones with multipliers), you get the first step (follow the arrows).
2. **The vision of the future self** is the strongest beat: the "max potential" idea, but told as a story moment, not a popup.
3. **The rival sets the end goal** ("I'll be waiting at the top") = a reason to play for weeks.
4. **The map flyover with numbers** shows the whole progression in 5 s.
5. **Spectacle:** a huge crowd, low hero angles, flash cuts (orange, white), god rays, comic text (CLANK!), a sweat drop, highlighted words in captions.
6. Captions: letterbox, speaker name small in gold, the line big bold white.
7. Ends straight in a clear task with arrows on the floor.
