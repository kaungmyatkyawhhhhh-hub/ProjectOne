# GYM ARC - Assets

Back to [[00 GYM ARC Home|GYM ARC Home]]

Every mesh, texture, image, clothing item and animation the game uses, with its id and where it is used. Our own
uploads belong to the group "Myanmar Builders Club" (owns the game). Sounds are in [[GYM ARC - Music and Sounds]].

**Creator Store:** nothing has been inserted from the Creator Store / toolbox. NPC hair and clothes are Roblox-made
catalog items only. The world (gyms, town, machines, plates, props) is built from parts by builder scripts in Studio.

## Body stage meshes (62 meshes, imported by hand)

`ReplicatedStorage.BodyStages` by `tools/body_stages_setup.luau`. The ids are inside the MeshParts in Studio (not

## Live muscle data (not an uploaded asset)
`ReplicatedStorage.MuscleData`: the muscle v9 unified mesh data (generated by `tools/gen_muscle_data`), turned into
EditableMeshes on your own character at runtime (7 meshes: torso front + back, two upper arms, two upper legs, waist).

## HUD icons
`Config/HudIcons.luau` has an `image` field for every tile (Stats, Muscles, Titles, Genetics, Wardrobe, Emotes, Crew,
Store) and pill (Coins, Quests, Daily, Settings). **All are empty**: the game uses its own built-in white line icons
(`Shared/UI/Icons`). Paste `rbxassetid://<id>` to use your own art.

## Built in Studio (no asset ids)
These are part models saved in the place, made by builder scripts (`tools/builders`, see `tools/bake.md`):
plate templates `ReplicatedStorage.GymAssets` (M WEIGHTS plates 55/45/35/25/10/5), the genetics screen gym
`ReplicatedStorage.GeneticsSet`, all gyms, machines, the town, NPC models, the gym cat, the mirror, leaderboard wall,
statue pedestal and season decor.
