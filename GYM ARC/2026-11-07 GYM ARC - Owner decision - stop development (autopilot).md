# GYM ARC: the owner stopped development (7 Nov 2026)

Back to [[00 GYM ARC Home|GYM ARC Home]].

**Decision (owner):** "i want to stop working on gym arc".

**Why (owner's numbers):**
- GYM ARC earns about 50K Robux a month (about 16.5 lakh MMK).
- The shareholder gets 70% (about 11.5 lakh) until he has received 1.75x his ad investment; the owner gets 30% (about 5 lakh).

**What it means (autopilot):**
- The game stays live and earning. Do NOT delete or make it private (that would cut the owner's 30% and wipe players' progress).
- No new features, art, events or weekly updates. No new GYM ARC prompts unless the owner asks.
- Fix only real breakage (errors, data loss), done by Claude Code with minimal owner time.
- Optional last fix, ready: [[2026-11-07 GYM ARC Prompt - Posing show results too intrusive]].
