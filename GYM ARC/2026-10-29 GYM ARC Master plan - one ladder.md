# Master plan: Hall of Fame + new features + old features as ONE ladder

Back to [[00 GYM ARC Home|GYM ARC Home]] · Built from: [[2026-10-29 GYM ARC Why players stop grinding - research]], [[2026-10-29 GYM ARC Abilities - research]], [[2026-10-29 GYM ARC Gym genre research]], [[2026-10-29 GYM ARC Hall of Fame - good or bad]], [[Playbook notes - Costly mistakes of big games]]

## The idea in one sentence
**"Lift to grow taller, from 4 feet to 8 feet, and enter the Hall of Fame."** Every feature (old and new) hangs on that one ladder, and each step of the ladder unlocks 1-2 things. Nothing is deleted; things are just shown at the right time.

## The ladder (who you become → what opens)
| Step | You become | Opens (old = ●, new = ★) | Main goal shown |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Start (0-5 min)** | 4'0" | ● Machines, ● Shop, ★ Future You card | First Growth Spurt (~4 min) |
| **Spurt 1** | 4'4" | ● Beach Gym + posing shows, ★ Power Jump | "Next: Trainers" |
| **Spurt 2** | 4'8" | ● Trainers (free roll), ● Arm wrestling, ★ Champion Ladder (first 3: Benny, Coral...), ★ Skill tree (first points) | Next champion + Spurt 3 |
| **Spurt 3** | 5'0" | ● Pro Gym, ● Genetics, ★ Ground Stomp | Pro Gym champion (Dante) |
| **Spurt 4** | 5'4" | ● National Show, ● Crews, ★ Iron Grip Throw + first "NEEDS X STRENGTH" objects | Lift the plaza boulder |
| **Spurt 5** | 5'8" | ● Powerlifting Gym, ★ Sprint Charge, ★ Server crown | Powerlifting champion |
| **Spurt 6** | 6'0" | ● Body Alters, ★ Flex Shockwave | Alter 1 |
| **Alter 2** | 6'0" (skin changes) | ● Strongman Yard + Showdown | Yard champion |
| **Alters 3-14** | 6'4" (Alter 5) → 6'8" (Alter 10) | ● New alter skins each tier, ★ more champions, ★ Hype Roar (Alter 5), ● Legends visit as ladder champions | Top of the Champion Ladder |
| **Alter 15** | 7'0" LEGEND (8'0" stays the max for Alter 30) | ● **Hall of Fame** (fixed, see below) | ★ stars, statue |

Always on (quiet, small UI): daily rewards, quests, streaks, codes, titles, leaderboards, profiles, photo mode, settings, skate board (fun movement, no tutorial step).

## Old features: what changes
| Feature | Change |
|---|---|
| Rivals (3) + Legends (8) | Become the **Champion Ladder** (prompt 92). Same characters, one board, visible rewards. |
| Tutorial | Only train → grow → next goal. **Remove the skate tip step** (and anything not about growing). |
| Skate park / skateboard | Stays, no guide until Spurt 2. |
| Basketball court | **Owner decides:** hide (gate it at Alter 5 as a side game, no HUD/guides) or cut. Recommended: hide. |
| Boosts (shakes, coconut water, creatine, protein, supplements...) | **Owner decides:** merge into 3 kinds: Stamina (shakes), Gains (supplements), Luck (rolls). Recommended. |
| Auras + gear | Keep their stats; unlocked through the skill tree (prompt 94), equip menu shows Current → New. |
| Looks + Alters | Unchanged (looks no stats). Shown on the Progression Road. |
| Titles, coins | Become the bonus, not the main reward. |

## Hall of Fame: the fix (before / right after Saturday, prompt 95)
1. **Run 2 is FAST:** ★1 = x2 EXP and start at Spurt 3 (~15-20 min for all spurts instead of ~1 h); a bit faster each star.
2. **Height is kept as status:** "PEAK 7'0" ★1" (their real peak) on the tag + profile; the statue shows the peak body; optional "Legend Form" (peak height) in the Hall and on the posing stage.
3. **Clear ENTER screen:** "Run 2 is 3x faster. You keep everything except your height and muscles."
4. **No cheaters as statues:** server checks for impossible numbers before anyone reaches the top-10 board or a statue.
5. **Measure:** HallOfFame_Entered + days played in the 7 days after; compare with Alter-15 players who didn't enter.
6. **Plan B (only if entered players quit):** "Fame without reset": stars from legend feats (top champions, records, weekly Hall challenges), keep your body; the reset stays optional for x2.
7. **Marketing:** lead with GROW; Hall of Fame = "Become a LEGEND" goal.

## Rules from the research (every update)
- Every new thing must make you taller/stronger, let you USE strength, or show it off.
- New content builds on old progress; never weaken owned items or move an old goal further away.
- No paid power in competitions. Original content only. Test events before launch.
