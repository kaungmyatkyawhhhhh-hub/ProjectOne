# GYM ARC - Supplements: why 0 sales, how to sell more (5 Nov)

Owner (5 Nov): Coach Dex's coin shop broke; players thought Robux was the only way and bought ~1,000 R$ of Pump Shop supplements. "before coach dex was broken, there was not a single supplement sale in a week". Owner: don't point to the coin route from the Pump Shop.
Based on: [[2026-10-29 GYM ARC Prices - every coin and Robux item]] (coin boosts 100-400 coins; Robux x1 25 / x5 99, same effect), [[Playbook notes - Monetization]] (value vs alternatives; offer at natural moments, when out of resources; calm wording for kids; no fake timers/discounts).

## Diagnosis
- The Robux supplement = the same boost as the coin one (100-400 coins, cheap mid-game). Nobody pays Robux for something coins buy. Demand exists (1,000 R$ in days) - the Robux version just has no reason to exist next to the coin one.
- Bought only where/when they're shown: Pump Shop only, no prompt at the moment they're needed.

## Fix (honest: never nerf the coin route to push Robux)
1. Robux supplements get something coins can't: a stronger / longer effect (e.g. coin Gains +25% 5 min vs Robux Gains +50% 15 min), works with Auto Rep, buyable from anywhere.
2. Show them at the moment of need: when stamina hits 0 (the "out of resources" moment Roblox recommends), on the HUD boost slot when no boost is active, after a Growth Spurt / NEW PR. Calm wording ("See boost"), max once per X minutes, never after a loss/fail.
3. Value shown clearly: exact numbers, x5 = real saving, "BEST VALUE" only if true.
4. Measure: sales per day by placement for 7 days.
