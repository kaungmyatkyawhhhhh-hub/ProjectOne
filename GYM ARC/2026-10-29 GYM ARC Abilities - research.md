# Abilities (like Super Power Training Simulator)? Research + pros and cons

Back to [[00 GYM ARC Home|GYM ARC Home]] · Goes with [[2026-10-29 GYM ARC Why players stop grinding - research]]

Owner, 29 Oct: "should we add abilities too like Super Power Training Simulator?"

## How SPTS does it
- 5 stats trained at their own equipment (Fist, Body Toughness, Psychic, Speed, Jump).
- Every main quest = "train X to a number" and unlocks a power:
  Invisibility (100 Psychic) → Energy Sphere Punch (1,000 Fist) → Damage Deflection (1,000 Toughness) → Water Run (1,000 Speed + Psychic) → Teleport (10K Speed) → Flight (10K Speed + Jump) → Bullet Punch (100K Fist) → Soul Attack (100K Psychic) → Conceal/Reveal Aura (1M in 3 stats) → Killing Intent (100M Psychic + 1,000 kills). One power (HellFire) was sold for 1,999 Robux.
- Powers are USED: flight opens new training areas, teleport range grows with speed, attacks for bosses and optional PvP.
- Result: one of Roblox's biggest games in 2018 (often top 4), created the "SPTS" genre and many clones; it fell when updates stopped. Remakes still get ~5-15k players. Players' complaints: repetitive, 40-60 h grinds, shallow stat-based PvP.

## Why abilities work (the reason to add them)
1. **They give strength a USE**, which is GYM ARC's main problem (see the grinding note). Each number you grind turns into something you can DO.
2. **Every unlock is a visible new desire:** "Train legs to X to get Ground Stomp."
3. **Movement powers make the map a reward:** a jump or flight that reaches a place you couldn't before.
4. **Shareable moments** (TikTok / friends): a stomp, a giant leap, throwing a boulder.
5. **They fit the skill tree** (nodes) and the Progression Road.

## Risks for GYM ARC
1. **Theme clash.** GYM ARC's rule is "gyms are REAL gym types, the fantasy is in the alters". Fireballs and teleport turn it into another superhero sim and copy SPTS's genre (copycat risk). Our unique hook is HEIGHT, not magic.
2. **Kids + PvP.** Damage abilities on other players = bullying new players, worse maturity rating (violence), more moderation risk. GYM ARC is a kids' game.
3. **Phones.** Every ability needs a button, a cooldown and VFX. More HUD clutter and lag on cheap phones (the game already has many systems).
4. **Exploits.** Speed, jump and flight are the first things exploiters fake; teleport/flight can skip gates and locks. Everything must be checked on the server.
5. **Scope.** 4-6 abilities = new UI, animations, sounds, VFX, balancing, tests. Weeks of work while the first-5-minute leak is still open.

## Verdict: YES, but "Strongman Feats", not superpowers
Small set, grounded in the gym fantasy (exaggerated like a cartoon, never magic), never hurts other players, each one has a job in the world.

| Feat | Unlock (trained group / spurt) | What it does | World use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Power Jump** | Legs, Growth Spurt 1 | Hold to charge a big leap (height scales with legs) | Reach rooftops, the skate park ledges, a secret rooftop gym |
| **Ground Stomp** | Legs + Core, Spurt 2 | Shockwave ring, plates and props bounce, dust | Break cracked floors / crates to open shortcuts, knock coins out of piñata-style props |
| **Iron Grip Throw** | Arms, Spurt 3 | Pick up and throw props (barrels, tires, boulders) | Strength-gated objects ("NEEDS 25,000 STRENGTH"), throw-for-distance board |
| **Sprint Charge** | Cardio / stamina, Spurt 4 | Short burst of speed with a wind trail | Races, getting around the bigger map |
| **Flex Shockwave** | Posing, Spurt 5 | A pose that sends a gold aura ring (cosmetic + small push on PROPS only) | Posing shows, the Hall of Fame, showing off |
| **Hype Roar** | 7'0" / Alters | Roar that gives nearby friends +10% gains for 60 s | Social glue: training with friends is better |

Rules:
- No ability ever damages or knocks back other players (props only).
- One ability button on phones (the last-used one) + a small wheel; no 6 buttons on screen.
- VFX pooled, capped, off with Reduced Motion; server checks every use (cooldown, distance, height).
- Abilities never sold for Robux (SPTS sold HellFire; for us that = pay-to-win + rating risk). Cosmetic variants (stomp colour, trail) can be sold.
- Each feat is a node on the skill tree and a stop on the Progression Road.

## When
After "Future You" + the Champion Ladder (they fix the early leak and give the ladder). Then Feats together with the strength-gated objects (step 3 of the grinding plan): the objects ARE the reason to want the feats.
