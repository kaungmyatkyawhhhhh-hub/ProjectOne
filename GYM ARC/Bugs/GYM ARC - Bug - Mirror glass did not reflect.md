# GYM ARC - Bug - Mirror glass did not reflect

Back to [[00 GYM ARC Home|GYM ARC Home]] · [[GYM ARC - Bugs index|All bugs]]

- **What happened:** The progress mirror's glass showed nothing of you.
- **Cause:** There was no live reflection at all.
- **Fix:** The glass shows a live mirrored copy of you and the room (only within 25 studs).
