# GYM ARC - Bug - Panels hidden under the tile menu

Back to [[00 GYM ARC Home|GYM ARC Home]] · [[GYM ARC - Bugs index|All bugs]]

- **What happened:** The Genetics screen and the Title Book sat under the new, wider tile menu; the top-bar pills showed on top of full-screen panels; long coach lines ran into the top-right buttons.
- **Cause:** Panels were placed for the old narrow side menu.
- **Fix:** They open to the right of the tiles, pills hide behind full-width panels, long coach lines use smaller text.
- **Commit:** 99a75dc
