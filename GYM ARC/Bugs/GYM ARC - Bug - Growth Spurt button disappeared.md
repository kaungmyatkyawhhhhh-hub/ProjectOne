# Bug: the Growth Spurt button disappeared

**What happened:** the Growth Spurt card on the right was gone.

**Cause:** at the 7'0" cap the card and the Stats panel button were hidden completely, and below the cap the card only showed when nothing more important did (one card at a time, and it had the lowest priority).

**Fix (15 Oct):** the card always shows under the current card. At the cap it reads "MAX GROWTH SPURT, Reached 7'0"" in grey, and the Stats panel shows a grey "Max Growth Spurt reached" button, as the design brief says.
