# GYM ARC - Bug - World signs in your face

Back to [[00 GYM ARC Home|GYM ARC Home]] · [[GYM ARC - Bugs index|All bugs]]

- **What happened:** a huge ARM WRESTLING sign with the white diamond could show right in front of you.
- **Cause:** the arm wrestling tables used the same big diamond sign as the machines, and some world signs drew through walls or from far away.
- **Fix:** the tables get a small gold name label instead, the diamond only shows on machines within 15 studs, and every world sign is sized in studs, never draws through walls and hides beyond 40 studs. The RIVAL tag only shows while that rival's challenge is on. (The starter table itself is in the gym, 33 studs from the spawn.)
