# GYM ARC - Bug - Gym-goers blocked the walk to the bench

Back to [[00 GYM ARC Home|GYM ARC Home]] · [[GYM ARC - Bugs index|All bugs]]

- **What happened:** Three NPC gym-goers stood shoulder to shoulder across the middle of the gym. The tutorial's first walk to the Flat Bench got stuck on them and the camera got stuck behind their heads.
- **Cause:** Their standing spots were placed in the main walkway.
- **Fix:** Moved in front of the dumbbell rack on the right wall, clear of the mat, arm wrestling table, dip station and pro gym door.
