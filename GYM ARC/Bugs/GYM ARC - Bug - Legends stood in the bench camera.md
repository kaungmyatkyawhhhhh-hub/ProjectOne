# GYM ARC - Bug - Legends stood in the bench camera

Back to [[00 GYM ARC Home|GYM ARC Home]] · [[GYM ARC - Bugs index|All bugs]]

- **Cause:** The legends' visit spot was inside the machine camera's view.
- **Fix:** Legends now visit left of the entrance aisle, away from every machine.
