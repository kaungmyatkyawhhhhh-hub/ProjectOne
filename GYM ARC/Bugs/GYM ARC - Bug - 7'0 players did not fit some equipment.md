# GYM ARC - Bug - 7'0 players did not fit some equipment

Back to [[00 GYM ARC Home|GYM ARC Home]] · [[GYM ARC - Bugs index|All bugs]]

- **What happened:** A 7'0" mass monster could not fit through the sauna doorway, and the flat bench, pull-up bars, hanging leg raise and dips were too narrow for big bodies.
- **Cause:** The equipment was sized for smaller players.
- **Fix:** Sauna doorway 4.4 wide and open to the top; big lifters slide clear of the bench uprights; pull-up and leg raise uprights 7.8 apart; dip handles 3.5 apart.
- **Commit:** 28ee50f
