# GYM ARC - Bug - Machine panel covered the player

Back to [[00 GYM ARC Home|GYM ARC Home]] · [[GYM ARC - Bugs index|All bugs]]

- **What happened:** The machine panel sat in the middle of the bottom of the screen and covered your character while lifting.
- **Cause:** The earlier redesign put the whole panel at the bottom center.
- **Fix:** Rebuilt to the owner's mockup: the weight panel is on the right, REP and PUMP are round buttons at the bottom right, STOP TRAINING sits right above the stamina bar, nothing in the middle.
