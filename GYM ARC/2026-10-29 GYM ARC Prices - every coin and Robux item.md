## How the coin economy works (keep)
- One currency: coins. Every reward and most prices are **minutes of coins at your stage**, so nothing becomes worthless as you grow.
- Base coins per minute of play (tests/pacing): Spurt 0: 57 · 1: 110 · 2: 152 · 3: 200 · 4: 220 · 5: 222 · 6'0"/all alters: 328.
- **Change:** coins per minute stop growing after 6'0", so in the alter phase every reward and price feels flat for hours. **Decision: +5% coins per minute every alter and +10% per Hall of Fame star** (numbers keep going up, the incremental feeling the genre runs on).

## UPDATE 29 Oct (owner): FIXED coin values, no "minutes of coins"
Owner: "make the coins fixed values, not like 2 minutes, because it's gonna confuse players." Decided: **every price and reward is one fixed number that never changes.** Players get richer because they EARN faster as they grow (rep coins by weight tier, gym bonuses, +5% per alter, +10% per star), not because prices move. This is how most simulators do it (eggs per zone have fixed prices). Older "minutes" numbers in this note are replaced by this table.
