# GYM ARC - why a 2nd Hall of Fame feels pointless, and the Alter 16-30 schedule (1 Nov night)

Owner: "when is the alter 30 update, my players feel like there is no point of getting hall of fames again after doing it 1 time".

## Where the plan stands (vault)
- [[2026-10-29 GYM ARC Master plan - one ladder]]: Alter 15 = 7'0" LEGEND, "8'0" stays the max for Alter 30"; **Alters 16-20 (Tier 4 GEM) = the January winter update** (whole game plan: Gem alters 23 Jan). **Alter 30 is not scheduled at all.**
- [[2026-10-29 GYM ARC Hall of Fame - good or bad]] predicted exactly this: "The risk after star 1: every new run is the SAME content again"; fix = each star faster + each star something you can SEE + choices (star perks / Legacy cards) + a star-only agency + a fastest-run board + Alters 16+ open after 15. Live now: stars only add EXP % and a faster start (★1 x2 EXP + GS 3 ... ★10 x4.5 + GS 5), the ★ tag, statue (top 3), title at 1 and 10. The star track (prompt 101) and Legacy cards (105) are planned, not confirmed built.

## Why the 2nd star feels pointless (research: loss aversion, "acceleration not punishment", visible rewards)
1. Star 2 gives only a bigger % (x2 -> a bit more): nothing to see, nothing new to do.
2. The run is the same content again (same gyms, alters, bullies).
3. Players lose their height (the main fantasy) again for a small reward.
4. Nothing else to do at Alter 15 if you don't reset (no Alter 16+ yet).

## Proposal: two updates, sooner than January
### Update A (next Saturday, 7 Nov): "STAR TRACK" - make every star worth it (mostly Config + cosmetics)
- The star track on the Hall of Fame screen: every star's reward shown before you enter.
- Every star = something you SEE: star colour on the tag (bronze 1 / silver 2-4 / gold 5-9 / diamond 10), a star aura (2+), statue pedestal tier, a Legend Form colour, a pose / title.
- Star-only content: Hall of Fame trainer agency opens at ★3 (line-up already drafted: Champ Marcus, Olympian Reyes, The Bronze Titan, Heracles); star-only gear piece at ★4.
- Weekly FASTEST RUN board (Alter 15 time since entering): a race only star players can join.
- Each run clearly faster (targets: run 2 ~3 h, run 3 ~2.5 h, ... ~1 h floor at ★10) - check with the 2-Volta normal player.
### Update B (bring forward to ~22-29 Nov, not 23 Jan): ALTERS 16-30 (Tier 4 GEM 16-20, Tier 5 ... 21-30)
- 7'0" -> 8'0" at Alter 30 (the master plan's cap), new alter skins per tier, the alter skip (4x) keeps working.
- Hall of Fame from Alter 15 OR later: entering from a higher alter = a bonus (extra star, or +1 perk pick), so players who stay and grow are rewarded too ("Plan B: fame without reset" in the HoF note).
- Pace with the 2-Volta normal player; numbers by calculation before building.
- Scope: Alters 16-20 first (one tier, ~1 week), 21-30 the week after if needed.
