## 2. Is the trend still there?
- **Yes, gym is an evergreen niche, not a dead fad.** The top ~12 gym/strength games added up to about 31k players at the moment of checking (6.1k + 4.6k + 3.6k + ...). Muscle Legends has lived 7 years, Strongman Sim 5.5, Boxing Beta 5.
- **But it's not a mega-trend.** No gym game is in Roblox's Top Playing Now chart (the ~100th game there had ~13.8k players). The 2025-26 mega hits are Grow a Garden, Steal a Brainrot, 99 Nights (Naavik: growth concentrated in a few viral hits).
- **Spikes crash.** Gym League hit 143k (viral) and is at ~1k; +1 Muscle to Break Walls 32k → 2.8k in 5 months; Muscle Transform 26.7k → 1.4k and stopped updating. **Survivors = the ones that update every week for years** (Muscle Legends, Arm Wrestle Sim "update every Saturday", Gym Star weekly events).
- **Two things rising right now:**
  1. **"+1 X" incremental games** (+1 Spider Arm, +1 Muscle to Push Boulder, +1 Fat to Fit): fast spikes, fast deaths, many clones. Not our lane.
  2. **Realistic lifting / bodybuilding** (Project: Iron +900% this week, Power Lifters +168%): **this is GYM ARC's lane and it's growing.** Good news (demand) and bad news (Project: Iron is a direct competitor: 22 exercises, 62 muscles, genetics rolls, bodybuilding shows + powerlifting meets, cars, a social-media app).

**GYM ARC's unique angles (nobody else has them together):** growing TALLER (Growth Spurts), the Hall of Fame, a kid-friendly bright town, Body Alters (gold/stone/metal skins). Project: Iron is realistic and grim; GYM ARC should stay bright, fast and kid-friendly, and own HEIGHT.

## 4. GYM ARC's features: keep, hide early, or cut?
| Feature | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Machines, muscles, Growth Spurts, Body Alters, gyms | **Core, keep** | This is the game |
| Trainers (collect + follow) | Keep | Collection = long tail (all survivors have one) |
| Auras, gear (with stats) | Keep, via skill tree | Multipliers are standard in the genre |
| Posing shows, Strongman Showdown, Hall of Fame | Keep | Competition + status |
| Arm wrestling | Keep, add real-player challenges | Its own genre (Arm Wrestle Sim 2.7k, 3.5 years) |
| Rivals + legends | **Merge into the Champion Ladder** | One ladder instead of two scattered systems |
| Titles, daily rewards, quests, streaks, codes | Keep (quiet) | Standard retention, small UI |
| Genetics | Keep, after Spurt 1 | Project Iron has it; fine as a later layer |
| Crews | Keep, after Spurt 2 | Social, not needed in minute 1 |
| Skate park + skateboard | Keep, but **not a main feature** | Fun movement, off-theme; no guides before Spurt 2 |
| Basketball court | **Hide or cut** | Off-theme (a gym game doesn't need basketball); costs UI, sounds, guides, performance |
| Photo mode, profiles, mirror | Keep, quiet | Small, optional |
| Many separate currencies/boosts (shakes, supplements, coconut water, creatine...) | **Simplify** | Kids can't track 6 boost types; merge into 2-3 |

## 5. Can players handle this many features?
**Short answer: not all at once. Kids can handle a LOT of features if they meet them one at a time.**
- Evidence:
  - Successful gym games pitch themselves in 3-7 lines (Strongman Sim: "drag items, use energy at the gym, reach new areas"). If you can't say the game in one sentence, a kid can't either.
  - Players leave in the first 30 seconds when there are "too many buttons" at join; the fix is showing the UI gradually through the tutorial (DevForum).
  - "Presentation matters more than count": games with many mechanics work when they're introduced one by one (Portal 2's gels); a casual game shouldn't start with deep inventories and stats (Game Developer, feature fatigue).
  - More content doesn't fix retention; friction in the core loop does. Players can't name that friction, so feature requests mislead (RoWatcher).
  - GYM ARC's own data: about half of new players leave before the first Growth Spurt, while ~60 systems exist. The features aren't reaching them; the first minutes are.
- **Rules for GYM ARC (do this with prompt 91):**
  1. **Rule of 3 for the first 5 minutes:** train → grow → see the next goal. Nothing else on screen (no basketball, skate, crews, genetics, shop pop-ups).
  2. **1-2 new features per Growth Spurt,** shown on the Progression Road as the reward ("Spurt 2: Trainers + Arm Wrestling"). Every feature becomes a new desire instead of clutter.
  3. **One-sentence pitch** everywhere (description, loading, thumbnails): "Lift to grow taller, from 4 feet to 8 feet, and enter the Hall of Fame."
  4. **Before adding any new feature, ask:** does it make you taller/stronger, or let you USE your strength, or show it off? If not, don't build it.
  5. **Weekly updates beat big features.** Every survivor updates weekly; Gym League crashed and came back by returning to weekly updates.
