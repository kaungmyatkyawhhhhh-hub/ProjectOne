## 1. Midnight Racing: Tokyo (the owner's favourite: its way wins, except interiors)
Studio devGem (sixpence, bigcrazycarboy, AviaTuned, RedTerminator3000). Version Alpha 1.1. Place 3339374541.

Sources:
- wiki pages: main, Dealership, Vehicle Tiers, Ingame Economy, Experience and Levels, Manufacturer levels, Milestones / Daily objectives, Daily Rewards, Race Events, Player VS, Tuning Customization, Performance Upgrades, Suspension, Transmission, Fitment, ECU, Visuals Customization, Bodykit Shop, Game Passes, In-Game Shop, Game Controls, Mobile Compatibility, Radio Stations, Clans and Clubs, Shuto Expressway, Tokyo City, Otsuki, Tokyo Bay Area, What's coming in Alpha, Timeline;
- videos:
  - official trailer 2026 (DevGem);
  - "Midnight Racing: Tokyo Beginners Guide 2026" (pwhat);
  - "Level Up FAST" (RaceLine);
  - "25+ Best Tunes 2026" (RaceLine);
  - "Why NOBODY Can Compete With Midnight Racing: Tokyo" (AGB, 119K views);
  - "Types of Players" (72cw);
  - nanstuffMRT clips ("The 450 HP Devil Z Incident", 221K views);
- a fan review on the Vehicle Simulator wiki: chassis 7.9/10 "realistic for Roblox, cars feel different from drifty to very grippy", customisation 7.6/10, gameplay 5.9/10.

### What it is
A "racing sim-cade" in a recreated Tokyo:
- 180-190+ cars;
- 7 maps, each its own place:
  - Shuto Expressway (a near 1:1 C1 loop);
  - Mt. Otsuki touge with lakes and a village;
  - Tokyo City (free roam and role play);
  - Ichikawa (2 touges);
  - Shirosato Racing Center (oval, drift track, drag strip);
  - Tokyo Bay Area (Wangan, Rainbow Bridge, Tatsumi / Daikoku parking areas: the famous meet spots);
  - Tsukuba Circuit.
- Servers hold only 8 players: small, calm, every car matters.
- "6,000+ bodykit parts" (trailer). The Discord has 145,000+ members.

### First 3 minutes
- You spawn on a map's spawn pad, which has **teleport pads to every shop** (dealer, tuner, bodykit) and an interactive map.
- Starting cash is ¥1.5 M: enough for a mid Tier 1 car, or a weak Tier 1 car plus upgrades. The game lets you choose between those two paths.
- The first milestones pay quickly: "Buy your first car" ¥200K, "Complete your first event" ¥200K, "Modify your car" ¥250K. The first goal is set by the game itself.
- No tutorial was found in the sources; the tuning menus have tutorial text boxes. *(general knowledge: you must press J for ignition before the car moves.)*

### Driving feel, camera and sound
- **Physics:** a full sim-cade chassis (A-Chassis based, upgraded 2020).
  - Manual / sequential / automatic gearboxes (M) with a real clutch (Shift).
  - Traction control on/off (T).
  - Handbrake, stalling.
  - Ignition (J), blinkers (Z/X/C), lights off/on/auto (L).
- Each car has its own weight, power curve (a dyno graph in the tuner) and drive layout. RWD cars slide; ECU power makes them harder to hold.
- **Steering:** keyboard, mouse steering (mouse moves the wheel), controller, mobile buttons, racing wheels via a remapper, and even VR support.
- **Camera:** *(general knowledge)* a low chase camera plus hood and bumper views. The interior view is not good on most cars, which is why the owner picks Driving Empire for interiors.
- **Sound:** *(general knowledge)* per-car engine sounds with turbo spool and blow-off valves. The wiki's turbo stages say: "small boost, no blow-off valve" → "medium boost, stock BOV" → "big boost, aftermarket BOV". So the sound is part of the upgrade.
- **Radio:** 7 stations, each one style:
  - Eurobeat FM (Initial D);
  - Night Kid Radio (lo-fi / phonk);
  - Lost Decade FM (city-pop);
  - Shutorai Pirate Radio (trance);
  - Transfusion (ambient);
  - Deluxe Touring FM (jazz-fusion);
  - Nippon Radio X (house).
- **Night lighting:** most maps are at night. The promo art is all night: sodium-orange highway lights, neon city signs, tail-light streaks, wet-looking roads. Lighting differs from map to map; the devs want them all equal and "new maps will have time cycles".

### Cars, dealer and trims
- **4 tiers:**
  - Tier 1: economy cars and old sports cars, ¥1-2 M;
  - Tier 2: the "sweet spot between fun and fast", ¥2-3 M;
  - Tier 3: supercars, classics and muscle, ¥3-5 M, no level needed;
  - Tier 4: track cars and 80s/90s supercars, **needs Level 40**.
- Price comes from a formula (acceleration + top speed × tier value). The wiki says plainly: "a car's price is not indicative of its performance".
- **Dealer UI:**
  - walk to the counter, press E;
  - brands along the bottom with < > arrows;
  - car list on the left, performance stats on the right;
  - name, BUY and EXIT on top.
  - Cars on display inside the building. Each map has a dealer; Tokyo City has three, one per tier.
- **Fictional names close to the real ones:** Nikuna (Nissan), Yokoda (Toyota), Hokura (Honda), Preusser (Porsche), Lambardi (Lamborghini), DMW (BMW), Mercalles-Henz, Shogun (Mazda), Subashu. This is the same idea as our Suzaki / Toyoka.
- **Special / exclusive cars:** special kits or rare models, bought with a lot of yen or with Robux.

### Tuning and customisation (the heart of the game)
**3 shops** on every map: Dealership, Tuner Shop, Bodykit Shop. You walk up to the counter and hold E, and your car sits in the middle of the screen while you change it.

**Manufacturer levels:** you level each brand by driving its cars. A higher brand level makes its tuning parts much cheaper, for example:
- Fitment: ¥1.5 M at level 0, ¥100K at brand level 7;
- ECU: ¥2 M, or ¥250K at brand level 15.

This makes players stay loyal to a brand.

**Performance parts:**
- **Engine:** stages 1-5 of intake, exhaust, fuel system, ECU and internals, each unlocked at a player level (Level 1 to 76).
- **Turbo:** 3 stages (Level 18 / 31 / 49).
- **Brakes:** 4 stages.
- **Weight reduction:** -5% to -20%.
- **Tyres:** Drift / Street / Race / Pro.
- **Transmission:**
  - final drive, gear ratios with a **dyno curve and a picture of each gear's speed range**;
  - gear cutting.
- **Suspension:** damping 50-500, stiffness 1,000-15,000, front and rear apart.
- **Fitment, camber and ride height:**
  - offset ±0.5; it changes the looks *and* the handling (more offset = more stable).
- **Steering** tuning.
- Every option is bought per car. The tutorial boxes explain each one ("Stiffer front, softer rear: understeer").

**Looks:**
- stickers, decals, banners;
- rims;
- licence plates and plate covers;
- bodykits by part (bumpers, skirts, wings…) from OEM and aftermarket brands with their own logos (fictional);
- passes for underglow, headlight colour and exhaust colour.

### Money and progress
- **Earn by:**
  - events (races): ¥100K-1.1 M each with XP. 1st takes the pot, 2nd half, 3rd a quarter, the rest an eighth. 1x/2x multipliers. Up to 8 players.
  - **Laps:** driving full laps of a map pays.
  - **Kilometres driven** also count.
  - PlayerVS duels;
  - daily objectives;
  - milestones;
  - badges.
- **Milestones (one-off, a ladder that sets every goal):**
  - first car, first event, first win, first mod (¥200-250K);
  - 5 / 15 / 30 / 60 / 100 events;
  - unlock tiers 2-4;
  - own 5 / 10 / 25 / 50 cars;
  - a car from every brand;
  - max out a car;
  - play 1 hour / 24 hours.
  - At the top: "King of Cars, own every car" pays ¥25 M.
- **Daily rewards:** ¥100K rising daily to ¥1 M; **day 7 = a free Tier 2 car**. Miss a day and it resets.
- **Levels:** XP from driving, laps and events. Level 40 opens Tier 4, and part stages open up to Level 76.
- The design goal in the wiki: *"money should not be too easy or too hard; earning should feel rewarding and not too much of a grind."*

### World
- **Real roads at real scale:** tunnels, junctions, toll gates.
  - Cash toll gates make you wait (free).
  - An Electronic Toll Pass (49 R$) lets you drive straight through; using the e-gate without it costs a ¥25,000 fine.
  - A small, funny, cheap pass.
- **Parking areas** are made for photos and car meets (Tatsumi, Daikoku). Every map has a restaurant, a gas station and a parking garage near spawn.
- **Traffic:** no wiki page; light. *(general knowledge: sparse NPC traffic on some maps; the racing is between players.)*
- **Police:** none found. MR:T has no police; the rule-breaking flavour comes from tolls and fines only.
- Seasons: Otsuki's lakes freeze every winter.

### Social
- 8-player servers.
- **PlayerVS:** pick a player, pick a finish point far enough away, race.
- Event lobbies.
- Clans and clubs: clans are clubs that beat an established clan on the competitive leaderboard. They're organised on Discord.
- Car meets at parking areas, photography.
- Group rewards for joining the devGem group.

### Shop and passes (fair)
- **Passes:**
  - custom light colours 300;
  - Mobile Showroom 269 (open the shops anywhere);
  - boombox 250;
  - skateboard 159;
  - US/Canada plates 150;
  - folding chair 90;
  - e-toll 49.
- **Dev products:**
  - yen: ¥1 M for 115 R$, up to ¥25 M for 2,825;
  - 2x XP: 30 min 72 / 1 h 160 / 3 h 235.
- Nothing sells race wins. Cars are mostly earned. Passes are fun or convenient, not power. **Fair.**

### UI
- Dark, simple, clean. Big condensed white type on the promo art.
- Menus are walk-up counters with the car in the centre and the list on the left.
- An icon row for each part type (bumpers, lights, wheels, engine, turbo, tyres, brakes, steering, suspension).
- Mobile UI overhaul since 2020.
- Some menus feel old (the wiki calls PlayerVS "very outdated").

### What makes Midnight Racing great
1. **Cars that feel different.** Weight, power curve, drive layout and gearbox all matter; tuning changes how the car drives, and the tutorial tells you how.
2. **Real roads you can learn.** A near 1:1 C1 loop, touges with hairpins, real junctions, tunnels and tolls. You get better at the same road for months.
3. **Night mood.** Sodium lights, neon, tail-light streaks, city-pop and Eurobeat radio. It feels like Wangan / Initial D.
4. **Deep, slow tuning.** Engine and turbo stages, gear ratios with a dyno, suspension, camber and fitment, all opened by level and brand level. There is always one more thing to unlock for *your* car.
5. **Bodykits by part, from fake aftermarket brands** (6,000+ parts). Every build looks different.
6. **Milestones that hand you every goal.** First car → first event → tiers → 5/10/25/50 cars → King of Cars, each paying well.
7. **Daily streak with a free car on day 7.** A strong reason to come back.
8. **Small servers and meet spots.** 8 players, parking areas built for photos, clubs and clans. It feels like a car scene, not a lobby.
9. **Fair shop.** Fun passes (lights, boombox, toll, skateboard), no paid wins.
10. **Real controls for real fans:** manual with clutch, TCS, ignition, mouse steering, controller, wheel and VR, plus simple buttons on the phone.

### Best 3 to learn
1. Brand levels and part stages unlocked by level: long progress tied to *your* car.
2. Milestone ladder plus a daily streak that ends in a free car.
3. Tuning that explains itself (tutorial boxes, dyno curve, gear range picture) and changes the feel.

### Worst 3 to avoid
1. Too many maps as separate places: a teleport loading screen between friends, and the 8-player servers feel empty.
2. Old menus (PlayerVS) and lighting that changes from map to map.
3. Very expensive tuning before brand levels (¥1.5-3.5 M per option) can feel like a paywall for new players.

## 2. Driving Empire (the biggest; its way wins for interiors, clusters and the in-car view)
Voldex (also owns Brookhaven). Started as Wayfort in 2019, renamed Driving Empire in 2020, bought by Voldex in Dec 2022. Place 3351674303. **33,700 playing, 3.4 B visits, 93% likes**, 22-player servers.

Sources:
- wiki pages: main, Customization, Customization Garage, Currencies, Jobs, Housing, Homes, Gamepasses, Police Station, Speed Traps, Photo Mode, Crates, Locations, Interstate 10, Leaderboards, and the player-made list "Cars with Interior graphic that include Light indicators";
- videos (titles and descriptions):
  - "The BEST CAR PROGRESSION (Starter Guide 2026)" (Dema);
  - "Starting Over From $0" (Dema, 130K views);
  - "FULL GUIDE To Make MILLIONS FAST 2026" (EJ Yoo);
  - "I Tried Driving Empire WITH SOUNDS!" (CarterPlays);
  - "Spookfest 2026" official;
  - "2 NEW LIMITEDS & Scooters Update".

### First 3 minutes
- A join song plays ("Love In Space").
- You spawn by the dealership in Wayfort and drive a free starter car right away.
- You earn **cash and Miles just by driving**. Speed traps pay too.
- The **Locations** button teleports you to every dealer, race, garage and the neighbourhood, and shows how many people are waiting at each race.
- The first goal is set by the guides: save for a better car. "Starting from $0" videos are popular, so the climb from nothing is the fun.

### Driving feel, camera, interiors (the owner's reference)
- **Licensed real cars:** Lamborghini, McLaren, Porsche, Audi, Dodge and more, 300+ cars and boats. (We stay fictional.)
- **In-car view:** first person on most cars, with **detailed cabins**: seats, dashboards, steering wheels that turn, and **live clusters**.
  - The community keeps a wiki list of cars whose **interior blinkers flash in the cluster** ("bright cluster indicators", "clean analog dash signals", "modern digital indicators", "police dash indicators").
  - Players care about this for role play: the cluster is part of the fun.
- *(general knowledge)* The first-person camera sits at the driver's eye height with a normal FOV, so you see the cluster under the wheel rim and the road over the bonnet.
- *(general knowledge)* The driver's hands grip the rim. The owner says they are **too big**; ours stay real-size.
- **Photo mode:** time of day slider, FOV, depth of field, hide character. A cinematic free camera on PC (Shift+P).
- Sound: engine sounds per car. A popular video is literally "I tried Driving Empire WITH SOUNDS", a mod-style video with better engine sounds, which shows players want **better engine audio**.
- Car radio pass for 15 R$ (very cheap).

### Cars and customisation
- **Dealers:** Cars & Motorcycles, Boats, Planes & Helicopters, Houses. Selling a car gives cash back, plus the value of its performance upgrades.
- **Customisation Garage** (drive into a bay, "Vine Hill Customs"). Mobile Customisation costs $50,000 in-game or a 50 R$ pass.
  - **Looks:** primary paint (colour wheel + RGB + reflectance), secondary, chrome, rims (type + paint), body kits, calipers, interior colour, spoilers, wraps, licence plate, police mode.
  - **Performance:** the separate tab.
- **Limited cars and events:** Spookfest 2026 (candy, smash pumpkins, Halloween race, limited cars, trailers and parts), Winterfest presents, Easter, a LEGO Batman event (big brand collabs).
- **Crates** (random cars for cash or Robux, odds shown) were **removed in Nov 2023 after player backlash**. A lesson: players hate paid random cars.

### Money and progress
- **Cash:** from driving, speed traps (pay more the faster you go), races, selling cars, and arrests for police.
  - Group members +15% while driving, VIP +50%, 2x cash events.
- **Miles:** a progress counter for badges and the leaderboard by the dealer.
- **Bounty:** speeding past a trap, or driving on the wrong side (−$150, +15 bounty).
- Cash packs: $2,500 for 20 R$ up to $1 M for 2,450 R$.
- **Races:** sprint, circuit, highway, drag and cross country, at fixed race pads. There are leaderboards and community world records (highway race laps, drift scores).

### World
- **One big map:** city (Wayfort / Vine Hill), Interstate 10 and 410 (a long highway "into nowhere" for top speed), farm, beach, Laguna Heights neighbourhood, airport, water.
- Knockable street lamps and signs.
- Speed traps flash with a camera sound.
- **Police:**
  - teams: Citizens, Outlaws (rob ATMs; the cash comes when a timer ends or you reach the hideout), Security / police (the red circle under the car fills up to arrest).
  - Jail is 60 s or bail; a Free Bail pass exists.
  - Citizens get bounty only for speeding at traps or driving the wrong way.
- **Jobs:** delivery driver (a pass for more), taxi event, bus.
- **Houses (the model for our housing):**
  - **8 blueprints** bought in the Houses Dealership: small $450K-750K, medium $1.45-1.9 M, large mansions $4.95-9.3 M.
  - Claim **one plot** in Laguna Heights, with a **30 s cooldown** between claims.
  - Interactive doors, lights, beds and sinks; **roommates**.
  - Free garage spaces: 3 on small / medium plots, 5 on large; more cost cash or Robux.

### Social
- Car meets and community events, a Discord.
- Races with waiting counts.
- Roommates.
- Police vs outlaws.

### Shop and passes
- Track Pass 450, VIP 500 (+50% driving cash), Luxury 399, Delivery 399, Water Fun 199, Premium Boost 125, Car Radio 15, Bus 25.
- Cash packs.
- Mostly fair: no random boxes since 2023. VIP's +50% cash is the strongest buy.

### UI
- Rounded white / grey panels with bright accent buttons.
- Icon buttons bottom left (Jobs, Locations, Shop in yellow).
- Landscape teleport menu with tabs on the left.
- Photo mode button top left.
- Weekly changing game icon (a new car each update).

### Best 3 to learn
1. **Interiors with live clusters** (needles, digital screens, blinker lights) plus a good first-person camera: players notice and make lists of them.
2. **Many small reasons to drive:** speed traps that pay more the faster you go, Miles, police and outlaws, jobs, races with waiting counts, all on one big map.
3. **Weekly updates plus big seasonal events** (Spookfest, Winterfest, collabs) with limited cars, so the game always has something new.

### Worst 3 to avoid
1. Paid random cars (crates): removed after backlash.
2. Real-brand licensing is out of reach for us (and against our rules). We copy the *detail*, not the names.
3. The driver's hands in first person are too big (owner).

## 3. Highway Legends (our target look)
By osddxx, since Nov 2023. Place 15288629078. **457 playing, 139 M visits, 92.6% likes, 9.0 M favourites** (a huge favourite count for its size: people save it and come back). 10-player servers.

Sources:
- wiki (highway-legends.fandom.com, "up to date as of the 04.09.2026 update"): main, Vehicle types, Gamepasses, Green Ring Map, Race Records, Stage 4 Tuning, Livery guide, Limited / VIP / Hyper / Group lists;
- Roblox page and passes;
- videos:
  - "FIRST TIME PLAYING ROBLOX HIGHWAY LEGENDS" (ProjectSupreme, 49K);
  - "PHYSICS + SOUNDS" (Inspiredside);
  - "HOW TO GET MONEY, XP, FAST & CODES" (ZCSMK, 45K);
  - "Only 500 MB?! Highway Legends Is INSANE!" (13 days ago).

### What it is
"The ultimate **highway cut-up simulator**: swerve in and out of traffic" at night.
- **Cars:** 280+ wiki pages. Fictional brands close to real ones: Karen (McLaren), Furrocco (Ferrari), Purba (Porsche), Mercury EMG (Mercedes-AMG), Baguette (Bugatti), Konada (Koenigsegg), Naxon (Nissan), Donk (Dodge).
- Car types: Classic, Luxury, Sports, Super, Hyper, Track, VIP, Tuner, Group, Special, **Limited** (60+).

### First 3 minutes
- Spawn at the highway dealership and garage, take a starter car, and drive straight into dense traffic.
- **Money comes from close passes at speed (cut-ups).** It's the same loop as ours. The first video titles are all "first time playing… buying a Hellcat": the first big goal is the first fast muscle car.

### Driving feel, camera, sound
- Highway at night under **sodium lamps**, wet-looking concrete, tyre smoke, dense mixed traffic (promo art).
- First person (V), handbrake, clutch (L Ctrl), lights, blinkers, hazards, gearbox mode (M): realistic controls like MR:T.
- "PHYSICS + SOUNDS" videos show its engine sounds and physics are a selling point.
- A small download ("Only 500 MB?!" video): it runs on phones.

### Cars, tuning
- Performance stages 1-3; **Stage 4 needs a pass** (199 R$). The wiki notes stage 4 tyres add grip, but stage 3 tyres are better for cutting up.
- Extra Customisation pass 299.
- **Livery pass** 149: preset liveries plus **community liveries by code** (a Top ID and Side ID shared in Discord).
- Car classes behind passes: Tuner 249, Hyper 399, Track 349, VIP 399. "Passes get new cars without the price going up."
- Group cars for joining the group; 60+ limited cars.

### Money and progress
- Cut-ups pay. 2x Money pass 249, 2x XP pass 299.
- **Green Ring:** a teleport to a long Nürburgring-like track with its own dealer and garage. The race pays $75K ($150K with 2x).
- Race records are kept by video proof on the wiki (Highway Race 1:32, Green Ring lap 3:17).

### World
- **A highway map** (no city) plus the Green Ring track.
- No police, no houses: pure driving.

### Shop
- 10 passes: Tuner 249, Hyper 399, Track 349, VIP 399, Stage 4 Tuning 199, Extra Customisation 299, Livery 149, 2x Money 249, 2x XP 299, Car Radio 99.
- Car classes behind passes feel a bit pay-to-own, but the passes keep growing in value.

### UI
- A clean dark HUD: bold italic gold and white logo, level bar, analog rev counter. This is our current HUD style (our earlier owner choice).

### Best 3 to learn
1. **One clear, skill-based money loop:** close passes in dense night traffic. Easy to learn, hard to master.
2. **Night highway mood:** sodium lights, wet concrete, tyre smoke, light streaks. Small download, runs on phones.
3. **Community liveries by code** and a race-records culture on the wiki and Discord.

### Worst 3 to avoid
1. Whole car classes (Hyper, Track, Tuner) locked behind Robux passes.
2. Nothing to do off the highway (no city, houses or jobs): fewer reasons to come back.
3. Stage 4 tuning paywalled.
