# Research: Southwest Florida's jobs (Roblox), 18 Nov 2026

> Owner: "study roblox southwest florida's jobs and tell me what it is. and i will approve if its good"

## How it works
- **Pick a job** (a team change). It is a roleplay life sim, so jobs are the main way to earn.
- **Paycheck on a timer** (guides say about once a minute) while you are on duty. The pay depends on your **rank** in that job.
- **Rank ladder per job:** you get promoted the longer you work (the wiki says the speed "can't be reliably estimated").
  - Most jobs have 5 ranks.
  - FinTech has 10, from Intern $125 to CEO $2,500.
- **Two kinds of job:**
  - **Stay at the workplace** (Cafe "Starblocks", Dealership, Automart, Pharmacy, Club): you must be inside the building to earn and rank up, and you roleplay serving customers.
  - **Earn anywhere** (Police, Community Service Aide): police enforce traffic, stop robberies (PVP switches on at a robbery) and get a gun, radar gun, cones and a taser. Extra weapons are gamepasses.
- **Gamepass:** Double Job Earnings doubles every paycheck. Another 2x stacks with it, up to ×4.
- **Example pay per paycheck (no pass):** Cafe Barista $250 → Owner $1,750; Police Officer $220 → Captain $1,375; Dealership Sales Associate $250 → GM $1,750; Southwest Speed (kart track) up to $1,875.

## Why it works (what to copy)
- Promotions give a long goal on top of each job ("one more rank").
- Paid time at a post suits roleplay and casual players.
- The 2x Job Earnings pass is the obvious buy for anyone who works a lot.

## Weak points (what not to copy)
- Standing in a building is basically AFK pay, and the active jobs pay no more.
- Promotion speed is a mystery to players.

## Fit with City Legends
Our plan of 17 Nov already has a Job Center with active jobs (delivery, taxi, ~600 cash/min) and passive posts (~1/3 of that, capped per hour). See [[2026-11-17 Car economy and Skyfreight crate design]].
The Southwest Florida idea that would add the most is **job ranks with promotions + a paycheck**, with Yangon workplaces.
