## The hall (for building it)
- **Structure:** one huge open-span shed. Grey painted steel portal frames (I-section rafters) with diagonal bracing and purlins (0793 photo). The roof underside is light grey panels. The side walls above about 6 m have steel girts and open/louvred bands. Height is unsure; the frames look more than 10 m up (0794 photo, unsure).
- **Ceiling services:** big silver duct runs with hanging air handler boxes (FCU-13 label), round high-bay lamps (warm-white dots), and long white tube lights in the side bay (0795 3.2 s).
- **Fabric:** grey drapes hang in swags under the roof along one side (0794 photo).
- **Floor:** bare polished grey concrete with stains and saw-cut joints, shiny where lights hit it.
- **Booth kit:** black stretch-cover tables and chairs; aluminium truss towers and gantries; par-light clusters (white and purple); feather flags (yellow/green NOMNOM); white oval mirrors; garment rails; green tarps over props. Red cable covers taped across the floor.
- **Light colour:** mostly cold white (high-bays and stage pars), with purple stage wash at the right side of the hall and one green-lit corner (0792 5.2 s). Nothing orange.
- **People:** few, set-up time feel: 2 cleaners (0793), 3-4 at the Roblox booth, scattered staff. Clothes: T-shirts, shorts, flip-flops, one in a white shirt.
