## 2. The bridge, item by item

### Pylons: shape and count
- **2 pylons** (p2 0:21, 0:29, 0:43, 0:51). The deck continues past pylon 2 onto a long approach viaduct on many piers (p2 0:43, right edge).
- **Shape (p2 0:47, 0:51; p1 0:13–0:23):** a tall diamond with a long single head, like an inverted Y over a V.
  - **Above the deck:** two legs rise from both outer edges of the deck, so the roadway passes *between* them. They lean in and meet about halfway up (p1 0:21).
  - **The head:** above that meeting point it is one tall head of **two parallel shafts with a long open vertical slot between them**, joined by a short crossbeam at the bottom of the slot (p1 0:21) and closed at the top by a block with a lit window/lamp (p1 0:21, p2 0:43).
  - **Below the deck:** the two legs **converge again into one narrow footing at the water** (a V) (p2 0:47, 0:51: clearly a single base in the river).
  - **Under the deck at the pylon:** a crossbeam with a row of about 5 bright lamps (p2 0:43, 0:51).
- **Material:** pale cream/beige concrete. Close up there are formwork lines and dark stains (p2 1:31).
- **Height feel:** the pylon top is about 2.5–3 times the deck height above the water (p2 0:51; rough estimate).

### Cables
- **Two cable planes,** one on each outer edge of the deck. They rise to the pylon head, so the two planes lean in toward each other (p1 0:21, p2 1:39).
- **Semi-fan layout:** cables leave the head along much of its height and spread to the deck evenly, on both sides of each pylon (p2 0:43, 0:51).
- **Count:** about 14–18 cables per side per plane. Exact counting is impossible at this resolution (sharper frames are requested).
- **Colour:** cream, the same as the pylon. They are thick round sheathed stays (p2 1:19).
- **Deck end of each cable: a cream bell/cylinder anchor cap,** chest-high, standing on the anchor strip outside the guard rail. Many have a small yellow round reflector (p2 1:19, 1:23; p1 0:21, 0:33). There are about 2–3 m between caps (rough).

### Deck, lanes and markings
- **Surface:** pale grey/beige. It looks like light concrete or light asphalt (unsure which), never dark black (p2 1:19, p1 0:21, 0:33).
- **Right edge (our side):** a **solid yellow edge line** about 2 m inside the kerb, leaving a shoulder strip between the line and the barrier (p1 0:21, 0:33; p2 1:59). A white dashed line further left is seen once (p2 1:59, far left, unsure).
- **Number of lanes on the deck:** not provable from these frames; the camera mostly looks out the side. Off the bridge on the Dala side there are **2 lanes each way** (p1 0:59–1:11). A dividing barrier on the deck is unsure (sharper windscreen frames are requested).
- **Yangon ramp:** a solid yellow line near the left barrier and a white edge line on the right (p2 0:05).

### Barriers and railings (outer edge, from the road outward; p2 1:19, 1:31; p1 0:21)
1. A low concrete kerb/plinth, about 40–50 cm.
2. A **steel guard rail with 2 horizontal rails** on posts, silver-grey, about 1 m high.
3. **The anchor strip:** a 1.5–2 m concrete strip where the cable anchor caps and lamp poles stand. Small bright light fittings at its base light upward (p2 1:59, bottom right; p1 0:01–0:09).
4. **An outer railing:** white/grey steel with vertical bars, about 1.2 m high, with posts every few metres (p2 1:23, 1:31).

On the approaches the edges are different: Jersey-type concrete barriers (Yangon ramp), concrete parapets topped with a white metal rail, and tall frosted/clear panel screens (p2 0:31, p1 0:59–1:11).

### Lamps
- **Tall single-arm poles,** grey, standing on the anchor strip of **both** edges, with the arm reaching over the road. The heads are flat LED fittings with **cold white light** (p2 1:31, 1:39; p1 0:37–0:53).
- **Spacing:** roughly one pole per 2–3 cable anchors, about 10 poles per half span on each edge (p2 0:51; rough).
- **Pylons:** the under-deck crossbeam has a row of ~5 lamps, and there is a lamp in the slot top block.
- **No orange sodium lamps** anywhere on the bridge or the approaches.

### Signs
- **"50 km/h"** speed limit: white disc, red ring, black "50" over a small "km/h", on a red/white striped post next to the pylon leg (p2 1:29–1:31).
- **A brown/bronze plaque** on the pylon leg at deck level (p2 1:31; text unreadable).
- **Dala side gantry (p1 1:31):** 3 green direction signs (Dala / Sat Ein Tann Beach / Twante), a "5.5 m" height limit sign and a yellow diamond hump sign.
- **Toll board:** lit cream panel with Burmese text (p1 1:41–1:53; unreadable except the probable last word "တံတား").

### Lighting colours (night)
- **Pylons:** washed warm white/cream. Some leg faces carry a **lilac/pink tint** (p2 0:43, 0:47), and the second pylon is also tinted lilac (p2 0:47). A faint greenish tint is seen once (p2 0:33, unsure).
- **Cables:** lit cream/white by uplights.
- **Deck:** cold white LED, and very bright under the poles.
- **River:** dark brown water with long light reflections (p2 0:47, 0:51).
- **Sky:** black. The far bank shows only scattered small warm lights (p2 0:29–0:51).
- **Toll:** white ceiling down-lights, a warm cream lit board and green/red lane lights.

### Traffic

- At the toll: a white pickup and a white car paying, and a man in a white shirt at a booth (p1 1:41–1:51).
- No buses, no motorbikes and no pedestrians on the deck.
