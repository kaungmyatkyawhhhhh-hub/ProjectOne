# Prompt: turn Creator Store mansion 9112441127 into one of our houses

_For Claude Code on the PC, with Roblox Studio connected (MCP). Read all of this first. Owner: talk short and simple (caveman mode, see `Preferences.md`). Also read `City Legends/Prompts/Housing like Driving Empire.md`: this house becomes one of its designs._

## The model
- **Creator Store ID `9112441127`**: "mansion" by Catsinbubbles, free, from March 2022, **73 👍 / 17 👎** (about 81% liked), with no scripts listed.
- It looks safer than the dealership model, but still **clean it before use**.

## Steps
1. **Test place only.**
   - Use the Studio named **"Dealer Test"** (or a new one saved as `House Test.rbxl`), never City Legends. Call `list_roblox_studios` and pick it by name.
   - Insert model `9112441127` while editing in Studio. The owner may need to click "Get Model" on the store page first.
   - Never make the game load it by ID while it runs.
2. **Clean it:**
   - Delete every Script, LocalScript and ModuleScript.
   - Check for `require(` with numbers, hidden folders, invisible giant parts, and sounds or GUIs with links.
   - Report what you removed.
3. **Remove all the model cars** in the garage and driveway (and any vehicles anywhere in it). Leave the garage empty with **marked car bays**: these become our **Garage setup** showcase spots, where the owner's own cars appear (the same system as our other houses).
4. **Remove any logos, brand decals, posters or text** that name a brand, game or person. Replace them with plain art or nothing.
5. **Change the design so it's ours** (more than a little, so it isn't a copy):
   - **Outside:**
     - a new colour scheme (for example warm white render with stone and dark-bronze frames);
     - change the roof shape or colour;
     - add or reshape the balconies / columns;
     - a new entrance (canopy or bigger doors);
     - new windows on one side;
     - our landscaping (palms, hedges, rock and flower beds, garden lights, like the owner's design pictures in `imports/refs/houses/`);
     - a **big opening gate** with pillars and lamps (see Part 8 of the housing prompt).
   - **Inside:**
     - new floor and wall materials;
     - rearranged furniture;
     - our interactive items (doors, lights, TV, fireplace, kitchen, shower, beds, curtains);
     - make sure **every floor can be walked into** with working stairs.
   - **Garage:** fit it to its tier's car bays. If it's one of the top 5 houses: a **10-car garage plus 3 driveway spots**.
   - Show a screenshot **next to the original** so the owner can see it's clearly different.
6. **Fit it into the housing system:**
   - Give it a fictional name (for example "Bellmont Estate") and a tier and price that match its size (probably **High**). Add it to the blueprint list.
   - Make it fit a High lot (on the summit, behind the gate). Check its footprint.
   - Include: the mailbox with "[Name]'s Home", the FOR SALE sign spot, the house gate, and the inside door to the garage.
7. **Performance:**
   - Report the part count.
   - Merge hidden detail.
   - Inside detail loads only when you're near (LOD / streaming).
   - It must run well on a phone.
8. **Show the owner:**
   - screenshots outside (day + night), inside, the garage with 2–3 of our cars placed in the bays, and the side-by-side with the original.
9. **Only when the owner says OK:**
   - add it to City Legends as a house design;
   - test buying, placing, the gate, and garage setup in Play;
   - save the place, commit and push any code;
   - note it in `City Legends/City Legends.md`.

## When done, tell the owner (short)
- what was removed;
- what changed;
- its name, tier, price and car bays;
- screenshots;
- the part count.
