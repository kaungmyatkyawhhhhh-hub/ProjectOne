## What the video shows (and what to build)
### 1. The roads
- **Asphalt:** dark grey, slightly worn, with patches.
  - Lines: **white dashed centre lines**, white solid edge lines, **zebra crossings** at almost every corner, and painted arrows at junctions (0:20–1:00, 6:00–6:30).
  - Yellow zigzag / dashed yellow lines at bus stops and delivery bays.
- **Sidewalks:** **terracotta-red / pink paving** (the Monaco look) with **light granite kerbs** (0:20–1:15, 6:00). In the casino quarter, light stone paving.
- **Hills everywhere:**
  - roads climb and curve between buildings, with **tall stone retaining walls** (cream / beige blocks, sometimes with arches and doors built into them, 0:10, 2:00);
  - **hairpins with stone or concrete walls** and views of the sea (2:20–2:45);
  - roads that pass **under buildings and platforms** (7:50).
- **The tunnel** (7:55–8:15): a long **curving tunnel by the sea**:
  - **warm yellow-orange sodium lights** in rows along the ceiling;
  - concrete ceiling, **round white concrete pillars** on the sea side, with daylight showing between them;
  - **blue and white chevron signs** on the curve;
  - white lines; the walls painted yellow-cream at the bottom.
  - This is a signature place: make it a highlight (great for races and the speed trap).
- **Harbour road** (7:00–7:35):
  - runs along the yacht harbour behind a **white stone balustrade** (rows of carved stone balusters);
  - big **umbrella pines** leaning over the road;
  - restaurants with **red awnings** and terraces on the other side.
- **Coast road east** (10:40–12:15):
  - palms, flags on tall poles, sea views over low walls;
  - **cars parked along both sides** under trees;
  - hillside villas with terracotta roofs above.

### 2. Street furniture (lots of it: this is what makes it feel real)
- **Street lamps, 3 kinds:**
  - tall **modern curved single-arm lamps** in dark grey (most streets);
  - **double-arm grey lamps** on main roads;
  - **ornate black cast-iron lanterns with 3–5 globes** in the old / casino quarter (4:20–4:35).
  - All warm-white / golden at night.
- **Bollards:**
  - short dark grey / black metal posts along sidewalks;
  - red-and-white striped ones at junctions;
  - white concrete cubes at some corners.
- **Railings:**
  - dark green or black metal railings along walls and drops (12:15, very common);
  - white stone balustrades by the harbour.
- **Signs** (shapes and colours only, fictional text):
  - blue square pedestrian-crossing signs (very many);
  - red-white round speed / no-entry signs (50, 30);
  - triangle warning signs;
  - **directional sign posts with many white arrow plates**;
  - **digital car-park boards** showing free spaces (1:25);
  - blue "P" parking signs.
- **Traffic lights** on dark poles, with pedestrian lights.
- **Bus stops:** glass shelters with ad panels. **White city buses with red stripes** in traffic (fictional livery, no real logo).
- **Planters everywhere:**
  - wooden-slat or stone planter boxes with palms, agaves and flowers;
  - **bougainvillea** (pink-purple) over walls (2:00, 6:40);
  - green walls / ivy-covered concrete (10:00).
- **Scooters and motorbikes parked in long rows** along the sidewalks (4:10–4:15, 5:15, 9:15): a very Monaco detail.
- **Construction cranes** (red and white) on the skyline (1:55, 2:40, 8:20): add a few, as scenery.
- Ad pillars / poster columns, benches, bins, bike racks.

### 3. Buildings (the mix is the key)
1. **Belle Époque buildings** (old Monaco, casino quarter, around 3:00–5:45):
   - 4–7 storeys in **cream, ochre, honey-yellow, peach, salmon and pink**;
   - carved stone balconies, **black wrought-iron railings**, arched windows, carved window frames;
   - **shutters** (grey-green, white or brown), mansard roofs with dormers;
   - ground floors with shops: awnings, big windows, gold lettering (fictional names).
2. **1970s–80s apartment blocks** (the most common, 0:20–2:10, 6:20–6:50, 8:20–9:30):
   - 10–20 storeys, **tan / ochre / beige concrete** with **long horizontal balconies** stacked all the way up;
   - often **curved fronts**, awnings and plants on the balconies;
   - some with stepped / terraced shapes going up the hill.
3. **Modern glass towers:** a few tall ones (two very tall slim twin-like towers on the skyline, 0:30–0:35, 9:30–9:45), white or glass with patterned fronts, plus curved glass-and-wood modern apartments (5:30–5:35, 7:20–7:45).
4. **Hillside villas** (east coast and the hills, 11:00–12:50): terracotta roofs, pastel walls, gardens, stone walls, lots of trees.
5. **Grand hotel / casino quarter** (4:20–5:00):
   - big cream classical buildings with columns and balustrades;
   - formal gardens, palms and round trimmed trees;
   - luxury shops with dark frames and awnings;
   - supercars parked out front.
- Pack them **tightly**: the city is dense, buildings touch, and the hill behind is covered with buildings stepping up.

### 4. Nature and the backdrop
- **Umbrella / stone pines** (big flat-topped pines): an icon of the Riviera. Many along the harbour and main roads (6:55–7:30, 9:20).
- **Palms** (tall fan and date palms), **cypresses**, oleander and bougainvillea bushes, trimmed hedges.
- **The mountain behind:** a big rocky limestone mountain with **grey cliffs** and green scrub (0:00, 2:30, 6:00–6:35, 12:40–12:45). It's always visible behind the city: build it as a big terrain backdrop above the top road.
- **The sea:** bright blue, with yachts and sailboats in the harbour (masts visible over the walls).

### 5. Light and colours (daytime)
- A clear **deep blue sky**, strong sun, crisp shadows, warm sunlight on the ochre and cream walls.
- Colours: cream, ochre, peach, salmon and pink walls; terracotta roofs; dark green railings and shutters; red-pink sidewalks; dark asphalt; bright green trees; blue sea.
- No haze. A little heat shimmer is OK.

### 6. Traffic and people (life)
- **Traffic mix:** lots of **white delivery vans**, black and white SUVs (luxury SUVs), small city cars, **scooters weaving**, buses, the odd supercar, police scooters. A traffic officer at one junction (0:15).
- **People:** walkers on every sidewalk, cyclists on the coast road, people sitting at café terraces.
- Use our own fictional cars and liveries. No real logos.
