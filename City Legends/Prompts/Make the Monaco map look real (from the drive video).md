### 3. Buildings (the mix is the key)
1. **Belle Époque buildings** (old Monaco, casino quarter, around 3:00–5:45):

2. **1970s–80s apartment blocks** (the most common, 0:20–2:10, 6:20–6:50, 8:20–9:30):

3. **Modern glass towers:** a few tall ones (two very tall slim twin-like towers on the skyline, 0:30–0:35, 9:30–9:45), white or glass with patterned fronts, plus curved glass-and-wood modern apartments (5:30–5:35, 7:20–7:45).
