## 2. Building and rooms: layout, sizes, materials

### 2a. Outside (IMG_0927 00:00 - 00:10)
- **Building type:** old reinforced-concrete walk-up apartment block, rendered and painted, typical Yangon 1960s-80s "tike" style. Floors seen: ground + at least 3 above (0927 00:00). Floor-to-floor height estimate about 3.2 - 3.5 m (11.5 - 12.5 studs).
- **Walls:** cream/off-white paint, RGB about (205, 200, 170), with heavy black-green mould streaks, RGB about (70, 75, 60) and grey patches (150, 150, 140). Mould is worst at the bottom 1.5 m and under every ledge. Horizontal moulded bands (projecting about 5-10 cm) at each floor line.
- **Ground floor shop/garage:** brown collapsible accordion shutter or roll shutter, RGB (90, 55, 45); opening about 3 m wide x 2.5 m high.
- **Red corrugated awning:** rusty red, RGB (150, 70, 60), sloped, about 0.8 m deep.
- **Blue corrugated awning** over 1st floor balcony: RGB (60, 90, 150).
- **Balcony grilles:** white painted steel square grid (1st floor), red-brown caged balcony (2nd floor) with pot plants.
- **Carport:** light grey corrugated sheet roof, RGB (200, 200, 195) underside; steel truss painted red-brown RGB (130, 55, 40) (front) / dark brown (in 00:06). Truss depth about 0.5 m, Z-pattern diagonals. Height to underside about 2.6 - 3 m.
- **Stair entrance:** round-top arch, about 1.2 m wide x 2.4 m high with a raised arch trim. Old steel grid gate (square bars, about 15 cm grid), dark grey/brown RGB (70, 60, 55). Semicircular fanlight grille with a star pattern and a hanging bare bulb in a red lamp holder (00:14).
- **Pipes:** light blue PVC drainpipe and horizontal pipe, RGB (150, 185, 205), about 10 cm diameter.
- **Ground:** dusty light grey concrete RGB (175, 170, 160), leaf litter, broken 6-hole cement blocks RGB (165, 160, 150).
- **Tree:** tropical almond (Terminalia catappa type) with big round leaves, over the building.
- **Cars:** black Honda MPV/wagon with 5-twin-spoke alloys (model unsure), very dusty; another dark car at 00:14; a white car seen from the stair window at 01:00.
- **Fence:** blue painted steel fence/gate outside (01:00).

### 2b. Stairwell (IMG_0927 00:10 - 01:24)
- **Plan:** dog-leg (two flights per floor, 180-degree turn at a half landing). Each half landing has a window on the outer wall. At each full floor: flat front doors with collapsible gates. Flights seen: at least 4-5 flights (00:18, 00:24-00:26, 00:34, 00:40, 00:46, 00:52, 01:04), so the camera climbs about 2 to 3 floors. Exact count unsure.
- **Steps:** cast concrete, no tile. Riser about 17-18 cm (0.6 studs), tread about 25-28 cm (0.9-1.0 studs), stair width about 1.0-1.1 m (3.6-3.9 studs). Steps per flight: about 8-10 (only 4-6 visible at a time, so unsure). Colour grey RGB (125, 125, 120) with dark worn centres (80, 80, 75), white-paint flecks, brown rust/water streaks on risers, a lighter cream line painted on some nosings.
- **Side of the flight:** a sloping concrete parapet/stringer (about 15 cm thick, cream with black dirt) with the steel rail fixed on top.
- **Railing:** black painted mild steel, RGB (30, 30, 30), heavily worn/rusty. Handrail flat/round bar about 4 cm. Rail height about 0.9 m above the nosing. Infill: vertical posts about 1 m apart with diagonal flat bars making a zig-zag/triangle pattern. At the top of a flight the rail turns in a squared "U" around the stairwell gap (01:04). In 00:52 the rail looks dark blue (could be light; unsure).
- **Walls:** cream to pale yellow paint RGB (220, 210, 165) (yellower upstairs, 01:02 looks (215, 210, 140)), dark dirt band at floor level, cracks, peeling patches, damp black stains. Moulded horizontal band at sill height on landings. Underside of flights and ceiling: white RGB (235, 235, 230).

- **Electrical:** black cables run diagonally across walls and up the stairwell; a grey single-phase meter in a frame (00:12); meter board with conduit in ground lobby (00:10); fluorescent batten lamp on a wall (00:24); bare bulb in red holder in the arch fanlight (00:14); white conduit pipe in a corner (01:02); a hole where an old fitting was (00:12).

- **Signs:** red plates with white Burmese text on walls (00:38, 01:04).
