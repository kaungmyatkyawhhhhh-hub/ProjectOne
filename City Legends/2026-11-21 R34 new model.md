The owner imported it into Blender. The `FINAL_MODEL_R3.fbx` / `GTRR34` hierarchy (CSR2 rip) was not exported or changed. The first model tried (Pooya_dh) had no interior: see the end of this note.

**Deviation from the brief:** the model's own interior and steering wheel were kept and fixed (shapes, colours, materials) instead of modelling new ones. The steering wheel is the model's own (owner rule 2026-11-13: the `STEER_MODEL` line is gone).

## Steps
1. Prep in Blender (working file `Games/CityLegends/build/r34/`): badges and emblems taken off, plain covers; no fictional emblems.
2. `auto_car.py` with wheelbase 2.665 m.
3. `tools/logo_paint/nisun_r34_fix.py` (new): one body object per material (auto_car left the cabin as one multi-material part, and finish_car names a part by its first material), the FR tyre moved from the body into the FR wheel group, stray caliper pieces joined / removed.
4. `finish_car.py` with `tools/logo_paint/nisun_r34.finish.json` (roles, `dissolve` Paint / Carbon 2.0, CabinDoor 3.0, `thin` Grille 0.55): 352k -> 276k triangles, 56 parts.
5. Lamps: `tools/lamps/lampfx_glb.py` + `tools/lamps/jobs/nisun_r34.json` (head core + glass, front and side indicators, tail, rear indicator, reverse).
6. Renders: `build/r34/render_fin/` (front 3/4, rear 3/4, side, driver, cluster, wheel) and `build/r34/v8/` (cabin views, MFD).
7. `pack_cars.py` -> Open Cloud Model (group 33151111): **pack 133193318401011** -> `InsertService:LoadAsset` -> `CarApply` with `build/r34/nisun_r34.apply.json` (made by `tools/logo_paint/nisun_r34_mkapply.py`) -> LampFx to Neon -> `CarModels`.
8. Code: `CarSkin` (cluster style `LiveR34`, `CLUSTER_RECT`, new `MFD_RECT` + the `DashMfd` part, old `DIAL_NEEDLES` gone, `LOW_ARMS`, STEER_MODEL line gone), `CarFinish`, `CarLamps` (XENON, no DRL), `Windscreens`, `BrandMarks` / `DialLooks` / `TailLights` old-model blocks removed, `Cars` (back on sale; `TrafficMix` rare entry kept at trim SPORT, NoTrims so `oldTrim` doesn't touch it), `Config.Credits` now comes from `Credits.lua` (Config is at 197k of the 200k limit).

## Cluster and MFD
- **Cluster** (`tools/lambo/cluster_imports.py` `r34`, live through `cluster_live.py`): the model's hood holds a plain dark face, so it was drawn from the real car's layout: speedometer 0-180 km/h with odometer and trip, the big tach 0-10k (red from 8k) with the shift lamp at 7,500, water temperature + fuel on the right dial. Start-up sweep (needles to full and back) and the bulb check (CHG, OIL, CHECK, BRAKE, BELT) for the first 2.6 s. Idle 0, redline 8 for this car only (the rev needle reads what the HUD reads). No gear read-out: the real car has none (the HUD keeps it). Images: **122718681499999 / 107626705741503 / 113119082543170**. Preview: `build/r34/cluster/r34_drive.jpg`.
- **The left / centre / right order of the dials was not confirmed by a photo** (drawn from memory of the real car); check against a real BNR34 cluster photo when one is at hand.
- **MFD** (`src/client/R34Mfd.lua`, new; test `tools/r34mfd_test.luau`): SurfaceGui on the `DashMfd` part: BOOST (lag, peak held 4 s), OIL and WATER temperature, a row with throttle %, intake and exhaust temperature and volts. Redrawn 20 times a second, only when the camera is within 14 studs; lit at night (LightInfluence 0), dark with the engine off. The water temperature also moves the cluster's TEMP needle (`DashGauges.waterShare`). The game has no turbo or oil model, so the numbers are made from revs, throttle, speed and time since start.

## Traps (also in the game CLAUDE.md)
- auto_car leaves a multi-material cabin as ONE part and finish_car names a part by its first material: split by material first (nisun_r34_fix.py).
- auto_car can leave one tyre in the body: check `Wheels.FR` etc. in Studio for a tyre. The cloud pack 133193318401011 was made before the fix that parents the FR tyre; the Studio copy was fixed by hand (FR_Tyre into Wheels.FR).
- `Config.Credits = require(script.Parent.Credits)` broke the Lune stubs: use the `pcall(WaitForChild)` form.
- Open Cloud calls fail while Studio is in Play (they need the Edit DataModel).
- Studio's CarSkin had edits that are in no git commit: patched with targeted replacements (multi_edit), not overwritten.
