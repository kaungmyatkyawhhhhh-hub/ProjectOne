## Changes for the game (most important)
1. **Signal heads:**
   - At the 0930 and 0929 city junctions each head is a 3-cell horizontal unit: lamp, 2-digit countdown, lamp. The digits take the colour of the lit lamp.
   - At the 0931 junction the head has **4 sections**, red at the outer right end. It has solar panels with a white box on the pole and no lit countdown.
   - There is no CCTV at 0931.
2. **Left turns get their own phase.** At 0930 straight green lasts about 55 s while the left arrow stays red the whole time (red 59 at 93 s, green 52 at 97 s, arrow 47 / straight 44 at 105 s). At 0929 the two heads count down together, the left arrow about 17 s behind (see the table under 0929).
3. **Plates have 3 lines:** a small region line on top, the big number, and a small dealer or extra line below. The region line reads `YGN-<number>`:
   - YGN-9 HH-4164 (red, EV City Taxi)
   - YGN-42 5D-5303 (Wish)
   - YGN-22 4C-897 (Crown)
   - also 4T-3975, 9J-5381, 4E-4767, FF-6071

   Compare with [[2026-11-19 Plates - Myanmar number plates (built)]].
4. **Yellow zebra crossing** with a white stop line at 0929 107 s. A yellow-black striped nose panel sits under a blue keep-left sign.
5. **Camera gantries:** 4 cameras on one arm across the road before the Waizayandar junction (0929 51 s), 3 cameras at 0930 75 s, a 2-camera arm at 0929 155 s, and a dome camera at 0929 61 s.
6. **Lane-arrow signs:** the last arrow is straight+right, not plain right (0929 55 s and 105 s). The 0931 junction has **4 approach lanes** (↑ ↑ ↑ ↱), not 2.
7. **Old flats have board-panel walls:** the 0928 bedroom walls are painted board panels with screwed joints and a mid-height joint, not plaster (31, 33, 55-57 and 75 s). There is a mint drop beam about 20-25 cm deep at the ceiling.
8. **Cars pack tight:** a car sits about 30 cm from our side at the 0930 junction (5 s). Drivers ride with an arm out of the window. People ride in the open backs of lorries and on truck loads (0930 65 s; 0929 53 s and 155 s).
