## What the game must match (ranked)
1. **Overhead wires**: thick black bundles sagging between concrete/steel poles, junction boxes on poles (0930, 0933, 0934). Every city frame has them. Check the map has wire bundles, not single clean lines.
2. **Trees**: toddy palm rows on avenues + big dark rain trees over the road (0930, 0932, 0937). Trees are the main colour by day.
3. **Traffic mix**: mostly white Japanese cars (Probox, Wish, Corolla, Vios), black EV City taxis with red plates, blue/yellow CNG taxis, yellow and purple YBS buses, Prado/Range Rover. Plates: black, red for taxis/buses (fits [[2026-11-19 Plates - Myanmar number plates (built)]]).
4. **Signals**: signal heads + **blue lane-arrow panels** on one long mast arm over the road (0931, 0932, 0933).
5. **Kerbs**: red-white on medians and islands (game already matches), black-white striped poles and bollards.
6. **Day sky**: light hazy blue with flat-bottom puffy clouds, strong sun, washed-out far distance.
7. **Buildings**: old blocks = barred balconies, laundry, AC boxes, stained walls; new = white condos with curved or slatted balconies; construction sites behind blue sheet hoarding.
8. **Roadside**: tall billboards on one pole, guard booths, tea shop signs, blue metal awnings.
9. **Out of town (if the map grows)**: toll plaza with tube canopy and chevron barriers (0935), km stones + yellow edge line on old highway (0937).
10. **Interiors (if we build flats)**: concrete stairwell + thin steel rail, scissor gate over a wood door, red varnished wood floors (0927, 0928). See [[2026-11-16 Research - Yangon interiors catalogue]].
