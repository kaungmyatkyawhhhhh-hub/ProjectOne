# GGFest showcase (21-22 Nov 2026): research and plan

> Owner (17 Nov 2026): "we need to put some GGFest stuff in the game, research what GGFest is. and then on saturday and sunday i will showcase my game at ymc center GGFest"

## What GGFest is
- **GG Gaming Fest:** a Myanmar gaming and esports festival.
- **When:** **21-22 November 2026** (Saturday and Sunday), 10 AM to 8 PM each day.
- **Where:** the **Yangon Convention Centre (YCC)**, 37 Kabar Aye Pagoda Road, inside the **Inya Lake Hotel** compound, Mayangone. This is the owner's "YMC center".
- **Tickets:** MMK 45,000 for one day, 70,000 for both.
- **Format:** 2 days live, plus 8 weeks of online content before the event (GG TV).
- **10+ zones:**
  - GG Arcade (arcade and VR)
  - GG Quest (points challenges)
  - GG Cosplay
  - GG TV
  - GG Contest (cosplay photos, webtoons)
  - GG Experience
  - GG Esports
  - GG Awards (20 Myanmar esports talents and streamers, fan vote)
  - food and art
- **Esports:** a Mobile Legends tournament, men's and women's, with a K40,000,000 prize pool. Yangon Galacticos is invited.
- **Branding:** a "GG" wordmark and a terminal/HUD-style tech look. No mascot.

## Why it fits City Legends
YCC sits on **Kabar Aye Pagoda Road, inside the Inya Lake Hotel compound**, which is in our 1:1 district **P2**. That lot was deliberately left empty for its landmark build. Players can drive to the real festival venue in the game.

## Plan
1. **YCC + GGFest venue in the 1:1 map:** the real Yangon Convention Centre, the Inya Lake Hotel and the compound, at real size. Inside it, during the event, there's a festival zone:
   - banners
   - a stage
   - "zones" echoing the real ones (arcade, quest, cosplay photo spot)
   - a countdown screen
   - text only: "GG Gaming Fest 21-22 Nov, Yangon Convention Centre"
   - no GG logo artwork unless the organisers allow it
2. **Event activities (time-gated 21-22 Nov):**
   - a **GG Quest** checklist of drive challenges for points
   - a **GGFest limited car wrap / number plate** for players who join during the event
   - a photo spot
3. **Booth kit:**
   - a stable showcase build
   - a booth tester save with every car unlocked
   - a QR code to the game page
   - a short trailer loop (pro-video-editing skill)
4. **Stability first:** the booth build must not crash. **Checkpoint Thursday evening:** if the 1:1 roads are fixed and QA'd, show the 1:1 map. If not, show the stable live map, with the GGFest zone placed there too.
