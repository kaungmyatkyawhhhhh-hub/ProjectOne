# City Legends

A Roblox night-city driving game. The code is in this vault, folder `Games/CityLegends`; its `CLAUDE.md` has the full technical notes.

Lessons from the top 10 driving games (2026-10-30): [[Research - Driving games]] (read before planning features).
- Owner picked (2026-10-30) improvements 1, 2, 4-10 from the research list (not 3, performance tuning, yet): Legends Path milestones, 7-day streak with a car on day 7 (replaces the flat daily gift), race lobbies and duels, 3 daily tasks, live map markers, meet spot + photo mode, radio stations, brand levels, 5-minute test drive. **All 9 built (2026-10-30):** GOALS button (Path, Daily, Streak), race lobbies at the race rings + duels (U next to a driver), live bank/casino/race markers on the minimap, LEGENDS MEET car park behind Legends Motors ($300/min per other car parked there, up to 5) + photo mode (O or Settings), 4 radio stations (Settings > Radio), brand levels (garage shows BRAND LV; cheaper Customs, trims sooner), TEST DRIVE button at Legends Motors (5 min, quarter pay). Duel/pot still need a 2-player test.

## Features so far
- **City:** a big night city with traffic that stops at red lights, and a tunnel intro cutscene ("CITY LEGENDS").
- **Driving and money:** you earn money by driving and by cutting up traffic, with combos. You buy cars in the garage.
- **Cars:** 55 cars in 3 tiers (Starter, Mid, High), remade 2026-10-10 from the owner's list of real cars. Names are close look-alikes (e.g. "BNW M3 F80", "Toyoka Land Cruzer 200", "Lambrini Urus"). Free starter: Suzaki Swiff (`suzaki_swift`).
- **HUD:** a Highway Legends-style HUD with level/XP bar, rev counter, cash, side buttons and a daily gift.
- **Weather:** a rain cycle with wet roads, plus blue exhaust flames.
- **Sounds and lighting:** a full soundscape and night lighting.

## How cars are picked (in order)
1. A model in `ReplicatedStorage/CarModels/<car id>` (any Creator Store model works; add a `Reverse` attribute if it faces backwards).
2. Built-in 3D meshes. Needs Game Settings → Security → "Allow Mesh / Image APIs".
3. The old block-built car.

## Driving feel (2026-10-11, to match Highway Legends)
- Body leans outward in turns, nose dips on braking, squats on acceleration.
- Steering a little slower than before.
- Less grip, closer to real life; slightly slippery on hard launches.
- Outside camera shakes a little; first-person view stays steady (only crashes jolt it).
- **Camera like the reference video (2026-10-29, `Prompts/Camera and car movement like the video.md`):**
  - Cameras (V cycles): Immersive -> **Low** (new: wheel-top height, off to the side, strong blur) -> Chase -> Cockpit.
  - Outside cameras: much calmer FOV (+11 at top speed, was +38); speed shows through lag (the car pulls away flooring it, comes closer braking), falling back at speed, more motion blur. In corners the car slides out in the frame and the camera rolls up to about 4.5 degrees, back to centre in half a second.
  - Cockpit: the FOV never changes. The driver's head is on a spring: slides out and tilts the horizon (up to 6 degrees) in corners, dips forward and down braking, pushed back pulling away, looks into turns. Breathing when slow or parked.
  - High-speed sway and shake from 100 mph (every camera), growing to 190.
  - Steering wheel turns up to 300 degrees each way at parking speed, less at speed; small corrections on the straight; hands re-grip (cross over) past 100 degrees.
  - **Settings > Camera FX:** Shake (Off / Low / Normal), Head move (Off / Low / Normal), Motion blur (On / Off), FOV 60 to 90 (outside cameras). Saved with the profile. Reduced Motion players start with shake and head movement off.
  - All the numbers: `Config.Camera` (Immersive, Low, Drama, Head, Shake) and `Config.DrivingFeel` (BodyRoll, BodyPitch, WheelLockSlow, LockFadeMph, HandReach, WheelWobble).
  - Body lean still tilts the whole car (physics box too), not only the visual model.
