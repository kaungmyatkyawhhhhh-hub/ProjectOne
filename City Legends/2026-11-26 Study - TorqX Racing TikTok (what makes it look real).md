## What makes it look real (frame evidence)
1. **Low golden-hour sun straight ahead** (0-5, 17-19): bloom, sun rays, haze over the far city, everything rim-lit. The low sun hides cheap far detail and makes every edge glow. Later it's plain blue-sky day, and the city looks flatter there (9-16).
2. **Long, soft, correct shadows**: the car's shadow runs at the camera, poles and palms cast long shadows across the road.
3. **The road surface**: asphalt with a real bumpy normal map and specular (it sparkles in the sun at 2.9 and 4.7), worn double yellow lines with gaps (19.6), zebra stripes with texture (27.6), manholes, painted arrows. The road is the biggest thing on screen and it's never a flat colour.
4. **Real car models**: recognisable shapes (R8, 488 Pista), real details: diffuser fins, quad exhausts, lit LED tail bars, readable number plates, brake discs, rim spokes. Paint has clear-coat reflections.
5. **Effects that stay**: tyre marks stay on the road for 5+ s, the red tail-light glow lights the road behind, flames light the tarmac orange, white smoke in drifts.
6. **A busy believable street**: bus shelters with glowing ads, traffic lights with arrow signs, chain-link fence, parking garage, water tower, Ferris wheel and an overpass far away, hydrants, mailboxes, hedges, pedestrians. Traffic is different real models (WRX, Raptor, BMW, Mercedes).
7. **Camera**: low and close behind the car, slight lag and swing in turns, no HUD, so it reads like a film.
8. **Sound (measured, not heard)**: mostly music. Rising engine-rev lines under it at 0-5 s and 24-28 s. 64% of the energy is under 150 Hz (bass and exhaust). The beat drop at ~5.5 s lands on the first burnout.

## Why it gets watched (the "greatest car game" feeling)
- The hook question + 💀🔥 invites comments ("no it isn't" / "yes"); 65 comments on day one.
- No dead second: something happens every 1-2 s (flames, slide, overtake, crossing).
- Two dream cars (Audi R8, Ferrari) any kid knows.
- One funny moment (the ragdoll), then straight back to driving.
- Slowed phonk-style music keeps it moody and "cinematic".

## Weak spots seen
- Gameplay only fills a 16:9 strip (half the phone screen is black).
- No HUD, so you can't tell what the game is (jobs? races?): it sells looks only.
- Midday shots (9-16) look flatter and emptier: few people, plain walls at street level (12-14).
- Tyre marks are hard-edged black strips, a bit too thick (31-32).
- Real brand logos on cars (Audi rings, Ferrari horse): Roblox IP risk; our rule is fictional car brands.

## For City Legends (what to copy)
- **Golden hour as the default look** for menus, trailers and the first spawn (we already open on the sunset, DayCycle `holdMenu`): add sun haze/bloom pointing down long roads.
- **Road detail**: worn paint, normal map + specular on the asphalt (our road meshes), manholes and arrows.
- **Lasting tyre marks + red tail-light glow on the road + backfire flames that light the ground**: check what Driving/DamageFx already does.
- **Ragdoll pedestrians** already exist (PedHits, Mild band): good for a clip beat.
- **Trailer format**: full-screen 9:16 gameplay, a question hook line, a beat drop on the first burnout, a cut at ~60% to a second dream car, a ragdoll gag near the end.
