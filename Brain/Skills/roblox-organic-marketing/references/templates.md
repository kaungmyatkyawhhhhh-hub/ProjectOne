# Templates: organic marketing

## Readiness (all must be yes before spending on creators)
- [ ] First session passes the impatient-kid sim (or D1 ≥ ~10%, 5-min retention ≥ ~45%)
- [ ] Icon readable at 150 px; thumbnail passes the squint test (20x smaller, 1 second)
- [ ] Nothing on the game page or first screen looks AI-made (quality-nerd kid sim)
- [ ] Description line 1 says what you do; no [ALPHA]-style tag
- [ ] The 3-second best moment is named: ______
- [ ] An in-game Codes page exists, so each creator can get their own code
- [ ] Phone, tablet and PC tested (Launch gate)

## Clip formats
| Format | Length | Caption pattern | Use for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Best moment, raw | 8–15 s | "this Roblox game lets you ‹verb›…" | launch, every week |
| Claim + proof | ~14 s | one bold claim + 2 proof overlays (CCU, likes, a clip) | milestones |
| Work in progress | 10–30 s | honest: "not proud of the end yet, what should I add?" | before release, dev log |
| Before → after | 6–12 s | "‹old› vs ‹new›" | every update |

Every clip:
- Hook in second 1.
- The game name in the caption plus a pinned comment ("search ‹name› on Roblox").
- Loop ending.
- Music from TikTok's library.

## Creator shortlist
| Handle | Followers | Median views (p25–p75) | Posts read | Contact | Genre fit | Audience looks | Asked price | Cost / 1K views |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Cost per 1K views = price ÷ (median views ÷ 1000).

Compare with ads: about $0.007 per play. At a 1% view-to-play rate, 1K views is about 10 plays, so a post beats ads if it costs under about $0.07 per 1K views. At 2%, under about $0.14. Those bars are low, so many paid posts won't beat ads. Say that plainly and let the owner decide.

## Outreach message (draft; the OWNER sends it from their own account or email)
> Hi ‹name›! I make the Roblox game **‹game›** (‹one line: what you do›). I like your game-rec videos, especially ‹a real video of theirs›.
> Would you be open to a paid post about it? Could you send a screenshot of your last-28-days analytics (average views, audience age and country) and your price for one video?
> We'd ask you to use TikTok's paid-partnership label. Thanks!

## Creator brief (after the owner agrees a price)
- **Game:** ‹exact Roblox name›, plus a link for their own use.
- **Show:** ‹the 3-second best moment›, ideally in the first 2 seconds.
- **Say:** ‹one sentence›. Use code **‹CODE›** in the game for ‹small reward›.
- **Attached:** ‹1–2 clips or raw captures›.
- **Required:** the paid-partnership label on; the game name in the caption.
- **Please don't:** promise Robux, say "free Robux", or use a fake "limited time" claim.
