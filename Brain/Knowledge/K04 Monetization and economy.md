# K04 Monetization and economy
Back to [[00 BRAIN]] · Sources: [[Playbook notes - Monetization]], [[Playbook notes - Passes and products catalogue]], [[2026-10-29 GYM ARC Prices - every coin and Robux item]], [[2026-11-09 Galaxy Incremental - Growth plan to 1k CCU (marketing, economy, retention, LiveOps)]]

## Owner's rules
- **Nothing over 449 R$** for kids (owner, 9 Nov). First pass under 400.
- **No paid random items** (no Robux crates; Robux → currency → random rolls counts too). VIP gives no Essence for that reason.
- No fake timers, no "was" prices, no countdown pressure, never weaken what players own, no paid power in competitions.
- GYM ARC stance: paying players should feel boosts clearly pay off.

## What sells
- The "auto" pass is the hero (GYM ARC: Auto Rep; Galaxy: Auto-Upgrade / Auto Roll). Then ×2 currency, VIP (+25%, trims, tags), slots (+1 Core Slot), a 29-49 R$ Starter Pack after the first big moment, time skips (15/39/99) and small impulse products (Offline ×2 25 R$).
- Offer the right thing at the right moment (small card, never in the tutorial, max once per X minutes). Products buried in tabs sell nothing.
- Fallback prices in game must match Roblox's real price (read GetProductInfo).
- Rewards and coin packs scale with the player's stage ("minutes of income"); cap total multipliers.
- Premium: +10% for Premium players (Premium Payouts pay for their playtime).

## Measuring prices
- Per item: Seen → Prompted → Bought (custom events). Prompt→buy < 3% with many views = overpriced or wrong moment (move the moment first). > 15% and repeat buyers = underpriced (raise on new players only, A/B by UserId). Judge by revenue per 1,000 players, 7-day tests.
- A/B anything risky on half the new players (e.g. GYM ARC Members-Gym offer only after the 2nd VIP "no").

## Real numbers
- GYM ARC: ~50K R$ per 2 weeks (~16.5 lakh MMK), 3,490 R$/day on 9 Nov; VIP Gym raised VIP sales but cut group joins from ~1,000/day to ~150/day.
- Galaxy (alpha): 20 R$/day, 0.31% payers. Goal: 85K R$/day at 1K CCU ≈ 1.5 R$ per DAU.
- 1 R$ spent ≈ 0.7 × $0.0038 = $0.00266 to us.
