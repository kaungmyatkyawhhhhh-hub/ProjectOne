# K01 Roblox platform and discovery (what Roblox rewards, 2026)
Back to [[00 BRAIN]] · Sources: [[Roblox Playbook - Rules for every game]], [[Playbook notes - Whole-game research (Oct 2026)]], [[Playbook notes - Discovery and marketing]]

## How Home recommends games (since 15 Jun 2026)
- Judged over **28 days** in 3 windows: day 1, days 2-7, days 8-28. Only players who came from Home count.
- Signals: play-through rate, **bounces under 60 s and 61-180 s** (count against you), playtime per player (capped 60 min/day), days played, **co-play days**, spend days, Robux spent. Since Aug 2026 Home favours games strong in BOTH retention and money.
- A game needs **at least one custom thumbnail** to be recommended (since 19 Oct 2026). Clickbait hurts (clicks turn into bounces).
- Video previews play on Recommended For You. "Standout Games" is curated (nomination form).
- Proof from Galaxy (9 Nov): with 3.5-min playtime, recommendations came ONLY from ads. The algorithm tests a game, sees short sessions and stops showing it; it keeps re-testing after the game improves (moving window).

## Reaching kids (Roblox Kids & Select)
- Unrated games can't be played: fill the Maturity & Compliance questionnaire, aim Minimal/Mild.
- To reach under-16s you need BOTH: (owner ID-verified + 2-Step + Plus 2 months, OR a refundable 1,000 R$ fee per game) AND **250 engaged plays from age-checked 16+ players within 60 days**. The 50K R$ expedited fee only skips the 250 (owner: "not even worth it"). Track: Creator Hub > Audience Reach.
- Notifications, social links and rewarded ads only reach 13+/16+. For kids the levers are in-game (offline progress, streaks, quests, friend boost, invites, a weekly event).

## Rules that get games hidden or removed
- Exact % odds behind a button before any paid random item (also when bought with Robux-bought currency) + PolicyService alternative. Our rule: no paid random items at all.
- No fake sales/timers, no links/QR codes in game, filter all player text, no real brands/people/copyrighted music, don't look like another big game (copycat flag).
- Duplicate games get reduced exposure ("non-unique"): **never re-upload a game to reset likes** (you also lose saves, badges, favorites, visits).
- Rewarding likes/favorites is not allowed; asking without a reward is fine.

## Money facts
- You keep 70% of passes/products. DevEx $0.0038/R$ (min 30,000). Creator Rewards: 5 R$ when an Active Spender plays 10+ min in a day; Audience Expansion 35% of a new player's first $100 via link/search.
- Subscriptions min 49 R$/month. Managed Pricing: read prices with GetProductInfo, never hard-code.
- Badges: first 5 per experience per 24 h (GMT) free, then 100 R$ each.
