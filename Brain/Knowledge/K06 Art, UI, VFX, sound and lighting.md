# K06 Art, UI, VFX, sound and lighting
Back to [[00 BRAIN]] · Lessons: [[Lessons - Galaxy to +1 SHRINK (feel - sound, VFX, screen FX, lighting)]] · Sources: [[Playbook notes - UI deep dive]], [[Playbook notes - Visual effects]], [[Playbook notes - Sound and music]], [[Playbook notes - Lighting, cameras and cutscenes]], [[Playbook notes - Top games art study (Oct 2026)]], [[Map placement rules (every game)]], [[Prompt for a friend's Claude - ChatGPT art pipeline for Roblox]]

## Art pipeline (owner: "ur the artist", ChatGPT draws, Claude processes)
- One-try prompts: exact canvas, exact grid, "NO text", background rule (UI on flat magenta #FF00FF; VFX/icons on black; textures seamless), the game's STYLE BLOCK in every prompt.
- Magenta key by **flood fill from the border** (distance < 70 + 3 px soft band + un-mix), never key every magenta pixel (ate the purple gift).
- VFX: make `_add` (only with LightEmission 1) and `_alpha` (noise floor) versions; check on light AND dark backgrounds (black boxes happened twice).
- Seamless textures: ChatGPT can't tile; quilt and check 2×2. Real-ESRGAN deletes faint stars: art only. rembg isnet-general-use is OK (Apache); RMBG is non-commercial.
- Upload to the GROUP that owns the game, with the groupId explicit (a stray upload went to the wrong group). Never realistic body/skin images (GYM ARC got a 1-day ban).
- Badges: circle crop, keep the subject inside the middle 80%.

## UI
- Designed art only, never placeholders; one UI kit per game (copy the closest existing screen); colour = meaning (green buy, blue normal, yellow claim, red close, grey locked).
- Phones first: ScreenInsets CoreUISafeInsets; layouts with UIListLayout/AutomaticSize; windows sized from the safe viewport with a UIScale; hide touch controls while a window is open; text ≥ 14 px; nothing centre-screen for people who didn't take part; world labels sized in studs with MaxDistance.
- Every visible layer gets the same rounded corner logic (inner radius = outer - inset).
- One WindowManager: close only via X / Esc / backdrop behind the window; windows sink input.

## Feel
- Every action: a sound + a visual + a number; bigger rewards = bigger effects. ~300 particles max on screen, 100/s per emitter on mobile, ReducedMotion respected, max 3 flashes/s.
- Tier budget for auras (low tiers 1-2 layers); the face is never covered.

## Sound
- Main music upbeat (115-140 BPM, bright, licensed Creator Store partners), a playlist of 3-5 with crossfades; dark/slow only for special moments; music bus ~0.4 under SFX, ducks under stingers; pitch-vary repeats ±4-6%.

## Lighting
- The play space is always bright whatever the theme; mood goes in the sky. Ambient/OutdoorAmbient ≥ ~110. Check from spawn on a phone at minimum graphics.

## World
- Boards: the framed art IS the board (no double frames); nothing in front of a board face; rings/rails use their own height band; guide arrows adorned to the target, never hard-coded positions.
- Interaction: tycoon-style step-on floor pads for buy buttons (click is the backup); fix the thing the owner names, don't build extras.

## Never looks like AI slop (owner 9 Nov, every game)
Owner: an AI-slop look gets dislikes because players think the game was "made in 1 prompt" with no hard work.
Fact (Roblox Q3 2026, PC Gamer): over 4/5 of the top 100 games used AI tools, and two almost-fully-AI games reached the top 20. **Players don't punish AI use, they punish the LOOK of no effort.**
The tells to avoid:
- **Art that doesn't match the game:** a glossy, painted ChatGPT thumbnail over a game that looks different = clickbait = bounces + dislikes. Thumbnails and icons are built from the REAL in-game models (Blender GFX); ChatGPT only for backgrounds, glow and polish, and everything shown exists in the game.
- **ChatGPT image tells:** warm yellow tint, soft "slimy" shapes, over-detailed noise, gibberish text, extra fins/teeth/fingers, everything perfectly symmetric. Check every image for these before use.
- **Mixed styles:** Toolbox free models + our models + different UI styles (DevForum Battle Cars: "a weird mix of ultra high poly and super low poly"). One art style, one UI kit, one font pair per game; our own models for anything the player looks at.
- **Placeholders:** default fonts/icons, plain text boards, pitched-down stock sounds (already a Lessons rule).
- **No game feel:** stiff models with no animation. Every main action gets an animation, a sound and a visual (squash/stretch, chomp, splash). Feel is the strongest "they worked hard" signal.
- **Empty generic map:** flat terrain with scattered props. Hand-placed named landmarks (a shipwreck, a sunken statue), each with a reason to go there.
- **AI-sounding text:** long, polished, emoji-stuffed descriptions and NPC lines. Short, kid-voice, specific ("the squid is back. bring friends.").
- **Show the work:** a real update log, Discord sneak peeks of WIP, "made by P&L Studios XP" in the description.

## Studio polish rules (deep research 11 Nov: [[2026-11-11 Research - Shark game world and polish bible (deep research report)]])
"Looks like a 20-person team made it" = **coherence, not volume**: one grammar (anticipation → white-hot impact → short fade) for abilities, VFX, SFX and UI, with size matched to importance ("if everything is important, nothing is").
- **Creatures:** every creature = a warning tell + a size where it flips from hazard to food + a reward for eating it (the Hungry Shark puffer heals 15 HP). Life = behaviour: schools (boids), bait balls, gentle giants passing by.
- **World:** depth zones are the progression ladder (sunlit → twilight → midnight → abyss → hadal "underworld"); each zone has its own fog, light, palette and ONE hero landmark; value key so zones differ even in greyscale. Kill the flat blue haze: colour near the player, warm accents in the reef, one bright + one dark value per zone, rim-light silhouettes, Contrast not lower Brightness.
- **Lighting (2026):** `Lighting.Technology` is replaced by `LightingStyle` (Realistic) + `PrioritizeLightingQuality`. Beams/transparent parts don't render behind Terrain Water. Highlight cap is 255 per client. Zone presets (starting values) are in the bible's lighting table; tune on a cheap Android.
- **Abilities:** one verb, one telegraph, one counter. PvP abilities only knock back/stun/slow (bites kill); same target can't be re-stunned for ~2 s; nothing in the safe zone; nothing paid affects power.
- **UI motion (starting values):** press 0.06–0.08 s to UIScale 0.92 (Quad Out) → release 0.18–0.25 s Back Out; window open 0.25–0.35 s Back Out from 0.85, close 0.15–0.2 s Quad In; toast in 0.25–0.3 / hold 1.5–2.5 / out 0.2 s; coin fly burst 0.5 s Quad Out, wait 0.25, fly 0.8 s Quad In, counter +30 % for 0.1 s then 0.15–0.25 s Back settle; shine = UIGradient.Offset −1→1 in 1 s Circular Out ×2, rest 2.5 s; idle breathing 1.5–3 s at 1.03–1.05 with a random phase. Max 1–2 idle movers per region, one loud at a time; springs (`spr`) for interruptible motion; honour Reduce Motion.
- **UI effects kit (MEGA BITE `MegaBite.UIFx`, 12 Nov):** Sheen (buttons that DO something, found tiles, the chosen setting), Glints (4-point, around featured art), Burst (ring + glints + bubbles for "you got it"), Pop, Wiggle (a padlock saying no). Tweens only, nothing runs while its window is closed, off under Reduced Motion, never a ray ring. Design rules from the same pass: cards fill their row on every screen; the hero model fills its card and pokes out; nothing from the world (outlines, tags, fish) draws over an open window; no fake SALE stickers. Note: [[2026-11-12 MEGA BITE - UI effects + design pass 1 (Shop, Index, Settings)]].
- **Ability sounds (MEGA BITE v3, 12 Nov):** one sound per ability, written on the clip's own timeline (read the clip keys for the beats), started on the clip's first frame, riser peaking exactly on the hit; the clip's other markers drive VFX only. Check the energy split by band (a -13 LUFS sound that is all under 120 Hz is silent on a phone). Note: [[2026-11-12 MEGA BITE - Ability sounds v3 (Frenzy, Torpedo, Hammer Ram, Breach, Tidal Roar)]].
- **UI sound set (MEGA BITE v3, 12 Nov, APPROVED by the owner 12:36):** one family in one key ("glass bubble" plucks in C), each action its own sound: tap, tab (pitch steps per tab), select (pop + the card's whoosh + landing chime), equip (two notes rising), open per window, close (falling), switch (pitch per option), swipe, hover (mouse only). One blanket click on every button must stay silent when the button has its own sound (`S.Tap`). Count the call sites of a sample and log `Sound.Played` in Play before and after. Note: [[2026-11-12 MEGA BITE - UI sound set v3 (Shop, Index, Settings)]].
- **SFX recipe:** transient (>1 kHz, 0–30 ms) + body (~200 Hz–5 kHz identity) + sub sine 30–60 Hz falling over 200–400 ms + dark reverb tail; risers curve up and PEAK exactly on the impact; phones −16 to −18 LUFS, peaks ≤ −1 dBTP; identity in 1–5 kHz (phone speakers lose bass); underwater = low-pass world sounds + a bright clean layer for rewards. **Owner 11 Nov: no Fish Audio, make SFX ourselves in Python synthesis** (the bible's Fish Audio section is obsolete). Mixing: SoundGroups are discouraged → Audio API faders + sidechain compressor.
- **Never:** a spiky sunburst anywhere (use a soft radial glow, light shafts or a bubble ring).

## 3D hero card (12 Nov, built in MEGA BITE as `MegaBite.HeroCard`; study: [[2026-11-12 Watch study - 3D pet card and egg hatch screen (owner recording 11 Nov)]])
The reference's Pets window = a flat panel on the left + ONE real 3D card on the right. Use it for the one selected item of a window (shark, skin, fish, armour set), never for a list.
- **Build:** the card is opaque Parts held in front of the camera, PARALLEL to the screen (facing the eye stretches it off-centre); SurfaceGuis at ~200 px per stud with LightInfluence 0; the real model about 0.6 studs in front (slide it toward the screen centre by offset x depth / distance); a soft round glow plate behind; a dark tint PLANE behind that (a ScreenGui veil darkens the card too); the world blurred with DepthOfField focused on the card (restore the old values on close).
- **Motion:** tilt toward the mouse on a spring (12° yaw, 8° pitch); phones sway ±5°; a full 0.5 s spin on change with the swap at the back; Reduce Motion = neither.
- **Input:** press the card's button through an invisible 2D proxy button placed over it each frame.
- **Room:** a yaw toward the screen centre makes the outer edge grow (about 45 px at 12°), so leave a margin; keep card text at 14 px or more as seen on the phone.
- **When the owner names a reference effect, find it in the recording first.** I first built a flat hover tilt and cursor sparkles; the owner meant this card.
- **Never put a frame inside a button that has its own layout or AutomaticSize** (a shine frame made the Shop's tabs grow without end), and wait past every timed rescan before judging a window.

## Models and skins from a sheet (12 Nov 2026, [[2026-11-12 MEGA BITE - HSE rebuild (Reef Shark model, designed skins, FishKit v3)]])
- **Judge a model with a flat, bright render at the sheet's exact pixel scale, side by side with the sheet,** then four 3D views. A dark studio render hides the faults.
- **Keep the sheet's painted face** (brow, eye socket, lip shading) in the texture and put the 3D eye exactly on the painted one. 3D brow tubes and "ignore circles" around the eye left streaks.
- **Body outline:** the true silhouette, bridged straight across each fin base. Pectorals come from the top view.
- **Mouth:** lip curves traced from the sheet, a red membrane between the lips so nobody sees through, no edge-line shell inside the mouth.
- **A skin is a design, not a tint:** own colours per zone, own pattern (spots, glowing dots, ribs, lava cracks), own eye, fin colour, fin tips and edge line. Shop pictures are renders of the real model.
- **Button anatomy:** tall lip, outline in the button's own dark colour, gloss on the top half, text with a drop shadow. A flat pill with a thin stroke reads as a placeholder. List tiles show the picture, not a name bar.
- **Kit rules learnt on five sharks (FishKit v3):** start every fin bridge BEHIND the fin's leaning tip; fins get their own piece of the sheet (rays, tips); a wide flat back or a hammer needs the top view on top; a hammer head needs a boxy section so its ends are faces, not edges; scale skin patterns by the fish's real size so spots stay round. A new upload can show grey for a few minutes: the asset is Approved but not delivered yet.
- **Triangle budget (12 Nov, food fish):** a model that appears many times gets a lite build (`run_lite.sh`: fewer body rings, coarser fin grid, same texture). First build 8-16K triangles a fish = +790K triangles for 71 fish; lite about 4K a fish and looks the same. Measure with `Stats.SceneTriangleCount`, models shown vs hidden, before calling it done.
- **Checking in Play without computer use:** Studio's `screen_capture` works during Play and shows the UI; a small Client snippet holds the camera on the thing to check.
- **Use a new image id only after the upload poll says Approved** (set while "Reviewing", the Studio session keeps the failed fetch: grey part). After installing textures, line every variant up in Studio and look.
- **Anything held in front of the camera (3D card, reveal) needs the world cleared in front of it:** food fish swam between the camera and the Shop card; `MB_FishClient` parks near fish while the Shop or Index is open.
- **Trace fin outlines by colour, not by eye** (largest fin-coloured component + 5 px for the outline); a hand-drawn polygon 25 px off leaves the painted fin showing or the 3D fin cut. Chubby bodies need `pect_w_k` about 1.1.
- **An uploaded FBX also uploads its embedded texture** (`InsertService:LoadAsset(id)` shows the id). Use it when a separately uploaded image is Approved but stays grey.
- **Phone layout lessons (12 Nov, MEGA BITE phone test):** fit windows to the screen MINUS `GuiService.TopbarInset` so nothing sits under Roblox's own buttons, and give every open/close tween the same offset (a tween to the old position silently undoes it). Size a 3D side card to the gap between the window's top bar and the list's bottom, never to a fixed fraction of the screen (on a 380 pt-tall phone a 70%-height card hits the top bar). A text line next to a badge needs its width to stop at the badge plus TextScaled with a 10-15 px constraint. Studio's device emulator: the phone/tablet list appears after clicking the device-type icon LEFT of the device dropdown; Studio MCP clicks by instance path miss in an emulated view, so click on the screen instead; switch back to "Current device" (laptop icon) after.
- **Reward stars / coin-fly (12 Nov, MEGA BITE EXP stars):** a 3D layer in a ViewportFrame gets its VFX from 2D layers ABOVE and BELOW it (glow halo + sparkle trail under, twinkle + landing flash over), positioned by projecting each star back to the screen. Mind the top-bar inset when one layer ignores it and the target's doesn't (the stars landed 58 px off). Sound: one burst sound, a ting per item on a pentatonic ladder that keeps climbing through a combo, one final strum; queue tings into 50 ms slots instead of dropping them (frame hitches land several at once); a pooled sound re-Played while ringing cuts itself off, so list the same id several times and pick a copy that is not playing. Note: [[2026-11-12 MEGA BITE - EXP star VFX + sounds]].
- **Sliding selection pills (12 Nov, MEGA BITE Shop tabs):** one pill that tweens Position + Size together (Quint Out ~0.34 s, no overshoot so it never leaves its capsule). Put it in a scale-sized child (an AutomaticSize parent counts offset children, so a moving/stretching pill resizes the bar); never use a UIScale for a "hop" inside a layout (it changes layout widths); don't let a scroll-follow re-pick tabs while a tab click's own scroll tween runs. Note: [[2026-11-12 MEGA BITE - Shop tab slider + first-person crosshair]].
- **ViewportFrame thumbnails (12 Nov, MEGA BITE Shop skins):** a static ViewportFrame does not redraw when a texture finishes loading (models showed grey). PreloadAsync the MeshParts, then nudge the camera CFrame by 1e-3 to force a redraw (and again when the window opens). Live 3D thumbnails beat pre-rendered pictures when the subject changes (the player's current shark x every skin).
- **A different mouth = a different MODEL (12 Nov night, MEGA BITE Megalodon):** closing an open-mouth model by turning its jaw bone (or baking that turn into the mesh) never reads right: the rigid jaw becomes a pale cup with a step at the corner, from the front it hides every tooth, and tuning angles only trades one fault for another (lip-biting, double chin). What worked: ChatGPT edits the approved sheet with ONE prompt ("keep everything identical, change only the mouth: closed, lips in one line, upper jaw overhanging, tooth tips peeking"), then rebuild from the new sheet. For a closed mouth in FishKit v3: lips meet (lo = up + 1-3 px), `tooth_fit 0` (it shrinks teeth to the lip gap), `jaw_w ~0.84` so the lower jaw sits INSIDE the upper and the upper teeth hang outside it. Always check the FRONT view too.
- **2D that looks 3D (12 Nov, EXP stars):** render the real 3D object once in Blender (Cycles, gloss + clear coat, 3-point light) as a 16-frame spin flipbook (a symmetric object only needs a half turn), add a dark outline in Python, play it with ImageRectOffset. Each copy starts on a random frame at its own speed and direction. Same look as a ViewportFrame, none of its cost.
- **Phones: what the fog hides is still drawn.** Measure with `Stats.SceneTriangleCount`, hide groups one at a time to find the cost, then (1) a client culler: `LocalTransparencyModifier = 1` past the distance where the fog test pillars were faint (MEGA BITE: 600 studs) or when a piece is under ~10 px on screen, never the skyline pieces; (2) lower-poly versions of meshes that repeat hundreds of times (Blender Decimate, collapse, UV seams kept; swap in place with `MeshPart:ApplyMesh`). MEGA BITE spawn reef: 2.2 M -> 0.5 M triangles, looks the same.
- **Studio MCP:** `ScriptEditorService:UpdateSourceAsync` can hang (80 s timeouts, link drop); setting `.Source` directly works. `ContentProvider:PreloadAsync` "Failure" for an image does NOT mean it won't render: look at it in the viewport before re-uploading.
