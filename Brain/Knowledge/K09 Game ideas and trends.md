## How trends win on Roblox
- A clear concept + weekly updates + creators/TikTok. Late clones rarely win: treat a trend as "theme + one twist", build small and fast (2-3 weeks first version).
- Patterns in trending games: one visible "how far" number, curiosity doors, rare reveals, max-count collect lists, automation as late rewards, a weekly event.

## What dead games teach (12 games)
- Loved: shared places, scarce dated events, homes to visit, things to care for, readable co-op combos, funny chaos, asymmetric roles.
- Died from: paywalls, PC-only/Flash, server + moderation costs (Roblox fixes these) and from skill barriers, needing a full team, slow updates, cosmetic-only money, empty queues (Roblox does NOT fix these).
