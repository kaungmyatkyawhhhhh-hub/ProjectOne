# K05 Marketing, ads and storefront
Back to [[00 BRAIN]] · Method: [[Data first]] · Sources: [[2026-11-05 PLAYBOOK - Roblox icon, thumbnail and logo recipe]], [[Playbook notes - Thumbnails]], [[Playbook notes - Icons and titles]], [[Playbook notes - Names and descriptions]], [[2026-11-03 Research - High CTR Roblox thumbnails]], [[2026-11-07 Video edit style - what makes an edit good (reference study)]], [[2026-11-02 GYM ARC Ads - best practice and ChatGPT prompts]]

## The funnel (find the biggest leak)
impression → click (CTR) → game page → **Play** → minute 1 → day 1 → pay.
- Galaxy ads (7 Nov): 167,557 impressions, 2,697 clicks, **CTR 1.61%** (fine), $0.011/click, only $28.73 spent. But only ~6% of clickers pressed Play: the **game page** leaked (38% likes, "[ALPHA]" in the title, looked empty).

## Ads (Roblox Sponsored Experiences)
- Plays goal = cheapest clicks; benchmarks ~$0.007 per play. Run 3+ days per test, $30-50/day; judge cost per PLAY and D1, not CTR. Kill a creative after 5,000 impressions under 1.2% CTR.
- **No ads while playtime is bad**: ad players who quit fast drag down the numbers Home uses.
- Investor-funded ads only make sense at ROAS ≥ the payback multiple (GYM ARC deal: needed 2.5× to not extend the payback).
- Best Galaxy creatives: a giant number ("1.2Qa/s") scaled best; the logo was steady; the black hole weakest.

## Storefront
- Icon ≥512, readable at 150 px, ONE bold subject; thumbnails 1920×1080, one template, tiny story (threat vs you / before→after / huge reward), text = logo or one big number. Never copy a hit's look. 2-5 in Thumbnail Personalization, ≥7 days each.
- Name short, says the genre, never renamed after launch; description line 1 = what you do.
- Game-page video: real gameplay only, Roblox-catalog music only, no voice/lyrics, no promo text, minor grading only, ≤30 s (newer 60), 16:9, mp4 < 100 MB, 3 uploads/month, loop ending.

## Short-form video (TikTok/Shorts)
- Music first; cuts, zooms, shakes and flashes ON the beat; speed ramps; whip blur; kinetic one-word text; warm saturated grade; no static shot > 2 beats; hook in second 1; loop ending.
- AI edits get roasted for: few zooms, no text animation, flat blue grade, not feeling the beat. Pipeline: skill roblox-game-video-pipeline.

## Group and community
- Group members are the owner's channel for every future game in that group (GYM ARC/MBC: free Members Gym drove ~1K joins/day).
- Announcements: once per 7 days to all members; Update Announcements 60 chars, 1 per 3 days (the only notification that reaches under-13s).
