# K10 Business, deals and how we work
Back to [[00 BRAIN]] · Sources: [[2026-11-07 GYM ARC - Owner decision - stop development (autopilot)]], [[MM Shop Bot - Business Plan]], [[Myanmar Online Business Research - Nov 2026]], [[Night shift queue]]

## Investor deals (learned the hard way, GYM ARC)
- The first deal let each ad spend restart a 70% payback (1.75×), so the owner's share could stay at 30% forever while the investor kept advertising. Renegotiated 9 Nov: investor 20%, owner 80%.
- Every future deal, in writing BEFORE money moves: a fixed end date or payback cap; ad spend counts only if both agree in advance; a buyout clause; no share in other games; "revenue" = DevEx USD actually received; a monthly statement. (Not legal advice.)
- Ads repay only if each $1 brings back more than the payback multiple ÷ the investor's share.

## How we work
- Owner's health rules: no game work after midnight Myanmar time, 7+ hours sleep, a stretch break every hour. The night shift (scheduled task) works after the cut-off: first run Mon 9 Nov 03:30, then every night 22:30.
