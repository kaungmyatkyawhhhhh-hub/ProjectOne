- Our games: GYM ARC D1 ~4-5.6%, 11.3 min sessions (9 Nov). Galaxy 3.5 min (bottom 1%) before the pacing fix.
- **No ads until D1 ~10% and 5-minute retention ~45%.** Small games are noisy: read weekly cohorts.

## The first minutes (most important)
- First action in seconds, first reward within ~10 s, something to do every ~3 s in the first 30 s, no wait over ~20 s in the first 10 min, first big goal (prestige) ~5 min.
- Galaxy's sim: 0 stardust at 1/s + no comets in tutorial steps 1-5 = 20% gone by 60 s. Fix: a welcome gift, cheap first pads, half-price first levels, comets from 8 s → 100% stay.
- GYM ARC: 54% left before the first Growth Spurt; fixes that worked: start next to the first station, GO TO teleport, reward on the first tap, the main goal visible from second one, a 2x new-player boost, milestones at 25/50/75%.
- Show, don't tell. Hints only when stuck, they stay until done, nothing stacked on a hint, no shop offers in the tutorial. Kids skip text and call things "for babies" fast.
- The HUD "NEXT" goal must always be reachable within ~60 s; the big goal (prestige bar) lives separately.
- **A broken game gets dislikes; a boring game gets quits.** Galaxy's 5/8 likes came from the broken phone launch.

## Why players keep playing (Regrowth / Immortality / Depth / top sims)
1. The progression IS the story: a one-line premise + chapters you can SEE (your place rebuilt by tycoon pads).
2. A big gate always in view that needs several things at once (breakthrough / Ignition).
3. Each chapter adds a NEW SYSTEM, not just bigger numbers.
4. Layers of resets; each makes the next run faster (run 2 ~2x faster).
5. Something new every ~30 s early, named tiers every 10 levels, a second track (XP → perks).
6. Updates named as parts with a code each; codes for milestones, events, apologies.
7. Power must be USED and SEEN: strength opens things, rewards others can see (auras, size, crowns, statues). Coins and titles are the bonus, not the prize.
8. Collection books with % complete and items that max out and leave the pool.

## Come-back hooks for kids
- Offline earnings, a 7-day streak (with grace for a missed day), daily quests sized to one session, playtime gifts (5/10/20/40/60 min), friend boost (+10% each, max 3), invite rewards, favourite prompt after the first big moment, a fixed Saturday event, a monthly party with a free limited item (Club Penguin).
- Players who FINISH leave: plan the endless loop (rebirths, weekly boards, events) before launch.

## Social
- Co-play days are a ranking signal. Server-wide goals (Star Surge, the Great Star), island visiting + stars, "strongest in this server" crowns, a big plaza to show off.
