# Data first

**Why:** on 9 Nov the owner said ad CTR was "under 1%"; the Ads Manager screenshots showed 1.61% and that the real leak was the game page (only ~6% of clickers pressed Play). Guessing would have fixed the wrong thing.
**Use when:** any "why is X bad" (ads, likes, playtime, revenue, group joins).

## Steps
1. Get the real numbers (Creator Hub analytics, Ads Manager, funnels, screenshots from the owner) before answering.
2. Do the maths (totals, rates per 100 players, before/after).
3. Find the biggest leak in the chain (impression → click → play → minute 1 → day 1 → pay).
4. Fix the biggest leak first; set up tracking (custom events, A/B halves) so next time it's measured.
