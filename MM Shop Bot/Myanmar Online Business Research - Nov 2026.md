# Myanmar Online Business Research — what to sell (Nov 2026)

Research note for FlexiToysMM / new online shop. Sources at bottom.

## 1. Market conditions (why most products fail right now)
- GDP −2% FY2025/26; inflation 24.6% (Apr 2026); poverty ~30%; UN: over two-thirds of population poor or at risk (Oct 2026).
- Purchasing power weak → buyers go for cheapest option on anything non-essential. Price-driven categories = price wars.
- Kyat volatile: in Myawaddy, Thai goods are bought in baht and prices in kyat roughly doubled. Anyone importing needs to re-price often.
- Import squeeze: Myanmar import-licence / export-earning-matching rules + the regime's 100-Day Plan clampdown on illegal goods (May 2026) halted much informal Thai/Chinese freight; "full shelves mask import crisis as foreign goods vanish" (Mar 2026). Formal Thai border trade down >50% vs 3–4 yrs ago; traders rely on informal routes. → Importing yourself is risky; buying from stock already inside Myanmar is safer.
- Chinese goods have been replacing Thai goods in the market (cheaper, easier route via Muse).

## 2. Where selling happens (channels)
- TikTok = #1 platform by reach (21M users, ~50% of adults), no VPN needed. Discovery + live selling; checkout happens off-platform (Telegram/Viber DM, KBZPay/WavePay, COD).
- Facebook: banned, ~13.7M still use via VPN; Facebook Live still the highest-converting format for small sellers (fashion, food, beauty, handmade). Cyber Security Law (Jul 2025) criminalises VPN use → legal grey area. Don't build a business that depends only on Facebook.
- Telegram = order/community channel; Shopee = largest structured marketplace (new-customer volume); Facebook Groups still very active.
- Fastest-growing online categories reported: mobile & electronics (≈¼ of online revenue), fashion & beauty (live selling), home & appliances, groceries/household.
- Biggest buyer complaint: sellers over-promising delivery times.

## 4. Recommendation: small power-cut products
Why: the one category where buyers are chasing sellers, not the reverse, and it stays that way until at least the 2027 monsoon.
- What: rechargeable fans (table + clip), rechargeable LED lanterns/bulbs, 20,000mAh power banks, small 100–300Wh power stations later. Add-ons: multi-plug surge boards, cables.
- Source INSIDE Myanmar (avoid border/import risk): Yangon electronics wholesale (Theingyi Zay, Bayint Naung, Mingalar Market area), Mandalay Zegyo. Chinese brands already imported. Price in a way that tracks the dollar.
- Sell: TikTok daily "real runtime test" videos (fan on high for X hours on a timer, lantern brightness after 8 h), Telegram channel for orders, KBZPay prepay + COD in Nay Pyi Taw, Royal Express nationwide. Honest capacity claims + 7-day replacement = the moat, because cheap no-name units fail and buyers know it.
- Content hook: "power's off till 2027 — this is what actually lasts the night".
- With $35: buy 2 rechargeable fans + 2 lanterns wholesale, sell, reinvest; move to power stations once capital reaches ~$150.

## 5. Watch-outs
- Don't over-promise delivery (top complaint).
- Don't rely on Facebook only (VPN legality).
- Re-price with the kyat; hold stock in dollar-value goods.
- Import yourself only via carriers that handle paperwork (AST Express, Thai Myanmar Logistics); informal freight is being seized under the 100-Day Plan.
