# Flash TopUp — logo prompts (pro GFX look), 6 Nov 2026
Owner: logo must not look AI-made, should look drawn by a highly paid GFX artist. Built from [[2026-11-05 PLAYBOOK - Roblox icon, thumbnail and logo recipe]] (logo template).
Split like a pro identity: WORDMARK (banners, site header) + MARK without text (round Telegram avatar, favicon). Attach ONE style reference (e.g. GYM ARC logo_nov\gymarc_logo_final_v9_pink.png).
Website art (pack icons, hero, bg tile) went live 6 Nov: public/art/*.webp in the flashtopup repo.

## Wordmark
Create an ORIGINAL professional game-style logo for a shop called "FLASH TOPUP", drawn like a highly paid Roblox GFX artist's work, matching the quality (not the content) of the attached reference. Do not copy the reference's words or shapes.
TEXT, spelled exactly: "FLASH" on top, "TOPUP" below it and slightly bigger, and a small "MYANMAR" on a dark ribbon under them. No other text.
LETTERING: bold custom italic display letters, all capitals, slanted forward for speed, with crisp sharp corners and perfectly consistent stroke widths, every letter clean, symmetric and evenly spaced (no melted, warped, merged or extra letters). "FLASH" in polished chrome-silver metal; "TOPUP" in rich gold metal; both with a deep 3D bevel, a bright shine line along the top edges and a thick near-black outline around every letter plus one outer stroke around the whole word.
MARK: a bold gold lightning bolt cuts diagonally behind the words from top right to bottom left, with sharp straight edges, its own dark outline, and a short white speed trail.
BEHIND THE WORDS: a dark navy-purple shield-shaped plate so both words are much brighter than it; electric blue rim light only on its outer edges; a few small sharp sparks and 4-point stars. No smoke blobs, no soft glow mush.
FINISH: clean vector-sharp edges like a pro esports or Roblox game logo, high contrast, readable at 200 px wide. Real transparent PNG with an alpha channel, not a checkerboard. 2:1, centered with space around it.

## Avatar mark (no text)
Create a 1024x1024 ORIGINAL logo mark for a game top-up shop, drawn like a highly paid Roblox GFX artist's work, matching the quality (not the content) of the attached reference. NO text at all.
A bold gold lightning bolt with sharp straight edges, a deep 3D bevel, a bright shine line, and a thick near-black outline, cutting diagonally across a single large blue faceted diamond with crisp hard facets. Behind them, a round dark navy-purple badge with a polished chrome-silver rim and a thin gold inner ring; a soft electric blue glow directly behind the bolt, darkening toward the rim; 3 or 4 small sharp 4-point sparkles.
Everything important at least 10% from the edges so it survives a circular crop. Clean, symmetric, vector-sharp edges, high contrast; it must read clearly at 48 px as "gold bolt on blue diamond". No soft blobs, no smoke, no checkerboard. Real transparent PNG with alpha outside the badge.

## Checks before use
Spelling exact (FLASH / TOPUP / MYANMAR), edges sharp at 100%, readable at 48 px (mark) and 200 px wide (wordmark), real alpha. If one letter is off: "keep EXACTLY the same image, only fix the letter X".
