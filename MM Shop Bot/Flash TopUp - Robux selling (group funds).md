# Flash TopUp - Robux selling & group funds (6 Nov 2026)

Owner sells own Robux (shareholders agreed game Robux can be used). Website Roblox tab: Group payout 33 Ks/Robux (first time: 15-day wait) and Roblox Plus 39 Ks/Robux (within 1 day). Bot: /robuxrate 33 39, /setrobloxgroup, /robuxlog, 📦 Delivered button.

## How Robux get into group funds ([source](https://generalistprogrammer.com/tutorials/roblox-group-funds-explained))
- Game passes / developer products in a GROUP-owned game, group store clothing/UGC, Premium Payouts.
- Sales are pending about 3-21 days before they can be paid out.
- Payout recipients must be in the group 14+ days; only the owner pays out; one-time payouts can't be undone.
- Roblox takes its marketplace cut (30%) on game pass and clothing sales.

## Decision: best way to move Robux into the selling group
- Game pass in a game owned by the selling group > shirt. Same 30% cut and pending time, but no 10 R$ upload fee, no template, no catalog moderation, any price instantly.
- Every move from a personal account into a group by buying your own pass/shirt loses 30% (1,000 in -> 700 in funds). Price that into the 33 Ks rate.
- Best of all (no extra 30%): Robux earned directly by a group-owned game. But selling from the game's own group risks the game if Roblox bans for reselling - keep the selling group separate.
