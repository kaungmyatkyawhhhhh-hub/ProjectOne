# Stomp Evolution v139: kid rerun, logo, Saturday update plan

## Impatient kid on v138 (`Calc/kid_v138.py`, `kid_v138b.py`; 2,000 kids, patience median 11 s)
| version | 5 min stay | 5 min @7 s patience | cards by 5 min |
|---|---|---|---|
| v127 (8 forms, 3 power-ups per form) | 99% | 89% | ~21 |
| v138 as built (1 power-up per evolve) | **84%** | **59%** | 9 |
| + 2 per form | 99% | 90% | 18 |
| **+ 3 per form for the first 9 forms, then 1 per evolve (BUILT, v139)** | **100%** | **96%** | 24 |

**Lesson:** the card picks between minute 2 and minute 5 are what keep impatient kids. Fewer, bigger evolves didn't make up for losing them. Adding the combo on its own made it slightly worse (faster growth, same number of rewards).

`C.Power` now gives 3 per form for forms 1-9 (to MAGMA), then 1 per evolve, then +1 per +15% height after the top (max 40). Cards run out at 20; after that each power-up is a +10% shard.
