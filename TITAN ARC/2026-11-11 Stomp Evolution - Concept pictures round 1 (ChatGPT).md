## The species now: the STOMPER (this replaces the "horn crest + shell segments" wording)
An upright two-legged creature with a smooth round body and no neck; ENORMOUS round flat feet with three big flat toenails (the biggest feature on every stage); short pillar legs; small arms with three stubby fingers; a wide round head with a short blunt snout; ONE tall backward-sweeping head crest like a fan (a single crest, never a pair of horns); a rounded back of overlapping banded shell segments like an armadillo; a short thick tail ending in a round club. One body colour from head to toe.
Attitude grows with size: stages 1-3 cute and friendly, 4-5 cheeky and tough, 6-8 fierce (lowered brows, glowing eyes, strong lower jaw with two small blunt tusks, broad shoulders, big fists).
The player's hat (a lime-green beanie in the pictures) stays on every stage: it is always "you". In the game it is the player's own avatar hat or hair.

## My check of try 3 (honest)
- **Good:** reads as one family; one clear colour per stage; the feet are the biggest thing on every stage; cute at the small end and cool at the big end; the low-poly facets match what we can build; nothing touches an edge on any sheet; 8 clean pieces to cut.
- **Outline test at 64 px: only half passed.** In black, stages 3 to 8 are the same body in the same pose and differ only by the crest; 3 and 4 are almost the same shape. Before modelling, each stage needs one more outline feature below the head (different shoulders, back, tail or arms).
- **Size:** stage 8 is 3.8 times stage 1 in the picture (asked: 5), and the steps are uneven (x1.07 to x1.39). The game's ladder (about x1.36 per form) comes from the config, not from this picture.
- **Look-alike check on try 3:** I do not see a known character in it. The owner should look too. The big stages are a muscular tusked giant, a common fantasy shape, so the crest, the giant feet and the club tail must stay strong.
- **ROCKHIDE sheet:** usable as a first look, not yet as the modelling contract: the side view has the arms raised (not the neutral pose) and the top view is slightly tilted. Redo it, plus a head close-up sheet, when modelling starts.
- **City props:** bright and friendly, three clearly different houses. Smoother than the monsters (less faceted): tighten the style words when the real prop sheets are made.
- **Bosses:** the JUNK boss is clearly junk and lopsided as asked. The ROGUE works as "one of us gone bad".

## Lessons for the method
- Inserting a long prompt with `document.execCommand('insertText')` on ChatGPT's `div.ProseMirror` is instant; typing 2,600 characters timed out (it still arrived: check the box before retyping).
- Downloads is not a connected folder: the Blender bridge copied the six PNGs from Downloads into the vault.
- Run the look-alike check on the FIRST picture, before making any follow-up sheet from it.
