# Stomp Evolution: model brainstorm (families, the model list, the style)

Method: [[roblox-3d-model-from-sheet]] skill (design sheet → trace in Blender → gates → owner approval). This note is the step BEFORE its step 1.
Read for it: [[K06 Art, UI, VFX, sound and lighting]], [[2026-11-01 Research - Modeling like a professional (stylized low poly)]], [[Playbook notes - Top games art study (Oct 2026)]], [[2026-11-09 MEGA BITE - Art style guide]], [[2026-11-09 MEGA BITE - Shark art research (models, animation, assets)]], the three kid sims and design doc v1.1 in this folder.

## 1. What the rivals look like (game pages opened 11 Nov)
| Game | What you are | Look |
|---|---|---|
| +1 Titan Evolution (1.7K playing) | a muscle titan | an Attack-on-Titan style giant on the icon (anime hair, bare chest, lightning), "+1" text all over; world 2 is called "Liberia". It sells to anime fans |
| Grow And Build Giants (2.5K) | your avatar; the giant is a pet in a pen | classic studded Roblox, a pale blocky golem-like giant |
| Bug Evolution (14K) | **the creature itself** (a spider, then 30+ insects on a "Bug Tree") | plain grassy hills and a castle, semi-real bugs; "become the King of the server"; a boss every hour |
**What it means:** in evolution games you BECOME the creature. So my earlier pick (your own avatar wearing a kit per form) is wrong for an evolution-first game: a kit on an avatar hides the evolution, and kits clip on the many avatar body types. **Changed: every form is a full creature model.** That is also exactly what the sheet method is proven on (7 sharks approved).
And nobody in this space owns a bright, hand-made toy-city monster look: one is anime, one is studs, one is semi-real.

## 2. The big choice: what do you evolve into?
Scores are my judgement, 1-5.
| Family | Reads in half a second | Cute → epic | IP safety | Fits a toy city | Build + animation cost | One colour per form | Endless weekly forms | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Element golems (pebble → rock → iron → magma...) | 3 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 29 |
| B. Toy robots (wind-up toy → tin bot → mega mech) | 4 | 4 | 3 | 5 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 29 |
| C. Kaiju lizard (hatchling → city-stomping monster) | 5 | 5 | 3 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 26 |
| **D. BIG-FOOT MONSTERS with element armour (C's body + A's materials)** | 5 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 5 | **32** |
- A is the easiest to build (rigid rock chunks, no skin to bend) but a golem reads as "that Minecraft / Clash thing", not as "I stomp cities".
- B fits the toy city best, but mech looks sit close to big robot brands, and our boss is already a robot.
- C is THE city-stomping fantasy and the cutest baby, but a plain giant lizard drifts toward one famous movie monster.
- **D (my pick):** a little monster with **oversized stompy feet** (the game's own signature: the foot is the icon), short arms, a round belly, a horn crest and a short tail. Every evolution grows a new ARMOUR MATERIAL in rigid plates over a simple body: stone, iron, magma, storm, crystal, star. Rigid plates = clean flat-shaded facets and no weight-painting trouble; one hero colour per form (the cutscene rule); a new element every week.
- **Never:** maple-leaf back plates, a beam from the mouth, skinless giants, or any look of a known monster, robot or anime giant.

## 2b. To 35/35: family D2, WIND-UP TOY MONSTERS (owner 11 Nov 02:30: "we need scores to 35/35")
D lost a point each on IP safety, fitting the toy city, and build cost. A score only means something if a 5 has a test, so each 5 below has one. Changing the design is what moved the scores, not the marking.

**The change:** the monster is itself a TOY: a wind-up toy monster come alive in a toy city. A wind-up key on its back turns as it walks and spins fast on a stomp. Shoulders and hips are ball joints. Each evolution's armour SNAPS ON as rigid toy plates with a click. The oversized feet stay the signature, and now **the feet tell the form** (stone boots, iron boots, glowing magma soles...), so each form also leaves its own footprint.

**The three IP fixes (checked 11 Nov):**
1. **The event can't be called RAMPAGE.** Rampage is a game series (Midway, now Warner Bros.) in which giant monsters destroy cities. Same word, same fantasy. Renamed **ROGUE ALERT** (a Rogue toy breaks loose and the server stops it).
2. **Form 5 loses the ember tail tip** (a flame on the tail is one famous pocket monster's mark). Its glow moves to cracks in the soles of its feet.
3. **Form 4 is IRONBOOT, not Ironfoot** (as far as I know, Ironfoot is a character's name in a famous fantasy series).
Also checked: no "Stompling" in Roblox search (first 10 results) or in a web search; the nearest toy names found are a marble game called Stomple and dinosaur shoes called Thunder Stompers. This is not a trademark search; one is still on the name-check list.
**Look-alike checklist for every sheet (all must be "no"):** back plates like maple leaves or a beam from the mouth; an ape, lizard or wolf climbing and punching buildings; bunny ears with a zig-zag grin; a bulb on the back or a flame on the tail; a boxy long-armed golem with a big nose; a humanoid robot with a chest window or a visor; a skinless giant.

**Three of the seven 5s are only promises until a sheet exists:** the outline test, the look-alike check and the cute/cool read need the first drawing (and your eye). If SPROUT's sheet fails one, the score drops and the design changes.

**Forms, updated:** SPROUT · STOMPLING · ROCKHIDE · **IRONBOOT** · MAGMABACK (glowing soles) · STORMHORN · PRISMFANG · STARCROWN. The Rampage titan is now the **ROGUE** of the ROGUE ALERT.

## 2c. TOY WITHDRAWN (owner 11 Nov 02:32: "u sure toy is good?"). No.
The 35/35 was true on my scoreboard and wrong for the game. The scoreboard had no row for the thing that matters most: **do kids want to BE this?** I added the wind-up toy idea to win three points, not because anything showed kids want it.
**What the data says (Roblox search, 11 Nov):**
- Toy-themed games are dead or horror: "Be a Toy!" 1 playing, "Transforming toy simulator" 6, and the rest of a "toy monster" search is scary toy-shop obbies and Poppy-style games. A wind-up key can read as creepy, or as "for babies".
- Evolution games that work let you become something COOL and a bit scary: +1 Venom Evolution 20.7K, Bug Evolution 13.3K, +1 Spider Arm 10.7K, +1 Power Fruit Evolution 8.5K, +1 Ghoul Evolution 1.9K.
- Giant-monster games have real players: Creatures of Sonaria 19.8K, Kaiju Alpha 5.5K, Kaiju Universe 864, TITAN BASH 764.
**What the vault already said, and I missed:** the owner called the round TOY Reef Shark "REALLY really bad" on 9 Nov. The fix then was a real, sleek animal with the cartoon only in the face, colours and size ([[2026-11-09 MEGA BITE - Art style guide]]). Same rule here.
**A key on the back of a 21 m monster is a joke, not a power fantasy.** It caps the epic end, which is what the older kids and the icon need.

**Back to family D, kept honest: a real little monster that grows into a city-stomper.**
- A real creature (not a toy): big stompy feet, horn crest, short tail, a face with a brow and a wide grin. Cute as a hatchling because it is small and big-eyed, never because it is a toy.
- **Natural armour in rigid shell segments** (like a beetle, a pangolin or an ankylosaur), growing a new material each evolution. This keeps the cheap, clean build (rigid plates, one rig) without the toy idea.
- Kept from 2b: the feet tell the form and leave its footprint; ROGUE ALERT instead of Rampage; no flame on the tail; IRONBOOT; the look-alike checklist.
- **The city is a bright cartoon city, not a toy city.** Things look small because YOU are huge. Nobody is inside the buildings (rating stays Mild).
**The scoreboard gets the missing row.** "Kids want to be this" (test: games with this kind of creature have players today): toy 2, golems 3, robots 3, real monster 5. With that row, the real monster wins. The numbers stay my judgement; the real tests are the first sheet, your eye, and what kids say.
**Rule for next time:** when asked to raise a score, first check the scoreboard measures what players want. Never add a feature to win a point.

## 3. The style in one line
**Bright, chunky "premium toy" cartoon, faceted low-poly with flat shading: cute when you are small, epic when you are big.** Same house rules as MEGA BITE: bold silhouettes, 2-4 flat colours per model plus one glowing accent, a clear face (two eyes, a brow, a mouth), nothing is a plain box, every form's outline can be told apart in black.

## 6. How much work, and the order
- **About 70 models** for launch: 9 forms, 27 prop variants, 5 rubble piles, 4 set pieces, 8 moving things, 12 dressing, 1 boss, 8 game objects. Props share one generator per kind, so the real work is about 30 designs.
- **First slice (Toy Town only):** forms 1-3, 6 prop kinds x 3 variants, 3 rubble piles, 1 set piece (the clock tower), 2 moving things. About 30 models.
- **Order, one at a time, each approved before the next:** (1) SPROUT, the cute proof every player sees first → (2) IRONBOOT, the epic proof, so we know the style holds at both ends → (3) the house x 3 variants + brick rubble, the first building → then the rest of the slice.
- Before the first sheet: a **reference study** for the picked family (the first shark failed without one): 6-8 references of top Roblox monster and kaiju games and toy-monster designs, and a list of what makes them read.
