3. CHEAPEST BUILD METHOD FOR PHONES

Recommended plan, phase A (fast, buildable by script in Studio):
- Build fillers from Parts: 1-3 per building (body box, roof box, optional shutter).
- Use SmoothPlastic and Texture instances from 3-4 shared 512 facade textures. Set StudsPerTileV to 4.9 so windows keep the right size on any box.
- Use Box collision.
- On non-colliding details, turn CanTouch, CanQuery and CastShadow off.
- No unions.
- Make each building or small block a Model with ModelStreamingMode Atomic, with StreamingEnabled on (Opportunistic stream-out, Improved model streaming).
- Hero buildings only get modelled window frames and shop fronts at street level.

Phase B, only if night driving matters:
- Make a mesh kit of 6-10 shell MeshIds, all UV-mapped to one 1024 atlas.
- Put one shared SurfaceAppearance on every shell, with an emissive window mask.
- A day/night script raises EmissiveStrength from 0 to 1-2 at night.
- Emissive masks need SurfaceAppearance, so they need MeshParts. Plain Parts with Textures cannot glow.
- The cheaper fallback (Neon behind cut-out windows) doubles the parts count. Never put a light in each window.

Budgets:
- Whole scene: about 500k triangles, about 500 draw calls, under 1.3 GB client memory, 60 FPS.
- Filler: 1-3 instances, 150-600 triangles.
- Hero: 15-30 instances, 1.5-3k triangles.
- Far skyline chunk: under 1k triangles.
- Filler textures 512 or smaller; at most one 1024 atlas; no normal or roughness maps on fillers.
- Check with Shift+F2 at phone size.

4. FAR-CITY BACKDROP

- Inner ring: 4-8 low-poly silhouette chunks, or scaled boxes with a tower texture, set to Persistent with CanCollide, CanQuery, CanTouch and CastShadow all off. Show tower clusters in the right directions: Hledan / M Tower (27 floors, 178 studs), Junction Square, and Myanmar Plaza.
- Outer ring: flat transparent PNG skyline cards.
- Tune Atmosphere Density so the join between the rings can't be seen.
- Suggestion (my own, not from the reports): add a gold Shwedagon spire card. It is the city's skyline signature, and its height must be checked first.

FLAGS (disagreements and unverified items)

- Myanmar Plaza tower: CTBUH says 26 floors, Wikipedia says 27 floors and 97 m, and the street report says 24-26 floors with 97 m unconfirmed. Tower 2's size is a guess. Lake Suites status after 2021 is unknown.
- Novotel: no height is published. "15 storeys" may or may not include the podium (I assumed the total is about 55 m). The two-tower layout is guessed. Sources give 351 or 366 rooms.
- Mall size: at true scale the mall podium is 256 studs, which is more than a third of a 704-stud span. Check the plot in Studio and compress to about 60% if needed. Also check that the map's MM Plaza spot is really on Kabar Aye Pagoda Rd.
- Optima: no photos or description of the building were found. If the map plan assumed a tower there, correct it.
- Kaba Aye: "34 m round the base" may mean circumference (about 17 studs across). Check against photos.
- Height caps: 6 floors within 1.6 km of Shwedagon and on listed roads such as Inya Myaing Rd. Keep towers off those streets.
- Not verified: all colours, lot widths, water tanks and AC details, and petrol brand colours (only SPC's red has a source).
- How Driving Empire and the other named games build their cities is undocumented.
- SLIM level of detail is still in beta, with reports of missing geometry and z-fighting. Test it before relying on it; StreamingMesh is the fallback.

## Building many buildings cheaply in Roblox (research agent)

CITY BUILDINGS FOR PHONES: RESEARCH FINDINGS (Nov 2026)

Note on the named games: Driving Empire, Southwest Florida, Greenville, Car Dealership Tycoon and Emergency Hamburg have not published how they build their cities. The DevForum threads describe one shared pattern for city games (Jailbreak, Mad City and similar): simple box or low-poly shells, windows painted into textures, a small set of modular pieces reused with different colours and sizes, streaming, and a fake skyline behind fog. Everything below comes from the sources listed, and my own recommendations are labelled.

1. PARTS vs MESHPARTS vs UNIONS
- Avoid unions. The 2024 build checklist says "Avoid unionOperations AS MUCH AS POSSIBLE". Unions create extra hidden triangles and collision wedges, have no Performance RenderFidelity option, and changing one from a script recalculates the whole object. https://devforum.roblox.com/t/checklist-for-building-optimization/3232350 , https://devforum.roblox.com/t/meshpart-usage-performance-optimizations/1319217
- Plain Parts are already stored on the device, so they cost no download. A simple box building made of one Part with Texture instances is fine.
- MeshParts that share a MeshId are downloaded once and the engine reuses the geometry. One forest example used only two MeshIds in total and got its variety from size, colour and rotation. Instancing (batching into fewer draw calls) needs the same MeshId, the same TextureID or SurfaceAppearance, and the same Material. A different texture ID breaks the batch. https://devforum.roblox.com/t/meshpart-usage-performance-optimizations/1319217 , https://devforum.roblox.com/t/does-roblox-use-mesh-instancing/399564
- Recommendation: build the city from 6 to 10 shell MeshIds (shophouse with 3, 5 and 8 storeys, a block of flats, a tower, a corner building). Make variety with scale, rotation and colour, not with new meshes.

2. TEXTURES AND WINDOWS
- Almost every city game imitates windows with textures. Modelled windows add up fast: one 10-storey building with 5 windows per side is 200 windows, or about 800 parts. https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-do-i-reduce-map-lag/668348
- Size: Roblox's texture specs recommend 512 for objects about 10x10 studs and 1024 for objects about 20x20 studs. Textures stream in at low quality first and get sharper only if the device has room. https://create.roblox.com/docs/art/modeling/texture-specifications
- Memory: by a community estimate, one 1024 texture uses about 3 MB of VRAM, so a full SurfaceAppearance with 4 maps uses about 12 MB. Keep normal and roughness maps off filler buildings. https://devforum.roblox.com/t/guide-to-texturesmaterials/1658926
- Checklist rules: Decal and Texture textures at 512 or smaller, reuse a few textures, use the SmoothPlastic material, use Box or Hull collision, set Box when CanCollide is off, turn off CanTouch, CanQuery and CastShadow where they aren't needed, turn off DoubleSided. https://devforum.roblox.com/t/checklist-for-building-optimization/3232350
- Recommendation: one shared 1024 facade atlas, with rows for several window and wall styles, UV-mapped by every shell mesh. All buildings then share one texture and keep batching. Model window frames and shop fronts only on hero buildings at street level.

3. LOD AND STREAMING
- Turn on StreamingEnabled, and set StreamOutBehavior to Opportunistic, ModelStreamingBehavior to Improved and StreamingIntegrityMode to PauseOutsideLoadedArea. Defaults are StreamingMinRadius 64 and StreamingTargetRadius 1024. Keep models small and modular, each with its own LOD setting. Use Persistent rarely, because it holds memory permanently. https://create.roblox.com/docs/workspace/streaming/techniques
- For Model.LevelOfDetail, SLIM is the newer option. It builds composite meshes and texture LODs in the cloud, removes hidden geometry, and shows the lighter version once a model streams out. It works only for static models with no Humanoid, Folders are ignored, and generating it needs Team Create. It has been in Client Beta on all platforms since Dec 2025 (announced 30 Oct 2025). There are reports of missing union geometry and z-fighting. https://devforum.roblox.com/t/client-beta-introducing-scalable-lightweight-interactive-models-slim/4034709
- StreamingMesh, the older option, draws a coarse untextured coloured mesh outside the streaming radius. https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/enums/ModelLevelOfDetail
- RenderFidelity: Automatic keeps full detail under about 250 studs and drops to about half the triangles between 250 and 500 studs. Performance also cuts triangles when FPS is low. Precise never cuts. Use Automatic on shells, Performance on far decoration and Precise only on landmarks. https://devforum.roblox.com/t/meshpart-usage-performance-optimizations/1319217
- Recommendation: make each building (or each small block) its own Model with ModelStreamingMode Atomic and LevelOfDetail SLIM. Use StreamingMesh only if SLIM turns out to be unreliable.

4. FAR-CITY BACKDROP
- The usual layering is low-poly skyscrapers near the edge, flat transparent PNG skyline layers behind them, then the skybox. Mid-distance buildings can be scaled cubes with a skyscraper texture. Atmosphere haze hides where the layers end. Don't put a SurfaceLight in every window, because it lags and disappears at distance. https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-can-i-make-a-skybox-that-works-with-tall-buildings-such-as-skyscrapers/336088 , https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-can-i-create-background-skyscrapers-like-this/3277133
- Recommendation: build a ring of 4 to 8 silhouette meshes around the map, each about 200 to 1,000 triangles and textured from the same atlas. Set them to Persistent so they always show, and set CanCollide, CanQuery, CanTouch and CastShadow to false. Add an outer ring of 2D skyline cards, and tune Atmosphere Density so the join can't be seen.

5. LIT WINDOWS AT NIGHT
- Emissive masks went live for published experiences on all clients on 12 Feb 2026. They are set on SurfaceAppearance with EmissiveMaskContent (a greyscale mask), EmissiveStrength and EmissiveTint, and scripts can change them at runtime. They glow like Neon but don't light the surroundings. https://devforum.roblox.com/t/emissive-masks-are-now-live-for-published-experiences/4357705
- Older fallback: a Neon part behind a texture that is transparent at the windows. Neon can't be used through a MeshPart's TextureID, so this needs two layered meshes. https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-can-i-create-background-skyscrapers-like-this/3277133
- Recommendation: one shared SurfaceAppearance (atlas plus a lit-window mask with a random pattern) on all shells. A day/night script moves EmissiveStrength from 0 to about 1 to 2. Because the SurfaceAppearance is shared, batching stays intact. Skip lights placed per window.

6. BUDGETS
- Whole scene: about 500k triangles, about 500 draw calls, client memory under 1.3 GB (so 2 GB phones work), 60 FPS. https://devforum.roblox.com/t/checklist-for-building-optimization/3232350
- A forum rule of thumb favours a building of about 700 triangles with a 1024 texture over high-poly meshes. https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-do-i-reduce-map-lag/668348
- My recommended per-building budget (not from a source):
  - Filler shell: 1 to 3 instances, 150 to 600 triangles, shared atlas, Box collision.
  - Hero building at street level: up to 15 to 30 instances and 1.5k to 3k triangles.
  - Far silhouette: under 1k triangles for a whole chunk of skyline.
  - Track triangles and draw calls with the Rendering Statistics overlay (Shift+F2) at phone size.
