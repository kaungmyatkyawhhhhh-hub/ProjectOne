# Research: what a "bacon hair" is, and is it best for thumbnails (3 Nov)
Owner: "is a bacon hair the best in thumbnails, also research what a bacon hair is in terms of roblox players".

## What it is
- "Bacon" / "Bacon Hair" = a player with Roblox's default starter hair, officially **Pal Hair** (brown layered hair that looks like bacon strips). Came with the 2014 avatar revamp; **still the default for new accounts in 2026**. Female default = "Bacon Girl" (also called "noodle hair").
- Before that the default was the **"noob"**: yellow head and arms, blue torso, green legs (2006-07 colours).
- Meaning for players: a bacon = a new player who hasn't customised yet. Used like "noob": often playful, sometimes mean (players who spent money look down on bacons). Some good players keep it on purpose; there is a "Bacon Hair Army" pride meme, and Roblox sells bacon plush / figures in its merch.
Sources: allthings.how (2026 guide), Sportskeeda (5 least favourite things), Roblox Fandom wiki "Noob", Beebom / Pocket Tactics / Exitlag guides.

## Is it best in thumbnails?
- Our art study (29 Oct, 32 top games + 9 gym games): in the simulator formula the "you" character is a **classic default avatar** (bacon hair or noob) so every kid reads it as "that's me" (Grow a Garden, Steal a Brainrot, Fish It!, gym games).
- No data says bacon beats other avatars on CTR (no study exists); it's the genre convention + the relatability logic.
- Best use: the SMALL / beginner / reacting character (shocked bacon looking up). It also carries the "new player" meaning = "you start here".
- Not for the hero/giant if he's meant to be aspirational... EXCEPT as a before/after story: the same bacon grows into the giant ("even a bacon becomes a legend") = progress + relatability in one picture.
- Test it: in Thumbnail Personalization, run one version with a bacon and one with a noob as the small player; keep the winner.

## Decision for our thumbnails
- GYM ARC: small player = bacon hair in the white tank top (also matches the game's start). Before->after thumbnail = the same bacon at 4'0" -> 5'8" -> 7'0". Giant keeps the bacon hair in that one only.
- Galaxy: the player on the island = bacon hair.
