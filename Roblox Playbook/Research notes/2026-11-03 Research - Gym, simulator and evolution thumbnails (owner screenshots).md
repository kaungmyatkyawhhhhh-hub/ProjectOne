## Rules confirmed
1. One hero, close, filling 50-70% of the frame; big readable face.
2. Bright saturated background (sky/cyan/purple), never a dark plain gym.
3. Progress shown as numbers or a growth row with arrows (+1, 7'0", LEVEL 100).
4. Seasonal/update frame when there is one.
5. Shirtless is the genre norm; we stay clothed (moderation) = also our difference.
