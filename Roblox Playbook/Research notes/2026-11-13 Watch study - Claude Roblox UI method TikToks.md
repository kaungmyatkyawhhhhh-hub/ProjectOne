The save rate is very high (A: 6.6% of views, B: 6.1%). People save "secret method" videos to try later.

## What they say (the same method; B copies A almost word for word)
1. Go to the top games (100K-1M players online: Steal a Brainrot and the like).
2. Screenshot every UI screen and element. A says: about 20 games.
3. (B only) Ask ChatGPT to make the screenshots' UI "transparent", meaning the pieces cut out from the game.
4. Give all the screenshots to Claude: "study what makes this UI good, then recreate it".
5. (B only) Connect Claude to Figma to build it.
6. The result is meant to look "handmade, like the viral games".

## How they're made
- **Hook (0-3 s):** a talking head straight to the camera, with the title on screen: "Ungatekeeping the Roblox Claude UI method". The first line is "the most gatekept thing ever, and I'm about to ungatekeep it".
- **A:** one shot, 0 cuts. A white caption box stays at the top the whole time. Moody low light.
- **B:** a talking head with 4 jump cuts (13.7 / 21.9 / 26 / 31.1 s). Small picture pop-ups over the head show each step (a game list, Roblox screenshots, the ChatGPT logo, UI icons, Figma). Word-by-word captions on the chest. Ends with a CTA: follow + Discord "free guides".
- **Neither one shows a finished UI.** The whole video is the promise of a secret.

## Compared with our method ([[Best UI method (skill draft)]])
- We already do steps 1-2: study the top 50 games' UIs, then write patterns + a decision. Then we go further: ChatGPT sheets in our own style, cut and 9-sliced, one UI kit, a phone QA loop.
- The only new piece: **more screenshots per game.** They take every screen (shop, inventory, popups, HUD) from ~20 games. Our step 1 takes a few gameplay pictures per game from Bing.
- Not for us: "make their UI transparent and recreate it" copies other games' art one to one. That is a copyright / look-alike risk, and the result looks like theirs, not ours.

## Video lesson (for our own TikToks)
A "secret method" title + one talking-head shot + an on-screen title box got 125K views on a 2.7K-follower account. The copy, with more edits, did 4x worse. The first post of the idea won, not the editing.
