# Icon and thumbnail testing (researched 30 Oct 2026)

- **Icons can NOT be A/B tested on Roblox.** It's still a DevForum feature request ("Icon A/B Testing", 2024; people still asking in July 2026). There's no Experiments menu for icons.
- **Thumbnails CAN be tested** with "Thumbnail personalization": Creator Hub → your experience → Configurations → Places → the place → Thumbnails → Home Page tab → Edit active thumbnails → Save changes → Start.
  - Needs at least 2 active thumbnails (2–5 recommended), under 3 MB, 1920×1080.
  - Results appear in the Thumbnail Performance table after a few hours.
  - Roblox advises testing new thumbnails with each major update, then leaving them until the next update.
- **To compare icons by hand:** swap the icon and compare qPTR (Analytics → Acquisition) over the same days of the week. Don't compare across a launch day or an explore surge, because traffic changes PTR (see the 133K-impression red barbell data).
- **GYM ARC decision (30 Oct):** use the barbell-overhead Hall of Fame icon (GYMARC_Icon_HallOfFame_final.png). It has the action, gold-vs-purple contrast, Halloween look and a covered chest.
