## The patterns
1. **One hero, big.** Character/face fills 50-70% of the frame. No collages.
2. **A visible emotion or transformation:** worried, angry, scared, or a half-and-half before/after on ONE face (Venom). Not a two-panel Noob→Pro (the gym search is full of those and they blend in).
3. **One glowing focal point:** neon rim light on muscles, glowing barbell, lava glow, jack-o-lantern. Dark backgrounds work only when this glow carries them.
4. **One huge number or nothing:** 999,999 POUNDS, 300 KG, X350, +1. Never sentences.
5. **Gym games sell the BODY + the WEIGHT together:** all 3 gym thumbnails show extreme muscles and 2 show the barbell/plates as the second hero.
6. **Polished render light** (rim light, shine on skin, depth), not an in-game screenshot.
7. **Seasonal skin wins attention** (Tryez Halloween) without changing the game.
8. Icons: the face/one object readable at small size; text only as one number.
