# Reference study: +1 Venom Evolution (11 Nov 2026)

Method: [[Reference study]]. For: [[TITAN ARC/2026-11-11 Stomp Evolution - Model brainstorm (families, model list, style)]] and the design doc in that folder.
**How it was studied:** the Roblox game page (video preview + 2 thumbnails) and about 20 still frames from 3 YouTube videos, opened muted in Chrome and sampled every 10-20%. **No sound was heard, so the SFX the owner praised are NOT studied here. Camera moves are read from single frames (speed lines, blur), not timed.** A proper pass needs a download or the owner playing it for 10 minutes while I watch.
**It uses a famous comic character (the symbiote). We never copy the character, the name or its look: only how the game is built.**

## The numbers (Roblox API + page, 11 Nov)
By Bottled Potential (verified). Made 5 Oct 2026, last update 10 Nov. About 20,600 playing, 56.5M visits in 5 weeks, 246K favourites, 96% likes (203K+ up / 6,562 down on the page), 16 players a server, Maturity: Mild (repeated mild violence). One video says there are 30 evolutions; a short shows "Evolve 28 → 29".

## 1. The loop
1. **Train** at sound stations (walls of speakers, boomboxes) on glowing floor rings. Zones sit side by side under huge floating signs: "x1 Venom", "x2 Venom · Join Group", "x3 Venom · 1 Evolve", "x6 Venom · 4 Evolves". You always see what the next zone gives and what it costs.
2. **Fight** in stage arenas: waves, then a named boss with a portrait health bar at the top ("The Choir 14M", "Crystal Brute 65M", "Hive Queen 291B"). A banner: "DEFEAT THE WAVE FIRST". "STAGE CLEARED!" pays Wins.
3. **Evolve:** needs max Level AND enough Wins. It resets your stats and gives a permanent multiplier. A window shows Current → Next with both silhouettes, names and multipliers ("Umbra 43Mx → Hive God 98Mx"), the two requirement bars, a rainbow "Skip Evolve 399 R$" button and a plain line "Need 53K more Wins".
4. **Worlds** (3 so far), a tower run to a glowing door with a "56/100" bar, a Halloween Tower entered with tickets that refill for free.
5. Extras: abilities bought with tokens and "mutated" 5 times (a Before → After stat window), a combo counter that RAISES income ("x22 COMBO +44% Venom"), potions (2x for 5/15/30 min), auras, quests, friend boost.

## 2. The first minute
- A plain avatar, and one line across the top: **"Train to Level 5 to grow your Venom head! (1/5)"**. The first goal is a body PART, five small steps away.
- The lobby shows a huge red spiky monster a few studs from spawn: the end goal, in view from second one.
- Parts grow on YOUR avatar step by step. The thumbnails sell exactly this: your own blocky avatar with a small creature on the shoulder → half covered → a giant monster that still has your hair on top.

## 3. Models and art
- **You stay you for a long time.** Early evolutions are your avatar with monster parts added. Later ones are full monsters with tendrils, wings, horns (a purple four-legged one, a red horned demon with a wall of teeth and a long tongue).
- Every form has a NAME and a number: "SIXTH FORM · GIANT", "Umbra", "Hive God", "OVERLORD".
- Enemies are simple dark blobs with one bright core colour; bosses are bigger with a portrait.
- World 1 is a bright pastel block city on a classic stud floor. Later worlds are violet with haze, neon rings and floating islands. Simple shapes; the light and glow do the work.
- Icon (owner's favourite): one face split down the middle, normal avatar / monster.

## 4. UI
- **One kit everywhere:** dark slate panels, a thin light outline, a violet header bar, a red X, green main buttons.
- **The theme breaks the frame:** a black tendril curls out of the corner of every window and every HUD tile. This one ornament makes the whole UI look hand-made.
- HUD: Wins counter top-left · a 2 x 3 grid of drawn icon tiles (Worlds, Evolve, Abilities, a gift with a timer or "CLAIM!", Backpack, Quests) with red "!" badges · the goal line top-centre · offers on the right (Starter Pack 39, "6x Venom ONLY 255", "x2 Wins OWNED") · bottom: Level bar, "Total Venom", "Evolve: +51K%", two ability slots (Q, and E locked behind a pass), and a strip of 4 buy-Venom buttons whose amounts grow with your stage (+25K → +1.1B, 25 to 999 R$) · "Friend Boost: +0% +".
- Before → After windows for upgrades, with the changed number in green.

## 5. VFX and camera (from stills)
- A reward number floats up from every training tick ("+800B"), big and white with an outline.
- Damage numbers in red, a red circle on the floor before a boss hit, the player glows gold on a power-up.
- **Radial speed lines** from the screen edges and a wider view while dashing or running the tower; blur at the edges on fast moves.
- Heavy bloom and coloured haze; neon rings on the floor mark every pad.
- **The evolve moment:** the screen clears, three stacked lines in the middle: "EVOLVED!" (white) / "OVERLORD" (red, huge) / "29Kx Venom" (green). Then a close-up of the new form's face filling the screen, then a **name card**: a slanted black band on a white-out screen with "SIXTH FORM", the name in red, and one line of story ("It outgrows its..."). A line then teaches the new move ("New move: Impale!").

## 6. Money
Passes: training chambers x8 79 R$ · x15 199 · x30 499 · x99 1,299; 2x Wins 99; Extra Ability Slot 199; VIP 399 (x1.25, tag, crown); 2x Mutagen 999. Plus Skip Evolve 399, the buy-Venom strip, Starter Pack 39. Several are far above our 449 ceiling, and paid multipliers up to x99 are pay-to-win by our rules.

## 7. Why it works (my read)
1. **The fantasy is "I transform", and it is always visible:** on the icon, in the first goal line, on your own avatar.
2. **Every screen shows the next thing and its price:** zone signs, the Evolve window, the Level bar.
3. **Every form is an event:** a name, a number, a story line, a face close-up, a new move.
4. **One hand-made UI ornament** carried across everything.
5. **Fighting gives the training a purpose** (Wins gate the evolve), and the combo makes fighting feed training.

## 9. Sound, measured (added 11 Nov, 03:20)
Full numbers: [[2026-11-11 Watch study - Titan Simulator clips + Venom sound measured (YouTube in the browser)]]. Measured in the browser, not heard.
- A sound starts about 4 times a second, all the time. Nothing is silent.
- Almost half the energy is under 250 Hz: thumps and bass.
- Big moments (boss kill, stage clear, big ability, cutscene) are 0.3 to 0.5 s full-range bursts, 13 to 23 dB above the bed, and rare: 5 in 500 s.
- A boss kill is a double hit: the kill burst, then a second sting about 1 s later with "STAGE CLEARED".
- Also seen: stages with waves and a clock, bosses named Scrap King, Warden Vale and Relay, "Level 35 MAX", paid "+10K to +2M Venom" buttons along the bottom.
