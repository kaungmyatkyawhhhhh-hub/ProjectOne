# Research: shark eat-and-evolve open-world idea (from Zev, 9 Nov)

Back to [[K09 Game ideas and trends]] · Related: [[2026-11-09 Research - Dead game method (shut-down multiplayer games as an idea library)]] (MONSTER GROW = same core loop on land)

## Zev's idea (owner's friend, Discord 8 Nov)
Hungry Shark-style, but an open world like Albion Online: a **safe zone** (no killing, slow levelling) and a **danger zone** (PvP, lots of EXP to evolve into the next shark). NPC fish that can kill you, and that you can only eat with the required shark. Your shark feels powerful. Sea bombs.

**What it means:** kids want this (DEVOUR got to 1.7K CCU and 4.5M visits in ~4 months), but the plain version already exists and is fading (103 CCU). K09 rule: a late clone rarely wins; it needs theme + one twist.

## Verdict
Good theme with proven demand. Build it only with a real twist, and not before Galaxy is fixed.
**The twist that wins: "bank your EXP" (extraction).** Danger-zone EXP is carried, not kept. Swim back to the safe reef to bank it. Get eaten in the danger zone and you drop what you were carrying (never your shark or your level). This is Albion's risk zone made kid-safe, and it's the "steal and run back" tension from the 20-trending-games research.

## Design notes
- Zones: Safe Reef (no PvP, x1 EXP) → Open Sea (NPC predators, x2) → Red Trench (PvP on, x4, the rare food; not "Blood": keeps the maturity rating Mild).
- Food tiers: a fish shows a lock until your shark is big enough (Zev's point 9), so the goal is always visible.
- NPC predators fill empty servers (dead-game lesson: empty queues kill games).
- Sea bombs = the comeback tool: they hurt big sharks most, so small players can lure a bully into a mine field (funny chaos, also from the dead-game research).
- Power must be SEEN (K02): size, scars, a crown on the top shark in the server.
- Never call it Hungry Shark or copy its art (Ubisoft's game + the copycat flag, K01). Original name.
- Phone-first swim controls; no paid random packs (our rule).

## Where it sits
Same core loop as MONSTER GROW, on the ocean instead of land. Treat them as one idea: **MONSTER GROW: OCEAN**. Next-game queue: Galaxy first, then the owner picks between this and +1 SHRINK.

## Name (decided 9 Nov)
**MEGA BITE**, Roblox title `MEGA BITE 🦈 Eat & Evolve`. (Owner rejected APEX SHARK as "too nerdy".)
- Two easy words a kid says out loud; it names the action (bite) and the end goal (the Megalodon is the last shark).
- Keeps the search words: shark (emoji + description), eat, evolve.
- Backups: SHARK CHOMP, CHOMP CHOMP SHARK.
- Not "Shark Evolution" (Hungry Shark Evolution, Ubisoft; crowded). No "blood"/"kill" (maturity Mild, K01).
- Before locking: search the name in Roblox, check no big game uses it.
- Update tag: `MEGA BITE [MEGALODON] 🦈 Eat & Evolve`.

## Concept v1 (9 Nov)
**One line:** start as a tiny fish, eat your way up the food chain and become the MEGALODON. The best food is in the dangerous water, and you only keep what you bring home.
- **Loop:** swim out → eat → carry EXP → swim back to the reef to bank it → evolve → go deeper.
- **Evolution ladder (each a new look, size and bite):** Minnow → Piranha → Barracuda → Reef Shark → Hammerhead → Great White → Megalodon. Later updates: Mosasaur, Kraken-hunter tiers.
- **Zones:** Safe Reef (no PvP, x1, spawn, bank) · Open Sea (NPC predators, x2) · Red Trench (PvP on, x4, rare glowing food, the server's top shark gets a crown).
- **Carried EXP:** a bag bar on the HUD; eaten = you drop the carried EXP as food others can eat; your shark and level are never lost.
- **Food locks:** every fish shows its tier; too small = a lock icon + "Evolve to Hammerhead".
- **NPC predators:** fill empty servers; big NPC sharks patrol the Open Sea; a boss (giant squid) spawns every ~15 min, a server goal.
- **Sea bombs:** mines in the Open Sea and Trench; they hurt big sharks most, so small players can lure bullies. Later: bombs you pick up and drop.
- **Show-off:** size, scars, crown, overhead tier tag; the biggest shark in the server is visible on a server board.
- **Come-back:** offline growth in the reef, daily streak, weekly event shark (Saturday), a monthly party skin.
- **Money (≤449 R$, no paid random items):** x2 EXP, bigger bag (carry more), VIP, skins/trails. Nothing that protects you in the Red Trench (no paid power in PvP, K08 rule 7).
- **First minute:** spawn in the reef, food right in front, first evolve within ~60 s, the Trench visible and glowing in the distance.

## Abilities v1 (owner 9 Nov: "OMG I WANT THIS ABILITY THAT THE BIG SHARKS HAVE")
Built on [[2026-10-29 GYM ARC Abilities - research]] (SPTS: every unlock = a visible new desire; powers must have a USE in the world; never sold; one button on phones; server checks).

### One signature ability per evolution
| Evolution | Ability | What it does | World use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Minnow | DART | quick dodge burst (everyone keeps it) | escape |
| Piranha | FRENZY | 3 fast bites; stronger with your school (friends nearby) | swarm big food together |
| Barracuda | TORPEDO | long charge that smashes coral walls | shortcuts, secret caves |
| Reef Shark | SHARK SENSE | see food and sharks through walls as glowing trails for 8 s | find rare glowing food |
| Hammerhead | HAMMER RAM | headbutt that knocks things away | crack shipwrecks and treasure chests |
| Great White | BREACH | leap out of the water and slam down: splash shockwave, seen from far | the show-off moment; reach surface food |
| Megalodon | TIDAL ROAR | roar + whirlpool that pulls food in and shakes smaller fish | the flex; boss fights |

### Mutations (second track, rare, earned only)
From rare Red Trench food and the boss: ELECTRIC (eel zap), INK CLOUD (escape), ANGLER GLOW (lure food), SPIKE SKIN (biters take a hit). Equip 1, shown as a glow + tag over your shark. Rare reveal ("1 in 500"), never sold.

### How the desire is built
1. **Seen:** big readable VFX + sound, and the ability name pops in the world ("BREACH!") for everyone nearby.
2. **Felt:** eaten by an ability → a 2 s replay card: "Eaten by a Great White's BREACH · unlocks at Great White · you are 3 evolves away".
3. **Tried:** a free 10 s try of any locked ability at the reef shrine.
4. **Chased:** the next ability always on the HUD with a % bar.

### Rules
- Abilities hit other players only in the Red Trench; elsewhere they affect NPCs, food and the world. Never a one-shot.
- Small fish always have DART + mutations as counterplay.
- Phones: 2 buttons (DART + your ability). VFX pooled and capped. Server checks every use.
- Abilities and mutations are never sold. Sell colour variants (golden BREACH splash, red TIDAL ROAR) only.

## Additions v1 (owner 9 Nov: "what should we do more")
From K02 (why players keep playing), K09 trend patterns and the dead-game lessons. In priority order:
1. **After Megalodon (the endless loop; "players who finish leave"):** FOSSILIZE = rebirth. Back to Minnow with a permanent bonus (+50% EXP, then more each time), a fossil badge on your shark, and each Fossil tier opens a new line: Prehistoric (Dunkleosteus, Mosasaur, Livyatan). Run 2 about 2x faster than run 1.
2. **SIZE = the one big number:** "You are 18.4 m". Grows with every bite, shown over your shark and on the server board. Biggest in the server wears the crown.
3. **FISH INDEX:** every fish, NPC shark, boss and shiny you've eaten, with % complete per zone. A reward per page (a skin or a title). Fish that max out leave the list.
4. **SHINY FISH:** rare golden versions ("1 in 2,500") with a server-wide alert: "A GOLDEN TUNA appeared in the Open Sea!" Everyone races to it.
5. **SCHOOLS (crews):** swim with up to 3 friends for +10% EXP each, shared FRENZY bites and a shared bank. Co-play days help Home recommendations (K01).
6. **SERVER EVENTS:** the squid boss every ~15 min, FEEDING FRENZY (5 min of x2 food spawns) every hour, a Saturday event shark with a limited skin.
7. **YOUR LAIR:** a cave in the reef that shows trophies (boss teeth, shiny fish skeletons, Fossil tiers). Friends can visit it.
8. **REMORA PET:** a little fish that follows you and picks up dropped food. Levels up with you (like Galaxy's LUMI).
Launch with 1-4 + the squid boss. Add 5-8 in the weekly updates.

Dislikes come from broken, unfair or confusing games, not from missing features (Launch gate; DevForum: spawn camping, punishing deaths, unclear goals, bugs, looking like another game).

### A. Anti-dislike (all must be in at release)
- PvP ONLY in the Red Trench; spawn in the Safe Reef; a 10 s shield after every respawn and when leaving the reef.
- Getting eaten only drops carried EXP (never level or shark). A big shark can't eat the same player again for 60 s (no camping).
- 24-player servers (not 60), pooled NPC fish, streaming on: smooth on cheap phones.
- First evolve within ~60 s; the next evolve % bar and food locks always visible.
- Nothing bought helps in a fight.
- Launch gate passed: fresh-account phone test, 2-device test, impatient kid sim, phone UI audit.
- Like prompt (no reward) after the first big moment: the first Reef Shark evolve or the first squid kill.
- Original name, art and sharks' look (no Hungry Shark look-alike).

### B. Content at release
7 evolutions + their 7 abilities (the ladder AND the desire), SIZE number + server crown, FISH INDEX, SHINY FISH alerts, the squid boss, FOSSILIZE tier 1 (rebirth + permanent bonus).

### C. Weekly updates after release
Week 1 mutations · week 2 schools (crews) · week 3 Feeding Frenzy + Saturday event shark · week 4 lair · then the remora pet and the prehistoric line (Fossil 2+).
