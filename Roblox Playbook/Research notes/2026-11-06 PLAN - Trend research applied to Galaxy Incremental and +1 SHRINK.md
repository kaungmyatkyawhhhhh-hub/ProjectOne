## Galaxy Incremental (launch 5 Dec): add before alpha
Owner's known problem: repetitive collecting feels boring.

1. **One visible "how far" number.** A galaxy distance bar at the top of the screen ("Distance: 2.4 light-years"). Each new island/sector pushes it.
   - Why: Depth's hook, and the "one big number" pattern.
2. **Something new every 2-5 min.** Buyable doors/portals that open a new island room, each with its own mini-system and currency.
   - Players' curiosity was the fun in every Depth video ("what is this?"), not the grind.
3. **Sol's-RNG style rare reveal** for rare stars/relics: a full-screen flash, "1 in 2,400,000", and stacked variant words (SHINY / GIGANTIC / VOID).
   - Seen in Throw Food For Animals, Break and Steal an Egg and Depth shinies.
4. **Max-count collect lists.** Each item maxes, then leaves the pool so rarer ones come up. Show % complete per island in the teleport menu.
   - This fixes "boring collecting": every pickup moves a bar.
5. **Workers/automation as rewards.** Manual first, then buy auto-collect, then "keeps on rebirth". It's the late-game dopamine in Depth, Sushi and Clean the Moon.
   - No manual hauling rooms after the first visit (Depth's most-hated room).
6. **Sector boss → key → rebirth room.** Make the boss's damage depend on your income, so after a rebirth players can one-shot old bosses (power fantasy).
7. **Retention kit:**
   - offline earnings popup (free claim; a Robux x2 is fine);
   - daily rewards calendar;
   - quests panel;
   - codes;
   - a Roblox Event for every weekly update;
   - lobby Free Rewards board (like / join group → claim).
8. **Title states the rule.** "Galaxy Incremental: how far can you go?" plus a thumbnail showing a tiny player and a huge galaxy number.
- Not copying: paid random packs, countdown starter packs, 8 h pay-only timers.

## Next game: +1 SHRINK (after Galaxy launch)
- Proof it's clickable:
  - "obby but you get smaller every stage": 11K CCU with zero economy.
  - "Every time I win I get SMALLER": 123K views in 7 h.
- **Formula:** Depth structure + shrink fantasy.
  - Buy each "shrink step"; the size counter goes 1.7 m → nm.
  - Layers: Backyard → Ant Colony → Pond Drop → Cells → Atoms → Quantum. Normal objects become giant worlds.
  - Side rooms per layer (catch bugs/microbes/particles with rarity lists and max counts).
  - RNG titles you keep on rebirth.
  - A boss per layer (Ant Queen, Tardigrade, Virus, Nucleus), whose key opens the rebirth room.
  - Automations as late rewards.
- **Add from trends:**
  - Bug Evolution-style trait card pick at milestones (3 cards, colour-coded);
  - an index book with %;
  - rare reveals;
  - a new layer every week with a Roblox Event.
- **Title:** "+1 SHRINK" or "Shrink Incremental: how small can you get?". Thumbnail: tiny bacon hair on a giant ant/cell.
- **Build fast:** the trend games were made quickly. Target a 2-3 week first version once Galaxy is live.
