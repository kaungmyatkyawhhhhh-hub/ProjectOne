## The pattern (what makes them feel expensive)
1. **One colour + one symbol per aura.** Every cutscene is ONE hue family (gold, teal, magenta, red, monochrome) and ONE central symbol: a sigil, star, eye, clock, zodiac wheel, compass rings, snowflake, crown, leaf bloom. Background = the same hue as a nebula / gradient / light shafts.
2. **Five beats, almost always:**
   1. Contrast opener: 1–6 s of silence, a low bass drone, or darkness (Nyctophobia 15 s, Archangel 8 s, Prophecy 6 s).
   2. Build: the symbol slowly grows, rotates or approaches; thin rings orbit; brightness rises steadily.
   3. A held breath right before the drop: a BLACK frame and/or one word or line ("Wake Up.", "POWER", lore text) with silence.
   4. Climax at ~70–85 % of the length: white-out flash + a big ray starburst + the loudest hit, all on the same frame.
   5. Short tail: cut back to the player wearing the aura (1–2 s), server message for globals.
3. **Length scales with rarity:** most 100M–800M auras are 9–17 s; the 1B+ ones run 16–38 s with custom music.
4. **Music drives the edit.** Cuts land on beats. The tempo matches the theme: horror 41–48 BPM with silence; holy = a sustained rising choir; energy 105–180 BPM with a cut every beat. The bass builds before the drop. The biggest hit is ALWAYS synced to the white flash.
5. **Text as rhythm:** single words or lore lines appear one at a time on beats; a title card with the name (+ rarity) at the climax.
6. **Mostly screen-space:** layered 2D art in front of the camera (rotating sigil images, rings, star glints, speed lines, panels), not big 3D scenes. That's why they look clean and run on phones.
7. **Escalation:** rarer = longer, more layers, unique music, global announcement.
