# Research: 20 trending Roblox games, gameplay videos watched (6 Nov 2026)

Owner: "watch youtube videos of 20 popular roblox games trending rn and do 1 frame per 5 seconds for every video" + "watch video, scan it and remove the useless screenshots".
Method: games picked from Roblox explore-api (Top Trending / Up-and-Coming / Top Playing Now, 6 Nov). One gameplay video per game, sampled every 5 s (~4,000 frames in total), near-duplicate frames removed automatically (colour-layout match against every kept frame), ~700 kept frames looked at.
Already researched separately: [[2026-11-06 Research - Depth Incremental (10 videos watched)]].

## Patterns across the 20 (what's working right now)
1. **The title IS the game** - a one-line rule or goal: "Don't Splash", "Can You Pass A Driving Test?", "30 Floors Up", "1M Clicks To Escape", "Sell a Million Sushi", "obby but you get smaller every stage", "Clean the Moon", "Survive Your Ideas". Kids know what to do before they join.
2. **One big number to chase** (1,000,000 clicks, 1M sushi, 30 floors, stage 99, depth) - shown huge on screen.
3. **Collect-and-luck reveals everywhere**: Sol's-RNG style full-screen "1 in 8,162,449" with stacked variant words (RAINBOW / DIAMOND / GIGANTIC / HEAVENLY / SPECTRAL), Index books (36/913).
4. **Steal/run-back tension**: Steal An Egg, Break and Steal an Egg - grab, red "RUN!" banner, others can steal (the #1 game on Roblox at 572K).
5. **+1 / evolve formula still printing**: Spider Arm, Anime Eyes, Bug Evolution (roguelite trait cards on level-up + evolve into new species).
6. **Realistic first-person is rising** (Criminal Simulator, 30 Floors Up, DONT SPLASH, Practical Basketball, Night Rider) - real lighting, almost no UI.
7. **Physical-world tycoon with workers that automate** (Sushi, Clean the Moon) - start manual, hire/buy vehicles, numbers explode.
8. **News/meme timing** (Tesla Roadster Chase = real Roadster hype, 15K in days).
9. **Story / endings** (Say Cheese - 2D hand-drawn, 14 endings, 2.1M-view "all endings" video) and **player-written chaos** (Survive Your Ideas: players type the disaster).
10. **Built fast**: YouTubers say +1 Anime Eyes was "vibecoded with Claude" - speed to market wins; quality bar for simple games is low, the IDEA + title + thumbnail carry it.
11. Common retention kit: offline earnings popup (free vs x5 Robux), daily rewards calendar, quests panel, starter pack popup, codes, index/collection %, admin-abuse events (owner joins and rains rare items).

- Look: bright flat grass, fences, huge low-poly creatures; top-right leaderboard; yellow captions.
