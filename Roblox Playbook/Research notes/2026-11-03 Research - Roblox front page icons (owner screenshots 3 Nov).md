# What the Roblox front page looks like (owner's screenshots, 3 Nov 2026)
Owner sent 12 screenshots of the Charts/Home rows (Top Playing, Trending in every genre, Top Earning, Up-and-Coming...) after our HEIGHT render "just dont look like those top 100 game thumbnails".

## Two families
1. Brand/anime art (Blox Fruits, Anime Adventures, TDS, Attack on Titan, Grand Piece): painted illustration. Not our style.
2. **Roblox-avatar simulator style** (Steal An Egg 614K, DONT SPLASH 100K, +1 Spider Arm, +1 Venom Evolution, Throw Food For Animals, Fish It!, Ragdoll Reality, Fake an Egg, Container RNG, Riot City, Superhero Arena, Fart Together, Drive a Cart, Hot Potato, Paint and Hide, STOP! Word Duel). This is OUR target.

## What the simulator-style ones all do (and our HEIGHT render doesn't)
- **ACTION, mid-motion**: running, falling, flying, being thrown, splashing, lifting something huge. Limbs flung, diagonal body. Never a T-pose or a standing pair.
- **The face is the hook**: big exaggerated panic / shock / evil grin, often painted bigger than the real Roblox face; close crop, face 15-30% of the image.
- **Classic blocky R6 bacon** (orange-brown hair, black shirt, blue arms/legs), smooth plastic, soft clean shading: no lumpy meshes, no textures noise.
- **One object + one reaction**: an egg/UFO, a wave, spider arms, a dinosaur, a potato bomb. The object is huge, colourful, simple.
- **Camera close + wide angle + dutch tilt**, subject breaks the frame edges.
- **Bright saturated simple backgrounds**: cyan sky, green grass, pink/blue blocks; or a blurred game scene.
- **Painted VFX**: water splash, explosion, motion lines, glow, "+1" pops. Clearly a paint-over after the 3D render.
- **Text tiny or none** (a few words max: "+1", "NEWS", a logo).
- Split before/after: +1 Venom Evolution = half bacon face / half monster face, close-up. Direct template for GYM ARC.

## For GYM ARC (decided)
- Concept A "SPLIT": half bacon face (scared, sweat) / half 7 ft giant face (grin), close-up, split by a jagged gold energy line. Before -> after in one face (like +1 Venom Evolution).

- Concept C "BARBELL CRUSH": a giant barbell slamming down, the bacon running away, dust explosion.
