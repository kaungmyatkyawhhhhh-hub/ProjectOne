# Roblox Game Passes and Developer Products: Catalogue Guide for a Solo Kids' Simulator Developer (2025-2026)

Research date: 2026-10-28. This builds on `Making Roblox games better/monetization.md`, which covers revenue shares, holds, Managed/Regional Pricing, odds disclosure and deceptive-practice rules. Those topics are not repeated here.

**Research limits.** In this session most fandom wikis, roblox.com store tabs and the Roblox APIs returned errors or bot blocks: the proxy refused a 403 CONNECT to roblox.com APIs, and Muscle Legends, Bee Swarm, Grow a Garden, Sonic Speed and Unboxing wiki pages all failed. Only Pet Simulator 99 has a full, verified price list. For the other named games, prices are **gaps**, not guesses.

## 1. Typical simulator catalogue and real price points

### Takeaway
Pet Simulator 99 is the only top simulator whose full pass list and prices were verified. It sells 14 permanent passes, from 175 R$ (Auto Farm) to 3,250 R$ (Huge Hunter), about 16k R$ in total.

The PS99 catalogue follows a clear template:
- **Cheap convenience (about 175–400):** auto farm, auto tap, VIP, more equipped pets.
- **Mid-tier luck and capacity (about 275–800):** Lucky, Ultra Lucky, more hatch slots.
- **Expensive "whale" passes (1,200–3,250):** magic eggs, super drops, huge hunter.

Grow a Garden's premium season pass costs 849 R$ (secondary source).

### Cited Findings

**Roblox platform rules that shape a catalogue**
- Promoted on-sale passes must be priced **above 49 and below 801 Robux**, and they cannot grant paid random items. — [Creator Hub: Passes](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/game-passes)
- Developer products can be priced from 1 to 1 billion Robux. — [Creator Hub: Developer products](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-products)
- The docs recommend creating "a unique pass for the Buy Robux page" "for best results". — [Creator Hub: Passes](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/game-passes)
- **Cross-game pass and developer-product sales are disabled from 2026-05-30.** Items are sold only in their own game, or outside it on Roblox surfaces when Listed. — [Passes](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/game-passes); [Developer products](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-products)

### Inferences
**A starter catalogue for a gym/running simulator, modelled on PS99's tiers**

Use about 6–8 passes at launch:
- **Convenience:** Auto Train / Auto Run (about 149–199).
- **Currency multiplier:** 2x Strength / 2x Coins (about 199–299).
- **Capacity:** +Pet/Trainer Slots (about 249–399).
- **VIP:** chat tag, VIP area, daily chest (about 299–449).
- **Luck:** Lucky Eggs (about 249–299).
- **Later premium tier:** Ultra Luck / 2x Luck (about 699–799).
- **Optional:** one "whale" pass at 1,000+.

Developer products (repeatable):
- currency packs in 3–5 sizes;
- 15–30 minute boost potions;
- rebirth or stage skips;
- a one-time starter pack.

These ranges are an extrapolation from PS99 plus the 49–801 promotion window, not verified medians.

- PS99's cheapest passes are the ones guides call "worth it". Low-priced convenience passes are the first purchase that converts a player into a buyer.
- PS99's "Super Drops! … exact effect not displayed" is an example **not** to copy, given Roblox's rule that luck boosts must state their numeric effect (see the earlier notes).

### Gaps
- No verified current pass or product prices for Muscle Legends, Strongman Simulator, Gym League, Bee Swarm Simulator, Race Clicker, Grass Cutting Incremental or the "+1 every second" games. Their wiki pages and roblox.com store tabs could not be retrieved (client errors, bot protection, store not in static HTML). Check them manually on each game's Store tab in a browser.
- Grow a Garden's passes and Robux shop items (seed/gear restocks, Sheckles packs, gifts) were not retrieved.
- No source with real developer-product (consumable) price ladders for these games.

## 2. Price psychology on Roblox

### Takeaway
Kids buy Robux in fixed bundles: 400 / 800 / 1,700 / 4,500 / 10,000 R$, at $4.99 / $9.99 / $19.99 / $49.99 / $99.99. Premium members get +10%. Price ladders should fit inside these bundles, so a player with a single 400 R$ purchase can afford something meaningful and has a reason to come back.

### Cited Findings
- **Bundles:**

  | USD | Robux | With Premium (+10%) |
  |---|---|---|
  | $4.99 | 400 | 440 |
  | $9.99 | 800 | 880 |
  | $19.99 | 1,700 | 1,870 |
  | $49.99 | 4,500 | 4,950 |
  | $99.99 | 10,000 | 11,000 |

  US prices were unchanged through 2026 as of 2026-05-14. Third-party source, **medium reliability**. — [RoWatcher, 2026-05-14](https://rowatcher.com/news/robux-prices-bundles-2026-guide)
- **Regional pricing:** regional and test prices mean the price a player sees can differ from your baseline. Display prices from `GetProductInfo`, not hard-coded numbers; on the client when price optimization is on. — [Passes](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/game-passes); [Developer products](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-products)
- **Recommendations:** `RecommendTopProductsAsync`/`RankProductsAsync` returns up to 50 items a user is most likely to buy, but needs at least one purchase in the last 28 days. Load it once at join because of rate limits. — [Developer products](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-products)
- **PS99's price ladder as observed:** 175, 275, 350, 375, 400, 625, 800, 1,200, 1,499, 1,600, 2,400, 3,250. Mostly round 25s, with one charm price (1,499). — [Pet Simulator Wiki](https://pet-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Gamepasses_(Pet_Simulator_99))
- **Season passes cluster at about 800–1,000 R$:** Adopt Me 800, Grow a Garden 849, Blox Fruits 1,000. Secondary source. — [OfZenAndComputing](https://www.ofzenandcomputing.com/grow-a-garden-first-battlepass-review/)

### Inferences
- **"Fits a bundle" pricing:** 399 or less fits the $4.99 bundle and 799 or less fits the $9.99 bundle.
  - PS99's 400 VIP equals one 400 bundle exactly. Its 800 Ultra Lucky equals one 800 bundle. Its 375 +15 Pets leaves 25 R$ over.
  - A good pattern: a first pass under 400, and combinations that use up a bundle (e.g. 149 + 249 = 398).
  - Leftover Robux pulls players toward cheap consumables (25–99 R$ products).
- **Anchoring and decoys:**
  - An expensive tier (Ultra Lucky 800) next to a cheaper one (Lucky 275) makes the cheaper tier look reasonable.
  - For currency packs, use 4–5 sizes with growing bonus % and a "Best Value" tag on a mid-to-large pack.
  - Bonus percentages must be real. The earlier notes cover the rule against permanent fake discounts.
- **Age:** under-13 players spend from gifted or parent-bought 400/800 bundles, so most revenue in a kids' game should come from items priced 25–400. This is an inference; no age-tier spending data was found.

### Gaps
- No Roblox or DevForum data on conversion by price point, and no measured effect of decoy pricing or first-purchase offers on Roblox.
- Mobile vs web Robux bundle differences were not verified.
- No public data on spending by player age.

## 3. Naming and descriptions

### Takeaway
Use benefit-first names that state the exact effect: "2x Strength", "Auto Train", "+15 Pets". Descriptions should give exact numbers, how the item stacks, and its limits. PS99's best names ("+15 Pets!", "Auto Farm!") say what you get. Its worst ("Super Drops!" with no stated effect) are vague.

### Cited Findings
- A pass's name and description are entered at creation. A **category** is chosen that "organizes items in your in-game Shop". — [Passes](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/game-passes); [Developer products](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-products)
- **PS99 examples:**
  - Names that state the effect: "+15 Pets!", "Auto Farm!", "Daycare Slots!", "Double Stars".
  - Descriptions that state limits: "Huge pets are still rare and not guaranteed"; "Starting a minigame disables it".
  - A counter-example: "Super Drops!" with the "exact effect not displayed". — [Pet Simulator Wiki](https://pet-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Gamepasses_(Pet_Simulator_99))

### Inferences
- **Template:** `[Multiplier/Number] [Thing]`, e.g. "2x Coins", "+3 Pet Slots", "Auto Lift", "VIP".
- **Description structure:**
  1. The effect, with numbers.
  2. Stacking rules.
  3. Duration: "Permanent" or "Lasts 15 minutes".
  4. Exclusions.
- Avoid "OP", "Best", or "guaranteed" unless literally true.
- For luck items, state the numeric change, e.g. "+200% egg luck". This also satisfies the odds-disclosure rule.

### Gaps
- Character limits for pass/product names and descriptions were not stated on the docs pages fetched.

## 4. Icons

### Takeaway
- **Format:** both passes and developer products take a 512×512 (max) .png, .jpg or .bmp.
- **Crop:** the icon is shown **cropped to a circle**, so keep key content inside the circle.
- **Custom icons:** Roblox recommends a custom icon on every item so players recognise it on external surfaces (homepage, search, post-purchase page, Buy Robux page).

### Cited Findings
- Pass icon: no larger than 512×512; must not include "important details outside of its circular boundaries"; .jpg, .png or .bmp. — [Passes](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/game-passes)
- Developer-product icons have the same 512×512 and circular-boundary rule, and Roblox recommends "adding a custom icon to each developer product so players can recognize it on external surfaces". — [Developer products](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-products)
- Listed passes can appear on the Roblox homepage, in search results and on the post-purchase page. — [Passes](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/game-passes)

### Inferences
- **Design pattern:** one big symbol (dumbbell, shoe, clover, crown) plus a bold number ("2x", "+3") in the centre circle. Use a thick outline and high contrast so the icon reads at small purchase-prompt sizes.
- **Colour-code by category** and keep the style consistent:
  - green = currency;
  - purple = luck;
  - gold = VIP;
  - blue = auto.
- Uploaded icons go through asset moderation. For group-owned games, create the items under the group's experience.

### Gaps
- Exact display sizes in the purchase prompt and the Store tab were not documented on the pages fetched.
- No DevForum/RDC icon A/B data was found. The DevForum icon-feedback threads found are old (2019).

## 5. In-game store presentation and purchase flow

### Takeaway
- **Passes:**
  - Check `UserOwnsGamePassAsync` on join.
  - Prompt with `PromptGamePassPurchase` only when the player doesn't own the pass.
  - Grant on the server via `PromptGamePassPurchaseFinished`.
  - Show an "Owned" state.
- **Developer products:**
  - Prompt with `PromptProductPurchase` on a player action.
  - Grant **only** in `ProcessReceipt`, never in `PromptProductPurchaseFinished`.
- **Prices:** read them live from `GetProductInfo` or `GetDeveloperProductsAsync`.

### Cited Findings
- `GetProductInfo(..., Enum.InfoType.GamePass)` returns name and price. Prompt with `PromptGamePassPurchase` if the user doesn't own the pass. Handle `PromptGamePassPurchaseFinished` and grant privileges on the server. Ownership is checked with `UserOwnsGamePassAsync` on `PlayerAdded`. — [Passes](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/game-passes)
- "You must manually assign pass privileges." Roblox doesn't keep per-user pass purchase history for you, so store it yourself. — [Passes](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/game-passes)
- **Developer products:**
  - Call `PromptProductPurchase` on user actions (button press, NPC vendor).
  - Validate the receipt's user ID, product ID and status.
  - Handlers must return true.
  - "Do not use the PromptProductPurchaseFinished event to process purchases."
  - `GetDeveloperProductsAsync` returns a Pages object listing products.
  - Listed products sold externally need a correct ProcessReceipt. — [Developer products](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-products)
- **Personalised offers:** `RecommendTopProductsAsync` can surface personalised "featured" offers; call it inside `task.spawn` so it doesn't block. — [Developer products](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-products)

### Inferences
- **Shop layout:**
  - Tabs: Featured / Passes / Boosts / Currency.
  - Featured shows the starter pack and the recommended items.
  - Grey out owned passes with an "Owned ✓" label.
  - Put a "Best Value" tag on one currency pack.
- **Gifting:** Roblox has no built-in way to gift a pass to a friend. Games usually sell a separate developer product (e.g. "Gift: 2x Coins"), then record the gift for the target UserId in a DataStore and grant it when they join. This is an inference; no official doc on gifting was found.

### Gaps
- No official Roblox documentation on gifting passes was found. Search results were spam or SEO pages; a third-party "Gamepass Buying and Gifting System" asset exists on BuiltByBit and was not evaluated.
- Native Roblox "Shop" category UI behaviour (how categories render in-game) was not described in detail.

## 6. Common mistakes

### Takeaway
The documented pitfalls:
- hard-coded prices, which break under regional and optimised pricing;
- granting products outside ProcessReceipt;
- vague effects;
- missing icons;
- stacked expensive "whale" passes that read as pay-to-win.

A pass total like PS99's 15,974 R$ is a scale a solo developer should not copy at launch.

### Cited Findings
- **Live prices:** prices should be fetched live, on the client under price optimization. — [Passes](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/game-passes)
- **Grant point:** never grant developer products from `PromptProductPurchaseFinished`. — [Developer products](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-products)
- **Scale:** PS99's 14 passes total 15,974 R$. Several high-end passes (Huge Hunter 3,250, Super Drops 2,400) do not guarantee results. — [Pet Simulator Wiki](https://pet-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Gamepasses_(Pet_Simulator_99))
- **Old DevForum threads:** threads on choosing passes (e.g. 2019) contain little concrete advice. — [DevForum: What gamepasses should I implement? (2019)](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/what-gamepasses-should-i-implement/374936)

### Inferences
- **Launch catalogue:** launch with about 6–8 passes and 4–8 products, adding more with updates.
- **Fairness:** every paid advantage should be earnable or roughly matched by free play, which limits pay-to-win anger.
- **Names:** keep them unambiguous ("2x Coins", not "Money Boost Pro").

### Gaps
- No verified DevForum or RDC postmortem quantifying the effects of too many passes, overpricing or confusing names.
