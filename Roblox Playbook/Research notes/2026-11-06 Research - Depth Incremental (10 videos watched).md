## What the game really is
**One endless pit you walk down, step by step.** You start at the top with $1/s.
- Every **stair step is bought** with cash; the price floats on the step and rises each step. The top bar shows Depth (m) and a progress line.
- You fall when you walk off the edge. T = teleport menu to any room.
- Along the stairs are **doors you buy** that open side rooms. Each room is one mini-system with big wall boards: title, upgrades, Buy / Max buttons.

**Layers (each = new colour palette + new rooms + a boss at the bottom):**
| Layer | Look | Rooms (mechanic) | Boss |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dirt (0-~100m) | warm brown cave, orange mushrooms, barrels | Cash Upgrades (More Cash / Faster Cash / Walk Speed); **Dirt Fossils** (stand on a glowing yellow square, auto-dig, T-Rex skull prop); **Worm Burrow** (pink room, worms spawn, walk over to collect; golden worm 10-15%); **Upgrade Tree** (a physical floor of tiles you walk onto, joined by glowing lines; Locked tiles say "Requires Rebirth 1"); **Titles** (roll room, round pad, Title Index board) | Worm Queen |
| Stone | blue-grey cobblestone, lanterns | Stone Fossils (Titan Skull, Geode Heart); **Mine cart** (carry coal crates from a chute to a cart, the cart laps a track and pays coal; later auto-load, more carts, lucky crates 10% = 5x); Deep Fossils (purple: Void Heart, Abyss Eye); **Trading Post** (merchant with stock + restock timer); **Special Upgrades** (neon corridor of cards: automations + "will not reset on rebirth") | Stone Golem |
| Spider Nest | green webs, slime | Web Fossils, **Hatchery** (fight a Mantis for eggs, eggs every 8 h + streak, egg upgrades), Nest Fossils, Spider Titles, Upgrade Tree 2 | Spider Queen (spiderlings, tight arena, the hardest) |
| Sunken Lake (240-360m) | teal underwater, coral, seaweed, jellyfish, boat | Lake Fossils (Lake Serpent Skull 1/50M), **Fishing Dock** (cast, wait, catch; fish = currency; Fish Index + luck), Abyss Fossils, Upgrade Tree 3 | **Kraken** (neon ring arena, "hit its head at the edge") |
| Fungal Grotto, then UPD4 | ... | ... | last boss **Ignaroth** (lava arena, ~1,790m) |

**Per room the loop is the same:** stand/collect → currency → that room's upgrade board → "MAX" → % complete. The Teleport menu shows every room with "% complete", grouped by layer and depth range.

**Fossils:** a rarity list per room, e.g. Dirt:
- Worm Cast 1/20 · Snail Shell 1/75 · Fern Imprint · Beetle Husk · Fish Bone 1/300 · Ammonite 1/1K · Raptor Claw 1/5K · Mammoth Tusk 1/25K · Rex Skull 1/100K · Amber Heart 1/1M.

Each fossil gives a small permanent buff (Cash/Luck/Worms/Coal...). Each has a **max count**; when maxed it leaves the pool, so rarer ones come up. Later: **Shiny fossils** (rainbow text) and "fossil counts twice".

**Titles = the RNG layer:**
- 1M cash per roll, with auto roll for free.
- Rarities from 1/7 up to 1/10M (Pebble Pusher, Worm Friend, Cave Crawler, Magma Heart 1/500K, Queen's Chosen 1/1M...).
- They give big cash/worm/coal multipliers and are **kept on rebirth**.
- Equip 1 (more slots via a gamepass/merchant).
- Each layer later gets its own title pool (Spider Titles).

**Currency chain:** cash → worms → **grubs** (convert worms to grubs; this resets worm upgrades but gives bigger boosts, a mini-prestige inside a room) → coal → eggs → fish.

**Rebirth (after each boss):**
- The boss drops a **key**, which opens the Rebirth room.
- Screen: "You keep: titles / You get: x10 cash, x3 worms, unlocks next layer".
- Resets the layer; then Special Upgrades let more and more things survive rebirth, plus auto-buy.
- Players say the 2nd run is "so quick" and that feeling is the reward.

**Bosses:**
- A sword with LMB, damage = your cash/s, hearts (3).
- Red floor telegraphs and minions.
- An "Enraged" phase.
- Retry any time.
- Players rebeat old bosses in 2 s after rebirth (power fantasy).

**Other systems:**
- Milestones (UPD3).
- Potions (inventory, timers bottom-left).
- Codes ("coolcode" = 1.2x luck).
- Daily gift timer.
- Lobby boards: Free Rewards (like, join group, follow dev → Claim), "Update Event" pad (notify), Community Bonuses.
- Other players share the same rooms (crowded fossil room = social proof).

## Monetization (seen in videos)
- **VIP**: 1 Day 64 / 3 Days 184 / 7 Days 320 / Forever 1,040 R$ (x1.5 cash, x1.2 luck, free daily potion, chat tag).
- **Bundles:**
  - Starter Pack 32 R$ (one time, with a timer, exclusive title, x1.2 title+fossil luck forever, cash potion).
  - Game Pass Bundle 1,360 (20% off).
  - Potion Bundle 960 (100 of every potion).
- **Gamepasses** like Title Master (~600 R$, extra title slot / roll from anywhere) and Fossil Master. YouTubers called them "a scam" and then bought them anyway because they were stuck.
- **Limited paid title packs** (Halloween Pack: 62.5/29/6/2/0.5% odds, x1 80 R$ … x8 560 R$; exclusive titles that don't use a slot, +% of the best title's power).
- **Merchant**: out-of-stock items buyable with Robux (160 / 280 R$, tagged Legendary/Mythic/Godly) plus a Robux Restock. YouTubers bought many of these.
- **Eggs**: 15 = 24 R$ … 1,300 = 1,600 R$ (the Hatchery is almost pay-only; players complained but bought).
- Note for us: the timer starter pack and paid random packs break our rules (no fake countdowns, no paid randomness). Not to copy.

## Why it's addicting (from what players said and did)
1. **One visible number that only goes down:** "how deep", the depth bar, "there's more after?!". Surprise that it keeps going is the hook in every video title.
2. **Every ~2-5 min something NEW to buy into** (a door, a room, a currency). The players' running commentary is "what is this? what do worms do?" They are curious, not grinding.
3. **Numbers explode:** quintillion → septillion. They read big numbers out loud.
4. **Many small simulators in one** ("a bunch of simulators inside one"). The player picks which room to work, and walks between them (rooms are physical, not menus).
5. **Rebirth makes you OP fast**, then automation removes the boring bits. Asking "what survives rebirth?" drives purchases.
6. **Collection %** everywhere (fossils, titles, rooms, Teleport menu shows completion).
7. **Bosses as checkpoints** with a real skill moment, and the key opens the rebirth room.
8. **Weekly updates add a layer** (UPD1-4 in one month), each with a Roblox Event for notifications.
- Complaints: the slow manual mine cart ("most boring room"), the Hatchery 8 h timer and pay-to-win, losing grubs/new-layer progress on rebirth, and no clear "what next" when stuck.

## Implications for our Depth-style game (Claude's decision; owner can override)
- **Theme warning:** Depth's layer 4 is already underwater (Sunken Lake, fishing, **Kraken**). A full ocean-dive game would look like a copy of their best layer. **Drop ABYSS as the main pitch.** Down = Depth. Up = Tower Incremental/Zenith. Space = our Galaxy.
- **New pick: "+1 SHRINK" (going smaller).** One visible number = your size, from 1.7 m down to 0.000000001 nm.
  - Layers: Backyard → Ant Colony → Pond Drop (microbes) → Inside a Leaf (cells) → Molecules → Atom → Quantum.
  - Each layer is a new world where normal things are giant.
  - Collect bugs/microbes/particles (rarity lists like fossils).
  - Bosses: Ant Queen, Tardigrade, Virus, the Nucleus.
  - Kid-friendly, very visual for thumbnails (tiny bacon hair next to a giant ant), not taken in today's charts.
  - Owner decides between SHRINK and ocean.
- **Keep the proven structure:**
  - buy each step forward;
  - physical side rooms off the path, each its own mini-system with a currency;
  - upgrade tree as a walkable floor;
  - collect lists with max counts;
  - RNG titles kept through rebirth;
  - boss + key → rebirth room;
  - automation and "keeps on rebirth" as the late rewards;
  - % complete in the teleport menu;
  - a new layer every week with a Roblox Event.
- **Do better than them:**
  - no manual hauling rooms (or automate them by the 2nd visit);
  - a clear "next goal" arrow;
  - no 8 h pay-only timers;
  - honest monetization: VIP, gamepasses (extra title slot, auto roll anywhere), boosts. No paid random packs, no fake timers.
- **Order:** Galaxy launch (5 Dec) stays first; this one is the next build after.
