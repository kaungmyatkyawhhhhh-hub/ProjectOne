# Research: a "mogging" game as the next game? (6 Nov)

Owner: "a mogging game?" (after "combining upgrade tree with tycoon" - Galaxy already is that).

## Data (Roblox API, 6 Nov 2026)
- **Mog and Gym** (was "Looksmax and Mog", by Mog Gurus, place 100629725231227): created 5 May 2026, ~3.0M visits, 16.4K favourites, 22.5K up / 2.2K down (91%), updated 29 Oct, **0 playing** now. GameRant (26 Jul) showed ~2.2K playing at its peak.
- The game itself has moved to gym: "Train your body and improve your looks… hit the gym… mogging battles against real players… NOOB to PSL GOD", auras, Elo.
- Older ones: Mewing Simulator, Looksmaxxing Simulator (2023-24 trend games). Steam: "MOGGED: Looksmaxx or Die".
- Sources: gamerant.com/looksmax-and-mog-codes-roblox, roblox.com/games/100629725231227, rolimons.com/game/14844477632.

## Read
- A meme trend that peaked mid-2026 and faded: the biggest one is at 0 players 6 months later; 3M visits total = small.
- Meme games need speed (ship in days while the meme is hot); this one is already cold.
- The best one turned into a gym game -> same audience/genre as GYM ARC (would compete with ourselves).
- Kids audience: "looksmaxxing / PSL" slang comes from appearance-rating forums; ranking players by looks is body-image-sensitive for kids (risk for reports/moderation and parents).

## Decision (Claude, owner can override)
- Not as a separate next game.
- Take the good part into GYM ARC instead: a **flex/pose-off battle vs a real player** (physique, poses, timing, crowd vote/Elo) - GYM ARC already has Posing Shows, so it's a small add and fits the trend's "dominate rivals" hook without the looks-rating.
- Next game = pick after Galaxy launches (5 Dec), from a trend with a bigger, growing audience.
