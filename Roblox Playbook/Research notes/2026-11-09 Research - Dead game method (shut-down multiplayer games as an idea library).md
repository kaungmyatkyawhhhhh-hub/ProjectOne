# The Dead Game Method: shut-down multiplayer games as an idea library (9 Nov)

Owner: "research discontinued multiplayer games, that had passionate communities, find out why players liked those games fun and why people stop playing and which mechanics player still miss and then take those mechanics and then reinvent them as original roblox game concepts, using forgotten games as an research library for the game ideas."
Rule: borrow the **mechanic and the feeling**, never the name, characters, art or story (no IP). Every concept below is original.

## 1. The library (14 games)
| Game (years) | Why players loved it (the mechanic) | Why it died | What players still miss |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Club Penguin** (2005-2017) | One shared map of hangout rooms; minigames pay coins; decorate your igloo and open it to visitors; pet puffles; **a party every month with a free item** (Halloween, Holiday, April Fools were the biggest) | Membership model: ~90% never paid; declining popularity, staff cut 2015; replaced by Club Penguin Island (dead 2018). Fan servers hit 11M users in lockdown, shut by Disney DMCA 2020-22 | The monthly parties and "were you there?" moments, the shared rooms, igloo visiting |
| **Toontown Online** (2003-2013) | Co-op **turn-based battles with cartoon gags** (pies, safes, anvils); combos (lure a robot into a trap); robots named after corporate jargon; SpeedChat friendship | Disney moved to mobile (closed Toontown, Pixie Hollow, Pirates Online in 2013) | So much that fans rebuilt it: Toontown Rewritten still runs and grows 10+ years later; ~1,700 players at its 2023 anniversary event |
| **LEGO Universe** (2010-2012) | Building + Diablo-style minifig abilities; kids cried at the shutdown | 14K preorders vs 180K target; subs plateaued at 30-40K of 2.3M registered; needed a high-end PC; child-safety costs; inappropriate player builds | Building your own place in a shared world |
| **Spellbreak** (2020-2023) | **Elemental combos**: toxic cloud + wind vortex = acid storm; fire trails cut routes; frost paths to skate on; creativity over aim | Studio bought by Blizzard, team moved to WoW; crowded battle royale market | "A living fireworks show of elemental combat", discovering new combos |
| **Knockout City** (2021-2023) | Team dodgeball: catch, pass, special balls (and curling up to be thrown by a teammate) | Cosmetic-only free-to-play "requires massive scale"; retention too low for break-even; inflation hit spending | The private server still has daily players and fan tournaments |
| **Rumbleverse** (2022-2023) | Melee-only wrestling battle royale; "nothing else like it"; great feel | Hard skill barrier with a weak tutorial; long queues; few modes; childish-looking art; no marketing; shut after 6 months | The unique melee feel |
| **Atlas Reactor** (2016-2019) | **Simultaneous turns**: everyone plans secretly, then watches the turn play out; prediction, no dice; failed plans are funny | Tiny player base; business model flipped F2P → premium → F2P | The plan-then-reveal comedy |
| **Evolve** (2015-2018) | 4 hunters track 1 player-controlled monster that **grows stronger by eating** | ~60 € each for a group of 4-5; needed full teamwork; patches every 3 months; balance broke once people played to win | Hunting / being the monster |
| **Glitch** (2011-2012) | Whimsical co-op world, exploration over combat, evolving world events | Not enough players to sustain it; built on Flash | Its kindness and humour |
| **FusionFall** (2009-2013) | Make your own cartoon-style hero; collect **"nanos"** (tiny hero buddies that give buffs); browser = easy access | Subscription first, F2P a year later; the audience didn't connect | Nano collecting |
| **Moshi Monsters** (2008-2019) | Raise a monster, collect cute creatures; 80M users at peak | Web subscription, missed mobile; revenue -35% in 2013 | Collecting + caring for a pet |
| **City of Heroes** (2004-2012) | A devoted community that rallied to save it | The publisher closed the studio | Its community and hero identity |
| **Titan Simulator** (Roblox, Oct 2017 → faded by ~2019; still online) | **Your body IS the leaderboard**: click to train power, grow into a giant, punch and **stomp** smaller players, rebirth, team up with friends against the big one. 91.7M visits, 704K favourites, 87% likes, 18-player servers | Its makers (rip_indra + mygame43, the Gamer Robot brothers) launched Blox Fruits in Jan 2019 and never came back; only 2 passes (499 + 599 R$), a hard cap (1,000,000 armour), a feature switched off instead of fixed ("disabled magic") | "I miss it" TikToks in 2025-26 ("2017 to 2019"); 13 people still playing 9 years later. Full pass: section 6 |
| **Titan Simulator X** (Roblox, Jun → Oct 2023) | Hunt NPC titans for loot, weapons, shields and pets; grow in size; craft HUGE items. **96% likes** | Updates stopped after 4 months (last one 11 Oct 2023); 2.08M visits, 0 playing now. Liked is not the same as kept | Nothing found: no fan posts. A game can be liked and still not missed |

## 2. Patterns
**Why players loved them (what to copy):**
1. **A shared place with a few fixed rooms** where you meet the same people (Club Penguin).
2. **Scarce, dated events** that create "were you there?" stories (monthly parties).
3. **A home you decorate and others visit** (igloos, LEGO properties).
4. **Something to care for and collect** (puffles, Moshlings, nanos).
5. **Co-op with silly, readable combos** (Toontown gags, Spellbreak elements).
6. **Funny chaos from simple inputs** (Atlas Reactor's reveal, Knockout City's ball throws).
7. **Asymmetric roles** (Evolve's monster vs hunters).
8-10 (11 Nov, from Titan Simulator): **the body is the leaderboard**, **one move that hits many small things**, **a gang can bring down the giant**. Detail in section 6.5.

**Why they died (what Roblox already fixes, and what it doesn't):**
- Paywalls (membership, subscription, 60 € boxes): **Roblox is free**, so keep everything playable free.
- PC-only / Flash / missed mobile: **Roblox runs on phones**.
- Server + moderation costs for kids: **Roblox carries them**.
- Owner/licence decisions: **the game is yours**.
- **What Roblox does NOT fix:** a high skill floor with a weak tutorial (Rumbleverse), needing a full team to have fun (Evolve), slow updates (Evolve's 3 months), cosmetic-only money needing huge scale (Knockout City), and empty queues. So every concept below has bots/solo play, a 60-second onboarding, weekly updates and Roblox-style progression money.

## 3. Original Roblox concepts (reinvented)
Scored for the owner's goals: kids, mostly phone, 1K CCU, Robux ≤ 449, a small team that's strong at incrementals.

### A. MONSTER GROW (from Evolve + incremental) ★ best fit
- **Hook:** "Eat. Grow. Get hunted." One player is a tiny blob monster that eats props to grow through 5 stages (an incremental inside a round). Everyone else are rangers with nets and tranquilizer darts.
- **Round (5 min):** the monster hides and eats; at stage 3 it can smash; at stage 5 it rampages. Rangers win by netting it before stage 5.
- **Meta:** evolution trees for monsters (fire, slime, crystal), gadgets for rangers, both levelled by playing. Shown on a growing size bar (the user's incremental know-how).
- **Fixes Evolve's death:** bots fill missing players, solo-friendly, 5-min rounds, free to start, weekly new monster.
- **Money:** monster skins and trails (fixed price), "start at stage 2" pass 199, VIP 399.

### B. PRANK PATROL (from Toontown)
- **Hook:** Grumble Bots are turning the colourful town grey. Team up and prank them back with pies, confetti cannons and whoopee traps.
- **Battles:** quick co-op turns (10 s to pick); combos: TRAP + LURE, a stack of the same gag = bonus. Every win repaints a street. The town is visibly rebuilt over the weeks.
- **Fixes:** solo with AI buddies; mobile-big buttons; weekly new Grumble boss.
- **Money:** gag skins, a house to decorate (fixed price), +1 gag slot 349.

### C. ELEMENT DODGE (from Spellbreak + Knockout City)
- **Hook:** Team dodgeball with element balls: throw FIRE into WIND = firestorm; ICE makes a slide lane; catch at the right moment to throw it back twice as hard; curl into a ball and let your teammate launch you.
- **3-minute rounds**, 3v3, bots fill. Combos to discover (a combo book).
- **Fixes:** big catch timing window (no aim skill), bots, short rounds.
- **Money:** ball trails, catch effects, emotes.

### D. PLAN & SPLAT (from Atlas Reactor)
- **Hook:** Everyone secretly draws their move (path + paint throw) in 8 s, then all moves play at once. Funny collisions, paint everywhere; most painted floor wins.
- **Perfect for phones** (draw with a finger, no aim). 2v2, 4 players, bots fill.
- **Money:** paint colours, splat effects.

### E. PET PARTY PLAZA (from Club Penguin + Moshi Monsters)
- **Hook:** A hangout plaza with a few rooms + minigames that pay coins; raise a pet blob; decorate your pod; visit friends' pods; **a themed party every month with a free limited item**.
- **Risk:** hangouts need people; launch with strong minigames first.
- **Money:** furniture and pet outfits at fixed prices, no membership wall.

## 4. Steal NOW for Galaxy (small, fits what's built)
1. **A monthly free party** (Club Penguin): a themed Galaxy event with decorations and one FREE limited cosmetic (a trail) only during it. The EventController can run it.
2. **Island visiting + likes** (igloos): "VISIT" a friend's island from the plaza, leave a star; the most-starred islands show on the plaza board.
3. **Server-wide project** (Glitch / Toontown's town repair): the Star Surge already fills per Supernova; make it build something visible in the plaza for everyone (a giant star statue that grows each week).
4. **A tiny companion** (puffles/nanos): LUMI as a pet that follows you and reacts to buys.

## 5. Recommendation
- **+1 SHRINK stays next** (already planned).
- **MONSTER GROW** as the following game: it uses the owner's strongest skill (incremental pacing) inside a social round game, is phone-friendly, and asymmetric hide-and-hunt games are proven on Roblox. Build a 1-week prototype (one monster, one map, bots) before anything else.
- Keep this note as the library: every new idea starts by adding the dead game, why it was loved, why it died, then the Roblox fix.

## 6. Pass 2 (11 Nov 2026): Titan Simulator (Roblox)
Owner (11 Nov): "research about Titan simulator in roblox and its like the dead-game-method". First ROBLOX game in the library. Numbers read from Roblox's own public APIs on 11 Nov 2026.

### 6.2 Why players loved it (the exact mechanic)
1. **Size = power = status, with no menu.** You click to gain power and your character physically grows. Everyone in the server can see who is strongest by looking. The game page's promise: train, fight, become the most powerful titan, team up with friends and make everyone fear you.
2. **The stomp.** One move from a giant hits everything small around it. YouTube series from 2017 are named after it ("stomp or be stomped"); a 33-video playlist describes the game as punching and stomping your way up.
3. **Rebirth as a show.** "Powering up to rebirth" was its own video topic (Dec 2017).
4. **Friends vs the giant.** 18-player servers: small enough to know who the big one is, big enough for a gang.
5. **Simple money:** Double Power (599 R$: 2x points per click, also doubles boosters and rebirths) and Demon Sword (499 R$: beats a punch, later beats a stomp).
6. **YouTubers carried it** in its first weeks (late Oct 2017): a Denis "TITAN SIMULATOR" video, "BIGGEST TITAN in ROBLOX", "HOW TO BECOME BIG FAST".

### 6.3 Why it died
| Cause | Evidence | Does Roblox fix it? |
|---|---|---|
| **The makers moved to a bigger game.** rip_indra (group owner) and his brother mygame43 launched Blox Fruits on 16 Jan 2019 (now ~60 billion visits). Titan Simulator's own group has that one game, the description "aaa" and no shout | groups API; Blox Fruits wiki lists Titan Simulator among their earlier games; fan TikToks date its good years "2017 to 2019" | **No.** Same as Spellbreak (team moved to WoW) |
| **Nothing left to chase.** A hard cap (max armour 1,000,000 Power), 2 passes, 0 badges, no pets, no worlds, no events | game page + passes + badges APIs | No |
| **Broken parts switched off, not fixed.** The last page note says the passes were fixed and magic was disabled | game page | No |
| **Paid power in PvP.** The 499 R$ sword out-hits a stomp at high level | pass description | No. Our rule already bans it |
| **Giants farm newcomers** (my inference from the design: open PvP, 18 players, no safe zone on the page). Every grandchild added the fix: a safe zone (Biggest Titan), a beginners-only hill (Titanus), team camps (Titan Training) | their game pages | No |
| **Above our price ceiling:** 499 and 599 R$ vs our 449 max | passes API | - |
Not found: any statement from the makers about why they stopped; exact peak CCU; how "magic" worked. I could not open the old YouTube videos or TikToks from here (blocked), so gameplay detail comes from the game page, pass texts, video titles and descriptions.

### 6.4 What players still miss
- TikTok nostalgia clips in 2025-26: one calls it a trip down memory lane (599 likes), another remembers 2017-2019 and says it is a dead game now (496 likes).
- 704K favourites and 13 players online, 9 years after launch, with no updates.
- The fantasy keeps selling: Titan Training Simulator reached 94.8M visits in 2024-25 with the same loop plus pets, rebirths and camps. Then its updates stopped (Aug 2025) and it went from ~340 players (rotrends snapshot, earlier in 2026) to 139.

### 6.5 New patterns (added to section 2)
8. **The body is the leaderboard** (Titan Simulator): progress everyone can see without opening a menu. The strongest version of K02's "power must be USED and SEEN".
9. **One move that hits many small things at once** (the stomp): the reward for being big is a different verb, not a bigger number.
10. **A gang can bring down the giant:** the big player is the server's event.
**Died-from list, new entries:** the makers leave for their next game (Roblox does NOT fix: this is the #1 killer of Roblox simulators, and it is OUR risk: GYM ARC was stopped on 7 Nov); a hard cap with nothing after it; 96% likes with no updates still dies in 4 months (Titan Simulator X).

### 6.6 Original concepts from this pass
Scores are 1-5 for kids / phones / 1K CCU / small incremental team.

**F. STOMP CITY** (from Titan Simulator's growth + stomp) · 5 / 5 / 3 / 5 = 18 ★ pick of this pass
- **Hook:** "Start ant-sized. End up stepping over skyscrapers."
- **Loop:** stomp things your size (flowers → bikes → cars → houses → towers) to gain mass; every size tier opens a bigger district of the same toy city; rebirth = a new city theme (candy, lava, space).
- **Session:** endless open map, solo-friendly, 12-player servers; a 3-minute STOMP RUSH event every 15 min where the whole server flattens one landmark together.
- **Borrows:** body-as-leaderboard and the stomp. **Avoids the death:** no PvP at all in the city (an opt-in arena later), so nobody is farmed; rebirth themes + a weekly landmark = no cap; the city rebuilding itself is the content treadmill.
- **Money (≤ 449, fixed, no random):** 2x Mass 249, Auto-Stomp 199, VIP 399, stomp shockwave skins 49-149.
- **1-week prototype:** one district, 5 size tiers, 30 breakable props from our own kit, one shockwave VFX + sound, a SIZE counter and a crown for the biggest.

**G. GIANT DOWN** (Titan Simulator's "team up" + Evolve's roles) · 4 / 4 / 3 / 3 = 14
- **Hook:** "One of you is the giant. Everyone else brings him down."
- **Round (4 min):** the server's top trainer is offered the giant; the rest tie ropes to ankles, climb and hit glowing weak spots; the giant stomps with a ring you can see coming. Between rounds everyone trains in the camp (the incremental part).
- **Avoids the death:** bots fill both sides, solo camp progress, a weekly new giant. **Risk:** needs 6+ players to be fun (the Evolve problem), so it scores lower.
- **Money:** giant skins, rope trails, VIP 399. **Prototype:** one giant rig, 3 weak spots, 5 bots, one arena.

**H. Not a new game: MEGA BITE already IS this fantasy in the sea.** Minnow → Megalodon is "grow until you are the titan", and its design already fixes 3 of Titan Simulator's deaths (Safe Reef, opt-in PvP, nothing bought helps in fights). So the best use of this pass is section 6.7, and STOMP CITY waits in the library behind +1 SHRINK.

### 6.8 How to research a dead ROBLOX game (new, reusable)
Roblox's public APIs give exact facts in one minute; read them before any article:
`apis.roblox.com/universes/v1/places/<placeId>/universe` → universe id · `games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=` (visits, playing, favourites, created, updated, max players) · `games.roblox.com/v1/games/votes?universeIds=` · `apis.roblox.com/game-passes/v1/universes/<id>/game-passes?passView=Full&pageSize=100` (what it sold) · `badges.roblox.com/v1/universes/<id>/badges` · `groups.roblox.com/v1/groups/<groupId>` (**the owner's name is often the real cause of death**) · `games.roblox.com/v2/groups/<groupId>/games`.
