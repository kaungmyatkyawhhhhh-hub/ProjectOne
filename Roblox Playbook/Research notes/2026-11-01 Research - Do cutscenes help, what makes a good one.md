# Do cutscenes help? What makes a good one? (researched 1 Nov 2026)

Owner: "does cutscenes do good too, research it and tell me" + "and what makes a cutscene a 'good' cutscene too".
Builds on [[Playbook notes - Lighting, cameras and cutscenes]] (which found no official Roblox data on cutscene length / skip).

## Answer: yes at the right moments, harmful at the wrong ones
- No public data shows cutscenes raise retention by themselves (looked: GameAnalytics, RoWatcher, analytics vendors; they only name "cutscene skip rate" as a metric to measure).
- What IS backed:
  - Drop-off is "almost always in the first 90 seconds"; lore / loading-screen monologues before playing and unskippable NPC dialogue are listed mistakes; let players reach the core fun right away (Adopt Me! puts a baby in your arms) - RoWatcher, Aug 2026.
  - Long forced intros / tutorials drive players away (FF XIII criticised) - GameAnalytics "16 reasons players leave".
  - Players skip what they've seen before, wordy dialogue, slow delivery, and when they "want to get back to the action" (a cutscene = 0 actions a minute) - "Why do players skip?" (fredzed).
  - Sakurai (Smash Bros): cutscenes still hold players if well directed, but make them skippable; no quick-time events in them.
  - CHI 2024 study (1,699 players): CURIOSITY was the strongest driver of enjoyment and the only one of playtime; feedback tied to the player's own success raised motivation; just making effects bigger (amplification) LOWERED it. -> spectacle alone doesn't help; it has to be earned and open a question.
- Roblox practice (Playbook): first-join intro <=10-15 s skippable; new area 3-6 s; rare reveal 2-4 s; rebirth 3-5 s; never on common repeated actions; unskippable only a <=3 s reward beat.

## What makes a GOOD cutscene (checklist)
1. **Earned**: plays right after something the player did (their Supernova, their first Legendary), never before they've played.
2. **The player is the star**: their avatar / their star system, not an NPC talking.
3. **Short**: 2-6 s for rewards; quick version on repeats.
4. **Skippable** after the first time (tap anywhere); skipping still gives everything.
5. **Shows what changed**: the camera lands on the new thing (new area, bigger system, new rank).
6. **Opens a question**: ends on a tease of the next goal / a cliffhanger (curiosity = playtime).
7. **Big but readable**: one clear idea per shot; sound timed to the picture; music ducks.
8. **Rare = special**: bigger the rarer; never on the 50th common thing.
9. **Others see it**: the server sees your beam / message (social proof).
10. **No reading walls, no QTEs**, controls and camera always come back (even on reset).

## Supernova Incremental use
- NO intro cutscene: you join and your asteroid is already earning.
- SUPERNOVA: first one ~5 s full cinematic (the star swells, bursts, shockwave, rank appears over your head), ends on a tease ("Supernova 3: the STAR FORGE awakens..."); later ones a 2 s version, skippable, and off with auto-Supernova.
- New layer unlocks (Star Forge, Black Hole, Ring Nebula): 3-5 s camera to the new thing.
- Star Forge: Legendary+ reveal 2-4 s, rarer = bigger; commons instant.
- Server sees a big burst in the sky when anyone goes Supernova.
