# Top games art study (icons + thumbnails, Oct 2026)

Back to [[00 Roblox Playbook]] · Related: [[Playbook notes - Thumbnails]], [[Playbook notes - Icons and titles]], [[Roblox Playbook - Updates, storefront and analytics]]

**What this is:** on 29 Oct 2026 I pulled the real icons and thumbnails of the **32 biggest high-quality games** on Roblox (Top Playing Now chart, 14k-920k players), plus the **9 biggest gym games**, and studied them one by one. These rules come from what the winners actually do, not from blog advice.

Games studied: Steal An Egg, Murder Mystery 2, Brookhaven, Steal a Brainrot, Adopt Me!, 99 Nights in the Forest, Blox Fruits, RIVALS, Jujutsu Shenanigans, Dress To Impress, Pet Simulator 99, Dandy's World, Fish It!, Fisch, The Strongest Battlegrounds, DOORS, Evade, Violence District, Tower of Hell, Volleyball Legends, Bee Swarm Simulator, Sol's RNG, Forsaken, Flee the Facility, Dead Rails, BedWars, Blade Ball, Grow a Garden, Tower Defense Simulator, Anime Adventures, Vanguards, Creatures of Sonaria.
Gym games: Gym Star Simulator (6.2k), Muscle Legends (4.6k), +1 Muscle to Break Walls (3k), Power Lifters (2.1k), Project: Iron (1.8k), Muscle Transform Simulator (1.5k), Gym League (1.2k), Strongman Simulator (1k), GYM ARC (12).

## 1. The 4 styles the top games use

| Style | Games | What it looks like | Copy it? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Famous brand** | MM2, Tower of Hell, RIVALS, BedWars, Blade Ball, TSB | One object or just the logo on a flat colour (a knife, a red ball, a glove). Thumbnails are often plain gameplay. | **No.** Only works because everyone already knows the game. |
| **B. Premium art** | Adopt Me!, Pet Sim 99, Dress To Impress, Blox Fruits, Volleyball Legends, TDS, Anime Adventures, Vanguards, Dandy's World, Creatures of Sonaria | Painted/illustrated character art, close-up, huge expressive eyes or face, dramatic light, logo built into the art, one colour mood. | **Yes, this is the "non-slop" look.** Usually made by a pro artist (Blender render + paint-over). |
| **C. Simulator formula** | Steal An Egg, Steal a Brainrot, Grow a Garden, Fish It!, all gym games | Blender 3D of a classic default avatar, bright sky/grass, a GIANT item or creature, a huge rainbow number ($997,151,167/s), red arrow + "YOU!", "!!" marks, shocked face. | **Yes for gym games, but done clean.** This is what the genre's players click. |
| **D. Horror story** | 99 Nights, DOORS, Forsaken, Violence District, Flee the Facility | Dark, a monster close behind an unaware player, tension. | Not our genre. |

## 2. What ALL the winners do (the real rules)

1. **One idea you get in half a second.** Every icon has ONE subject that fills 70-100% of the square. No small full-body characters in icons. No scenery in icons.
2. **A face is the hook.** About 7 in 10 icons are a face looking straight at you: huge cartoon eyes (Pet Sim 99, Adopt Me!, Dandy's World), a strong emotion (Fish It! panic, Volleyball Legends grin), or a scary stare (99 Nights). Even objects get faces.
3. **Crop tight, break the edges.** Hair, arms, wings and weapons go past the frame. It makes the subject feel big.
4. **Simple background.** Flat colour (Pet Sim 99 blue, Jujutsu Shenanigans orange, RIVALS white), a soft gradient, or heavy blur. The subject never fights the background.
5. **Text = logo or a number, not sentences.** Icons have only the game logo (Adopt Me!, Blox Fruits, Brookhaven, TDS) or nothing. Simulators use ONE giant number instead of words ($89,121/s, 999,999 POUNDS, LV.1000).
6. **Thumbnails tell a tiny story.** The best 16:9s show tension or change:
   - **Threat vs you:** Fish It! (panicking player pulled by a monster), Steal An Egg (snake/yeti chasing you with the egg), 99 Nights (monster behind a sleeping player).
   - **Before → after:** Muscle Transform "LV.1 → LV.1000", +1 Muscle "1 → 6,700 → 99,999" in 3 panels, Evade half-and-half character.
   - **Huge reward:** Grow a Garden and Steal a Brainrot: a giant rainbow money number over a giant item, tiny player looking up.
7. **A template, not random pictures.** Fish It!'s 4 thumbnails are the SAME shot (same player, same dock, same pose); only the creature changes. Steal a Brainrot the same. This makes the game recognisable and makes A/B tests fair (only one thing changes).
8. **Icon and thumbnails look like one family.** Same character, colours and logo style everywhere.
9. **Events change the frame, not the game.** Many games wrapped their normal icon in the same red "The Hollow" event frame, or added pumpkins / a Halloween tag. Update = small recognisable change.
10. **"You" is a default avatar in the simulator formula.** The classic avatars (default hair, noob) are used so every kid reads it as "that's me".

## 3. What makes art look "slop" vs "premium"

| Slop | Premium |
|---|---|
| Many things fighting for attention | One subject, one idea |
| Everything equally bright and saturated | One bright focal point, the rest calmer or darker |
| Several fonts, random emojis, stickers | One logo style, one number style |
| AI mush: melted hands, extra fingers, unreadable text, non-Roblox anatomy | Clean Roblox shapes (blocky head, round hands), crisp edges |
| Flat even lighting | Strong key light + rim light that outlines the subject |
| Shows things the game doesn't have | Shows the real game's character, item and hook |
| New random style each time | Same template and colours across icon + thumbnails |

## 4. The gym genre right now (our competition)

- Everyone shows a **huge muscular body**. Most are **shirtless with glowing abs** (Gym Star Sim, Power Lifters, Project: Iron). GYM ARC must NOT do this (owner's account was hit over a muscle-body image once). Clothed is also a way to look different.
- **Gym League already owns "gold muscle god"** (gold body, lightning, Greek temple). GYM ARC's gold giant is close to this, so we need our own hook, or we look like a copy.
- Common gym formulas: glowing muscles with "+100" pop-ups, "LV.1 → LV.1000 TRANSFORM!", a giant barbell with "999,999 POUNDS", a 3-panel growth strip.
- **What nobody in the gym genre shows: getting TALLER.** All of them show width and muscles. GYM ARC's real hooks are **growing in height (5'8" → 7'0")**, the **Growth Spurt**, the **Hall of Fame** and the **Strongman Yard**. These are our unique angle.

## 5. GYM ARC art direction (decided from this study)

- **Style:** C (simulator formula) done clean like B: Blender-style 3D Roblox characters, bright simple background, rim light, one idea.
- **The hook in every picture: HEIGHT.** A tiny player next to a giant, a height ruler/measuring line, "5'8" → 7'0"", the giant's head going out of the frame.
- **Characters always clothed** (black tank top + shorts like the in-game giant).
- **Thumbnail template (use for all of them, swap one thing each time):** tiny shocked default-avatar player on the left, the giant on the right breaking the top edge, one giant word or number top-left, bright blue sky background with a gold glow behind the giant. Swap: the giant's pose / item (gold barbell, atlas stone, Hall of Fame trophy) per update.
- **Icon:** the giant's face + flexing arm, close-up, looking at you with a confident grin, flat bright blue background, gold rim light, no text. Event version: same icon + an event frame/tag.
- **Keep away from:** shirtless bodies, Greek-god/lightning gold look (Gym League), cluttered gym backgrounds, sentences of text.

## 6. Ready ChatGPT prompts (follow the study)

### Thumbnail template, "HEIGHT" (main)
```text
Make a 16:9 Roblox game thumbnail (1920x1080) for a gym game called GYM ARC. Use the attached screenshots as the character reference (same Roblox blocky style, black messy hair, gold muscle body).
ONE IDEA: a player grows from normal height to a 7-foot giant.
- LEFT, small: a normal default Roblox avatar (classic look) in a white t-shirt, looking UP with a SHOCKED face, wide eyes, open mouth.
- RIGHT, huge: the GOLD muscular giant, fully clothed in a black tank top and black shorts, standing tall next to a tall gym height-measuring ruler; his head breaks out of the top of the frame. Confident grin looking down at the small player.
- A big white number "7'0"" with a thick dark navy outline next to the giant's head. No other text.
BACKGROUND: very simple, bright blue sky gradient with a soft warm gold glow behind the giant. No clutter.
STYLE: premium Roblox Blender render like front-page simulator games: clean blocky Roblox shapes, round Roblox hands, strong key light + white rim light outlining both characters, high contrast, saturated, sharp. Kid-friendly, fully clothed, no real brands, no AI-looking details.
```
Swap only one thing for the next thumbnails: "holding a giant gold barbell", "lifting a huge atlas stone", "standing on the Hall of Fame podium". Keep the rest the same (template rule).

### Icon
```text
Make a square 1:1 Roblox game icon (1024x1024) for GYM ARC. Use the attached screenshots as the character reference.
ONE SUBJECT filling the whole square: the GOLD muscular Roblox giant from the chest up, face close to the camera, flexing one huge gold arm next to his head, confident grin, eyes looking straight at the viewer. Fully clothed in a black tank top. His arm and hair break past the edges.
BACKGROUND: flat bright blue with a soft gold glow behind the head. Nothing else.
NO text. Strong white rim light outlining him. Premium Roblox Blender render, clean blocky Roblox shapes, high contrast, must read clearly at 150 px. Kid-friendly, no real brands.
```

## 7. Checklist before uploading any icon/thumbnail
- [ ] Shrink it to 150 px (icon) / 300 px (thumbnail): can you still tell what it is in half a second?
- [ ] One subject, one idea, one word or number at most.
- [ ] A face with clear emotion, looking at the viewer (icon) or reacting (thumbnail).
- [ ] Shows something really in the game, and the first minute delivers it.
- [ ] Not close to a current hit (copycat flag) and not close to Gym League's gold god.
- [ ] Characters clothed, kid-safe.
- [ ] Same family as the icon and other thumbnails (same template, colours).
- [ ] Test it in Thumbnail Personalization 7+ days; keep the winner, not the favourite.
