# Costly mistakes of big games (and the rule each one gives us)

Back to [[00 Roblox Playbook]] · Used by GYM ARC: [[2026-10-29 GYM ARC Gym genre research]], [[2026-10-29 GYM ARC Hall of Fame - good or bad]]

Researched 29 Oct 2026: top games and gym-genre games that lost lots of players because of something the developers did (or stopped doing). Some causes are reported by fan/news sites and not confirmed by the developers; that's marked.

## The cases

| Game | What they did | What happened | Rule for us |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fisch** (Second Sea, Mar 2025) | Big update behind level 200, a NEW currency so everyone started at zero, old rods weakened, level cap 1,000 → 2,500 | "Felt like a new game, not an update"; many quit; ~50,000 voted against it in a poll; devs considered removing it | New content builds on what players already have. Never reset their wallet or weaken what they own. |
| **Grow a Garden** (2025-26) | Pets took over the plant game; more and more systems + an "overwhelming" guide; Ascension Shop takes all your money for a new currency; recycled events; expensive bundles | From ~21M peak to far less; one week alone -400k CCU (1.8M → 1.4M); fans: "too complex now", "rewards not worth it" (fan/seller sites) | Keep the core fantasy the core. Don't let side systems take over. Resets must feel like a reward. Events need new things, not reskins. |
| **Pet Simulator 99** (Update 5) | Nerfed event rewards (pinata gifts ~20 → ~6), "impossible" F2P missions, a clan battle you could buy your way through | "Soured the mood", "pay-to-win clan battle", item prices crashed | Don't nerf what players earned or bought; buff the alternatives instead. No paid power in competitions. |
| **Pet Simulator X** (Nov 2021) | Sold NFT pets that only 12 people could own, off Roblox | Backlash, "boycott" calls, Terms-of-Service risk | Never sell exclusive power or off-platform items. Fair monetization. |
| **SPTS** (2018 → ) | Updates stopped after the 2018 Halloween update; one power (HellFire) sold for 1,999 Robux | Fell from top-4 on Roblox; the clones took its players; now ~500 players | Keep updating. A stopped game is a dead game. Don't sell power. |
| **Muscle Transform Simulator** (2025-26) | Stopped updating in June 2026 | 26.7k peak → 1.4k | Same: no updates = slow death. |
| **Gym League** (2024-26) | Hit 143k, then fell ~99%; description now says "Great RESET has begun, we are BACK TO WEEKLY UPDATES" (exact cause of the fall not public) | ~1.1k players now | Viral spikes don't stay. Weekly updates are what they returned to. |
| **Bee Swarm Simulator** | Solo dev, long gaps (e.g. no big update in Mar 2026); top players caught exploiting the leaderboards | Players notice gaps; dev burnout risk; trust hit by cheaters on top | Plan a pace you can keep. Protect leaderboards/statues from cheaters (server checks). |
| **Fisch** (trading) | Trading turned off after an update, likely because of bots and duped fish (not explained by devs) | Players confused and upset | Server-side authority from day 1, so you never have to remove a feature because of exploiters. Explain changes. |
| **Anime Adventures, Anime Fighting Sim(s)** | Used another company's IP/assets | Taken offline (reported DMCA), 1.9B-visit game unplayable | Original content only. No real brands, characters or music. |
| **Toilet Tower Defense** (2023) | Built on someone else's viral character | Put "under review" after copyright warnings (reported) | Same: original ideas only. |
| **The Hatch** (Roblox event) | Rushed, copy-paste games, buggy merge system, little direction, silence about problems | #BoycottTheHatch, record dislikes | Test events before launch; tell players clearly what to do; communicate fast when something breaks. |
| **Adopt Me!** (Mar 2026) | -122k players in 24 h (cause unconfirmed: an update, competitors, or the algorithm) | Players moved to Dress To Impress, Blox Fruits, RIVALS | Even giants lose players fast to fresher games. Watch daily numbers and react. |

## The 12 rules (short)
1. **Update every week**, small is fine. A long silence kills games (SPTS, Muscle Transform, Bee Swarm gaps).
2. **New content builds on old progress.** No "everyone starts from zero" updates, no new currency that wipes the old one (Fisch, Grow a Garden).
3. **Never make an old goal further away** or weaken what players own (Fisch rods, PS99 nerfs).
4. **The core stays the core.** Side features can't take over the game's identity (Grow a Garden's pets).
5. **Add depth slowly.** Complexity creep scares new players away (Grow a Garden).
6. **Resets must feel like acceleration**, never a tax (Grow a Garden's Ascension Shop).
7. **No paid power in competitions**, no exclusive power for money (PS99 clan battles, SPTS HellFire, PSX NFTs).
8. **Server decides everything; protect boards and statues** from cheaters, so you never have to switch off features (Fisch trading, Bee Swarm).
9. **Original content only** (Anime Adventures, Toilet Tower Defense takedowns).
10. **Events: new rewards, tested before launch, clear instructions**, never recycled reskins (The Hatch, Grow a Garden).
11. **Talk to players**: Update Announcements, a short "What's new / what changed and why" in-game (Fisch, The Hatch).
12. **Watch the numbers every day after an update** and be ready to undo with a Config switch (Adopt Me, Fisch polls).

## GYM ARC check (29 Oct)
- Rule 1: Saturday slot exists (prompt 90). Keep each week small enough for one person.
- Rules 2-3, 6: **Hall of Fame** resets height; follow [[2026-10-29 GYM ARC Hall of Fame - good or bad]] (make run 2 fast, keep peak height as status).
- Rule 4-5: **Basketball court, skate park, many boost types** = risk of drifting from "grow taller in the gym". See [[2026-10-29 GYM ARC Gym genre research]].
- Rule 7: auras/gear with stats: never sold as power that wins showdowns/arm wrestling/posing; trainer rolls already show odds.
- Rule 8: leaderboards, Hall of Fame top 10 and statues need server checks for impossible numbers before a cheater ends up as a statue.
- Rule 9: original characters only (already a rule).
- Rule 10: test the Saturday Hall of Fame launch with the TestLaunchIn switch first.
- Rule 12: Configs let us change star bonuses and switch features live.
