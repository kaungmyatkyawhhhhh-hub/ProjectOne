- **Gym League 2** (giant lifter, huge black plates in front, wall of fire, red/orange): closest to our red-gold logo -> style for #1 "the lift".
- **Gym League 1** (gold glossy giant, lightning, temple, dramatic light): style for the icon / a gold variant.
- **Power Lifters 1** (glossy clean render, plates as second hero, readable): plate treatment.
- **Muscle Legends 2** (black silhouette giant with red glow + fire hair, red storm) + **Muscle Legends 1** (glowing outline figure on black): style for #2's shadow giant.
- **Strongman 2** (tiny skinny kid vs huge buff guy, same frame): the size-contrast read for #2.
