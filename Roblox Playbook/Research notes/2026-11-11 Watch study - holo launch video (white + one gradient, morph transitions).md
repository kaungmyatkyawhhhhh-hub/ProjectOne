## Style guide (grammar only)
- **White ground, black type, ONE gradient** (orange -> pink -> violet -> blue) used as a material: it fills letters, the blob, the sphere, the chart bars and the frames of the cards. No second accent colour.
- **Type:** one clean grotesque, 2-3 words per beat, centred, big. Inactive list words in light grey.
- **Morphs instead of cuts:** each shape becomes the next (blob -> pill -> pixels; pill -> search bar -> toggle; tiles collapse into the next word). Hard cuts are saved for 5 big changes.
- **Real product shown in frames:** every claim (ads, socials, emails, SEO) gets the real output in a rounded card with a gradient edge.
- **One surprise in the middle:** the 3D mascot at 13 s, when attention normally drops.
- **Proof last:** the title ("Chief Marketing Officer") and the money counter come right before the end card.
- **Rhythm:** something new about every 1.2 s; a tick on every element.
