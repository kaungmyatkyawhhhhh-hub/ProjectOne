# Research: Hungry Shark Evolution as inspiration for MEGA BITE (11 Nov 2026)

Owner at 17:13: "take inspiration from the hungry shark evolution mobile game". This came right after the owner rejected the Tap REBORN restyle ("i think the old style was better", 17:09) and restored the v1 low-poly sharks.

Rule: we borrow the **feel and design principles**. We never copy HSE's names, characters, tattoos, scars or art. Everything we make is original.

## What HSE does (sources below)
- **Each shark has its own personality, silhouette and style.** The Ubisoft and Future Games of London concept work states this as the goal for every shark. The sharks are designed to read in a swimming pose.
- **The ladder is the same family scaled up.** The Megalodon is "virtually identical" to the Great White, just much bigger, with one extra mark: a crack-like scar from the snout to the gills and oversized gills. Size is the reward you can see.
- **Colour:** the Great White is ocean-blue instead of a real grey. The Megalodon has a slate-grey back and a lighter belly. Flat readable colour blocks; the countershade line reads at phone size.
- **Faces:** a permanent toothy grin with a row of big triangular teeth, pink gums and a dark mouth. Small mean eyes under a heavy brow. The mouth is the main feature, and the eyes carry the attitude.
- **Character marks:** a tattoo on the Great White's fin, a scar on the Megalodon, notched fins. Small story details make a shark feel like a character, not a stock fish.
- **Tiers:** Common, then Titan, Special (Electro, Ice, Robo, Pyro, Ghost), then Evolved forms of earlier sharks. It's a long unlock ladder, with element "skins" as separate sharks.
- **Gameplay hooks:** a hunger meter that always drains, so you must keep eating. Gold Rush is a frenzy multiplier (x64 for the Megalodon at 125,000 points). Baby-shark pets protect against hazards (for example, a Lava Baby protects from mines). Accessories (hats, jetpacks, lasers). The big shark gets stuck in tunnels and is an easy target, which balances size against agility.

## What MEGA BITE takes (decisions)
1. **Art (pilot: Megalodon):** keep our own shark line and colours, but move from faceted low-poly to a **smooth, muscular, semi-real body with a big head and grin**. Keep round glowing pupils (owner: no slit eyes), an angry brow from the Barracuda up, original claw scars, and notched fins on the boss. No blood.
2. **Same family, scaled up:** the Reef Shark, Great White and Megalodon share one body plan. Each step up adds marks (scars, glow cracks, bigger gills), so the Megalodon reads as the "final form" of the line.
3. **Personality marks per shark (original):** the Piranha gets an underbite, the Barracuda a pointed snout and silver stripes, the Hammer a wide head with dots, the Great White a notched dorsal, the Megalodon claw scars and glow cracks.
4. **Gameplay ideas to check against the live game later** (not built):
   - A frenzy mode in the style of Gold Rush.
   - Baby-shark pets that give one protection each (cosmetic or utility only, never paid PvP power).
   - Hats and accessories as fixed-price cosmetics, at most 449 R$.
   - A "big shark is clumsy" trade-off: slower turning at the top evolutions.
