# MEGA BITE wins by fixing what DEVOUR broke

MEGA BITE's concept points the right way. The research says success depends less on adding features and more on three things the current competitors get wrong: **retention (long, readable sessions on phones), fairness (no bullying, no paid power), and a steady update rhythm.** The shark eat-and-evolve niche on Roblox is small right now, about 1,280 combined CCU across the seven tracked games. Its best live benchmark is **Be Fish (97% likes, 19.1 min average playtime, 16-player servers, five cheap passes)**, not DEVOUR ([Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/94503612388426)). DEVOUR proves the shark-to-prehistoric fantasy draws players (1,747 peak CCU within about 2.5 months). It also shows how to lose them: 81% likes, 8-minute sessions, 231 ms average ping, 60-slot servers averaging under 13 players, and paid evolution trees ([Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/129827535941999)). The owner's concept already answers most of those failures: a Safe Reef with opt-in PvP in the Red Trench, carried-and-banked EXP, a free ladder to Megalodon, and no paid power. It needs five corrections. Servers should hold 16–20 players, not 24. PvP needs much stronger anti-bullying rules. There should be several bank points so a trip is a 1–3 minute bet. Scars must come out to keep the Mild maturity rating. And the release plan must account for Roblox's 2026 Kids/Select gate, which keeps every new game 16+ only until **250 highly engaged, age-checked 16+ players** have played it within 60 days ([Roblox Creator Docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/kids-and-select)). That last point changes the order of the launch more than anything else in this report.

## The open lane is a fair shark game that beats Be Fish's 19 minutes

The genre splits into old hits that are not pure eat-and-evolve games and a wave of 2025–2026 newcomers that are all fading. **SharkBite 2** (502M visits, 383 CCU, survivors-vs-shark rounds), **Be a Shark** (116M visits, 130 CCU) and **Shark Evolution** (52M visits, 16 CCU, not updated in a year) hold the big visit counts. **DEVOUR** (4.5M visits, 103 CCU), **Fish Evolution** (5.1M, 22 CCU) and **Grow A Fish** (7.7M, 5 CCU, monthly peaks falling from 90 in June to 20 in October) are the newcomers ([Rolimons SharkBite 2](https://www.rolimons.com/game/8908228901); [Rolimons Be a Shark](https://www.rolimons.com/game/15334675936); [Rolimons Shark Evolution](https://www.rolimons.com/game/5600963870); [bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/stats/games/grow-a-fish-9484532023)). No live game combines Be Fish's approval and session length with a shark-evolution fantasy. That combination is the gap MEGA BITE should aim at.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| DEVOUR | 4.5M | 103 | 81.0% | 8.0 min | 60 (avg 12.75 used) | The fantasy sells; lag, paywalled trees and short sessions bleed players |

DEVOUR deserves the closest look because it is almost the same game. It has three zones (Reef, Open Ocean, The Deep), a menu-driven Fish Tree, traits at Level 19 and a "YOU ARE THE APEX" crown ([DEVOUR fan wiki](https://devoureatfishoceananimals.wiki/)). No source explains its fall from 1,747 to about 103 CCU. The visible evidence points to four weak spots: sessions a third as long as Be Fish's, poor ping for a game about timing bites, an 81% rating that limits recommendation reach, and evolution lines sold as passes. The **Prehistoric Tree costs 499 R$**, so the game's Megalodon-equivalent sits behind a paywall. Its Halloween and Elasmosaurus events lifted it only to a 285 peak ([Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/129827535941999)). The concept's **free Megalodon** is therefore a real competitive edge, not just a principle.

Two adjustments to the concept follow. First, **switch the benchmark from DEVOUR to Be Fish**. The concept note's 94%-vs-81% comparison still holds, but Be Fish's 97% and 19-minute sessions are the bar that matters. Second, the like-ratio target needs context. A March 2026 DevForum thread claims "nearly every game with a 90%+ rating" rewards liking, usually bundled with a group join ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/the-issue-of-bribing-players-for-likes/4495969)). That claim is unverified, and it is not known whether Be Fish does this. The concept's no-reward like prompt is the right call, since Roblox is reported to be cracking down on engagement-farming rewards ([TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/113316/roblox-cracks-down-on-games-that-reward-players-for-doomscrolling/index.html)). It does mean an honest 90% is harder than it looks, so **D1/D7 retention and session length should be the main success numbers**, with likes second.

The off-Roblox hits confirm the core mechanics. Hungry Shark passed 1 billion downloads on size-gated prey (a reef shark cannot eat stingrays, a mako can), hunger drain and escalating hazards ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungry_Shark); [Pocket Gamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/hungry-shark-world/franchise-one-billion-downloads)). Agar.io reached 113M mobile downloads with extreme simplicity and creator-driven growth. It also showed the risk: teaming "cartels" that dominated leaderboards ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agar.io)).

## Pace the ladder at roughly 3x per tier, and make every bite readable

No source publishes real EXP curves or time-to-evolve for these games. The structural pattern is still clear and matches the concept. You can eat things about 25% smaller than you: agar.io needs about 1.25x the target's size, and its camera zooms out as you grow ([agar.io reverse-engineered mechanics](https://windfis.ch/git/agario-bot/tree/reversing_game_mechanics/game_mechanics.txt)). Costs rise faster than rewards. Hungry Shark Evolution's coin prices go Mako 1,000, Hammerhead 5,000, Tiger 15,000, Great White 35,000, Megalodon 50,000, and each shark unlocks only after maxing the previous one ([Hungry Shark Wiki](https://hungry-shark.fandom.com/wiki/Shark)). Building on that 3–5x step and the owner's existing pacing rules, this timeline is a proposal to test with the impatient-kid sim, not a sourced figure:

| Milestone | Target (first run) |
|---|---|
| First fish eaten | under 5 s |
| Minnow → Piranha | 60–90 s (confirms the concept's "first evolve within ~60 s") |
| Piranha → Barracuda | 4–5 min (the first big goal) |
| Barracuda → Reef Shark | 12–15 min (first danger trip and first bank) |
| Reef Shark → Hammerhead | 35–45 min |
| Hammerhead → Great White | 2–3 h total |
| Great White → Megalodon | 6–10 h total, the first Fossilize; run 2 about 2x faster |

Readability matters more than numbers for kids. Every fish should carry a colour outline: **green = edible, white = same size, red = danger**. That outline sits on top of the concept's lock icons. Speed should fall on a soft curve with size, as agar.io's speed = 86.05 / size^0.45 does, but reset at each evolution so a new shark never feels slower than the last. Each evolution also needs a dramatic pull-back with the old body's outline beside the new one. Katamari's designer said growth stops feeling impressive once players forget how small they started ([Game Developer postmortem](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/postmortem-the-singular-design-of-namco-s-katamari-damacy-2004-)). About 5–10 visible levels within each shark keep the big SIZE number ticking between evolutions.

The research **confirms the one-signature-ability-per-shark design**. Hungry Shark Primal gives each shark one ability, often tied to its boost ([Hungry Shark Wiki](https://hungry-shark.fandom.com/wiki/Shark)). It suggests one addition. Hungry Shark World's most proven "feel powerful" moment is **Gold Rush**, a meter filled by eating golden prey that briefly makes the shark invincible and able to eat almost anything ([BlueStacks guide](https://www.bluestacks.com/blog/game-guides/hungry-shark-world/hsw-beginner-guide-en.html)). MEGA BITE should give FRENZY or TIDAL ROAR a Gold Rush-style meter, outside PvP: eat X fish to get 8–10 s of "eat one tier up." Each ability also needs a unique colour and sound visible from about 50 studs away, so small fish see it and want it. Slither.io's shimmer on boosting snakes works the same way ([Slither wiki](https://slitherio-archive.fandom.com/wiki/How_to_boost)).

The carry-and-bank twist holds up, but it needs softening to fit kids. Modern extraction games cushion loss with a protected stash, progress that counts on death, and catch-up help. ARC Raiders' "Scrappy" gives extra materials to players on a losing streak, because tests showed struggling players were falling behind ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/arc-raiders-embark-extraction-virgil-watkins-interview-2000639879); [Naavik](https://naavik.co/f2p-mobile/extraction-shooters/)). Naavik also warns that auto-collected loot that means nothing removes the tension. For MEGA BITE, a death should keep about **25% of carried EXP** in a safe pocket. Players below Reef Shark tier should keep 100%. Fish Index entries, shinies and quest kills should count the moment they happen. Two deaths in a row while carrying should trigger a +50% carry bonus on the next trip. A pulsing "CARRYING: 1,240 EXP" counter and an arrow home make the danger readable. The concept's **x4 Trench multiplier is confirmed** by the 3–5x range that makes the risk worth taking.

The concept also needs two changes here. **Fossilize should keep the Fish Index, shinies and mutations**, because Hungry Shark World's thin endgame after the top tier shows the risk of having no prestige layer ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/iap-inspector-hungry-shark-world/)). And **phone controls should use auto-forward swimming** with the default thumbstick, a big BOOST button, an ability button at least 1.5x normal size, and automatic pitch so kids never manage depth by hand. Agar.io's most common mobile complaint was "floaty" controls, and many phone players play muted, so every threat also needs a visual cue: a red edge flash and an arrow ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agar.io); [Naavik](https://naavik.co/f2p-mobile/extraction-shooters/)).

## One compact map, about 2,000 studs across, with a landmark in every zone

Successful ocean games put a hand-made landmark in the middle of each biome and let depth mean danger. Subnautica places wrecks at biome centres, and its designers describe the progression as "caves become mini-bosses; deep trenches become mega-bosses" ([Radiator blog](https://www.blog.radiator.debacle.us/2018/02/mapping-sea-floors-of-subnautica.html)). Getting lost is the main underwater failure. Hungry Shark World sells a paid **Map item** for each level "to help you find your way around," which suggests even a commercial shark game knew its players got lost ([Ubisoft HSW FAQ](https://ubisoft-mobile.helpshift.com/hc/en/29-hungry-shark-world/faq/141-what-different-levels-and-maps-can-i-play)). Subnautica 2's designers add that fog is what makes a fly-anywhere world optimizable, and that every area must read from every angle ([mein-mmo interview](https://mein-mmo.de/en/exclusive-why-we-hate-underwater-levels-but-love-subnautica-2-heres-what-6-of-the-game-designers-say,1570132/)).

This **confirms the concept's three zones and hand-placed landmarks** (shipwreck, sunken statue, glowing vents, squid cave). It adds three requirements. First, use **one connected map** arranged by depth: bright Reef, deeper-blue Open Sea, red-lit Trench behind a visible gate. Each zone's landmark should be tall enough to see through the fog from the zone next to it, so the statue's head is visible from spawn. Second, give players a **free compass arrow and minimap** from the first second, plus a full-width entry banner ("RED TRENCH: PvP · x4 EXP"). Albion Online's Blue/Yellow/Red/Black zones rely on map colour, and kids on phones will not read a legend ([GuildOrder Albion wiki](https://guildorder.com/games/albion/wiki/zone-color-system)). Third, keep the world dense rather than large. No source measures kids' travel tolerance. One DevForum developer warned that players "shouldn't have to swim for 1/2 an hour" across an ocean ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/in-need-of-some-input-on-map-scale/2794683)). For a 16–20-player server, a playable area of about **1,500–2,500 studs** (Reef about 400–500, Open Sea band 600–800, Trench 400–600) gives a 1–2.5 minute crossing at typical swim speeds. That is a calculation to test, with something to meet every 10–20 seconds.

On the technical side, use **Terrain Water** (server-visible swimming, harder to exploit than client-side part water). Since June 2021, a `WaterTransparency` of 1 gives about 2,000 studs of visibility, so low values plus high `Ambient` set a bright, fogged play space ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/underwater-transparency-change-in-visibility-behavior/1268584)). Turn on StreamingEnabled with the default MinRadius 64 and Opportunistic stream-out, and make landmarks Atomic ([Roblox Docs: streaming](https://create.roblox.com/docs/workspace/streaming)). Draw calls matter more than triangles: build a kit of 10–15 hero meshes reused hundreds of times with tinted textures, keep textures at 512×512 or below, and use no Humanoids on NPCs ([Roblox Docs: performance](https://create.roblox.com/docs/performance-optimization/improve)). A 2025 DevForum demo ran **10,000 NPCs at 55–60 FPS on an iPhone 8 Plus** by having the server send bit-packed positions that clients interpolate on single meshes ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/10000-physical-npcs-running-with-60-fps-on-low-end-device/3660293)). A few hundred food fish and a few dozen predators are well within that. These are the specific means to beat DEVOUR's 231 ms ping and 51 FPS.

## PvP fairness and Roblox rules decide whether kids can even play

The concept's PvP rules (Trench only, a 10 s shield, carried EXP only, a 60 s no-re-eat cooldown) are the right shape but too loose. The best-documented anti-griefing system on Roblox is **Blox Fruits' bounty rules**. Kills pay only within a 25% level band. Players under 2.5M bounty lose nothing. The same pair can trade bounty only **3 times per 72 hours**. Leaving while combat-tagged is penalised, and suspicious kill patterns are filtered out ([Blox Fruits Wiki](https://blox-fruits.fandom.com/wiki/Bounty_and_Honor_System)). Deeeep.io adds a size-gap rule: the largest and smallest tiers often cannot damage each other ([TV Tropes](https://www.tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/DEeEEPIO)). Mope.io's "tail-biting" lets small animals bite top-tier ones for XP, which gives the small player a role instead of only being prey ([.io Games Wiki](https://io-games.fandom.com/wiki/Mope.io)). Be a Shark shows the opposite. It pays Skulls for eating players, and guides tell players to hunt "exposed or distracted" ones ([AllRoblox](https://allroblox.com/en/game/be-a-shark)).

Taken together, MEGA BITE's Trench rules should be: the 60 s cooldown plus **zero reward after 3 eats of the same player in about 30 minutes**; little or no EXP for eating sharks far below your tier; no EXP drop at all below Reef Shark tier; a combat tag that drops carried EXP if you flee to the reef or log out right after being bitten; **EXP split, not duplicated, when two sharks eat the same target**, against agar.io-style teaming; big sharks needing 2+ bites to kill; big sharks turning wider so small fish can out-turn them; small-only kelp tunnels; eating a player paying only a fraction of their carried EXP; and the existing sea bombs that hurt big bodies most. The concept's crown should become a hunted target: the top shark is marked on everyone's map and drops a big EXP chest when beaten, the dead-game "Apex Hour" idea. Bites should be checked on the server with a small grace radius, so no one feels "eaten from far away."

The maturity rating is a hard constraint. Roblox Kids (5–8) and Select (9–15) accept only **Minimal or Mild** content. Mild covers "unrealistic depictions of violence, such as bodies disappearing the moment their health reaches zero" and unrealistic blood, meaning pixelated or a different colour or shape. A single realistic moment pushes a game to Moderate, which drops the Kids audience ([Roblox Content Maturity](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/content-maturity)). An eat loop is "repeated" violence by nature, so expect Mild and answer the questionnaire honestly. Eating must read as a cartoon gulp: chomp, a cyan or gold bubble burst, an EXP orb, and no red clouds, chunks or leftovers. **This contradicts one concept item: "scars" as a show-off mark should be cut**, since wound-like cosmetics sit on the realistic side. Use size, a crown, fossil badges and glow instead. "Red Trench" can stay as a name with red lighting, but the blood effects must never be red. The squid boss and deep NPCs must stay mildly scary (no jump scares or horror audio), because fear-based content also raises the rating.

The 2026 Kids/Select gate is the biggest gap in the current concept. Since **19 May 2026**, a game reaches under-16 players only after passing an evaluation. The creator account must be age-checked and verified with 2-Step Verification. The creator must pay a refundable per-game fee or hold a Roblox Plus/Premium subscription that is **at least 2 months old**. The game must log **250 unique plays by highly engaged, age-checked 16+ users within 60 days**, where "highly engaged" includes a recent Robux purchase anywhere on Roblox. Then a safety review runs ([Kids and Select docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/kids-and-select); [Nerdschalk](https://nerdschalk.com/robloxs-new-publishing-and-alternative-requirements-for-roblox-kids-and-select-will-go-into-effect-on-may-19-heres-what-you-need-to-know/)). An expedited path costs a refundable 50,000 R$. Until the game passes, it stays 16+ only. A kids' game therefore launches first to teenagers and adults, and has to be fun and fair for them.

Several smaller rules complete the picture. Kids accounts have chat off by default, and custom preset chat systems now face strict limits (12 presets, a 10 s rate limit, filtering, no cross-age communication), so MEGA BITE should use built-in chat and icon-only emotes ([DevForum roadmap](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/march-26-2026-an-update-on-our-age-check-to-chat-fast-follow-roadmap/4539685)). Rewards earned purely through gameplay need no odds disclosure, so the concept's earned "1 in 500" mutations and "1 in 2,500" shinies are compliant. Anything random bought with Robux, including luck boosts, would need exact odds and an `ArePaidRandomItemsRestricted` fallback ([Paid Random Items](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-random-items)). On IP, Ubisoft holds a 2024 US registration for HUNGRY SHARK EVOLUTION covering game software ([trademark record](https://trademarkregistration.app/Trademark/Details/98447361)). That **confirms the concept's decision to avoid "Shark Evolution"** and keep "Hungry Shark" out of the title, tags, description and ads. Species names such as Great White and Megalodon are generic animals. The residual risk is a look-alike icon, so use the planned front-facing stylized shark rather than Hungry Shark's side-on toothy breach. A trademark search on "MEGA BITE"/"MEGABITE" was not done and should come before locking the name.

## Borrow the boss hunt and the living ocean from dead games, not their paywalls

The dead-game library gives MEGA BITE more lessons than features. **ARK: Survival of the Fittest** died with fewer than 300 CCU because its matches were slow, complex and about an hour long, so players could not drop in ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/ark-survival-of-the-fittest-is-an-experiment-that-didnt-catch-on-but-we-do-think-theres-a-fun-game-there-says-studio-wildcard)). That supports short 3–5 minute drop-in events such as Feeding Frenzy, which the concept already plans. **Monster Hunter Frontier** ran 12 years (2007–2019) on co-op hunts of big original monsters refreshed by expansions ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monster_Hunter_Frontier_Z)). That supports the squid boss as a shared server goal, with loot by contribution and a new boss each season. **Endless Ocean Luminous** was panned because sea life barely reacted and multiplayer felt solo, while its deep zones were the high point ([Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/review-endless-ocean-luminous-is-a-shallow-dive-into-a-disappointing-sea/)). So fish schools should scatter from big sharks, gather around small ones, and get stunned into easy food by bomb blasts. That costs little and answers the "dead ocean" feeling. **Club Penguin Island** died partly because membership gated most rewards and old fans were negative about missing features, and its fan-requested update came too late ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Club_Penguin_Island)). That supports keeping daily quests and progress free. **The Stomping Land** raised $114,000 on Kickstarter, peaked at 136,593 players, then died when its weekly updates stopped ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Stomping_Land)). Roblox hosts the servers, but it cannot fix developer silence.

Several dead-game ideas fit the concept's later updates. **FishVille's** tanks, friend visits and gifting map onto the planned Lair ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FishVille)). Adding a daily friend gift bubble also feeds Roblox's co-play signal. Dated, never-returning event cosmetics give collection value without paid randomness. Deeeep.io's **branching evolution** (several choices at each of up to 9 evolutions) and biome meters for pressure, temperature and salinity ([TV Tropes](https://www.tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/DEeEEPIO)) are proven, but they conflict with a simple 7-step ladder for 5–8-year-olds. They work better as the Fossil-tier prehistoric lines than at launch. Mope.io's slide after its sale, blamed on "joke features" ([.io Games Wiki](https://io-games.fandom.com/wiki/Mope.io)), argues that weekly updates must stay meaningful rather than filler.

## Sell cosmetics and convenience, update every Saturday, and buy plays only after retention holds

The concept's store (≤449 R$, no paid random items, nothing that helps in the Trench) is confirmed and sits in line with competitors. Be Fish sells five passes at 99–649 with 97% likes. DEVOUR's x2 Levels and Prehistoric Tree are both 499. SharkBite 2's gadgets run 99–950 ([Rolimons Be Fish](https://www.rolimons.com/game/94503612388426); [Rolimons DEVOUR](https://www.rolimons.com/game/129827535941999); [Rolimons SharkBite 2](https://www.rolimons.com/game/8908228901)). DEVOUR's priciest pass is the Prehistoric line, and Megalodon reveals headline ocean-game YouTube videos ([Recapio digest](https://recapio.com/digest/me-fui-a-pescar-al-megalodon-secreto-del-oceano-silviogamer-en-fish-it-by-silvio-gamer)), so Megalodon is the genre's strongest hook. MEGA BITE should **earn the Megalodon and sell a cosmetic prehistoric skin line** at around 299–449. Suggested anchors, which are proposals and not researched prices: x2 EXP 299–399, Bigger Bag 149–249, VIP 199–399, and trails and ability colours as 49–149 developer products. Skip DEVOUR-style mutation-luck passes entirely. One caveat: the Bigger Bag does not protect anyone in a fight, but it does raise Trench payouts. Kids may still read it as an advantage, so cap its bonus modestly and test sentiment.

For LiveOps, the concept's Saturday rhythm matches the breakout pattern of 2025. Grow a Garden held a live "Admin Abuse" event **one hour before** each Saturday update, posted as a Roblox Event so players saw it in local time ([Gamepressure](https://www.gamepressure.com/newsroom/what-is-admin-abuse-in-grow-a-garden-gag-we-explain-what-to-expec/z58490)). The 23 Aug 2025 Admin Abuse war pushed Roblox to a **47.4M CCU** record ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/how-youtubers-and-tiktokers-move-the-charts-in-2026)). MEGA BITE should run the Saturday event shark and Feeding Frenzy as a 14:00 UTC live event, ship the update at 15:00 UTC with a new code, and add a roughly monthly numbered season. DEVOUR has never released codes and patched through unscheduled quiet republishes ([DEVOUR updates hub](https://devourfeedandgrow.wiki/updates/)), so a predictable calendar is an easy edge.

The marketing evidence argues for designing clips into the game. Roblox YouTube passed 1 trillion views. One game went from **233 to 878 CCU within 60 minutes** of a micro-creator promotion through an influencer marketplace, and the Creator Affiliate Program shares revenue on referred players' purchases for six months ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/how-youtubers-and-tiktokers-move-the-charts-in-2026)). MEGA BITE's clip moments are already in the concept: minnow-to-Megalodon, a BREACH seen across the server, a school swallowed by TIDAL ROAR, and a "Day 1 vs Day 7" size comparison. Ads are cheap per play: the official Maximize Plays average is **$0.008 per play** ([DevForum Ads Manager](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ads-manager-updates-acquire-new-users-continuous-campaigns-and-more/3862159)). They only pay off if retention holds. Since June 2026, Recommended For You ranks games on **28-day retention (D1, D2–7, D8–28), play-through, session quality, co-play and spend** ([Roblox Newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/06/optimizing-discovery-great-games-reach-millions-players-roblox)). Co-play now counts toward recommendations, so this report **changes the concept's update order**: a simple "swim with friends: +EXP" bonus should ship at launch, with full Schools (crews, shared bank) following later. A commonly quoted bar of D1 ≥ 30% before scaling ads comes from a low-reliability source, so treat it as a rough guide.

## A release plan built around the Kids/Select gate

The concept note puts Galaxy Incremental first, and nothing here changes that. One step should start now regardless: the **2-month Premium clock** (or budgeting the refundable fee), creator age check and 2-Step Verification. These take calendar time, not work. A late-November or December 2026 launch would catch the Christmas break, but with seven original shark models, Galaxy still in front, and a 16+ trial still ahead, it is unrealistic for the full scope. Plan the dates around gates instead.

| Phase | What ships | Gate to move on |

| 0. Now (parallel to Galaxy) | Account age check, 2SV, Premium started; maturity questionnaire drafted as Mild; name trademark check | Premium 2 months old or fee budgeted |

## Conclusion

The research turns MEGA BITE from a "better DEVOUR" into a different kind of bet. The niche is small and every newcomer is fading, so the prize is not stealing DEVOUR's players. It is being the first shark game that Roblox will actually show to kids, and that holds them for longer than Be Fish's 19 minutes. Under Roblox's 2026 rules, fairness and a Mild rating are distribution requirements, not polish. A game that bullies small players, shows realistic gore or sells power risks failing the safety review, being shut out of the 5–15 audience, and being ranked down by a recommender that now looks at 28 days of retention. The concept's extraction twist is its strongest idea, and it only works if losing feels like a near-miss rather than a punishment.

Several important unknowns remain. No player complaint threads were found for any competitor. No EXP curves or kids' travel-time data exist, so every timing above is a hypothesis for the impatient-kid sim. Fish Evolution's numbers differ between trackers: one page records a 429 all-time peak with no passes noted, while another records 156 with no passes listed, possibly because they track different place IDs. And it is not known whether Be Fish's 97% is helped by like rewards. The first real data MEGA BITE collects in its 16+ trial will be worth more than any of this desk research, so the trial should be instrumented for D1, D7, session length, deaths-while-carrying and rage-quits from day one.
