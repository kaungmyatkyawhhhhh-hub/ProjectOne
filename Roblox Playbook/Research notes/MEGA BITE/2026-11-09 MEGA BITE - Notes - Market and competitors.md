# MEGA BITE: Competitor Market Map for Roblox Shark / Fish "Eat to Grow and Evolve" Games (as of 9 Nov 2026)

Data note: the Rolimons pages fetched on 9 Nov 2026 are sometimes cached. Relative dates on them ("created 2 years ago") can lag the real date, so use the absolute dates given. Where a Rolimons page would not load, other trackers (bloxodes, rowatcher) are used and labelled.

## 1. Which games exist and what are their stats?

### Takeaway
The genre splits into two groups. The first is big, older hits that are not pure eat-and-evolve games: SharkBite 2 (502M visits), Be a Shark (116M), Be Fish (105M) and Shark Evolution (52M, mostly dead now). The second is 2025–2026 eat-to-grow newcomers: DEVOUR (4.5M), Fish Evolution (5.1M) and Grow A Fish (7.7M). All of the newcomers now sit at 5–150 CCU. The highest-rated live game in the space is Be Fish (97% likes, 619 CCU, 19 min average playtime). It, not DEVOUR, is the main benchmark.

## 2. What does each game do (loop, evolution, zones, PvP, abilities, monetization, events)?

### Takeaway
DEVOUR is the closest copy of MEGA BITE. It has 3 zones (Reef / Open Ocean / The Deep), a fish tree, traits at Level 19, an "Apex" crown, and paid evolution trees plus a season pass. Fish Evolution is a pet and rebirth simulator with very aggressive pay-to-skip passes. Be Fish monetizes lightly (5 passes, all 99–649 R$). Be a Shark mixes PvP skull farming with egg hatching and trading.

### Cited Findings
**DEVOUR**
- Loop: start small, eat smaller fish, "eat to grow with no size limit," evolve into sharks and prehistoric creatures, fight players. Zones are the Reef, the Open Ocean and The Deep. The Deep is framed as a dare — [Rolimons description](https://www.rolimons.com/game/129827535941999); [fan wiki](https://devoureatfishoceananimals.wiki/)
- Progression uses a menu-driven Fish Tree (not automatic evolution). A "Choose a Trait" panel opens at Level 19 (a Healing trait has been seen). A "YOU ARE THE APEX" live-server crown exists, sometimes with 2x Shells. The death screen showed a +300 payout. The side menu has Fish Tree, Mastery, Shop and Packs. Bite (click or tap) is the only confirmed attack — [fan wiki](https://devoureatfishoceananimals.wiki/); [controls page](https://devoureatfishoceananimals.wiki/guides/controls/)
- Passes (R$): Prehistoric Tree 499, Abyssal Tree 299, Crustacean Tree 99, x2 Levels 499, x2 Shells 299, x2 Mutation Luck 299, x4 Mutation Luck 399, Season 3 300, Permanent Ocean Pass 799 — [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/129827535941999)
- Events: Halloween limited skins ran 2 Oct – 1 Nov 2026. A Tier 9 Elasmosaurus event ran 24–31 Oct 2026, and the "ELASMO" name prefix was removed on 30 Oct. Season 2 Ocean Pass was announced with Halloween, and the title now reads "[S3]". There were no codes at checks on 29 Sep and 9, 11 and 30 Oct — [fan wiki](https://devoureatfishoceananimals.wiki/). Rotrends tracked the game as "ELASMO DEVOUR" at one point — [rotrends](https://www.rotrends.com/game/10404293465/ELASMO-DEVOUR-Eat-Fish-Ocean-Animals)

**Fish Evolution**
- Loop: eat to gain levels, unlock new fish, rebirth for talents, collect pets and equipment. It offers premium, friend and group boosts — [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/71619988682604)

**Be Fish**
- Loop: start small, eat food to grow, unlock new fish, avoid being eaten by other players (16-player servers). Passes: Double Growth 649, Food Magnet 499, Double Loot 449, Fast XP 299, Lucky 99 — [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/94503612388426)

**Be a Shark**
- A "shark simulator and tycoon": eat fish and other sharks to grow, eat players to earn Skulls, collect and trade rare shark skins. Premium gives +100 starting Skulls. It claims weekly updates — [Roblox page](https://www.roblox.com/games/15334675936/Be-a-Shark)

- DEVOUR sells evolution branches themselves (Prehistoric, Abyssal, Crustacean trees), and its prehistoric apex sharks (the Megalodon equivalent) sit behind a 499 R$ pass. A kid who sees a paid Megalodon in the server will feel it is pay-to-win. MEGA BITE should keep all 7 evolutions earnable and sell cosmetics, convenience and boosts instead.
- Be Fish shows that a small, cheap pass list (five passes, all 99–649 R$) can coexist with 97% likes.
- Shark Evolution's 1,999 R$ x2 Damage is the classic pay-to-win pattern. Its 86% likes and dead CCU are consistent with that, but no direct evidence links the two.

## 3. What do players praise and complain about?

### Takeaway
Direct player reviews (Reddit, YouTube comments, Discord) could not be retrieved, so complaint evidence is indirect. The best proxy is the downvote share: 19% for DEVOUR and about 12% each for Be a Shark, SharkBite 2 and Shark Evolution, against 3% for Be Fish and 6% for Fish Evolution. The measurable technical complaint risk is DEVOUR's average ping of 231 ms.

### Cited Findings
- Downvote counts: DEVOUR 4,050 of 21,311; Be a Shark 17,549 of 147,130; SharkBite 2 53,900 of 460,298; Be Fish 20,794 of 693,213 — [Rolimons DEVOUR](https://www.rolimons.com/game/129827535941999); [Rolimons Be a Shark](https://www.rolimons.com/game/15334675936); [Rolimons SharkBite 2](https://www.rolimons.com/game/8908228901); [Rolimons Be Fish](https://www.rolimons.com/game/94503612388426)
- DEVOUR performance: average ping 231 ms with a maximum of 1,173 ms, average FPS 51 — [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/129827535941999)
- One DEVOUR fan site lists "teleport bugs, void deaths, and mobile controls" as categories it tracks, but describes no specific reports — [updates hub](https://devourfeedandgrow.wiki/updates/)
- In agar.io, the closest precedent for eat-to-grow PvP, players feed mass to each other to signal teaming, and "cartels" dominated leaderboards — [Wikipedia: Agar.io](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agar.io)
- Be a Shark's design rewards hunting players for Skulls. Guides tell players to target "exposed or distracted" players, which is the mechanism behind griefing big-on-small — [AllRoblox](https://allroblox.com/en/game/be-a-shark)

### Inferences
- Any game where big players earn the main currency by eating small players (Be a Shark's Skulls) builds griefing into the economy. MEGA BITE's no-PvP Safe Reef plus PvP limited to the Red Trench answers this directly. Banking carried EXP at the reef is also a known anti-snowball pattern.
- Expect teaming in the Red Trench (agar.io precedent). Consider anti-teaming measures such as reduced EXP for repeat kills on the same victim, and spawn protection when leaving the reef.

### Gaps
- No Reddit, YouTube-comment, DevForum or Discord complaint threads were found for DEVOUR, Be Fish, Be a Shark or Fish Evolution, and no fandom wiki "criticism" pages either. Searches returned only aggregator or SEO pages. This needs a manual check of YouTube comments and the Roblox game pages.

## 4. Why did DEVOUR rise and fall (1,747 → ~103 CCU)?

### Takeaway
No source states why. The data points to a short-retention game with sparse, laggy servers and evolution trees behind paywalls. Its promotion-driven peak came on 11 Sep 2026, and seasonal events (Halloween, Elasmosaurus) only lifted it to about 285 CCU.

### Cited Findings
- Visits grew from about 1.24M (mid-Aug) to 4.36M (30 Oct) to 4.48M (9 Nov 2026), so growth slowed to roughly 120K visits in the last 10 days — [updates hub](https://devourfeedandgrow.wiki/updates/); [fan wiki](https://devoureatfishoceananimals.wiki/); [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/129827535941999)
- Peak 1,747 on 11 Sep 2026. The highest peak in the last 30 days was 285, even during the Halloween event and the Elasmosaurus event (24–31 Oct) — [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/129827535941999); [fan wiki](https://devoureatfishoceananimals.wiki/)
- Average playtime is 7.98 min, against 19.10 min for Be Fish. Servers average 12.75 players out of 60. Ping averages 231 ms — [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/129827535941999)
- Three evolution trees are paid (99–499 R$), with a 300 R$ season pass and a 799 R$ permanent pass. No codes have been released — [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/129827535941999); [fan wiki](https://devoureatfishoceananimals.wiki/)
- The game has been renamed several times ("ELASMO DEVOUR," "[S3🔥] DEVOUR") — [rotrends](https://www.rotrends.com/game/10404293465/ELASMO-DEVOUR-Eat-Fish-Ocean-Animals); [fan wiki](https://devoureatfishoceananimals.wiki/)

### Inferences
- The likely causes are: (a) the September spike was probably ads or a sponsored or algorithm push that the game did not retain, given 8 min sessions; (b) lag, since 231 ms average ping is poor for a bite-timing game, especially on phones; (c) the 81% like rating cuts Roblox recommendation reach; (d) paid trees make free players feel locked out. These are inferences, not confirmed.
- Lesson for MEGA BITE: the first session must last well over 8 minutes (Be Fish reaches 19). Keep servers small and full (12–20 players). Keep the evolution path free.

### Gaps
- No developer statement, DevForum post or Discord announcement explaining the decline was found. No CCU history chart was retrieved for Aug–Oct, so a specific update gap could not be confirmed.

## 5. What made the off-Roblox games successful, and which mechanics transfer?

### Takeaway
Hungry Shark passed 1 billion downloads, agar.io had 113M mobile downloads, and Maneater sold 14M+ copies. They share three things: a constant hunger or size pressure, visible "I can eat that now" thresholds, and escalating hazards and spectacle. Agar.io's lesson is extreme simplicity plus growth driven by creators. Its warning is teaming.

### Cited Findings
- Hungry Shark: health drains faster over time, so you must keep eating. Bigger sharks unlock, and stronger sharks can eat species weaker ones cannot (a reef shark cannot eat stingrays, a mako can). Hazards escalate (helicopters dropping explosive barrels, fishermen). Sharks have upgradeable abilities (freezing breath, boost). The map is open and the game is freemium with gold and gems. Hungry Shark World had 10M downloads in its first 6 days (May 2016), and the franchise passed 1B downloads in 2022. Hungry Shark Primal was cancelled in January 2025 — [Wikipedia: Hungry Shark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungry_Shark); [Pocket Gamer: franchise 1 billion downloads](https://www.pocketgamer.com/hungry-shark-world/franchise-one-billion-downloads)
- Feed and Grow: Fish (released 8 Jan 2016, early access): you eat fish to grow into larger creatures and get creatures with abilities, 3 maps, and Deathmatch and Crab Madness modes. It is 80% positive from 13,096 reviews, costs $14.99, and has had no developer update in over 4 years — [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/429050/Feed_and_Grow_Fish)
- Agar.io: eat pellets and smaller players, larger cells move slower, mass slowly decays, splitting and viruses let small players fight back or hide. It had 10M mobile downloads in the first week and 113M by Dec 2016, 2B YouTube views by Mar 2016, and grew through YouTubers (PewDiePie, Vinesauce). Teaming "cartels" were a known problem — [Wikipedia: Agar.io](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agar.io)
- Depth (2014): asymmetric sharks vs divers, where sharks gain abilities by eating divers. Metacritic 65; critics praised the tension and faulted the balance — [Wikipedia: Depth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Depth_(video_game))
- Maneater has sold over 14 million copies — [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/15758-maneater-surpasses-14-million-copies-sold-since-launch)

### Inferences
Mechanics that transfer to MEGA BITE:
- Size-gated prey ("you can eat this now" glow) from Hungry Shark.
- Bigger means slower, plus hiding spots for small fish (agar.io viruses). This counters big-on-small griefing without banning it.
- Gentle hunger pressure, so players move between zones instead of AFK-farming.
- Escalating spectacle hazards, which MEGA BITE's sea bombs and squid boss already cover.
- Visible top-of-server status (agar.io leaderboard, DEVOUR's "Apex" crown).
- One standout ability per shark, matching Hungry Shark's freezing breath and boost.

### Gaps
- No primary data was found on the age makeup of these games' audiences or on kid-specific retention.

## 6. What gaps could a new game own?

### Takeaway
No live Roblox game combines all of these: a free, readable shark ladder up to Megalodon; protected newbie zones with opt-in high-reward PvP; full small servers with good mobile performance; sessions longer than 15 minutes; and fair monetization. Be Fish proves fairness and retention can reach 97% likes. DEVOUR proves the shark and prehistoric fantasy draws clicks, at a peak of 1,747 CCU in under 3 months.

### Cited Findings
- Be Fish: 97% likes, 19.1 min playtime, 16-player servers, 5 cheap passes — [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/94503612388426)
- DEVOUR: 1,747 peak CCU within about 2.5 months of its 27 Jun 2026 launch, despite 81% likes and paid trees — [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/129827535941999)
- Fish Evolution and Grow A Fish both rate about 94–95%, but with tiny CCU (22 and 5) and stale updates, so there is no strong active competitor in the "fair eat-and-grow" lane — [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/71619988682604); [bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/stats/games/grow-a-fish-9484532023)
- Shark Evolution once peaked at 18,714 CCU (2020) with a 40 min average playtime, showing demand for a deeper shark-evolution RPG, but it has not been updated in a year — [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/5600963870)

### Inferences
Gaps MEGA BITE can own:
1. **Free Megalodon.** All 7 evolutions are earnable, unlike DEVOUR's 499 R$ Prehistoric Tree and Fish Evolution's 29,999 R$ level gifts.
2. **Safe Reef, opt-in PvP.** No competitor clearly protects new players. Be a Shark pays players for eating players.
3. **Mobile performance.** Target well under DEVOUR's 231 ms ping and 51 FPS. Use 16–20-player servers, not 60.
4. **Session length.** Beat Be Fish's 19 min, with banked EXP and the squid boss as reasons to stay.
5. **Live updates.** Fish Evolution, Shark Evolution and Grow A Fish are all stale. A weekly update rhythm (Be a Shark claims weekly updates) plus codes, which DEVOUR has never released, is an easy win.
6. **Anti-teaming in the Red Trench**, learning from agar.io's cartels.

### Gaps
- Not validated with actual player feedback (see section 3). The demand estimate rests on CCU and like-ratio proxies only.
