# MEGA BITE: Monetization, LiveOps and Marketing (research as of 9 Nov 2026)

Method note: Roblox's own APIs (games/game-pass endpoints) were not reachable from the research sandbox, so store data comes from Rolimons/RoWatcher snapshots fetched on 9 Nov 2026. Sales counts and "most popular" labels are not public on Roblox store tabs and were not found anywhere; that question stays a gap.

## 1. What eat-and-grow / fish / shark games sell, and at what prices

### Takeaway
The closest direct competitor, DEVOUR (launched 27 Jun 2026, peaked at 1,747 CCU), sells x2 multipliers at 299–499, extra evolution "trees" (new creature lines) at 99–499, paid luck passes, and a season pass plus a 799 "permanent" pass. SharkBite 2, the big shark game of the genre, sells utility/gadget passes at 99–950. A 449 cap fits most of this. The exceptions are DEVOUR's 499 x2 Levels / 499 Prehistoric Tree / 799 permanent pass and SharkBite 2's 500–950 items.

### Cited Findings
- **DEVOUR** ("[S3] DEVOUR Eat Fish & Ocean Animals", Elasmonian Games). Created 27 Jun 2026, 60-player servers. Snapshot on 9 Nov 2026: 4,481,080 visits, 30,558 favourites, 81.0% rating (17,261 up / 4,050 down), average playtime 7.98 min. All-time peak 1,747 CCU on 11 Sep 2026. Past-30-day peak only 285, and 103 players at the time of the snapshot. It is already on "Season 3" and was updated 5 days before the snapshot. — [Rolimons: DEVOUR](https://www.rolimons.com/game/129827535941999)
- **DEVOUR game passes (Robux):** PREHISTORIC TREE 499; x2 Shells 299; x2 Levels 499; ABYSSAL TREE 299; x4 Mutation Luck 399; x2 Mutation Luck 299; CRUSTACEAN TREE 99; SEASON 3 pass 300 (priced 1 week earlier); PERMANENT OCEAN PASS 799 (priced 1 week earlier). — [Rolimons: DEVOUR](https://www.rolimons.com/game/129827535941999)
- **DEVOUR history:** public release ~27 Jun 2026 with three zones (reef, open ocean, THE DEEP) and predator unlocks. About 1.24M visits by mid-Aug 2026. Patches shipped as "quiet place republishes" on no fixed schedule. As of Aug 2026 the fan wiki found no confirmed public codes. — [DEVOUR Feed And Grow wiki: updates](https://devourfeedandgrow.wiki/updates/)
- **SharkBite 2** (Abracadabra, created 22 Feb 2022). 502,161,571 visits, 740,277 favourites, 88.3% rating, average playtime 10.67 min. All-time peak 61,026 CCU on 22 Oct 2022. On 9 Nov 2026: past-30-day peak 1,404 and past-7-day peak 1,265, with a Halloween tag in the title. — [Rolimons: SharkBite 2](https://www.rolimons.com/game/8908228901)
- **SharkBite 2 game passes (Robux):** Oxygen Tank 199; Flare Gun 99; Naval Mine Trap 699; High Rank Server Instant Access 500; Starter Pack 950; Boat Radio 450; Bait Bucket 295; Sea Scooter 800. — [Rolimons: SharkBite 2](https://www.rolimons.com/game/8908228901)
- **Fish Evolution** ("Species evolution"). Released Jun 2025, 5.1M visits, 94.3% like ratio, all-time peak only 156 CCU (Mar 2026), no game passes listed. — [RoWatcher: Fish Evolution](https://rowatcher.com/games/7999885745/fish-evolution)
- Other ocean games found but not examined: Mega Fish Evolution, Grow a Fish Pond, SCP-Megalodon Shark. — [RoWatcher: Mega Fish Evolution](https://rowatcher.com/games/8403788222/mega-fish-evolution); [RoWatcher: Grow a Fish Pond](https://rowatcher.com/games/7519170515/grow-a-fish-pond); [RoWatcher: SCP-Megalodon Shark](https://rowatcher.com/games/8545036896/scp-megalodon-shark)

### Inferences
- DEVOUR is the clearest template and a warning at the same time. It hit about 1.7K CCU in its third month and then fell about 85% (285 peak in the past 30 days) despite seasonal content. Its 8-minute average session is short. Retention, not monetization, seems to be the genre's weak point. This is a guess from the CCU curve, not from DEVOUR's own analytics.
- MEGA BITE's planned x2 EXP pass matches DEVOUR's x2 Levels and x2 Shells. Under the 449 cap it would sit at about 299–399 (DEVOUR charges 499).
- DEVOUR's "evolution tree" passes (99/299/499) are a strong genre item: they sell **new creature lines**, not raw power. For MEGA BITE, a paid tree would need to give cosmetic or PvE-only differences to meet the "nothing bought helps PvP" rule. A cosmetic "Prehistoric skin line" (Megalodon, Dunkleosteus) unlocked by pass is one way to do this.
- DEVOUR's Mutation Luck passes are luck boosts on random outcomes. Under the owner's "no paid random items" rule, MEGA BITE should skip this category.
- SharkBite 2 shows that gadget and convenience items (oxygen, scooter, bait) sell in shark games. Its 950 Starter Pack suggests bundles are normal, but MEGA BITE would cap at 449.

### Gaps
- No public sales counts or "most popular" labels for any of these passes. Roblox does not show pass sales, and Rolimons/RoWatcher do not track them.
- Grow a Fish!, Feed-and-Grow-style clones and "+1 every second" ocean games were not reviewed (out of tool budget). Their passes and prices are unknown.
- No developer-disclosed revenue for DEVOUR or SharkBite 2 was found.

## 2. Cosmetic / convenience items in kids' games (2026); is Megalodon content a driver?

### Takeaway
No hard 2026 sales data for skins, trails, auto-collect or offline gains was found. The evidence is indirect. The competitors' stores favour multipliers, new creature lines and convenience gadgets. Megalodon or prehistoric content is clearly used as premium: DEVOUR's priciest tree is "PREHISTORIC".

### Cited Findings
- DEVOUR's highest-priced tree pass is PREHISTORIC TREE at 499, versus ABYSSAL 299 and CRUSTACEAN 99. — [Rolimons: DEVOUR](https://www.rolimons.com/game/129827535941999)
- SharkBite 2's convenience/gadget passes run 99–800 (Oxygen Tank, Sea Scooter, Bait Bucket). — [Rolimons: SharkBite 2](https://www.rolimons.com/game/8908228901)
- Megalodon is used as a headline hook in other ocean games: a "secret Megalodon" in Fish It gets Spanish-language YouTuber coverage, and SCP-Megalodon Shark exists as a standalone game. — [Recapio digest of SilvioGamer Fish It video](https://recapio.com/digest/me-fui-a-pescar-al-megalodon-secreto-del-oceano-silviogamer-en-fish-it-by-silvio-gamer); [RoWatcher: SCP-Megalodon Shark](https://rowatcher.com/games/8545036896/scp-megalodon-shark)

### Inferences
- The suggested store, all items 449 or less, uses the competitors' prices as anchors. These prices are proposals, not researched figures:
  - x2 EXP (about 299–399)
  - Bigger Bag (about 149–249, a carry-limit convenience comparable to SharkBite 2's Oxygen Tank at 199)
  - VIP (about 199–399)
  - Prehistoric/Megalodon cosmetic skin line (about 299–449, the premium slot)
  - Trails and ability colour variants (about 49–149 developer products)
- Making Megalodon an *earned* top evolution, with a paid *cosmetic* Megalodon skin variant, keeps the PvP rule intact and uses the genre's strongest hook.

### Gaps
- No 2026 source quantified skin vs. trail vs. auto-collect vs. offline-gains revenue in kids' simulators.

## 3. LiveOps patterns (cadence, events, codes, admin abuse, limited creatures)

### Takeaway
The breakout pattern of 2025–2026 is a fixed weekly update with a live "Admin Abuse" event an hour before it. Grow a Garden ran this on Saturdays. The owner's Saturday 15:00 UTC plan matches this exact pattern. In the shark/ocean niche, games run seasonal tags (SharkBite 2's Halloween title) and numbered seasons (DEVOUR S3 within ~4 months).

### Cited Findings
- Grow a Garden's "Admin Abuse" ran **one hour before the Saturday update** (9 AM EST). During it, developers join servers, trigger special weather that mutates plants, and hand out free items such as a pet. It was posted as a Roblox Event so players could see local times. — [Gamepressure, 23 Aug 2025](https://www.gamepressure.com/newsroom/what-is-admin-abuse-in-grow-a-garden-gag-we-explain-what-to-expec/z58490)
- The 23 Aug 2025 "Admin Abuse War" between Grow a Garden and Steal a Brainrot pushed Roblox to a 47.4M platform-wide CCU record. A Grow a Garden update on 21 Jun 2025 had already taken it to 21.3M CCU. — [RoWatcher, 2 Feb 2026](https://rowatcher.com/news/how-youtubers-and-tiktokers-move-the-charts-in-2026)
- DEVOUR moved to "Season 3" about 4 months after launch, with a 300-Robux season pass. Its patches were unscheduled quiet republishes. — [Rolimons: DEVOUR](https://www.rolimons.com/game/129827535941999); [DEVOUR wiki: updates](https://devourfeedandgrow.wiki/updates/)
- SharkBite 2 runs holiday-themed title tags (Halloween in Nov 2026) and posts codes during seasonal updates (e.g., an "XMAS UPDATE" code list in Dec 2025). — [Rolimons: SharkBite 2](https://www.rolimons.com/game/8908228901); [MobileMatters: SharkBite 2 codes (Dec 2025)](https://mobilematters.gg/redeem-codes/sharkbite-2)

### Inferences
- MEGA BITE's weekly rhythm could be:
  - Saturday 14:00 UTC: "Feeding Frenzy" admin event, with free buffs, a boss shark spawn and free cosmetics.
  - Saturday 15:00 UTC: the update, with a new code in the update log.
  - Each update posted as a Roblox Event so the time shows in every player's own timezone.
- A limited-time *earnable* creature or skin each week, given free during the event, fits the rules: nothing paid and nothing random. DEVOUR's slow, unscheduled patching may be part of why it decayed. This is a guess.
- A roughly monthly numbered "season" (DEVOUR-style) gives a reason to return without needing a new map every week.

### Gaps
- No SharkBite 2 Shark Week event documentation was found. Searches returned only TV and Discovery material.
- Fisch event examples were not researched (out of budget).
- No data was found on group-reward conversion in ocean games.

## 4. Marketing: TikTok/Shorts trends, YouTubers, how ocean games got first players

### Takeaway
Roblox YouTube/TikTok is the main discovery funnel for simulators. Mid-tier and micro creators, and influencer marketplaces, can move CCU within an hour. No shark-specific 2025–2026 TikTok trend data or DEVOUR launch-channel data was found.

### Cited Findings
- Roblox YouTube videos passed 1 trillion all-time views by Aug 2025. In peak summer months, 1 in 5 game-related TikToks mentioned Roblox. Star Codes ended in Oct 2025 and were replaced by tiered Creator Rewards. — [RoWatcher, 2 Feb 2026](https://rowatcher.com/news/how-youtubers-and-tiktokers-move-the-charts-in-2026)
- Big Roblox creators and their reported subscriber counts: KreekCraft 16M+, Flamingo 14M+, GamingWithKev ~12.7M, ItsFunneh 11M+, Leah Ashe ~5.2M on YouTube. — [RoWatcher, 2 Feb 2026](https://rowatcher.com/news/how-youtubers-and-tiktokers-move-the-charts-in-2026)
- The influencer marketplace Social Seed reported 10,000+ developers/creators in 2025. One game went from 233 to 878 CCU within 60 minutes of a creator promotion. — [RoWatcher, 2 Feb 2026](https://rowatcher.com/news/how-youtubers-and-tiktokers-move-the-charts-in-2026)
- Roblox's Creator Affiliate Program gives referrers a revenue share on Robux purchases from referred users during their first six months. — [RoWatcher, 2 Feb 2026](https://rowatcher.com/news/how-youtubers-and-tiktokers-move-the-charts-in-2026)
- The SilvioGamer Fish It video, about fishing up a "secret Megalodon", shows that Megalodon reveals are used as YouTube hooks in ocean games. — [Recapio digest](https://recapio.com/digest/me-fui-a-pescar-al-megalodon-secreto-del-oceano-silviogamer-en-fish-it-by-silvio-gamer)

### Inferences
- Clip-ready moments for MEGA BITE short videos:
  - A tiny fish evolving into a Megalodon
  - A big shark eating a whole school of fish
  - Getting eaten by an NPC predator
  - "Day 1 vs Day 7" size comparisons

  These match the "design for clips" pattern the RoWatcher article draws from the Grow a Garden and Steal a Brainrot cases.
- A cheap first creator push could use Social Seed-style micro-creator marketplaces or the Creator Affiliate link, rather than top-tier YouTubers.

### Gaps
- No data on which YouTubers specifically cover eat-and-grow games in 2026.
- No 2025–2026 TikTok trend metrics for "shark evolution" or "eat everything" content.
- No source said how DEVOUR got its first players. Its Sep 2026 peak may reflect ads or a creator video, but this is unverified.

## 5. Roblox ads (Sponsored/Ads Manager) costs and Home recommendation signals (2026)

### Takeaway
Official cost-per-play is under 1 US cent (about $8 per 1,000 plays on Maximize Plays, Aug 2025). Since June 2026, Roblox's Recommended For You ranks games on 28-day retention (D1, D2–7, D8–28), play-through, session quality, co-play and spend. Ads only pay off if retention holds afterwards.

### Cited Findings
- Official average cost per play: Maximize Plays $0.008 (also described as "currently under $0.01"), Drive Retention $0.007, Reactivate Users $0.011. Acquire New Users targets players who haven't visited in 180 days, and Roblox recommends it for established games. New or small experiences are pointed to Maximize Plays. There are continuous campaigns, with auto budget recommendations and no stated minimum. Posted 5 Aug 2025. — [DevForum: Ads Manager Updates](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ads-manager-updates-acquire-new-users-continuous-campaigns-and-more/3862159)
- A community calculation in that thread (unofficial): about $80 per 10,000 plays, and 16 ad credits/day gives about 2,000 visits on Maximize Plays. — [DevForum thread replies](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ads-manager-updates-acquire-new-users-continuous-campaigns-and-more/3862159)
- RoWatcher claims that about 5,000 Robux/day for at least 7 days is a "floor". It also says simulators "reportedly" spend 20,000+ Robux/day, CTR is 1–2%, and 5,000 Robux/day gives about 300–600 visits/day. It advises spending only after D1 retention is above 30%. It cites no sources, and its dates are inconsistent (published 2025-01-16, modified 2026-06-21), so treat it as low reliability. Its visits-per-Robux numbers are far worse than the official cost-per-play. — [RoWatcher: minimum ad budget](https://rowatcher.com/news/roblox-ads-in-2026-the-minimum-budget-that-actually-works); contradicted by [DevForum official CPP](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ads-manager-updates-acquire-new-users-continuous-campaigns-and-more/3862159)
- Roblox Newsroom (15 Jun 2026, Chief Growth Officer John Ciancutti), on Recommended For You:
  - The lookback window grew from 7 to 28 days.
  - Ranking signals are retention at D1, D2–7 and D8–28.
  - The old qualified play-through rate was split into play-through, session quality and spend.
  - Returning players, co-play (bringing friends) and spending also count.
  - The platform had 132M DAU (Q1 2026).
  — [Roblox Newsroom, 15 Jun 2026](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/06/optimizing-discovery-great-games-reach-millions-players-roblox)

### Inferences
- First spend for MEGA BITE: a small Maximize Plays campaign. At official rates, about $10–20/day buys about 1,250–2,500 plays/day. Run it for 3–5 days after soft-launch fixes, and judge it by D1 and D7 retention in Creator Analytics, not by visits.
  - Scale up only if D1 is 30% or higher and D7 is healthy (the D1 30% bar is from RoWatcher, low reliability).
  - (From the user's memory, not research: GYM ARC ads ran at about $100/day for 2 days.)
- Because co-play now counts, a "bring a friend" bonus (EXP boost when playing with friends, free trail for inviting) directly feeds the algorithm.
- The 28-day window favours games that keep players for weeks. The weekly Saturday cadence plus seasons supports the D8–28 signal.

### Gaps
- No official 2026 CPM or CPC numbers were found. Roblox reports cost per play.
- The detailed DevForum post on signal weights ("Recommended For You algorithm improvements that better value long-term retention") was referenced but not fetched.

## 6. Seasonal timing (Shark Week, school holidays, December) and launch timing

### Takeaway
Shark Week runs in July or early August: 7–13 Jul 2024, 20–26 Jul 2025, 26 Jul–1 Aug 2026. Roblox's biggest CCU peaks came in northern-summer holidays (Jun–Aug 2025). DEVOUR launched in late June 2026 and peaked in mid-September.

### Cited Findings
- Shark Week dates: 2024 Jul 7–13, 2025 Jul 20–26, 2026 Jul 26–Aug 1. It is usually in July and sometimes runs into August. — [Wikipedia: Shark Week](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shark_Week)
- Grow a Garden hit 21.3M CCU after a 21 Jun 2025 update. The Roblox platform record of 47.4M CCU came on 23 Aug 2025, during the summer holidays. — [RoWatcher, 2 Feb 2026](https://rowatcher.com/news/how-youtubers-and-tiktokers-move-the-charts-in-2026)
- DEVOUR was created 27 Jun 2026 (summer) and hit its all-time peak on 11 Sep 2026. — [Rolimons: DEVOUR](https://www.rolimons.com/game/129827535941999)

### Inferences
- Today is 9 Nov 2026. Launching in late Nov or early Dec 2026 catches the December/Christmas break. A winter or Christmas event could follow in about the 3rd week of December.
- Shark Week 2027 (expected late July, dates unconfirmed) is the natural point for a big "Shark Week" season, once the game has 6+ months of retention data.
- Launch on a Friday or Saturday so the first Saturday 15:00 UTC event lands in launch week.

### Gaps
- No 2026 Roblox data on December vs. summer CCU seasonality.
- Shark Week 2027 dates are not yet announced.
- Regional school holiday calendars were not researched.
