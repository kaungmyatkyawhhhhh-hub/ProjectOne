## The map style (what to copy)
1. **Underwater without water:** the camera is in thick blue Atmosphere/fog, not Terrain water. Close things are clear and colourful; far things fade to a deep royal blue (#1A3F8C-ish). Top of the screen is brighter blue (surface light).
2. **Pale sand floor** (very light blue-white, #CFE3EE-ish) with soft ripple marks and big **diagonal light shafts / caustic stripes** across it. The floor is the brightest thing in the scene, so everything on it reads.
3. **Big background rock formations:** chunky low-poly mesas and pillars with flat tops, blue-tinted by the fog, some topped with red/pink **table coral "mushrooms"**. They frame the play area like walls (natural borders) and give depth layers.
4. **Props, cartoon low-poly, saturated:** blue-violet **sea palms / fern plants**, orange-yellow **bubble coral clusters**, pink-red **starfish**, green **kelp sprouts**, small round sea plants, rocks with bevels. Dense around set pieces, sparse in between.
5. **Neon + marquee bulbs** on landmarks: the sign has a dark navy plate, chunky cyan bevelled letters, a ring of warm white bulbs and a red sub-banner. Glass domes glow cyan (Neon/ForceField-like) with bloom.
6. **Readable gameplay markers:** round pads with **black-yellow hazard stripes**, wooden notice boards with gold title text and a green call to action.
7. **Life:** bubbles rising everywhere (particle streams from the floor and props), small fish swimming past, a whale silhouette far away.
8. **Lighting:** Roblox Future-like: bright key from above, cool blue ambient, bloom only on neon/bulbs, soft shadows. Colour palette: cyan/turquoise + deep blue, with warm accents (red, orange, gold) for contrast.

## How MEGA BITE uses it
- Safe Reef = this look exactly (pale sand, blue fog, rock mesas as borders, sea palms + bubble coral + starfish, light shafts, bubbles). The bank vent / evolve shrine get the neon + bulb treatment like the SHARK SHOP sign (our own design and words, never copied).
- Open Sea = the same kit, fog deeper and darker blue, bigger set pieces; Red Trench = the same kit tinted violet with red glowing vents.
- Fits the style guide (bright, chunky, mood in colour not darkness) and the map plan (natural rock borders, set pieces, ambient life).
- Roblox setup to get the look: Lighting Technology Future; Atmosphere Density ~0.45, Offset ~0.1, Color/Decay blue; no Terrain water (swimming is our own controller); ColorCorrection slight saturation up; Bloom threshold high; a big transparent-blue sky cover; caustics as a scrolling Decal/Texture on the floor; light shafts as tall transparent Neon wedges or Beams.
