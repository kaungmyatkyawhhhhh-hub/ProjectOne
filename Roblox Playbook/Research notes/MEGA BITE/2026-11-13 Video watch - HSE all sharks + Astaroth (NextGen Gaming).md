## What the game does (things to learn from)
1. **Shark select = tier tracks.** Four rows of small slots at the bottom: COMMON (teal), TITAN (purple), SPECIAL (gold), DEMON (red). The current shark's slot is lit. The WHOLE ROOM changes colour for the tier (blue sea normally, blood red for Demon). You can see every shark you do not own yet, with its price, on the same screen.
2. **The demon shark recolours the whole world.** During the run the sea, sky and rocks are all red. The shark itself is the opposite colour (teal glow, pink body) so it always stands out.
3. **The world fights back when you are strong.** New hunters come only for demon sharks: red submarines, spiky mines, mecha eels. Being hunted is the reward for being big.
4. **Landmarks are everywhere and are used:** a stone ring with a glowing spiral PORTAL (seen twice), a GIANT CRASHED ROBOT/ship the size of the screen, a beach town with palm trees and houses on the surface, junk boats, cracked rocks with blue lightning, jellyfish swarms, floating crates. About one new set piece every 10-20 s of swimming.
5. **Pun pop-ups for every feat** (white, small, near the shark, a different one per prey type): FISH SOUP!, SCHOOLS OUT!, CHOMP FEST, CAN OPENER!, JUNK FOOD!, SNACK ATTACK!, SURF N TURF!, GOTTA LOTTA BOTTLE, PROTEIN SHAKE!, CORAL THIEF!, FREE RANGE!, NEEDED A BIGGER BOAT!, SUPER SCOFF, THREAT NEUTRALIZED!, ELIMINATED!, MAX BOOST, BINGE BONUS X2.
6. **Big gold multiplier at top centre:** X2, X6, X8, X60, X90, X120. It is the biggest text on screen.
7. **A named rush meter at bottom centre:** "DEMON RUSH" with a flame icon and a bar. Same place as our Gold Rush meter.
8. **Kill feed with faces:** "Player EATS [other shark]" with two round portraits, then "FINSANE KILL" in big gold letters.
9. **Ability buttons, right side:** two small round buttons with cooldown numbers + one yellow hexagon with a % charge and 3 pips under it. Joystick ring bottom left. Health and boost bars top right; star score + run timer top left.
10. **Shield bubble** (blue sphere) and a big yellow burst on the ultimate.

## Sound (measured 300-380 s, not heard)
- Loudness about -16 dB RMS, peak -1.8 dB. A constant music bed (steady low lines in the spectrogram) under almost continuous effects.
- About 2 sound starts per second. A low thump (60-120 Hz) roughly every 0.6 s (62 in 80 s) = the bites. Bright sparkle sounds (5-10 kHz) 30 in 80 s.
- Energy by band: 24% deep bass, 31% low, 32% mid, 11% upper, 2% top. Bass-heavy: on a phone speaker the bites would lose most of their weight, so they also carry a mid click.
