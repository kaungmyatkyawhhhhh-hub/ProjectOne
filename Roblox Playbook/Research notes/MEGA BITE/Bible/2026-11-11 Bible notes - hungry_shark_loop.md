# Hungry Shark Series: Core Loop, Systems, Economy, Retention (for MEGA BITE)

Scope: Hungry Shark Evolution (HSE, 2012 to now, still updated in 2026), Hungry Shark World (HSW, 2016), competitor contrast (Maneater, SharkBite 2, Roblox "Be a Shark"). Researched 11 Nov 2026. Note: many fandom/walkthrough pages blocked fetches, so some systems (accessories list, exact hunger drain numbers, juice/feel) have thin sourcing; see Gaps.

## 1. Exact core loop and pacing (run length, why players die, how hunger drives movement)

### Takeaway
Every run is a single life: health doubles as a hunger meter that drains constantly and is refilled only by eating, so the player must keep swimming toward food; the run ends when health hits zero from starvation, hazards, or bigger predators, and drain accelerates the longer you survive, which caps run length naturally. Coins from the run are spent between runs on upgrades and the next shark.

### Cited Findings
- Health doubles as a stamina meter: it drains if you stop eating and the run ends at zero; larger sharks, jellyfish and other hazards also lower it. Pacing alternates calm swimming with feeding frenzies; little of the world is open at the start. — [AppSpy review](https://www.appspy.com/hungry-shark-evolution/review/)
- Coins collected during a run are spent on upgrades after death; missions unlocked by nudging sea shells award coins and XP; levelling up grows the shark into a greater species. — [AppSpy review](https://www.appspy.com/hungry-shark-evolution/review/)
- Players complain "the longer you stay alive your health starts to drain ridiculously fast"; around 300k score one player is "repeatedly attacked by submarines, great whites and electro sharks" and finds it unfair; another: "THE SHARKS DIE WAAAY TO FAST!" but calls the challenge fun. — [JustUseApp HSE reviews](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/535500008/hungry-shark-evolution/reviews)
- HSW: "Late in the game, the toughest enemies stop progressing, and your health drains faster until you can't keep up." — [TV Tropes HSW](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/HungrySharkWorld)
- HSE health drain differs by shark: Reef Shark slowest, Megalodon and Big Daddy drain rapidly (especially on land), Ghost Shark very slowly. Health pools: Behellmouth 750 (highest), Behemoth 700, Abyssaurus 575. — [Hungry Shark Wiki: Shark](https://hungry-shark.fandom.com/wiki/Shark)
- Death causes in HSW: larger enemy sharks, dolphins ("Devious Dolphins attack sharks of any size"), garbage (eating it harms you), toxic barrels, late-game drain. Smaller sharks can latch onto you and slow you for a few seconds. — [TV Tropes HSW](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/HungrySharkWorld)
- HSE hazards: mines, toxic waste barrels, poisonous jellyfish, seals that bite back; divers' spears; bigger sharks. — [Common Sense Media](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/app-reviews/hungry-shark-evolution); [Latin Times guide](https://latintimes.com/hungry-shark-evolution-tips-get-megalodon-defeat-giant-crab-and-submarine-200354)
- Score multiplier ladder within a run (non-rush max 8x; per-shark multipliers stack with gold rush). — [Hungry Shark Wiki: Gold Rush](https://hungry-shark.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_Rush)
- Mission type "survive for a set time while maintaining a specific score multiplier" exists, implying the multiplier is lost if you stop eating / get hit. — [Ubisoft FAQ: missions](https://ubisoft-mobile.helpshift.com/hc/en/8-hungry-shark-evolution/faq/76-what-are-the-missions-and-how-to-complete-them)
- HSW: eating prey above your size can cost you health; larger sharks become prey once you unlock bigger sharks. — [Pocket Gamer HSW review](https://www.pocketgamer.com/hungry-shark-world/review)
- FGOL rejects the "coffee-break" session rule, deliberately allowing long sessions, and refuses energy mechanics ("they just get in the way of the experience for us" — Ian Harper). — [MCV studio spotlight](https://www.mcvuk.com/development/studio-spotlight-future-games-of-london)

### Inferences
- Hunger is the engine of movement: because standing still kills you, the map's food distribution is effectively the level design. Sparse food near hazards = tension; dense schools = relief/frenzy.
- The rising drain is a soft timer that ends runs without an energy system; tune it so a "good" run on a new shark is a few minutes and an expert run is longer, but always finite. Players perceive the late spike as unfair when combined with stacked aggressive enemies, so for kids ramp gently and telegraph (screen edge red pulse, heartbeat sound).
- Per-shark drain is a balancing knob: bigger sharks hit harder but must eat more, which keeps big sharks moving and hunting rather than idling.

### Gaps
- No source gave exact per-second drain values or run-length averages; a typical run length (minutes) was not found in reliable sources.

## 2. Every system and how it hooks players

### Takeaway
HSE/HSW layer many small goal systems on top of the eat loop: gold rush (point-milestone invincible frenzy), mega gold rush (HSW: eat anything), per-shark missions found via shells, a shark ladder unlocked by growth + coins, baby sharks/pets with stat bonuses, accessories and set bonuses, portals with timers, bosses (Giant Crab, Submarine, Roboshark 2.0), hidden collectibles (HUNGRY letters, fossils, sunken items), daily chest, contests, and frequent themed live events. Each provides a reason for "one more run".

### Cited Findings
**Gold Rush**
- First gold rush triggers at a per-shark point total; later ones when the coin bar at the bottom of the screen fills. During it the shark is invincible, has unlimited boost, all live prey (except jellyfish) turn golden and give coins; creatures that could have turned gold but didn't give a guaranteed gem. — [Hungry Shark Wiki: Gold Rush](https://hungry-shark.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_Rush)
- Points to trigger first rush (HSE): Reef 2,500; Mako 5,000; Hammerhead 10,000; Tiger 16,000; Great White 25,000; Megalodon 125,000; Big Daddy/Mr Snappy 150,000; Alan 175,000; Moby Dick/Nessie 200,000; Leo/Sharkjira/Abysshark/Kraken/Luminite 400,000; Pyro 500,000. — [Hungry Shark Wiki: Gold Rush](https://hungry-shark.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_Rush)
- Gold rush multiplier / max: Reef 2x/16x, Mako 3x/24x, Hammerhead 4x/32x, Tiger 5x/40x, Great White 6x/48x, Megalodon 8x/64x, Big Daddy 10x/80x, Mr Snappy 12x/96x, top sharks 15x/120x. HSW by tier: XS 2x/24x up to Apex 9x/108x. Duration varies (Great White's rush much longer than Megalodon's). — [Hungry Shark Wiki: Gold Rush](https://hungry-shark.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_Rush)
- Variant rushes per special shark: Ghoul Rush (Ghost Shark: not invincible, faster drain, speed boost) and Moon Rush (Wereshark: everything else slowed). — [Hungry Shark Wiki: Gold Rush](https://hungry-shark.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_Rush)
- Reviewers specifically praise the gold rush mechanic as satisfying/addictive. — [JustUseApp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/535500008/hungry-shark-evolution/reviews)

**Mega Gold Rush (HSW)**
- After several gold rushes, a Mega Gold Rush lasts longer and lets you eat any size of creature or object; the Cool Crown extends gold rush duration 25%. — [TV Tropes HSW](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/HungrySharkWorld)
- Pocket Gamer describes it as charged by collecting coins and granting unlimited boost for a short time. — [Pocket Gamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/hungry-shark-world/review)
- Bigger enemy sharks can't be eaten outside a Mega Gold Rush until you play as a !! shark. — [TV Tropes HSW](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/HungrySharkWorld)

**HUNGRY letters**
- H-U-N-G-R-Y letters appear in random spots each level; collecting all temporarily super-sizes your shark. — [TV Tropes HSW](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/HungrySharkWorld)

**Missions**
- Each standard HSE shark has nine missions with large coin rewards. — [Hungry Shark Wiki: Coins](https://hungry-shark.fandom.com/wiki/Coins)
- Missions are revealed by finding shells in the ocean; types: score goals, targeted hunts for specific prey, survive with a multiplier. — [Ubisoft FAQ](https://ubisoft-mobile.helpshift.com/hc/en/8-hungry-shark-evolution/faq/76-what-are-the-missions-and-how-to-complete-them)
- Complaint: mission targets are hard to find when needed but plentiful otherwise; praise for hidden missions and daily challenges. Bug complaint: missions not progressing. — [JustUseApp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/535500008/hungry-shark-evolution/reviews); [Kimola](https://kimola.com/reports/unlock-hungry-shark-evolution-insights-dive-deeper-google-play-en-140945)
- HSW: cosmetics unlock after a set number of missions; daily missions judged unengaging by Pocket Gamer.biz. — [TV Tropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/HungrySharkWorld); [PG.biz IAP Inspector](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/iap-inspector-hungry-shark-world/)

**Shark ladder / evolution**
- HSE: next regular shark unlocks when the previous reaches level 10 with 100% XP, or by gems. Tiers: Common, Titan, Special (Kempstein's Lab, lab points), Evolved, Demon. HSW tiers XS, S, M, L, XL, XXL, !!, Apex; costs in coins, gems, pearls or schematics; some sharks via Splash Pass. — [Hungry Shark Wiki: Shark](https://hungry-shark.fandom.com/wiki/Shark)
- Unlock costs (HSE): Mako 2,000 growth pts + 1,500 coins; Hammerhead 3,950 + 6,000; Tiger 7,350 + 15,000; Great White 14,500 + 35,000; Megalodon 42,550 + 50,000. — [Latin Times](https://latintimes.com/hungry-shark-evolution-tips-get-megalodon-defeat-giant-crab-and-submarine-200354)
- Signature abilities on special sharks: Electro paralyses prey, Ice freezes prey, Sharkjira atomic breath, Abyssaurus Rex mind control + angler pets, DarkHammer ghost pets + shield, Shar-khan stun shout. — [Hungry Shark Wiki: Shark](https://hungry-shark.fandom.com/wiki/Shark)
- Gold upgrades speed, bite, boost in HSW. — [PG.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/iap-inspector-hungry-shark-world/)
- Player quote: "won't stop playing until you get every shark in the game." — [JustUseApp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/535500008/hungry-shark-evolution/reviews)

**Baby sharks / pets**
- HSE baby sharks help eat and add stamina: Baby Reef +5 stamina for 1,000 coins up to Baby Megalodon +30 stamina for 900 gems. — [Latin Times](https://latintimes.com/hungry-shark-evolution-tips-get-megalodon-defeat-giant-crab-and-submarine-200354)
- Baby versions exist for Common and Titan sharks plus Electro, Ice, Robo, Pyro, Natasha. — [Hungry Shark Wiki: Shark](https://hungry-shark.fandom.com/wiki/Shark)

Pets increase points earned. Kraken pet was an early-download gift; killer whale a daily contest prize. — [TV Tropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/HungrySharkWorld); [PG.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/iap-inspector-hungry-shark-world/); [Gamezebo](https://www.gamezebo.com/reviews/hungry-shark-world-review-sink-your-teeth-in/)

- HSE also has a portal to the Titanic's passengers. — [Common Sense Media](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/app-reviews/hungry-shark-evolution)
- Sharks big enough eat humans and birds, and can leap to the surface (pelican catching). — [TV Tropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/HungrySharkWorld); [AppSpy](https://www.appspy.com/hungry-shark-evolution/review/)

**Bosses**
- Giant Crab in a lair bottom-left: dodge 3 pincer strikes to expose a glowing weak point; bigger crabs have longer reach. Submarine: shots drain health fast; beaten only by boost-ramming its front head-on. — [Latin Times](https://latintimes.com/hungry-shark-evolution-tips-get-megalodon-defeat-giant-crab-and-submarine-200354)
- HSW: Colossal Squid boss during an event; ghost sharks immune unless certain pets/items or gold rush. — [TV Tropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/HungrySharkWorld)

**Collectibles / contests / daily**
- HSW fossils unlock certain sharks; dying before a fossil registers loses it. Contests reward gems by performance; premium cosmetics for top 2%. Pearls only from Extinction mode. — [TV Tropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/HungrySharkWorld)

- Daily Bonus present gives coins and gems; schools of fish may give coins; golden creatures give escalating coins. — [Hungry Shark Wiki: Coins](https://hungry-shark.fandom.com/wiki/Coins)

### Inferences
- The hook pattern is "nested meters": hunger (seconds), multiplier (tens of seconds), gold rush bar (a minute or two), mission progress (session), shark growth XP (several sessions), next-shark unlock (days). Every bite advances several meters at once, which is why a single chomp feels valuable.
- Gold rush is a scheduled power fantasy: it is earned by score, gives invincibility + unlimited boost + money, and scales its trigger with shark size so every shark gets them at a similar pace.
- Portals with timers are mini-runs inside a run (refill-by-eating timer), a cheap way to add a fresh biome; the cooldown/ticket was a monetisation lever (avoid real-money skip for kids; use a free cooldown or a quest key).
- Size-gated barriers turn the shark ladder into map unlocks, so progress is visible in the world, not just in a menu. For MEGA BITE this maps naturally to safe zone -> deeper zones.
- Signature abilities per shark (freeze, shock, breath, stun shout) are what make small players covet big sharks — directly relevant to the user's goal of "abilities big sharks have that make small players want them".

## 3. Monetisation and what players feel is fair/unfair

### Takeaway
Two currencies (coins soft, gems hard; HSW adds pearls/schematics), with gems used to skip shark unlocks, buy premium sharks/babies, revive, and skip portal cooldowns; rewarded ads give coins and revives. Players accept it because there is no energy system and early progress is smooth, but complain about gem prices, late-game grind and rising revive cost.

### Cited Findings
- HSW IAP: gold 10,000 for $4.99 up to 330,000 for $99.99; gems 100 for $4.99 to 3,300 for $99.99 (1 gem ~ 100 gold). Revive via gems or rewarded video; video revives capped at 2 and gem cost rises with each death. Great White can be unlocked immediately for ~$60. Costs rise faster than income at higher tiers — the "main hook". — [PG.biz IAP Inspector](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/iap-inspector-hungry-shark-world/)
- HSE largest coin pack 200,000 coins for $49.99 USD; ads (15-30s) give 100 coins, limited by playtime. — [Hungry Shark Wiki: Coins](https://hungry-shark.fandom.com/wiki/Coins)
- Things in HSW not purchasable: mission cosmetics, top-2% contest prizes, shark levelling. — [TV Tropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/HungrySharkWorld)
- FGOL: free-to-play needed "to get that kind of scale"; tested price points; no energy mechanics. — [MCV](https://www.mcvuk.com/development/studio-spotlight-future-games-of-london)
- Player sentiment: "the money for just a couple of gems is CRAZY!!"; early sharks cost hard-to-earn gems; request for coin-to-gem trading; praise for few ads ("only about a 30 second ad after every game"); complaint about forced full ad before exit then dying again after a revive. — [JustUseApp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/535500008/hungry-shark-evolution/reviews)
- Gamezebo: without spending "a grind", XXL sharks take very long. Pocket Gamer: "does get a bit grindy". Common Sense: paying or watching ads to continue "doesn't help"; rated 8+; game "kind of redundant and lacking in challenge". — [Gamezebo](https://www.gamezebo.com/reviews/hungry-shark-world-review-sink-your-teeth-in/); [Pocket Gamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/hungry-shark-world/review); [Common Sense Media](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/app-reviews/hungry-shark-evolution)
- HSW has a Splash Pass (season pass) delivering some sharks. — [Hungry Shark Wiki: Shark](https://hungry-shark.fandom.com/wiki/Shark)

### Inferences
- Fair-feeling: no energy/lives, free revives via ad, cosmetics earnable by play, early ladder quick. Unfair-feeling: hard-currency-gated sharks, escalating revive cost, late-game cost curve outpacing income, lost progress from bugs.
- Roblox translation: Robux dev products for revive (one cheap, non-escalating), x2 coins pass, extra pet slot, VIP-only cosmetic sharks; keep core sharks earnable; avoid pay-to-skip cooldowns and loot-box style pressure on kids.

### Gaps

## 5. Competitors and what a kid-friendly Roblox version should copy, improve, avoid

### Takeaway
Maneater adds RPG body-slot evolutions and growth stages; SharkBite 2 is asymmetric 1-shark-vs-boats rounds with a single currency and builder; Roblox "Be a Shark" is open eat-to-grow with PvP eating for skulls and tradable skins. MEGA BITE should copy Hungry Shark's hunger + gold rush + size-gated world + per-shark abilities, and add Roblox-native social/PvP and trading, while removing gore and pay-to-skip pressure.

### Cited Findings
- Maneater: six evolvable parts (Jaw, Head, Fins, Tail, Body, Organs) giving damage/armour buffs; unlocked by killing apex predators, finding secrets, objectives; stages Pup -> Teen (L4, double jump, lung capacity) -> Adult (L10, evolve at The Grotto) -> Elder (L20, breaks new gates) -> Mega (L30 cap). — [Green Man Gaming](https://www.greenmangaming.com/blog/maneater-how-to-unlock-all-evolutions/)
- SharkBite 2: random player becomes shark vs boat survivors; 250-second rounds; Shark Teeth currency (earned or Robux) buys boats/parts/skins; Titanic boat 100,000 teeth; boat builder. — [Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/sharkbite-2-a-definitive-guide)
- SharkBite 2 stats: 502.2M visits, 474 CCU (9 Nov 2026), peak 3.4K, 18-player servers, 88% like ratio, modelled ~$4M lifetime revenue. — [Profitable.app](https://profitable.app/roblox/games/sharkbite-2)
- Roblox "Be a Shark" (Nov 2023): eat fish and sharks to grow, eat players for Skulls, collect/trade rare skins; 115.9M visits, peak 703 CCU (May 2026), 88.1% likes. — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/5289155961/be-a-shark)
- HSW lacks competitive/multiplayer features and has a weak endgame after the top tier. — [PG.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/iap-inspector-hungry-shark-world/)
- Pocket Gamer: HSW more entertaining than slither.io; good for younger players. — [Pocket Gamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/hungry-shark-world/review)

### Inferences
- Copy: hunger-as-health; point-milestone gold rush (invincible, unlimited boost, golden prey, special music); scaling multiplier; nine-ish missions per shark found in-world; size-gated barriers per tier; timed portal biomes; bosses with readable weak-point patterns (Giant Crab dodge-3-then-bite, Submarine boost-ram); HUNGRY-letters collectible that supersizes; baby shark pets with clear stat bonus; per-shark signature ability.
- Improve: endgame (HSW's weakness) via PvP zone, trading, leaderboards, limited event sharks; gentle late-game drain curve with warnings; mission targets that spawn near the player when a mission is active; save progress server-side so no lost runs.
- Avoid: blood (use bubbles/sparkles), eating humans (swap to beach balls, floaties, rubber ducks, boats knocked over), stereotyped costumes, escalating paid revives, real-money cooldown skips, ad-like forced waits.
- Competitor ceilings: Roblox shark games observed here peak at a few thousand CCU, so the 10K+ CCU goal needs stronger progression/retention than existing Roblox shark titles.
