# Dead Game Method: dead and dying ocean, creature and kids' games as a feature library for MEGA BITE (as of Nov 2026)

Scope: adds 10 new games to the owner's library. It does not redo Club Penguin, Toontown, LEGO Universe, Spellbreak, Knockout City, Rumbleverse, Atlas Reactor, Evolve, Glitch, FusionFall, Moshi Monsters or City of Heroes. The partial findings already gathered (ArcheAge, Pirates of the Caribbean Online, Darkspore, Free Realms, WildStar, Depth, Atlas) were not re-verified this round; see Gaps. Every MEGA BITE idea below is an original concept. None copies a name, art or story.

## Which dead or dying games fit, and for each: the loved mechanic, the cause of death, and what fans still miss

### Takeaway
The best fits are the .io ocean food-chain games (Deeeep.io, Mope.io), the eat-and-grow fish sandbox Feed and Grow: Fish (stalled for years), dinosaur PvP survival games (The Stomping Land, ARK: Survival of the Fittest), co-op boss hunting (Monster Hunter Frontier), kids' social worlds (Club Penguin Island, FishVille), creature sharing (Spore online) and shared-ocean exploration (Endless Ocean Luminous). They died for the same few reasons: the developer abandoned the game, matches were too long or complex to drop into, the core loop was thin, free players were locked out by a paywall, or an old tech base could no longer be maintained.

### Cited Findings

**1. Deeeep.io (browser .io ocean evolution game; still online, popularity status unverified)**
- Loved mechanic: you start as one of five tiny fish (or an aquatic worm), eat food and dodge other players, and can evolve up to 9 times, with several choices at each step. There are 10 tiers and 107 playable animals, 41 of them in Tier 10 — [TV Tropes: Deeeep.io](https://www.tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/DEeEEPIO)
- Biomes and environment meters: Ocean, Deep Ocean, ocean surface, Arctic and Swamp. Being in the wrong place drains a specific meter: oxygen (fish out of water), temperature (non-Arctic animals in the Arctic and the reverse), salinity (salt-water animals in the swamp and the reverse) and pressure (deep-sea animals swimming up, others going down) — [TV Tropes](https://www.tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/DEeEEPIO)
- Signature abilities: a pelican's pouch grab, a leatherback turtle's shield, a whale's vacuum inhale. Some abilities stun, poison or slow. Shelled animals have armor, and some animals pierce it — [TV Tropes](https://www.tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/DEeEEPIO)
- Fairness rule: very large top-tier animals and very small low-tier animals often can't damage each other. A defeated animal drops meat that grants XP — [TV Tropes](https://www.tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/DEeEEPIO)
- Kotaku covered it early as "an undersea game where you evolve to the top of the food chain" (not fetched) — [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/an-undersea-game-where-you-evolve-to-the-top-of-the-foo-1796994096)

**2. Mope.io (animal food-chain .io game; faded after it was sold)**
- Loved mechanic: you spawn as a mouse or shrimp (depending on where you spawn), forage for XP and must find water to stay alive. Each level-up offers one choice per biome (water, forest, desert, snow) — [.io Games Wiki: Mope.io](https://io-games.fandom.com/wiki/Mope.io)
- "Tail-biting": lower-tier animals can bite higher-tier ones for XP and damage, and this is common against top-tier animals. At 1 million XP you can become a Dragon or a Kraken, and Dragons upgrade to Black Dragons at 10 million XP — [.io Games Wiki](https://io-games.fandom.com/wiki/Mope.io)
- Why it faded: creator Stan Tatarnykov sold it to Addicting Games, and it lost popularity because many new features were seen as "joke features." A new central Volcano biome later won some players back — [.io Games Wiki](https://io-games.fandom.com/wiki/Mope.io)

**3. Feed and Grow: Fish (Steam eat-and-grow fish sandbox; in early access since 2016, with no developer update in about 4 years)**
- Loved mechanic: you hunt and eat other fish to grow into larger creatures, starting as a small fish in an open-world animal survival sandbox. It has three maps and Deathmatch and "Crab Madness" modes — [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/429050/feed_and_grow_fish)
- Status: released 8 January 2016. Early access was promised for about one year, multiplayer is still "in testing," and Steam notes the last developer update was over four years ago — [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/429050/feed_and_grow_fish)
- What fans still want: players keep buying and reviewing it (Very Positive, 80% of 13,096 English reviews, and 149 reviews in the last 30 days) despite the stalled development. That shows lasting demand for eat-and-grow fish played together — [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/429050/feed_and_grow_fish)

**4. The Stomping Land (2014 dinosaur tribe survival game; abandoned)**
- Loved mechanic: tribal hunters on an island fight rivals and dinosaurs, tame and ride dinosaurs or hunt them for meat, craft gear and build tipis and totems. Servers held up to 16 players — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Stomping_Land)
- Hype: a $20,000 Kickstarter goal raised $114,000. The game peaked at 141st of the 400 most popular Steam games of 2014, with 136,593 players — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Stomping_Land)
- Death: weekly updates stopped in June 2014 (the developer cited a switch to Unreal Engine 4). A refund petition gathered nearly 3,000 signatures, a modeler left in January 2015 and the game was pulled from Steam in February 2015. Kickstarter barred the developer from future campaigns — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Stomping_Land); [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/the-stomping-lands-dinosaur-modeler-gives-up-moves-to-a-new-game/)
- At launch it had only 6 of 15 planned dinosaurs, and critics cited glitches, poor AI and thin gameplay — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Stomping_Land)

**5. ARK: Survival of the Fittest (dinosaur PvP arena spin-off; flopped as a standalone)**
- Concept: a battle royale-style survival arena with dinosaurs in PvP. It began as an ARK mod, went free-to-play standalone in early 2016 and was later folded back into ARK — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/ark-survival-of-the-fittest-is-an-experiment-that-didnt-catch-on-but-we-do-think-theres-a-fun-game-there-says-studio-wildcard)
- Why it died: matches were slow, complex and about an hour long, so players couldn't drop in and out the way they could in PUBG. It had fewer than 300 concurrent players, against over 350,000 for PUBG at the time, and the small studio prioritised the main game — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/ark-survival-of-the-fittest-is-an-experiment-that-didnt-catch-on-but-we-do-think-theres-a-fun-game-there-says-studio-wildcard)
- A 2023 console re-release was reported (headline only, not verified) — [MMOs.com](https://mmos.com/news/ark-survival-of-the-fittest-console-launch)

**6. Monster Hunter Frontier (2007–2019 co-op boss-hunting MMO)**
- Ran from 21 June 2007 (PC, Japan) until all online services ended on 18 December 2019 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monster_Hunter_Frontier_Z); [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/2019/06/19/monster-hunter-frontier-z-is-shutting-down-after-12-years-of-service-on-december-18-2019/)
- Loved mechanic: online parties hunting large original monsters with original weapons. Its "Seasons" and "Forwards" expansions kept adding monsters and armor sets — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monster_Hunter_Frontier_Z); [Automaton](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/20220706-13841/)
- Cause of death: it was built on the older Monster Hunter Dos base, which created "various limitations." Even with hardware and software updates, a satisfying long-term service became hard to keep up — [Automaton](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/20220706-13841/)
- What fans miss: in 2022 fans were still asking for an offline mode or a sequel, and the official account said both would be extremely difficult. The fan server emulator Erupe lets people self-host Frontier, is backed by three Discord communities and was archived (read-only) on 18 July 2026 — [Automaton](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/20220706-13841/); [GitHub: Mezeporta/Erupe](https://github.com/Mezeporta/Erupe)

**7. Club Penguin Island (2017–2018 kids' mobile successor; not the original Club Penguin)**
- Mechanics: Adventures (quests), Daily Challenges, XP and level-up item packs, clothing designed from blueprints, fabrics and decals, igloo customisation, multiplayer minigames, seasonal events and a "phone" hub — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Club_Penguin_Island)
- Why it died: most rewards and content needed membership, and old fans were negative about missing features. It also competed with private servers of the original Club Penguin and Toontown. Disney cited "global competition" (announced 27 September 2018) and shut the servers on 20 December 2018, laying off the whole team — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Club_Penguin_Island); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2018/09/28/disney-shuts-down-club-penguin-island/)
- Lesson: a fan-requested update in November 2017 was received better, but it came too late — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Club_Penguin_Island)

**8. FishVille (Zynga Facebook aquarium game, 2009–2012)**
- Loved mechanic: fish grow from Baby to Junior to Adult in real time. Selling grown fish earns coins and XP, and unfed fish get sick and die. Players could own up to 8 tanks, unlock decorations by levelling up and train fish with super foods in Arena Tanks to fight other fish. Friends visited each other's tanks and traded gifts and medicine — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FishVille)
- It still had 1.6 million monthly users in October 2011 and was discontinued on 5 December 2012 in a wider cost-cutting closure of Zynga games — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FishVille); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2012/12/30/zynga-shuts-down-petville-fishville-mafia-wars-2/)

**9. Spore's online features (2008; neglected for years, small official revival in 2024)**
- State: by October 2024, online features on Steam reportedly still worked but account registration was broken. A new EA team posted its first update in five years and opened an official Discord, with no plans for major updates or a sequel — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/sim/after-years-of-silence-the-legendary-life-sim-spore-has-crawled-back-to-life-like-a-3-eyed-amphibian-emerging-from-a-primordial-alien-sea)
- The Discord "blew up way faster than we anticipated" with only two moderators, which shows lasting fan attachment — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/sim/after-years-of-silence-the-legendary-life-sim-spore-has-crawled-back-to-life-like-a-3-eyed-amphibian-emerging-from-a-primordial-alien-sea)

**10. Endless Ocean Luminous (2024 Nintendo Switch online diving game; poorly received)**
- Mechanic: dive with up to 30 players in a shared sea, scan marine life (over 500 species) and collect seafloor salvage to unlock suit colors, stickers and emotes — [Siliconera review](https://www.siliconera.com/review-endless-ocean-luminous-is-a-shallow-dive-into-a-disappointing-sea/)
- Why it disappointed: sea life barely reacts to the player, multiplayer felt like solo play (contact was limited to emotes and waves), missions repeated, and scan quotas locked progress. The reviewer said it "lacks heart" compared with the earlier games — [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/review-endless-ocean-luminous-is-a-shallow-dive-into-a-disappointing-sea/)
- The deeper zones (sharks, anglerfish, coelacanths) were singled out as eerie and the high point — [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/review-endless-ocean-luminous-is-a-shallow-dive-into-a-disappointing-sea/)

### Inferences
- The .io games prove the exact MEGA BITE fantasy (tiered evolution with a choice at each tier, wrong-zone meters, signature abilities, revenge-bites on big players) can hold mass audiences. Mope.io shows that low-effort "joke" content after an ownership change can drive players away.
- Feed and Grow and Endless Ocean show an ocean that feels dead (no reactions, no shared goals) loses players even when the core idea sells.
- ARK SotF teaches the most for a kids' phone game: short sessions you can drop into beat long, complex matches.
- Club Penguin Island's warning: paywalling most rewards angers a returning kids' audience.

### Gaps
- Deeeep.io's current ownership, player counts and any Steam successor were not verified (the TV Tropes page did not cover them).
- No reliable source was found on Tamagotchi online worlds, a multiplayer "Shark Attack" game, Ecco online, or Hungry Shark's predecessor features. These were not researched further for time.
- The earlier partial findings (ArcheAge, Pirates of the Caribbean Online, Darkspore, Free Realms, WildStar, Depth, Atlas) were not re-verified this round, so the report writer should treat their details as unverified.
- No Reddit "I miss" threads were fetched, so fan nostalgia here is taken from revival activity (Erupe, the Spore Discord, ongoing Feed and Grow reviews), not quotes.
- Monster Hunter Frontier's specific system details (guild features, Road mode) were not covered by the fetched sources.

## Which mechanics fit a kids' phone shark game

### Takeaway
The strongest fits are a tiered evolution with a branch choice at each tier, zone pressure (depth, temperature, salinity), shared co-op boss hunts, a home that grows (aquarium or reef), visible revenge mechanics for small players, short drop-in events, and scanning or collection with cosmetic rewards. Long survival matches, deep crafting and paywalled progress are the parts to avoid.

### Cited Findings
- Branching evolution and biome meters keep .io players choosing and exploring — [TV Tropes: Deeeep.io](https://www.tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/DEeEEPIO)
- A small-bites-big mechanic gives low tiers a reason to engage top predators — [.io Games Wiki: Mope.io](https://io-games.fandom.com/wiki/Mope.io)
- A real-time growth timer, tank homes, friend visits and gifting suit casual and social players — [Wikipedia: FishVille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FishVille)
- Co-op hunts of big original monsters, refreshed by expansions, sustained a 12-year service — [Wikipedia: Monster Hunter Frontier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monster_Hunter_Frontier_Z)
- Hour-long complex PvP failed to hold players — [PC Gamer: ARK SotF](https://www.pcgamer.com/ark-survival-of-the-fittest-is-an-experiment-that-didnt-catch-on-but-we-do-think-theres-a-fun-game-there-says-studio-wildcard)
- Quests, daily challenges, seasonal events and customisation are a kids' standard, but membership-gating them failed — [Wikipedia: Club Penguin Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Club_Penguin_Island)

### Inferences: 12 original feature ideas for MEGA BITE
1. **Bloodline Fork evolution:** at each tier, pick one of 2–3 branches (for example a speed hunter, an armored bruiser or a deep-sea lurker), each with one signature ability. Taken from the .io branching tiers.
2. **Pressure, Chill and Brine meters:** each shark line is comfortable in some zones and slowly takes damage in others. This pushes exploration and makes the deep PvP zone feel dangerous without walls. Taken from Deeeep.io's environment meters.
3. **Nibble Bounty:** small sharks that land bites on a top-tier player earn bonus XP and a "Brave Bite" streak badge, and the big player's health bar is shown to everyone nearby. This answers the "big players bully me" problem. Taken from Mope.io's tail-biting.
4. **Size-gap shield:** the very largest and very smallest tiers can't hurt each other, so newcomers aren't one-shot by apex sharks. Taken from Deeeep.io's fairness rule.
5. **Leviathan Hunts:** every 20–30 minutes a server-wide boss (an original sea monster) appears, and everyone who hits it shares the loot by contribution. New bosses each update act as "Seasons." Taken from Monster Hunter Frontier.
6. **Drop-in Frenzy events:** 3–5 minute events (feeding frenzy, a whirlpool or a bomb rain) join automatically for whoever is in the zone, with no matchmaking. This is the lesson of ARK SotF's failure.
7. **Home Reef:** a personal reef base that grows trophies (boss teeth, rare fish) and can be decorated and visited by friends, who leave a gift bubble once a day. Taken from FishVille's tanks, visits and gifting.
8. **Pup Nursery:** hatch baby shark pets that grow Baby to Juvenile to Adult on real-time timers, need feeding and later follow you as companions. Taken from FishVille's growth stages, without fish dying, which would be too harsh for kids.
9. **Sonar Codex:** scan species with a sonar ping to fill a collection book. Completing a page gives cosmetic fins, trails or emotes. Rare species appear only at night or in deep zones. Taken from Endless Ocean's scanning, fixed so fish react to you by fleeing, schooling or charging.
10. **Living Ocean reactions:** fish schools scatter from big sharks and gather around small ones, and bomb blasts stun nearby fish into easy food. This answers the criticism that Endless Ocean's sea felt dead.
11. **Dated Tide Trophies:** limited-time cosmetics stamped with the event date ("Blood Moon Tide, Dec 2026") that never return. They create collect-them-all and trading value while staying cosmetic only, avoiding Club Penguin Island's paywall anger.
12. **Pod Crews:** 2–4 player crews with a shared crew color and a crew evolution bonus when hunting together. Crews raise their Pod Banner level by completing hunts. Taken from the Monster Hunter Frontier party and tribe ideas.
13. **Apex Hour:** the current top player on a server is marked on everyone's map and drops a big XP chest when defeated. This creates a recurring "invasion" moment without a long match.
14. **Free daily quests:** daily and weekly challenges and seasonal story quests stay free. Monetise only speed, cosmetics and private servers, never core progress (the Club Penguin Island lesson).

### Gaps

## What Roblox fixes and what it does not

### Takeaway
Roblox removes the cost, platform and server problems that killed several of these games, but it does not fix thin content, empty-server dynamics or abandonment by the developer.

### Cited Findings
- FishVille, Club Penguin Island and Monster Hunter Frontier died from the publisher's cost or tech decisions. Roblox hosts servers, so the developer has no server bill to drop — [Wikipedia: FishVille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FishVille); [Wikipedia: Club Penguin Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Club_Penguin_Island); [Automaton](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/20220706-13841/)
- The Stomping Land and Feed and Grow died or stalled from developer silence. No platform fixes that — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Stomping_Land); [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/429050/feed_and_grow_fish)
- ARK SotF's long matches were too hard to get into, which is a design problem, not a platform one — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/ark-survival-of-the-fittest-is-an-experiment-that-didnt-catch-on-but-we-do-think-theres-a-fun-game-there-says-studio-wildcard)

### Inferences
- **Roblox fixes:** a free entry price (Feed and Grow costs $14.99 and Endless Ocean Luminous $49.99), phone access, hosted servers, built-in friends and party join, and fast patching.
- **Roblox does not fix:** a stale update schedule (fix it with a visible weekly or biweekly event calendar), empty or low-population servers (fix it with smaller servers, bots or NPC fish that fill the food chain, and matching players into busy servers), the skill floor in PvP (fix it with the size-gap shield and Nibble Bounty), and low-effort content after launch (Mope.io's "joke features").

### Gaps
- No Roblox-specific data on concurrent players for shark or eat-and-grow games was gathered this round. The SharkBite and other Roblox competitors were not researched.
