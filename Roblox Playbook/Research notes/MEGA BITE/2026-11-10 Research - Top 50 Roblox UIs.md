# Research: the UIs of the top 50 Roblox games (10 Nov 2026)

Owner (18:14): "make a UI skill, search top 50 UIs and then use the ChatGPT method and try to make the BEST UI ever."
Method: top 50 by live players from [endgametools charts, 9 Nov 2026](https://endgametools.com/en/roblox/charts); for each game the best gameplay/UI screenshot from a Bing image search, laid out as a contact sheet in Chrome and studied (8 of them again at full size). Plus [Roblox Docs: UI and UX design](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/ui-ux-design). Honest limits: one screenshot per game, some were thumbnails (BlockSpin, Evade, Tap Sim, LifeTogether, Cameraman TD, Fatal Baboons) so their in-game UI wasn't visible.

## Patterns (what the kid hits do)
1. **Icon-first HUD buttons.** The biggest kid games (Steal An Egg #1, Adopt Me #5, Steal a Brainrot #16) use BIG illustrated, glossy, 3D-looking icons with a short bold label under them, stacked on one side. The icon IS the button. Our current HUD puts small icons inside flat plates.
2. **Themed frames beat generic frames.** Throw Food For Animals (#6, a new top-10) frames its windows in its own world (a cookie/wood rim, paw sliders). MEGA BITE equivalent: an ocean frame (sea-glass rim, pearls, coral/shell accent, bubble details).
3. **Title on a ribbon/tab sitting ON the top edge** (PS99, Anime Dice, Throw Food), never plain text inside.
4. **Red round X overhanging the top-right corner** (PS99, Anime Dice); same place on every window.
5. **Chunky pill buttons**: a vertical gradient + a white shine bar + a darker lip; colour = meaning (green go, red cancel, gold buy/claim, blue normal, grey locked).
6. **Rarity-coloured cards** in grids (Anime Dice, Dandy's World) and rarity-coloured world text (Steal An Egg).
7. **Big moments get a centre pop**: Fish It's catch card ("NEW", "1 in 20", name + weight) = our new-fish / evolve / golden pop.
8. **Counters**: money bottom-left or top-right as a pill with an icon and a green "+" (Adopt Me, Steal An Egg).
9. **Mobile**: action buttons bottom-right big and round (Fish It, sports games), hotbar bottom-centre, never centre-screen clutter.
10. **Two style camps**: bright/glossy/outlined (kid sims, 60% of the top 50) vs dark/sleek glass (PvP, horror, anime). MEGA BITE is a kids' game → bright camp, with the ocean theme.
11. Rated poorly / feels cheap: default Roblox white panels (Brookhaven-style), thin text, debug-looking lists.

## Decision for MEGA BITE UI v2
Bright kid-sim camp + an OCEAN THEME frame (like Throw Food's themed frame): glossy sea-glass turquoise rims with a dark navy outline, deep-teal inner panel, small pearl/bubble details, ribbon titles, red round X, chunky pill buttons, icon-first side buttons (big glossy icons that break out of a round sea-bubble base), rarity-coloured cards. Same 16 icons kept (they already match), new frames/buttons from ChatGPT.
