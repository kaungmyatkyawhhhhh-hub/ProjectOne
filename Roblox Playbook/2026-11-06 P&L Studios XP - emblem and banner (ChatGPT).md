# P&L Studios XP - community emblem + banner (6 Nov)

The spec comes from [[Roblox Playbook - Updates, storefront and analytics]] (studio mark section):
- emblem: 1024 square, mark inside an 80% circle, readable at 48-64 px;
- banner: 1440x456 (DevForum, Dec 2025).

Both were made in the owner's ChatGPT by Claude (chat "Create Game Logo"):
- **Emblem:** gold glossy "P&L" monogram, star sparkle on the L, cyan "XP" badge, glowing cyan ring, navy-violet background.
- **Banner:** space nebula with planets, the same monogram, "P&L STUDIOS XP" in gold and "PLAY. LEVEL UP. REPEAT." in white, centred so phone crops keep the text.

Emblem prompt:
```text
Create ONE square image, 1024x1024: a game studio emblem / logo for a Roblox game studio called "P&L Studios XP". Solid deep navy-to-violet background filling the whole square (no transparency, no border). In the centre, inside a circle about 80% of the width so a round crop never cuts it: a bold glossy GOLD monogram "P&L" (chunky italic letters, thick dark outline, subtle bevel and shine), with a small bright 4-point star sparkle on the top-right of the L and a small cyan "XP" badge tucked under the ampersand. Behind the monogram a soft glowing ring with a few tiny stars, cosmic-arcade feel. Style: clean glossy mobile-game logo like popular Roblox studio emblems, simple shapes, only 3 main colours (gold, navy-violet, cyan), must stay readable when shrunk to 48x48 pixels. Spell the letters exactly P&L and XP, no other text, no words, no characters, no people, NOT a photo.
```
Banner prompt (sent in the same chat):
```text
Now make the matching community BANNER for the same studio, same style and colours as this emblem. ONE wide image, exactly 1440x456 (about 3.16:1). Background: deep navy-to-violet space with soft nebula glow, a few small planets and stars, light streaks, filling the whole image edge to edge. In the CENTRE (keep everything important in the middle 60% because the edges get cropped on phones): the same gold glossy "P&L" monogram with the cyan "XP" badge on the left, and to its right the words "P&L STUDIOS XP" in big bold glossy gold italic letters with a thick dark outline, and under it smaller white text "PLAY. LEVEL UP. REPEAT.". Spell every word exactly like that, no other text, no game logos, no characters, no people, NOT a photo. Clean glossy Roblox game-studio style.
```
