# Supernova Incremental - why players keep playing (and don't leave bored)

Owner (1 Nov): "we need the game to be fun and remember ur research, why do people keep continuing on playing the game and not leave of boredom".
From: [[Playbook notes - Incremental games]] (Pecorella GDC + Math of Idle Games, Tuts+, GCI RoTrends data, postmortems), [[2026-11-01 Celestial Incremental - 10 incrementals played]], [[2026-10-29 GYM ARC Why players stop grinding - research]].

## The 10 reasons people keep playing
1. **Number always goes up, check-ins feel great** (Pecorella): progress without effort, constant positive feedback, coming back pays more the longer you were away.
2. **Something new every few minutes**: Roblox incremental sessions ~32 min (GCI); the first prestige must land in the first session (10-20 min), a real unlock every few minutes.
3. **Curiosity**: hidden "?" nodes (Noob tree, Circle), locked things shown with what opens them (Mafia LEVEL rows, GIS walls). Never reveal everything up front.
4. **Choices, not chores**: several upgrade paths whose best pick keeps changing (Tuts+). Constant required clicking is a listed pitfall. (Owner: collecting is tiring.)
5. **Walls you feel, then a reset clearly worth it**: put each layer at a wall; the first reset should make you ~2-3x faster; each layer adds ONE new mechanic (Tower's floors). Vary reset sizes so they don't feel samey (Pt III).
6. **Luck moments**: a roll with shown odds (Immortality breakthrough 98% rating, Noob runes).
7. **Progress others can SEE**: rank over the head, a bigger world around you, server crown, boards (GYM ARC lesson: coins/titles alone feel pointless).
8. **Together**: shared goals, friend bonus, the server watching your big moment.
9. **A reason for tomorrow**: offline earnings, daily streak, events, weather, weekly limited stuff.
10. **A finish line**: "Cosmos Charted 37%" completion meter (Circle 0/723) and a collection book.

## Boredom killers to avoid (researched mistakes)
- Linear costs; unlocks too fast or too slow; numbers that jump e10 a click and mean nothing (use softcaps, new currency per layer, show "x2" not raw); the first prestige longer than one session; absurd daily quests (Noob: "play 2h53m"); popups that won't close; generators going useless (milestone x2s on every tier).
