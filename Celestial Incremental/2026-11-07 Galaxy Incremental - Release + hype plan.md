## New Roblox rule that changes the plan (researched 7 Nov)
Source: [Kids and Select](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/kids-and-select), [Content maturity](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/promotion/content-maturity), DevForum Oct 2026.

- **A new game starts in a trial: only age-checked players 16+ can play it.** It opens to kids (Kids/Select audiences) only after **250 unique plays by "highly engaged" age-checked players within 60 days**, plus a safety review.
  - "Highly engaged" = account tenure + playtime + some Robux spend anywhere on Roblox in the last 60 days.
  - Roblox said it will lower the threshold to 100 "later this year" (DevForum, Oct 2026, no date).
- For a group game, the **group owner** must meet the creator requirements:
  - account in good standing;
  - **government ID verification** (18+);
  - **2-Step Verification**;
  - the **full Maturity & Compliance questionnaire** (target rating Minimal or Mild);
  - **Roblox Premium/Plus for 2 months in a row OR a one-time refundable publishing fee**.
- **Expedited review:** 50,000 Robux refundable fee per game. NOT without the owner's explicit yes (spending rule). Not worth it at our size.
- An unrated game (no questionnaire) is restricted for everyone.
- Track progress in Creator Hub → Audience Reach.

**Our own data (owner, 7 Nov): GYM ARC reached the 250 engaged plays in about 1 week.** So we expect Galaxy to open to kids about a week after the alpha (~28 Nov), before the 5 Dec full launch. If P&L Studios XP is owned by the same account as Myanmar Builders Club, the ID / 2-Step / subscription steps are probably already done; only the questionnaire is per game.

**What it means:** the first hype has to reach **16+ players** (ads + Shorts + group), and the alpha's real job is to get 250 engaged plays fast. The kids' audience (our main one) unlocks after that. So **do the eligibility steps NOW**; the 60-day clock and the subscription months take time.

## SOCIAL MEDIA PLAN (owner: "i mean the plan like social media and stuff", 7 Nov)
Research:
- vault [[Playbook notes - Discovery and marketing]] (Creator Rewards, share links, Moments);
- [BloxClips](https://www.bloxclips.com/articles/how-to-promote-a-roblox-game-on-tiktok): open on the best moment; many creators beat one account; start before release; concentrate posts (Kill Streak: 10M views in 10 days, half of it pre-launch);
- [GameSpace](https://gamespace.com/all-articles/news/roblox-tiktok-marketing-working-guide/): post daily; 3-5 versions of each moment; 10-30 s; hook in 1-3 s; the game name and code on screen + in the caption; boost only clips that already beat your average; track everything.

**Channels:**
- One brand account "P&L Studios" on TikTok + YouTube Shorts + Instagram Reels; the same vertical clip goes to all 3.
- The P&L Studios XP Roblox group (shouts).
- A Discord server (announcements, sneak-peeks, bugs, codes).
- A Roblox Experience Event (Notify Me).
- Roblox Moments.
- Small Roblox YouTubers/TikTokers (they reach the under-13s our own account can't).
- **Every link is a Creator Rewards Share Link**, never the plain URL (it's tracked and pays 35% on new users' first $100).

**Clip types for Galaxy (10-30 s, vertical, captions, starts mid-action, no logo intro):**
1. "From 1 rock to a whole galaxy": a timelapse of the star system growing. The core fantasy.
2. Supernova cinematic: the hook is the explosion in frame 1.
3. Forge roll: a "1 in 10,000 CELESTIAL" reveal (rare find).
4. Black Hole pull-in: "everything gets sucked in…" then the dark pulse.
5. Dev clip: "I made this game in 3 weeks with AI": before/after of the map or boards. Devlogs work well for small studios.
6. Update teaser: the new thing in second 1, then "Saturday 9:30 PM MMT".
Each moment gets 3-5 versions (different hook text / length / sound), spread over days.
