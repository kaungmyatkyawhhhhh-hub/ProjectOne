# Supernova Incremental - map plan (1 Nov night)

Owner: "ok so about the game map, rn it look so bad too". From the owner's HUD screenshot + [[Playbook notes - Top games art study (Oct 2026)]] (one colour mood, one clear subject), [[Playbook notes - Lighting, cameras and cutscenes]], [[Roblox Playbook - Rules for every game]] (few lights, no glass / see-through), [[2026-11-01 Research - Popular incremental HUD layouts]] (top incrementals = compact hub with boards), [[2026-11-01 Celestial Incremental - Revamp 2 plan (no more collecting)]].

## What's wrong now
1. Everything glows (path tiles, crystals, lamps, sky flares, Lost Star blobs): no focal point, nothing says "go here".
2. Navy ground under a navy sky: low contrast, the world and the UI blend.
3. Props scattered at random (crystals, glowing mushroom trees, loose slabs): no layout you can read.
4. Candy pink / cyan / yellow with no meaning.
5. A big flat empty field (built for walking around collecting, which is gone in Revamp 2): the player looks lost in it.
6. Generic buildings (dome, temple) that don't do anything you can see.

## The new map: an observatory hub where your star system is the show
- **Centre: the SUPERNOVA CORE** - a big star on a brass ring altar. Goes Supernova (swells, bursts) when anyone resets: the server's shared moment.
- **Ring of player pads** (one per player, like tycoon plots; 8 per server): a round brass platform with your name; YOUR STAR SYSTEM orbits above it and grows as you buy (asteroid -> moon -> planet ... quasar). Others see how far you are: progress you can see.
- **Around the plaza:** the STAR MAP (opens the Tree) and an UPGRADE board (quick buys, like Noob's boards), leaderboards at spawn, the STAR FORGE (visible from day 1, locked with "Supernova 3"), the BLACK HOLE gate (locked), and across the void the RING NEBULA island (locked).
- **Compact:** everything within a short walk; spawn faces the Core and your pad.

## Look rules
- One mood: cool deep violet / indigo sky and nebula; WARM ground (pale stone + brass) so the floor separates from the sky.
- Value contrast: the world stays calm and darker; bright = something to look at or press (the Core, your system, the thing that's ready).
- Gold = interactive (boards, altar rims, pad edges). Glow only on interactive things.
- Few lights, only near the plaza; no glass, no see-through parts; streaming on; test on a phone.
- Props in small framed groups (a crystal cluster beside each board), not scattered.
- The same star-chart motif as the UI: brass rings, chart lines etched in the floor, small stars at corners.
