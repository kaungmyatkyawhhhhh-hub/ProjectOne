## Why it works (my reading, to check by playing)
1. **Paid access = a filter + a promise**: only interested players get in (better ratings, longer sessions), "ALPHA" + release rewards make buyers feel like early supporters, not customers.
2. **Huge depth for the optimisers**: 610 upgrades, 75+ currencies, 6 chapters: there's always a next thing (the "20% nerds" pay and stay).
3. **A story with a goal**: restoring a planet gives meaning to the numbers (chapters = clear progress), unlike plain "number go up".
4. **Living updates**: renamed with each update (element tag), updated hours ago, codes for player-count milestones (500Active) = a community that grows with the game; a 15K group.
5. **Runs well**: 60 FPS average.
6. **Monetisation stacked on top**: access + passes up to an "every pass" bundle.
Risk seen: peak 897 -> ~450 in 2 weeks: paid games don't get the free-traffic flood; content must keep coming.

## Lessons for Supernova Incremental (proposal)
- A story arc for the layers (e.g. the dying star you rebuild, chapter per layer) = meaning for kids + structure.
- Name tag for updates in the title ("[☄️ BLACK HOLE] Supernova Incremental").
- Milestone codes (500Active-style) + a group with a join bonus.
- Depth list in the description ("4 layers, 40 tree upgrades, 42 Star Cores...").
- Paid access: NOT for us at launch (we need free Home traffic; Regrowth already has its own community) - revisit later as "early access to the next layer"? Owner's call.
