# Research: why Immortality Incremental and Regrowth keep players playing (2 Nov)

Owner (2 Nov, about Galaxy Incremental's story): "make it like how regrowth incremental and immortality incremental keeps us making us play".
Regrowth: [[2026-11-01 Regrowth Incremental - play log and how to play]], [[2026-11-01 Research - Regrowth Incremental (paid access)]].

## Immortality Incremental (sources: earnaldo guide, RobloxDen codes page checked 31 Oct 2026, Destructoid codes Oct 2026)
- Dev Cryyptics. Cultivation (xianxia) theme. ~4,500 CCU, group 140K+ members, Discord is the wiki (no official wiki).
- **No plot: the progression IS the story.** Loop: gather Qi -> ascend realms (sub-stages) -> reset layers -> BREAKTHROUGH.
- **Breakthroughs = gates with several conditions at once** (a Qi amount + a reset count + sometimes Jade / Tickets). A clear big goal that needs everything you built.
- **Several reset layers**, each resets the one below for a bigger permanent multiplier; multipliers multiply.
- **Worlds as chapters**: up to WORLD 9 (expansions in parts: "W3 PT1", "WORLD9PT1"); each world adds a whole system: dungeons (DUNGEON 1-6), mining, alchemy, crafting, Dao Paths, Marks, weather, a Leviathan boss. "Open Marks" / "Paths" = build choices.
- **Currencies with one job each**: Qi (main), Jade (upgrades / breakthroughs), Tickets (special features / events), Potions (temporary multipliers).
- **Codes as a come-back engine**: 84 active codes (!), named after milestones (10KSERVER, 100KGROUP, 5MVISITS, 5KCCU, 100KFAV), updates (UPDATE7, HOTFIX, PART2), content (WORLD4, DUNGEON4, MINING) and events (HALLOWEEN, HOLIDAY), plus apologies (SORRYFORMISTAKE). Rewards = Jade, Tickets and POTIONS (a code gives a temporary boost to use right now).
- Leaderboards; seasonal events with their own potion.

## Regrowth Incremental (our play log)
- A light premise with a goal: "bring an entire planet back to life", 6 chapters; you SEE your place being rebuilt (tycoon pads) = the story is shown, not told.
- Something new every ~30 s in the first 30 min, tiers every 10 levels, 75+ currencies, 610 upgrades.
- Tutorial pays premium currency per step with a step counter; guide book (G).
- ALPHA: early supporters get release rewards.

## What both do (the pattern)
1. **The story is the progression**: a one-line premise + chapters you can see (Regrowth) or realms / worlds with names (Immortality). No cutscene walls of text.
2. **A big gate always in view** (breakthrough / next chapter) that needs several things at once.
3. **Each chapter adds a NEW SYSTEM**, not just bigger numbers.
4. **Layers on layers**: each reset makes the next run faster.
5. **Constant updates named as parts** (WORLD9PT1, PT2) with a code each.
6. **Codes everywhere**: milestones + updates + events + apologies; rewards are things you use now (potions / boosts / premium currency).
7. **Community as the wiki**: big group + Discord.
