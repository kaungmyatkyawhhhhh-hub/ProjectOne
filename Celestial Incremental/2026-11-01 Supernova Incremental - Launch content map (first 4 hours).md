# Supernova Incremental - launch content map: the first 4 hours (1 Nov night)

Owner: "on the first launch, we need to add a lot of gameplay that is not boring and tiring to about 4 hours and then later we can update and extend 4 hours to more".
From [[2026-11-01 Supernova Incremental - Why players keep playing]] (a new thing every few minutes, first big reset in session one, walls then a clearly-worth-it reset, each layer one new mechanic, curiosity, reasons to come back), [[Playbook notes - Incremental games]] (Roblox incremental sessions ~32 min (GCI); challenges + achievements give replay without new layers; updates add a new layer at the top), [[Roblox Playbook - Rules for every game]] (Saturday update slot; new content goes after existing goals).

## Shape of 4 hours
- 4 h of play = about 8 sessions of ~30 min, so over 2-5 days for most players. Every session ends with something to come back for (offline earnings, a timer, the next unlock in sight).
- Never more than ~5 minutes without something new; never a wall longer than ~10 minutes of play.
- 4 layers at launch, each one new mechanic: Stardust (bodies + tree) -> Supernova (shards, rank) -> Star Forge (rolls) -> Black Hole (automation + dark tree) -> Ring Nebula (fusion). Big Bang = the first update.

## Keeping it not boring / not tiring
- Automation arrives exactly when repeating gets old (auto-buy at ~1 h, auto-Supernova at Black Hole).
- No grind walls: if a goal is far, the tree always has a cheaper useful node.
- Varied play: building, choosing, rolling, hunting, fusing, challenges.
- Offline earnings up to 4 h (longer via tree), shown on return.

## Updates (Saturday slot)
- Update 1: Big Bang layer (+2-3 h). Then: new Forge cores, new challenges, events. New content always after the current end, old goals never moved further away.

## Scope warning (1-week goal, ~8 Nov)
Must-have for launch: layers 1-3 (Stardust, Supernova, Forge) polished + Black Hole. Ring Nebula fusion can be the first update if time runs out (then 3:00-4:00 = Black Holes + challenges + Forge). Decide after the numbers sim.

## Who plays (owner, 1 Nov night: "i think nerds mainly play incremental games")
- Vault gap: no sourced age / type data for Roblox incremental players ([[Playbook notes - Incremental games]] "Gaps"); a 1 Nov web search found none either.
- What we know: the PC / web genre (Antimatter Dimensions, r/incremental_games) is built for optimisers who love maths, layers and efficiency. The big Roblox ones (Noob Incremental 4.4K playing, Grass Cutting Incremental 30M visits) reach ordinary young Roblox players too (vault inference: younger, social).
- Decision (owner confirmed: "yes design for both"): design for BOTH. Simple, flashy surface for everyone; depth underneath for the "nerds":
  - Stats screen: income per second broken down by every multiplier, total multiplier, time to next Supernova.
  - Exact numbers in every tooltip ("x1.5 per level, next: 3.4K -> 5.1K"), odds shown.
  - Number format setting: short (1.2M) / scientific (1.2e6).
  - Optimising choices: tree branches that compete, Supernova timing (shards formula shown), challenges.
  - Bragging for optimisers: fastest-Supernova and fastest-Black-Hole leaderboards, achievements %, Cosmos Charted %.
