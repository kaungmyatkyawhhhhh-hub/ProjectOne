Research date 2026-11-16. Hotel: Meliá Yangon, 192 Kabar Aye Pagoda Road, Bahan Tsp. It is part of Myanmar Centre (HAGL), together with Myanmar Plaza mall and Myanmar Centre Tower. 400 to 430 rooms, opened 2016.

## Lobby

### Photo L1: lobby atrium, front view (best overall reference)
https://dam.melia.com/melia/accounts/f8/4000018/projects/127/assets/fc/51243/1fa7d1df63561e86e6589d26d775ff1c-1609410163.jpg
Caption: "Grand hotel lobby with swirling chandelier, reception desk, and modern lounge seating."

- **Layout:** A symmetrical double-height hall. A central axis runs from the entrance to the reception at the back. Lounge seating groups sit on both sides of the axis. A round rug in the middle holds a tall flower stand: a rectangular frame table about 1.0 m high, with 3 white-orchid and green-leaf arrangements.
- **Mezzanine gallery:** It wraps the hall on three sides. Its edge is a solid timber-clad band about 1.2 m deep. Above that is a timber handrail balustrade with vertical bars, about 1.1 m high. The gallery floor is at about 6.0 m.
- **Ceiling:** The central ceiling is white plaster, flat, at about 10 to 11 m. It is lined with a cove of indirect light and round recessed downlights.
- **Chandelier:** A large spiralling crystal chandelier hangs over the centre. It is an inverted cone made of strings of clear glass rods, about 4 m tall and 3.5 m across at the top, narrowing to about 1 m. Its bottom is about 5.5 m above the floor.
- **Columns:** Four big square columns, about 1.2 x 1.2 m. They are clad in warm teak-tone timber, sRGB about (176,120,62), with a dark grey stone inlay strip on the face, sRGB about (80,80,80). They run full height to the ceiling.
- **Screens:** Between the columns stand decorative screens about 3.0 m high. They are bronze/black metal frames with a grid of small dark tiles and gold square panels, giving a mixed black (30,30,30) and gold (170,130,70) pattern. They separate the lounge bays from the axis.
- **Floor:** Polished white marble with soft grey veining, sRGB about (232,230,225).
- **Rugs:** Round, taupe/grey (150,140,128).
- **Seating:**
  - Long low sofas in terracotta/rust (180,90,60) with grey cushions.
  - Tub armchairs in grey-blue upholstery (120,125,130) with light oak frames.
  - Mustard/ochre accent armchairs (190,150,80).
  - Round light-oak coffee tables about 0.9 m across.
- **Back wall:** The reception backdrop is black at the far end with white lines (see L3).

## Link to Myanmar Plaza

**What is confirmed (text):**
- Meliá's own site says the mall is "Adjacent to Meliá Yangon ... Accessible via a covered walkway" (melia.com hotel page, read 2026-11-16).
- Press from 2016 says it is "directly connected to the hotel" (hoteldesigns.net, journaldespalaces.com).
- Wikipedia: the hotel "is attached to the plaza".
- CTBUH Skyscraper Center lists Myanmar Plaza, Myanmar Centre Tower and Meliá Yangon as ONE building, "Myanmar Center Phase 1": 26 floors above ground, 2 basements, office/hotel/retail, completed 2015.

**What the photos show (aerial photos 51247 / 51248 on the Melia gallery, plus Commons exteriors):**
- The hotel tower is an L-shape: a tall main slab plus a lower stepped wing with green roof terraces. It stands on its own low podium of about 3 floors, which carries the rooftop pool deck.
- The mall is a separate low podium of about 5 floors with a large white roof. It sits directly next to the hotel podium on the hotel's west side (left in 51248). The two podiums touch at the back of the hotel. The Myanmar Centre office tower rises from the far end of the mall.
- So in practice it is a joined complex. The hotel and mall podiums abut, and the "covered walkway" is a short enclosed link between them.

**What is NOT known:**
- No photo or text says which floor the walkway is on, or what it looks like inside.
- No floor plan was found.
- No review describing the route was readable: TripAdvisor and the Melia reviews were blocked or had nothing on it.

**Build rule:**
- Build the hotel and mall as two touching podiums.
- Do NOT build the interior of the walkway. At most, place a closed door marked to the mall at the hotel side.
- The mall's own entrances (Commons photo 5386: glass doors under a "MYANMAR PLAZA" sign with a canopy and white bollards) are photographed and can be built.

## Mall escalators

Sources: Commons photos `Myanmar_Plaza_IMG_20180406_190503_yankin_yangon.jpg` and `Myanmar_Plaza_IMG_20180406_191522_yangoon.jpg` (both by Bjoertvedt, 2018, CC BY-SA 4.0, downloaded here). OPPLE lighting case study: atrium roof 20 m, mall of 5 floors.

**Atrium:**
- A large round/oval central void through all 5 levels (G + 4). Each gallery edge is a continuous curve.
- The ceiling is a flat grid of square perforated metal panels, beige-grey (200,195,180), with round recessed lights and a few big round high-bay glows.
- Roof is about 20 m above the atrium floor, so floor-to-floor is about 4.5 to 5 m.
- The top-floor fascia is a band of warm gold / timber-tone panels, (200,140,70) when lit, running round the void under the ceiling.
- **Gallery edges:**
  - The slab edge is a white or very light fascia about 1.2 m deep. In 2018 it was wrapped in yellow sponsor banners, so the real colour is light beige/white (235,230,220).
  - On top is a clear glass balustrade about 1.1 m high, on small stainless posts, with a stainless top rail.
- **Atrium floor:** Event space. In 2018 it held artificial grass, a big clear dome and tables. Normally it is polished stone.

**Escalators:**
- **Layout:**
  - Pairs of single-width escalators, up and down side by side, criss-cross the void.
  - Each pair rises one floor along a straight run, about 10 to 11 m long on plan for about 5 m rise at 30°.
  - Upper pairs cross the atrium diagonally between galleries, so seen from below they appear stacked in a scissor pattern.
- **Width:** One adult per step, sometimes two children, so the step is about 1.0 m (1000 mm type). Each unit is about 1.6 m wide overall. A pair with its centre deck is about 3.4 m.
- **Side cladding (under-truss and outer side panels):**
  - Solid warm brown / copper-bronze metal panels, sRGB about (125,65,45). They read as reddish brown in both photos.
  - The underside is the same brown, with a lighter edge strip.
- **Outer deck / balustrade:**
  - The flat top cover strip and the outer skirting between handrail and side are white/light metal, (230,230,230).
  - In 2018 this was wrapped in "SAMSUNG Galaxy S9" vinyl. The base colour is white or silver.
- **Balustrade:** Clear glass panels about 0.9 m high above the step nose.
- **Handrail:** Black rubber, (20,20,20).
- **Steps:** Brushed aluminium / silver grey, (150,150,150), with yellow demarcation edges (a standard detail; too small to confirm in the photo).
- **Landing:** Comb plates are silver. The floor around them is the mall's polished granite.

**Mall corridor floor (Ooredoo photo):**
- Polished beige/grey speckled granite tiles, about 0.6 x 0.6 m, (175,168,155).
- Bordered by a band of warmer golden-beige stone, (195,170,120), along the shopfront line.
- Shopfront bulkheads are white, about 1.2 m deep, above shop openings about 3.2 m high.
- The corridor ceiling is white plaster at about 4 m, with round recessed downlights.
