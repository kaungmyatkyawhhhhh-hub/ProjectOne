# Reference photos

Real car photos for matching clusters and interiors. For looking at only (copying dial layouts), not for use inside the game.

## Bugatti Veyron (`bugotti_veyron`)
- `veyron_cluster_lit_vitesse.jpg`: the best one. A Veyron Grand Sport Vitesse cluster, lit, close up (ablogtowatch.com, "Regular guy drives Bugatti Veyron"; © the site). Five round dials on black, each in a thin silver ring:
  - Middle, biggest: rev counter 0-8 (x1000), white numbers, a wide blue band round the dial, red needle with a silver hub, small grey screen low in the middle, "1/min x 1000".
  - Left: power meter 0-1200 PS (the Vitesse; the 16.4 reads 0-1001), blue band, red needle.
  - Right: speedometer 0-280 mph (km/h on the inner ring), blue band, red needle.
  - Two small dials above, between them (fuel and oil temperature).
- `veyron_interior.jpg`: 2012 Veyron Grand Sport dash (Wikimedia Commons, "2012 Bugatti Veyron G.S. interior"). `veyron_cluster_crop.png`: its cluster cut out (blurry).
- `veyron_cockpit_16-4.jpg`: 2006 Veyron 16.4 cockpit (Commons, "Bugatti Veyron 2006 Cockpit CECF 9April2011 (14600910255)"): the 16.4 layout is the same (power meter left, speedo right).

## Lamborghini Aventador LP700-4 (`lambrini_lp700`)
- `aventador_lp700_cockpit_lit.jpg` + `aventador_lp700_cluster_lit_crop.jpg`: LP700-4 at Geneva with the cluster on (Commons, "Lamborghini interior (5488709918)", CC BY 2.0). Red gear letter (N) in the middle of the round dial, a cyan-blue glow on the silver ring, cyan odometer digits under it, left screen: white clock (21:4x), date and outside temperature on dark navy with thin amber lines.
- `aventador_lp700_cluster_strada_video.jpg` (+ `_2`): STRADA mode, frames from YouTube "Aventador instrament cluster" (GR5t2pk3YPc): white rev numbers 0-9 round a big ring, red from 8.5, a cyan inner ring with an orange line, a red-tipped needle, the speed scale round the outside, the clock screen left in amber.
- `aventador_lp700_cluster_startup_video.jpg`: start-up, from YouTube "Lamborghini Aventador LP700-4 - StartUp & Stop" (mvOh-_ErsJs).
- `aventador_lp700_dash.jpg`: the cluster switched off (Commons, "CES 2012 - NVIDIA Lamborghini (6764011133)"): shape of the hood, the big silver ring and the angular side screens.

## Lamborghini Aventador SV (`lambrini_lp750`)
- `aventador_sv_cluster_lit_video.jpg` (+ `_2`): the real SV cluster lit, frames from YouTube "2017 Lamborghini Aventador SV gauge cluster startup" (lI_h6DZGrC4): yellow middle with a wide rev arc 2-10 (red from 9), big black N, R 1234567 A strip, STRADA, odometer, "Fasten Seatbelt"; left: clock, date, °F and two bar gauges on yellow; right: fuel range and the car picture.
- `aventador_sv_cockpit.jpg`: SV cockpit (Commons, "Aventador int.jpg"); the cluster can't be read. The SV shows its model's own cluster (owner, 2026-10-23).

## Other
- `gallardo_cluster.jpg`: Lamborghini Gallardo Spyder dashboard (Commons calls it Aventador, but it's a Gallardo): rev counter left with red needle and orange-red numbers, speedometer right, small round gauges at both ends, a small screen in the middle. Good for the Gallardo LP570.
