# Reference photos

Real car photos for matching clusters and interiors (from Wikimedia Commons, free licence: credit the photographer if shown in the game).

- `veyron_interior.jpg`: Bugatti Veyron Grand Sport dashboard ("2012 Bugatti Veyron G.S. interior", Wikimedia Commons).
- `veyron_cluster_crop.png`: the cluster cut out and enlarged (blurry). Three round dials in silver rings on black:
  - Left: power meter (0 to 1001).
  - Middle, biggest: rev counter (0 to 7+, red near 6.5).
  - Right: speedometer in km/h (up to about 400).
  - White needles with silver hubs.

- `gallardo_cluster.jpg`: Lamborghini Gallardo Spyder dashboard (Commons calls it Aventador, but it's a Gallardo): rev counter left with red needle and orange-red numbers, speedometer right, small round gauges at both ends, a small screen in the middle. Good for the Gallardo LP570.
