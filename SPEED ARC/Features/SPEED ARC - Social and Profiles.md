# SPEED ARC - Social and Profiles

Back to [[00 SPEED ARC Home|SPEED ARC Home]]

GDD 14 + 18, built in M4.

## Titles and the tag over your head
- Titles: FIRST RACE, FIRST WIN, 10 / 100 / 1000 WINS, PARK RUNNER, STADIUM STAR (Go Pro), HOMETOWN / PARK / STADIUM CHAMPION (Champion rank), PB HUNTER (10 PBs), WEEKLY CHAMPION / RUNNER-UP / THIRD.
- A new title is worn right away; change it in your profile (CHANGE TITLE).
- Over the head: the title (gold), then "RANK - L1   3 MEDALS".

## Profile
- Tap another runner (not while training or racing) = VIEW PROFILE / RACE THEM / ADD FRIEND. Tap your own rank badge = your profile.
- Shows: name, title, league + rank, speed, top speed in this league, wins, playtime, medals, best Sprint per area, equipped coaches, trail.

## RACE THEM
- A request card for the other runner (RACE! / NOT NOW, 15 s). Both must be in the same area and free.
- A private 1v1 Sprint on the outside lanes. Coins only for the first 3 a day (1st 3 min, 2nd 1.5 min); no medals or league wins.

## Friends and invites
- **Friend Boost:** +10% training per friend in the server, max +30%, with a toast like "Maya is here! Friend Boost +10%".
- **INVITE** (left menu): Roblox's invite screen. A friend who joins from your invite gives you +10% training for 30 min of play.
- **Favourite prompt:** once, after your first Go Pro.

## Where it lives
`Server/Services/SocialService`, `Server/Services/SessionBoosts`, `Client/Controllers/SocialController`.
