# MEGA BITE: ChatGPT prompt, Reef Shark concept turnaround (design first, then model)

Why: the owner judged every code-invented shark "so bad, even compared with games that failed". Pros DESIGN the shape first (a concept/turnaround), then model it. ChatGPT designs; Claude traces the side/top/front silhouettes in Blender and builds the low-poly model to match ([[2026-11-09 MEGA BITE - Model quality research report]]).
Processing: key the magenta, extract the side + top silhouettes (profile curves), the front view (cross-section), and the fin outlines; loft the low-poly cage to fit them; compare the render against the sheet (silhouette IoU > 0.9).

```text
Create ONE image, 1536x1024. A professional 3D game character MODEL SHEET (orthographic turnaround) of ONE original cartoon shark for a Roblox game, shown as a clean stylized LOW-POLY 3D model with flat-shaded facets (large clear polygon planes, hard edges, no smooth shading). Show three orthographic views of the SAME shark at the SAME scale, each with wide empty space around it, nothing touching the image edges or each other. TOP ROW: the exact SIDE VIEW (left side, nose pointing RIGHT), the whole shark large and centred across the width. BOTTOM ROW LEFT: the exact TOP VIEW from directly above (nose pointing RIGHT), the same length as the side view and directly under it. BOTTOM ROW RIGHT: the exact FRONT VIEW looking straight at the nose. The shark: a powerful, sleek-but-chunky reef shark about 4.5 times as long as it is tall, deepest one third back from the snout, a short rounded cone snout, a wedge-shaped head with a sharp angular brow ridge over a small GLOWING CYAN eye, a wide mouth open in a menacing grin with a dark maroon inside and big white triangular blade teeth on the top and bottom jaws (about 6 large teeth per side, sizes varied), 5 dark gill slits, a tall swept-back triangular dorsal fin, long swept pectoral fins angled down and back, a small second dorsal fin, small pelvic and anal fins, a big crescent tail with a longer upper lobe, a ridge on the tail stock. Colours: slate blue-grey back (#4E6F8C), white belly (#EEF1F3) with a sharp jagged zig-zag boundary along the whole flank, black tips on every fin. Style: high-quality stylized low-poly 3D like the best Roblox fishing and shark games, chunky readable shapes, soft even studio lighting, no outlines, no texture noise, no scales, no blood, no scars, cool and a little scary but appealing to kids. Background: flat solid MAGENTA #FF00FF, no gradient, no floor, no shadow on the background, no magenta anywhere in the shark. NO text, NO numbers, NO labels, NO arrows, NO grid lines. NOT a photo, not realistic.
```
