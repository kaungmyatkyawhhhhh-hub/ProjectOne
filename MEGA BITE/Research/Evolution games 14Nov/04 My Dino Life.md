## Steal for MEGA BITE
1. Several evolution LINES picked at the start (by biome) - for us: by ocean zone.
2. Evolution moment shows the free next form + a locked premium variant beside it.
3. A cash-bought permanent upgrade web with "your upgrades save forever!" and a time-locked node.
4. 1 free revive per hour that keeps your form.
5. A white beam to the nearest food for the first quest.
6. Each form gets its own named special on R with a one-line description toast.
7. Main menu with a live "UPDATE IN" countdown + a sticky-note changelog with the code.

## Avoid
- Spawn-camping: new players spawn next to big predators (4 deaths in 20 min).
- Aquatic forms dying instantly when they touch the shore without a clear warning.
- A long intro (cinematic + menu + picker) before the first second of play.
