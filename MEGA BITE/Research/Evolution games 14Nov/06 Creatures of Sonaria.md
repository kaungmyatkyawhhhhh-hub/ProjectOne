## Steal for MEGA BITE
1. Trading-card starter pick (role name in colour + 4-5 trait bullets) - maybe one paid gold card.
2. A painted MASCOT guide that speaks the tutorial in a ribbon.
3. Paint-your-creature layers (primary/secondary/pattern/eye) with Randomize + share codes.
4. Silhouette teasers for every new creature on the events tab, "Added This Week" in the description.
5. Scent/sniff trail to water/food = an in-world guide that fits the fiction (for us: "sense" sonar pulse to prey).
6. Age growth stages (baby -> adult) as a second progression axis.
7. Vocal calls (friendly / aggressive) for social play.

## Avoid
- Hiding basic needs (drink/eat) behind non-obvious keys.
- 3 screens before you can move.
