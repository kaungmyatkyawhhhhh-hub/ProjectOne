## Play log
### Start (06:22-06:28)
- No lobby: loading -> full-screen choice over an animated water-light background (soft caustic waves + drifting bubbles):
  "what do you eat?"  big green "plants" (filter mouth) vs big coral "meat" (jaws). Rounded friendly font (Nunito-like, all
  lowercase, white with dark outline). Diet = your playstyle from second 1.
- You start INSIDE AN EGG next to a huge membrane cell: top-left objective "hatch / push your way out", centre "space to push".
  Tap Space a few times -> out. Then "grow / every half stage you get to pick a card".
- VIEW: 2.5D TOP-DOWN (camera looks straight down at a pond plane; depth layers of blurred bubbles/plankton = parallax). Feels
  like Agar.io / Spore cell stage. Scale bar bottom-left "10 µm" -> "20 µm" (shows your real size; grows as you grow).
- Character art: GLOSSY 3D CUTE: blobby bodies, big googly eyes (2-3), tusks/horns, spots, cilia hairs. Every creature
  readable as a character. Very high appeal for kids. My cell "zinadrop": dark speckled blob + 2 googly eyes + 2 white
  mandibles. Others: green spiky slug with eye-stalks, pink striped "lancer", green blob with spots, pink spiky "colossus".
- MOVEMENT: WASD swims (smooth glide, turns to face the direction), SPACE = DASH (white key cap button "space dash" bottom right),
  eating = touching. Meat diet: green plant bits give nothing; you take down cells.
- HUD (minimal, all frosted-glass rounded rects, lowercase):
  * top centre pill: "● next event 3:07" countdown;
  * top right "biggest here" leaderboard: name / "era 13" (gold) / size "130 µm";
  * bottom centre: big "12 dna" counter, a 3D DNA DOUBLE-HELIX progress bar (beads light up in colour as you fill it),
    "zinadrop  stage 1 ... 12/35 to stage 2", a coral health bar under it;
  * bottom left "dna x2.0  boost 4:47" (new player 2x boost) + µm scale bar;
  * left: "index 4/316 found (tab)", gift (red badge), pond pass (star bookmark), settings.
- BOSS EVENT: "the colossus rises / something huge is coming. team up and take it down" - big coral title animates in letter by
  letter ("the colo..."), a long coral boss HP bar at the top "the colossus", an off-screen ARROW at the edge pointing to it
  labelled "colossus". The colossus is a PLAYER ("coconut the colossus", era 13) - the biggest player becomes the server boss.
- KILL FEED under the event pill: "coconut drilled 4442qqwsawrjhf" (verb = the attack part used: drilled, ...).
- INDEX: seeing a new creature type adds it ("+ indexed lancer", "+ indexed abyssal gen 3") - 316+ entries (a Pokedex by sight).
- GIFTS panel ("free stuff for playing. come back every day for more"): every day (miss a day = start over): d1 +80 dna, d2 +1
  point, d3 2 free rerolls, d4 10 min 2x dna, d5 +2 points, d6 rare card +120 dna, d7 +4 points + 15 min 2x dna. "for playing
  today" timeline: 3 min +60 dna, 8 min 1 free reroll, 15 min +1 point, 25 min +150 dna, 40 min +2 points (progress bars,
  "next gift in 1:44 of play"). Bottom: join the group (+2 points and dna), like the game (+2 points, "play 3 min" first),
  bring friends ("eat together. or eat them").
- QUESTS panel (appears after the first gift): "dash 15 times 3/15", "take down 6 cells 1/6", "meet a mauler 0/1", "6m left",
  "this week 0/3  2d left".
- POND PASS (battle pass): "season 2: first bloom · ends in 23d 0h", tier 0 "40/100 xp to tier 1", buttons "finish all R$399",
  "+5 tiers R$99", "unlock premium R$199". Free row: +100 dna, 1 roll, +1 point, 2 card rerolls, 1 roll, +150 dna ...;

  icons in a flat 2-tone style. Calm, "indie" feel (matches the ambient drone music list).
