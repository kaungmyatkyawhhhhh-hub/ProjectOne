  / 3 Select / 4 Transform / 5 Paint; top Menu / Saves / Spawn / Help. Key hints: U reset rotation, X hold delete, R T Y rotate, Left

- SPAWN dialog: "All good! You can spawn! - You won't lose any DNA on death." Back / Spawn.
- WORLD: spawned tiny in TALL REALISTIC GRASS on a big low-poly island (blocky trees, cliffs, a lake, ocean with realistic water).
  HUD = only 3 bars top centre (green health 11, yellow food 26, blue DNA bar 10000) + "Return Home" + "Spawn Shield Active" + "Age: 2
  (+1 XP)" (age ticks every ~20 s and gives XP/DNA). No quest, no arrow, no tips in the world.
