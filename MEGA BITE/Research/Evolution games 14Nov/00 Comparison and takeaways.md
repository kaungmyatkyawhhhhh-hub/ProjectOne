## Two genres hide under "evolution"
1. ARCADE EVOLUTION SIMS (Bug Evolution, Ant Evolution, Evolve a Cell, My Dino Life): eat orbs -> bar fills -> evolve into the next
   form in seconds/minutes; gacha + boosts; short sessions; kids 7-12. Growth = fast ladders (16-20 stages).
2. CREATURE SURVIVAL RPGs (Creatures of Sonaria, Primeval Earth, Evolve 3D): hunger/thirst/stamina, ageing, roleplay, packs, many
   species unlocked with currency; long sessions; older/dedicated fans. Growth = age up + collect species.
MEGA BITE (fish -> shark -> leviathan) sits in genre 1 with a hint of genre 2 (zones, size, predators). The money and CCU now are in
genre 1 games that launch fast (Ant Evolution 8.4K at 17 days) and in genre 2's one giant (Sonaria).

## What every winner does
- First evolution in under 60 s (Ant Evolution: 20 s). Losers make you sit through menus/cinematics/pickers first.
- One visible next step at all times: quest card with key hint, chevron/beam/scent trail to the target.
- A ladder you can SEE: current -> next portraits, locked silhouettes with names you want ("Star Dragon Ant", "Omega Ant").
- Each evolution = a new move/special with a toast (Roll, Pounce, Venom Spit).
- Server-wide events on a timer with big titles (Feed the Queen, Feeding Frenzy, Colossus, Meteor Shower, Golden Cell).
- Soft death: drop one stage / descendant keeps 80% / 1 free revive per hour. Hard deaths (My Dino Life spawn-killing) = low ratings.
- Daily 7-day streak + playtime gifts + 3 daily quests + achievements; a live "update in" countdown and weekly admin abuse.
- Icons/thumbnails: "Hour 1 -> Hour 100" or "tiny -> huge" before/after, a big character face, countdown icons before updates.

## Monetization patterns seen (ranked by aggressiveness)
1. Always-on Robux shelf on the gameplay screen (Ant Evolution: 6 buttons). Highest CCU growth, but cheap-looking.
2. Limited stock packs with counters + global purchase banners + copy numbers (Ant Evolution R$499 / R$4,499).
3. Gacha with pity counters and shown odds (mutations, pets, Tikits, rare looks).
4. Paid next-step shortcuts: Evolve Now R$25, Hatch Now R$25, Grow Now R$19, card reroll R$19, revive R$25, skip reward timer R$80.
5. Premium variant beside the free evolution (My Dino Life "Spider Dilophosaurus").
6. Fair: cosmetics, VIP, 2x passes, creature unlocks also buyable with earned currency (My Dino Life dual prices, Sonaria Shooms).

## MEGA BITE action list (proposal, not built)
1. Make the first evolution happen within ~30 s of spawning (minnow -> next fish), with a portrait bar "Minnow -> ???".
2. Add a glowing trail/sonar ping to the nearest food for the first 3 objectives; one objective line top-centre.
3. Give each evolution a named special with a toast + key hint (we already have sprint/dart; add one per fish).
4. Server event every 5-8 min with a countdown pill: "Feeding Frenzy", "Leviathan rises" (biggest player = boss bar), "Golden fish".
5. Soft death: respawn one evolution lower or "keep 80%" + 1 free revive/hour; spawn protection bubble; never spawn next to a big predator.
6. Evolution ladder panel with locked silhouettes and cool names (abyss/kaiju names for the late tiers).
7. Index = character select (respawn as any fish you've found) - Evolve a Cell's best idea.
8. Thumbnail: "Hour 1 -> Hour 100" minnow -> leviathan underwater; icon: one big shark face; countdown icon before updates.
9. Monetization: VIP + 2x + cosmetics + a fair gacha with pity; avoid a permanent Robux wall and paid hatch buttons on soft-locks.
10. Calm, consistent UI style (Evolve a Cell) beats loud wood/stud panels for "quality" ratings.
