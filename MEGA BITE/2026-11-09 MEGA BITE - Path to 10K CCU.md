# MEGA BITE: the path to 10K+ CCU (9 Nov)

Sources: [[2026-11-09 MEGA BITE - Notes - Market and competitors]], [[2026-11-09 MEGA BITE - Notes - Money, LiveOps and marketing]], [[K01 Roblox platform and discovery]], [[K02 First session and retention]], [[K08 LiveOps, updates and analytics]], [[2026-11-09 MEGA BITE - Debate kids simulation]] (launch = $200 of ads, no YouTubers).

## What 10K means
| Peak CCU | Minutes per player per day | Daily players needed (avg CCU ≈ 60% of peak) |
|---|---|---|
| 1,000 | 15 / 20 / 25 | ~58K / 43K / 35K |
| **10,000** | 15 / 20 / 25 | **~576K / 432K / 346K** |
- The shark genre today: ~1,280 CCU across all 7 tracked games. All-time peaks: SharkBite 2 61K (2022), Shark Evolution 18.7K (2020, 40-min sessions), Be Fish 6K (2025, 97% likes, 19 min), Be a Shark 2.2K, DEVOUR 1.7K (2026, fell 85%).
- **Ads can't do it:** $100 buys ~550-12,500 plays; 10K needs hundreds of thousands of players a day. With no YouTubers, the only engine big enough is **Roblox Home recommending the game** + **players bringing friends** + **free short videos**.

## What Roblox Home rewards (since Jun 2026, 28-day window)
D1 / D2-7 / D8-28 retention of players who came from Home, playtime per player (capped 60 min/day), days played, **co-play days**, spend days and Robux. Bounces under 60 s and 61-180 s count against you. So 10K = top-percent retention + friends playing together, not more features.

| Stage | Peak CCU | Gate to move on | What we do |

| 4. 10K+ | 10K+ | holds through a tentpole | Christmas break 2026 / Shark Week late Jul 2027, a Megalodon/prehistoric season, admin-event before each update |
Benchmarks (K02): D1 median 10.3% / top 1% 22%; D7 median 1.6% / top 1% 9%; session median 9.8 min / top 5% 16.6 min. **10K needs top-1% retention.**

## The 6 levers (what moves those numbers)
1. **Retention first:** the first 5 minutes (sims: 89% → aim 95% on the real build), something to chase every session (7 sharks, mutations, Fish Index, Fossilize), a reason to come back (daily streak, offline growth in the reef, the weekly event shark).
2. **Co-play (a direct Home signal):** Schools at launch, a +10% friend bonus, the co-op squid boss, bring-a-friend rewards, a "join friend" button. Friend groups = the viral loop that replaces YouTubers.
3. **Kids/Select access** (most of Roblox's young audience): start the account checks + Premium clock now; get the 250 engaged 16+ plays in launch week.
4. **The game page** (turns impressions into plays): ≥90% likes, real-gameplay thumbnails from our models, the page video, no [ALPHA], a short promise line.
5. **Free short videos (the owner's own TikTok/Shorts, video pipeline skill):** a Megalodon eating a whole bait ball, BREACH from below, "Minnow to Megalodon" time-lapse, the crown shark getting taken down. Hook in second 1, loop ending. Start once the slice passes the gate.
6. **Updates that never stop:** every Saturday 15:00 UTC (+ a code, a group shout, a clip), a bigger drop every 2-4 weeks, an admin event 1 h before. Silence killed SharkBite-era and Shark Evolution-era games.

## Honest odds
10K+ is the top of this genre: only 2 shark games ever passed it. It's reachable only if the retention numbers hit the top-1% bar AND a tentpole (Christmas / Shark Week) lands on a strong update. A realistic first goal: hold 500-1K CCU with D1 ≥ 20% after Kids/Select opens; 10K is the stage-4 goal, months after launch.
